# Generalized Kolakoski sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Kolakoski sequence](kolakoski-sequence.md)

The generalized Kolakoski sequence is the generalization of the [Kolakoski sequence](kolakoski-sequence.md) where you don't need to restrict yourself to 1,2 but can instead use any a,b pair.
