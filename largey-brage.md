# Largey Brage

↑ **Parent:** [Google](google-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Dynamic duo](dynamic-duo.md)

The outcome of [Larry Page](larry-page.md) and [Sergey Brin](sergey-brin.md) performing a [Dragon Ball](dragon-ball.md) [fusion dance](https://dragonball.fandom.com/wiki/Fusion_Dance).

Both of them attended [Montessori education](montessori-education.md) at some point. Interesting! Mentioned in a talk by Sergey and highlighted at [The Google Story](the-google-story.md).

They stepped down from leading [Google](google-split.md) roles in 2019: [https://www.npr.org/2019/12/03/784570156/google-founders-brin-page-step-down-pichai-takes-over-as-alphabet-ceo](https://www.npr.org/2019/12/03/784570156/google-founders-brin-page-step-down-pichai-takes-over-as-alphabet-ceo)

But in 2023 they were somewhat pulled back in by the [AI](artificial-intelligence-split.md) race and competition with [ChatGPT](chatgpt.md).[https://archive.ph/TPq1k](https://archive.ph/TPq1k)

As [The Google Story](the-google-story.md) puts it about [Largey](largey-brage.md):

> Scholarship was not just emphasized in their homes; it was treasured.

[Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) likes that.
