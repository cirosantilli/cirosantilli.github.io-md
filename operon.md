# Operon

↑ **Parent:** [Transcription (biology)](transcription-biology.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operon)

Sequence of genes under a single [promoter](promoter-genetics.md). For an example, see [E. Coli K-12 MG1655 operon thrLABC](e-coli-k-12-mg1655-operon-thrlabc.md).

A single [operon](operon.md) may produce multiple different [transcription units](transcription-unit.md) depending on certain conditions, see: [operon vs transcription unit](operon-vs-transcription-unit.md).
