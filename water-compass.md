# Water compass

↑ **Parent:** [Compass](compass.md)

We define a "water compass" as a compass made by placing a magnet floating on a water surface to reduce friction and allow it to align with the [Earth's magnetic field](earth-s-magnetic-field.md). This is a common children's scientific experiment.
