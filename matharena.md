# MathArena

↑ **Parent:** [List of math AI benchmarks](list-of-math-ai-benchmarks.md)

[https://matharena.ai/](https://matharena.ai/)

This project tests various models against various competitions.

How they "ensure" that models are not contaminated:

>  By evaluating models as soon as new problems are released, we effectively eliminate the risk of contamination

Most of their problems come from [high school knowledge olympiads](pre-university-knowledge-olympiad.md) and they are therefore completely irrelevant for 2025 [LLMs](large-language-model.md).
