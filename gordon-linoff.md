# Gordon Linoff

↑ **Parent:** [List of Stack Overflow users](list-of-stack-overflow-users.md)

[https://stackoverflow.com/users/1144035/gordon-linoff](https://stackoverflow.com/users/1144035/gordon-linoff)

Infinitely many [SQL](sql-split.md) answers.

As mentioned at [Ciro Santilli's Stack Overflow contributions](ciro-santilli-s-stack-overflow-contributions.md), he just answers every semi-duplicate immediatly as it is asked, and is therefore able to overcome the [Stack Overflow maximum 200 daily reputation limit](stack-overflow-maximum-200-daily-reputation-limit.md) by far. E.g. in 2018, Gordon [reached 135k](https://stackexchange.com/leagues/1/year/stackoverflow/2018-01-01?sort=reputationchange) ([archive](https://web.archive.org/web/20191013090451/https://stackexchange.com/leagues/1/year/stackoverflow/2018-01-01?sort=reputationchange)), thus almost double the 73k yearly limit due to the 200 daily limit, all of that with accepts.

This is in contrast to [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md)'s contribution style which is to only answer questions as he needs the subject, or generally important questions that aroused his interest.

2014 [Blog](blog.md) post describing his activity: [http://blog.data-miners.com/2014/08/an-achievement-on-stack-overflow.html](http://blog.data-miners.com/2014/08/an-achievement-on-stack-overflow.html), key quote:

> For a few months, I sporadically answered questions. Then, in the first week of May, my Mom's younger brother passed away. That meant lots of time hanging around family, planning the funeral, and the like. Answering questions on Stack Overflow turned out to be a good way to get away from things. So, I became more intent.

so that suggests his contributions also take a [meditative](meditation.md) value.

[http://www.data-miners.com/linoff.htm](http://www.data-miners.com/linoff.htm) mentions he's an SQL consultant that consulted for several big companies.

[LinkedIn](linkedin.md) profile: [https://www.linkedin.com/in/gordonlinoff/](https://www.linkedin.com/in/gordonlinoff/) says he now works at the [New York Times](new-york-times.md).

2021 [Reddit](reddit.md) thread about him: [https://www.reddit.com/r/programming/comments/puok1h/a_single_person_answered_76k_questions_about_sql/](https://www.reddit.com/r/programming/comments/puok1h/a_single_person_answered_76k_questions_about_sql/) mentions that by then he had:

> answered 76k questions about SQL on StackOverflow. Averaging 22.8 answers per day, every day, for the past 8.6 years.
