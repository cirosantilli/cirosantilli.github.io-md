# X-ray crystallography

↑ **Parent:** [Type of microscopy](type-of-microscopy.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Crystallography](crystallography.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/X-ray_crystallography)

One of its main applications is to determine the 3D structure of [proteins](protein-split.md).

Sometimes you are not able to crystallize the proteins however, and the method cannot be used.

Crystallizing is not simple because:
- you need a considerable amount of the protein
- sometimes it only crystallizes if you add some extra small chemical that stabilizes it

[Cryogenic electron microscopy](cryogenic-electron-microscopy.md) can sometimes determine the structures of proteins that failed crystallization.
