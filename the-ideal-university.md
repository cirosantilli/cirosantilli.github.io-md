# The ideal university

↑ **Parent:** [University](university-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Cirism](cirism-split.md), [Education is broken](education-is-broken.md)

This is a version of [free gifted education](free-gifted-education.md) but more focused on [university](university-split.md).

This is [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md)'s ideal university system. It is a system that actually lives up to the name "[Open University](open-university.md)":
- no enrolment, no prerequisites. [Exam as a service](exam-as-a-service.md) examination style, likely free to anyone who wants to take them, only to determine:
  - who gets to use physical facilities, notably [laboratories](laboratory.md)
  - which students do you want to pick as apprentices/workers/[PhDs](doctor-of-philosophy.md)
- no tuition fees: [free gifted education](free-gifted-education.md)
- [school must offer free accommodation for students](school-must-offer-free-accommodation-for-students.md)
- [force teachers to publish their teaching material with an open license](force-public-university-teachers-to-publish-their-teaching-material-with-an-open-license.md)
- [how to teach](how-to-teach-split.md)

So far as of the early 2020's, the university that comes closest to some but not all of these principles is the [University of the People](university-of-the-people.md). It's sad that it's such a crappy unknown thing, but it is what it is.
