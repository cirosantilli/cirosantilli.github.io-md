# Algebra over a field

↑ **Parent:** [Vector field](vector-field.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebra_over_a_field)

A [vector field](vector-field.md) with a [bilinear map](bilinear-map.md) into itself, which we can also call a "vector product".

Note that the vector product does not have to be neither [associative](associative-property.md) nor [commutative](commutative-property.md).

Examples: [https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Algebra_over_a_field&oldid=1035146107#Motivating_examples](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Algebra_over_a_field&oldid=1035146107#Motivating_examples)
- [complex numbers](complex-number.md), i.e. [$\R^2$](real-plane.md) with complex number multiplication
- [$\R^3$](real-coordinate-space-of-dimension-three.md) with the [cross product](cross-product.md)
- [quaternions](quaternion.md), i.e. [$\R^4$](real-coordinate-space-of-dimension-four.md) with the quaternion multiplication
