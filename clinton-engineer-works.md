# Clinton Engineer Works

↑ **Parent:** [Oak Ridge National Laboratory](oak-ridge-national-laboratory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clinton_Engineer_Works)

Precursor organization to the [Oak Ridge National Laboratory](oak-ridge-national-laboratory.md), name that it took in January 1948.

Produced the [enriched uranium](enriched-uranium.md) used for [Little Boy](little-boy.md), located in the area/predecessor of [Oak Ridge National Laboratory](oak-ridge-national-laboratory.md).
