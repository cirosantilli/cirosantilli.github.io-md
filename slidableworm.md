# SlidableWorm

↑ **Parent:** [WormAtlas](wormatlas.md)

[https://www.wormatlas.org/SW/SW.php/index_html5.html](https://www.wormatlas.org/SW/SW.php/index_html5.html)

Fantastic resouce that contains cross sections of [C. elegans](caenorhabditis-elegans.md) at various lengths of its body. Presumably frozen and cut with a [Microtome](microtome.md) and then scanned with [electron microscopy](electron-microscope.md).

Shame that there are so many parts missing.

It's the [Visible Human Project](visible-human-project.md), but for [C. elegans](caenorhabditis-elegans.md)!
