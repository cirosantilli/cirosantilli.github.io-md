# Li sao (guqin song)

↑ **Parent:** [Guqin piece](guqin-piece.md)

[https://www.facebook.com/131402556881886/posts/655763214445815/](https://www.facebook.com/131402556881886/posts/655763214445815/) gives an origin:

> Li Sao was composed by Cheng Kangshi in late [Tang dynasty](tang-dynasty.md) based on the [poem Li Sao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Li_Sao), authored by Qu Yuan (340-278 BC) in the [Warring States period](warring-states-period.md) of ancient China.

The [silkqin.com](silkqin-com.md) entry: [http://www.silkqin.com/04qart/07sqmp/57ls.htm](http://www.silkqin.com/04qart/07sqmp/57ls.htm) does not mention this however.
