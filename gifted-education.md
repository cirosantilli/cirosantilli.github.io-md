# Gifted education

↑ **Parent:** [Education](education-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gifted_education)

If [school weren't bullshit](ourbigbook-com/motivation.md), 99% of students would be in gifted education for what they truly love and are good at.

What is sad about many programs is that they are exclusivist and non scalable, selecting people some how and non scalably educating them. We need a more "here's some projects let's do them whoever can" approach to things, maybe like [Google Summer of Code](google-summer-of-code.md).
