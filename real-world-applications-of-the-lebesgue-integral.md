# Real world applications of the Lebesgue integral

↑ **Parent:** [Lebesgue integral vs Riemann integral](lebesgue-integral-vs-riemann-integral.md)

In "practice" it is likely "useless", because the functions that it can integrate that Riemann can't are just too funky to appear in practice :-)

Its value is much more indirect and subtle, as in "it serves as a solid basis of [quantum mechanics](quantum-mechanics-split.md)" due to the definition of [Hilbert spaces](hilbert-space.md).
