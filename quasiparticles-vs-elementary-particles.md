# Quasiparticles vs elementary particles

↑ **Parent:** [Quasiparticle](quasiparticle.md)

As a phisicist once amazingly put it in a talk Ciro watched:

> It all depends on how much energy you have to probe nature with. Previously, we thought [protons](proton.md) were [elementary particles](elementary-particle.md). But then we used more energy and found that they aren't.
> 
> If some [alien](extraterrestrial-life.md) race had even less energy, they might not know about [electrons](electron.md) at all, and could think that [anyons](anyon.md) are actually elementary.
> 
> Being an "elementary particle" is always a possibly temporary label.
