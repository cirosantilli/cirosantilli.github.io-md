<h1 id="becquerel-s-rays">Becquerel's rays</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Henri Becquerel](henri-becquerel.md)

These must have been [gamma rays](gamma-ray.md).

> Just before he left [Cambridge](cambridge.md) for Montreal in 1898, [Rutherford](ernest-rutherford.md) conducted a simple, systematic experiment to study the absorption of rays from [uranium](uranium.md). \[...\] In 1901 he determined that [Becquerel's rays](becquerel-s-rays.md) are indeed [electromagnetic rays](electromagnetic-radiation.md). He called them γ (gamma) rays.

This terminology is used e.g. in [Marie Curie's Polonium paper](on-a-new-radioactive-substance-contained-in-pitchblende.md):

> Some minerals containing [uranium](uranium.md) and [thorium](thorium.md) ([pitchblende](uraninite.md), chalcolite, uranite) are very active from the point of view of the emission of [Becquerel rays](becquerel-s-rays.md).
