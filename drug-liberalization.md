# Drug liberalization

↑ **Parent:** [Recreational drug](recreational-drug.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Good](good.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drug_liberalization)

[Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) supports full legalization of all drugs, because he feels that it would be better overall for the world to have cheaper drugs and more [drug addicts](drug-addict.md), but way, way less [organized crime](organized-crime.md).

These should be extremely controlled of course, with extremely high [taxes](tax.md) that puts their price just below the current illegal market, and a complete ban on any positive [advertising](advertising.md).

Ciro believes that maybe the [government](government.md) could even go as far as giving free drugs to [drug addicts](drug-addict.md) so they don't have to [rob](theft.md) to get a fix.

This is notably considering that drug-led organized crime completely dominates and corrupts the [politics](politics.md) of many production and trafficking zones, which are already generally poor [fucked](sexual-intercourse.md) up places to start with:
- [Mexican drug war](mexican-drug-war.md)
- [Golden Crescent](golden-crescent.md)
- [Golden Triangle](golden-triangle-southeast-asia.md)
Ciro's experiences in Brazil such as mentioned at [São Remo, the favela next to USP](sao-remo-the-favela-next-to-usp.md), although much less extreme than the above, also come to mind.

Drug traffic corrupts everything. It prevents development of honest people. It is a [cancer](cancer.md), which we have failed time and time a gain to cure. The only cure is to accept the other less insidious of addiction.
