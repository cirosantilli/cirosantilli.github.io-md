# Prokaryote

↑ **Parent:** [Taxonomy](taxonomy-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Domain (biology)](domain-biology.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prokaryote)

Anything that is not [eukaryote](eukaryote.md), i.e. [archaea](archaea.md) and [bacteria](bacteria.md), see e.g.: [Figure 1. "Coral of life by János Podani (2019)"](coral-of-life.md#image-coral-of-life-by-janos-podani-2019).

Not a [clade](clade.md), and therefore [a term better forgotten](non-clade-groups-are-evil.md)!

A clade name for [arkarya](arkarya.md) is a proposed clade name for [archaea](archaea.md) plus [eukarya](eukaryote.md).
