# Procedural AI training game

↑ **Parent:** [AI game by type](ai-game-by-type.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Procedural generation](procedural-generation.md)

We define a "Procedural AI training game" as an [AI training game](ai-game.md) in which parts of the game are made with [procedural generation](procedural-generation.md).

In more advanced cases, the generation itself can be done with [AI](artificial-intelligence-split.md). This is a possible [Path to AGI](path-to-agi.md) which reduces the need for human intervention in meticulously crafting the AI game: AI training AI.
