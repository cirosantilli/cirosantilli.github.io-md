# Sum of three cubes

↑ **Parent:** [Waring problem with negative numbers allowed](waring-problem-with-negative-numbers-allowed.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Open problem in mathematics](open-problem-in-mathematics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sum_of_three_cubes)

Compared to [Waring's problem](waring-s-problem.md), this is potentially much harder, as we can go infinitely negative in our attempts, there isn't a bound on how many tries we can have for each number.

In other words, it is unlikely to have a [Conjecture reduction to a halting problem](conjecture-reduction-to-a-halting-problem.md).
