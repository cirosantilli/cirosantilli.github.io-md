# Continuous spectrum (functional analysis)

↑ **Parent:** [Spectrum (functional analysis)](spectrum-functional-analysis.md)

Unlike the simple case of a [matrix](matrix.md), in [infinite dimensional](infinite-dimensional.md) vector spaces, the spectrum may be continuous.

The quintessential example of that is the spectrum of the [position operator](position-operator.md) in [quantum mechanics](quantum-mechanics-split.md), in which any [real number](real-number.md) is a possible [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md), since the particle may be found in any position. The associated [eigenvectors](eigenvector.md) are the corresponding [Dirac delta functions](dirac-delta-function.md).
