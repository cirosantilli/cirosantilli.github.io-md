# Chico Buarque

↑ **Parent:** [The Holy Trinity of popular Brazilian music](the-holy-trinity-of-popular-brazilian-music.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chico_Buarque)

Within the [The Holy Trinity of popular Brazilian music](the-holy-trinity-of-popular-brazilian-music.md), Chico tends to approach the more down to Earth and heavy topics.

- <a id="video-quem-te-viu-quem-te-ve-by-chico-buarque"></a>
  **[Video 48](#video-quem-te-viu-quem-te-ve-by-chico-buarque). Quem Te Viu, Quem Te Vê by Chico Buarque.** [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yQZ65SkiSKI). Performed for television for the Hebe Camargo show on TV Record in 1966.

  Present in the [1967 album "Chico Buarque de Hollanda - Volume 2"](https://pt.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chico_Buarque_de_Hollanda_-_Volume_2).

  The song is about [sambist](samba.md) woman who moved up in social rank, and does not dance samba with her impoverished old friends anymore.

  ---
- <a id="video-apesar-de-voce-by-chico-buarque"></a>
  **[Video 49](#video-apesar-de-voce-by-chico-buarque). Apesar de Você by Chico Buarque.** [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=33-bMTOlvx0). Released 1970. [Portuguese wiki page for the song](https://pt.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apesar_de_Você).

  It is a song against the [military dictatorship in Brazil](military-dictatorship-in-brazil.md). "Você" (you) can ambiguously refer to either a loved woman, or to the dictatorship itself throughout the song.

Song against the [military dictatorship in Brazil](military-dictatorship-in-brazil.md), a pun on the [homonym](homonym.md) "Cálice" (chalice) and "Cale-se" (shut up), a reference to [censorship](censorship.md) during the dictatorship.
