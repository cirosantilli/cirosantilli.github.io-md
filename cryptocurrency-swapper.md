# Cryptocurrency swapper

↑ **Parent:** [Cryptocurrency exchange](cryptocurrency-exchange.md)

A "Cryptocurrency swapper" is a service that swaps one type of cryptocurrency for another.

It is basically the same as buying and selling from exchanges for [fiat](fiat-currency.md), except that you only get fiat.

Swappers are in general able to receive send coins from any address, including self custody addresses.

Centralized swappers were a good way to workaround the endless [Monero](monero.md) bans from exchanges circa 2024, e.g. [https://x.com/cirosantilli/status/1771900725649371240](https://x.com/cirosantilli/status/1771900725649371240) as they effectively serve as proxies for exchanges that are still legal in other countries.

They will eventually have to ban [Monero](monero.md) of course, and then the only way left will be [decentralized exchanges](decentralized-exchange.md).

This leads to a scenario where the only effective way to ban [Monero](monero.md) is to also ban all other cryptocurrencies. The question is if countries will go that far or not.
