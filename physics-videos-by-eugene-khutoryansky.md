# Physics Videos by Eugene Khutoryansky

↑ **Parent:** [Particle physics YouTube channel](particle-physics-youtube-channel.md)

[https://www.youtube.com/user/EugeneKhutoryansky](https://www.youtube.com/user/EugeneKhutoryansky)

These videos can give some geometric insight and do have their value.

But they are sometimes too slow, [there are never any mention of experiments, just "the truth"](how-to-teach-and-learn-physics.md).

And when things get "[mathy](mathematics-split.md)", it sticks to a more qualitative view which may not be enough.

Very over the top with [sexy](sexy.md) demons and angels making appearances, and has some classic aesthetic artistic value :-)
