# Classical limit

↑ **Parent:** [Classical physics](classical-physics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classical_limit)

The idea tha taking the limit of the non-classical theories for certain parameters ([relativity](relativity-split.md) and [quantum mechanics](quantum-mechanics-split.md)) should lead to the classical theory.

It appears that classical limit is only very strict for [relativity](relativity-split.md). For [quantum mechanics](quantum-mechanics-split.md) it is much more hand-wavy thing. See also: [Subtle is the Lord by Abraham Pais (1982)](subtle-is-the-lord-by-abraham-pais-1982.md) page 55.
