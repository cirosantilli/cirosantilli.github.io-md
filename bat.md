# Bat

↑ **Parent:** [Placentalia subclade](placentalia-subclade.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bat)

As of 2020, account for about 20% of the known [mammal](mammal.md) species!!! [https://www.sciencefocus.com/nature/why-are-there-so-many-species-of-bat/](https://www.sciencefocus.com/nature/why-are-there-so-many-species-of-bat/) mentions some reasons:
- they can fly, so they can move out further
- their eating habits are highly specialized
