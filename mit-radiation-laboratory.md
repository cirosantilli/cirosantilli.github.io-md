# MIT Radiation Laboratory

↑ **Parent:** [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](massachusetts-institute-of-technology.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIT_Radiation_Laboratory)

Made huge advances in [radar](radar.md).

Notably, [Isidor Isaac Rabi](isidor-isaac-rabi.md) was a leading figure there, and later he was head at the [Columbia University](columbia-university.md) laboratory that carried out the crucial [Lamb-Retherford experiment](lamb-retherford-experiment.md) and the [anomalous magnetic dipole moment of the electron](anomalous-magnetic-dipole-moment-of-the-electron.md) published at [The Magnetic Moment of the Electron by Kusch and Foley (1948)](the-magnetic-moment-of-the-electron-by-kusch-and-foley-1948.md) using related techniques.
