# System of partial differential equations

↑ **Parent:** [Partial differential equation](partial-differential-equation.md)

In many important applications, what you have to solve is not just a single [partial differential equation](partial-differential-equation.md), but multiple partial differential equations coupled to each other. This is the case for many key PDEs including:
- [Maxwell's equations](maxwell-s-equations.md), see: [Section "Explicit scalar form of the Maxwell's equations"](explicit-scalar-form-of-the-maxwell-s-equations.md)
- [Navier-Stokes equations](navier-stokes-equations.md)
- [Schrödinger equation](schrodinger-equation.md), see: [Section "Why are complex numbers used in the Schrodinger equation?"](why-are-complex-numbers-used-in-the-schrodinger-equation.md)
