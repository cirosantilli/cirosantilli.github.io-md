# Mt. Gox

↑ **Parent:** [List of cryptocurrency exchanges](list-of-cryptocurrency-exchanges.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mt._Gox)

The first [Bitcoin exchange](cryptocurrency-exchange.md). Coded as a hack, and they didn't manage to fix the hacks as the site evolved in a major way, which led to massive hacks.

Their creation is clearly visible on the archive history of [bitcoin.org](bitcoin-org.md): [https://web.archive.org/web/20100701000000*/bitcoin.org](https://web.archive.org/web/20100701000000*/bitcoin.org) which started having massively more archives since [Mt. Gox](mt-gox.md) opened.
