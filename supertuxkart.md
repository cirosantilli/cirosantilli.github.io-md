# SuperTuxKart

↑ **Parent:** [Mario Kart 64 clone](mario-kart-64-clone.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Open source video game](open-source-video-game.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SuperTuxKart)

It is a shame, but this game just doesn't feel good. The controls are just not as snappy as [Mario Kart 64](mario-kart-64.md), the levels are too wide which limits player interaction, and the weapons feel clumsy weak and unexciting. These are all aspects that the closed source [smashkarts.io](smashkarts-io.md) gets pretty well.
