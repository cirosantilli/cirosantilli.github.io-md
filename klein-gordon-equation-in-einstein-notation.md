# Klein-Gordon equation in Einstein notation

↑ **Parent:** [d'Alembert operator in Einstein notation](d-alembert-operator-in-einstein-notation.md)

The [Klein-Gordon equation](klein-gordon-equation.md) can be written in terms of the [d'Alembert operator](d-alembert-operator.md) as:

$$
\Box \psi + m^2 \psi = 0
$$

so we can expand the [d'Alembert operator in Einstein notation](d-alembert-operator-in-einstein-notation.md) to:

$$
\partial_i \partial^i \psi - m^2 \psi = 0
$$
