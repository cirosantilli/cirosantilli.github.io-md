# Basal (phylogenetics)

↑ **Parent:** [Phylogenetics](phylogenetics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basal_(phylogenetics))

When a characteristic is basal, it basically means the opposite of it being [polyphyletic](polyphyly.md).

E.g. [monotremes](monotreme.md) laying eggs did not evolve separately after function loss, it comes directly from [reptiles](reptile.md).
