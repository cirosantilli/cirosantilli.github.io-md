# Literature

↑ **Parent:** [Art](art-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Literature)

[Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) used to read books when he was younger ([Harry Potter](harry-potter.md) up to the 4th, Lord of the Rings), but once you are reading code, technical articles and news the whole day, you really just want to [watch videos of people doing useless things on YouTube](magic-the-gathering-split.md) to rest, enough text.

Books are slow. No patience. Need faster immediate satisfaction.

Paradoxically Ciro feels like he's becoming a writer of sorts though, one semi independent section/[answer](the-machiavellian-stack-overflow-contributor.md)/piece of knowledge at a time.

Writing is not just giving out information. It is re-feeling it.
