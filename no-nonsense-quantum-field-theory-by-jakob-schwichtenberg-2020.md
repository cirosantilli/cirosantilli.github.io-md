This book really tries to recall basic things to ensure that the reader will be able to understand the more advanced ones.

Sometimes it goes a little bit overboard, like defining what a [function](function-mathematics.md) does several times.

But [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) really prefers it when authors error on the side of obvious.
