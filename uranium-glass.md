# Uranium glass

↑ **Parent:** [Uranium](uranium.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Fluorescence](fluorescence.md), [Glass](glass.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uranium_glass)

[Glass](glass.md) with [Uranium](uranium.md) added to it to become [fluorescent](fluorescence.md) due to [Uranium](uranium.md)'s chemical properties. This is unrelated to [Uranium](uranium.md)'s nuclear properties.

However it was this [fluorescence](fluorescence.md) that led [Henri Becquerel](henri-becquerel.md) to discover [radioactivity](radioactive-decay.md) while studying [fluorescence](fluorescence.md), which led him to have [Uranium](uranium.md) compounds and [photographic](photography.md) material in close proximity. I love science I guess.
