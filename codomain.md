# Codomain

↑ **Parent:** [Domain, codomain and image](domain-codomain-and-image.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Codomain)

Vs: [image](image-mathematics.md): the codomain is the set that the function might reach.

The [image](image-mathematics.md) is the exact set that it actually reaches.

E.g. the function:

$$
f(x) = x^2
$$

could have:
- codomain $\R$
- image $\R_{+}$

Note that the definition of the codomain is somewhat arbitrary, e.g. $x^2$ could as well technically have codomain:

$$
\R \bigcup \R^2
$$

even though it will obviously never reach any value in $\R^2$.

The exact image is in general therefore harder to characterize.
