Europe has basically become an outsourcing hub for the [United States](united-states-split.md). The fact that its starts are all sold if they become large enough just means that R&D is also outsourced.

[ASML](asml-holding.md), and perhaps more meaningfully its parent/predecessor [ASM International](asm-international.md) from 1964 is perhaps the biggest exception.

The key problem is that there are so many small countries in [Europe](europe.md), that any [startup](startup-company.md) has to deal with too many incompatible legislation and cannot easily sell to the hole of [Europe](europe.md) and scale. So then a larger company from a more uniform country comes and [eats](eating.md) it up!

Talent mobility is another issue:
- people can't generally work remotely from different countries for the same company as regular employees, only as contractors. This is because of fiscal incompatibilities across countries[https://www.fragomen.com/insights/remote-work-in-the-european-union-back-to-the-feuture.html](https://www.fragomen.com/insights/remote-work-in-the-european-union-back-to-the-feuture.html)[https://www.fragomen.com/insights/remote-work-in-the-european-union-back-to-the-feuture.html](https://www.fragomen.com/insights/remote-work-in-the-european-union-back-to-the-feuture.html), and has become an increasing problem in the 2020's with the increase in remote work possibilities during/after [COVID-19](covid-19.md).
- it is quite rare for people to study at university in different countries than their own, because the entry examinations are in the native language and have local history knowledge components. This also means that people from different countries don't easily recognize which are the best Universities of other countries, making you take a hit if you want to search for jobs elsewhere

So why can't [Europe](europe.md) unify its laws?

Because the countries are still essentially walled off by languages. [Europe](europe.md) is the perfect example of why [having more than one natural language is bad for the world](having-more-than-one-natural-language-is-bad-for-the-world.md).

There isn't true mobility of people between countries.

You just can't go study or work in any other country (except for the UK, when it was still in the EU) without putting a huge effort into learning its language first.

Without this, there isn't enough mixing to truly make cultures more uniform, and therefore allow the laws to be more uniform.
