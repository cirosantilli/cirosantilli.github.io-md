# Diophantine equation

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial](polynomial.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diophantine_equation)

[Polynomial](polynomial.md) (possibly a [multivariate polynomial](multivariate-polynomial.md)) with [integer](integer.md) coefficients.

Sometimes systems of [Diophantine equations](diophantine-equation.md) are considered.

Problems generally involve finding integer solutions to the equations, notably determining if any solution exists, and if infinitely solutions exist.

The general problem is known to be [undecidable](undecidable-problem.md): [Hilbert's tenth problem](hilbert-s-tenth-problem.md).

The [Pythagorean triples](pythagorean-triple.md), and its generalization [Fermat's last theorem](fermat-s-last-theorem.md), are the quintessential examples.
