# Higgs boson

↑ **Parent:** [Elementary particle](elementary-particle.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Higgs_boson)

Initially there were mathematical reasons why people suspected that all [boson](boson.md) needed to have 0 mass as is the case for [photons](photon-split.md) a [gluons](gluon.md), see [Goldstone's theorem](goldstone-s-theorem.md).

However, experiments showed that the [W boson](w-boson.md) and the [Z boson](z-boson.md) both has large non-zero masses.

So people started theorizing some hack that would fix up the equations, and they came up with the [higgs mechanism](higgs-mechanism.md).
