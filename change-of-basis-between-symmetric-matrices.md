# Change of basis between symmetric matrices

↑ **Parent:** [Change of basis](change-of-basis.md)

When we have a [symmetric matrix](symmetric-matrix.md), a [change of basis](change-of-basis.md) keeps symmetry iff it is done by an [orthogonal matrix](orthogonal-matrix.md), in which case:

$$
N = BMB^{-1} = OMO^T
$$
