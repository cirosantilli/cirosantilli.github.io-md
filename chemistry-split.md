# Chemistry

↑ **Parent:** [Natural science](natural-science.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemistry)

Chemistry is fun. Too hard for precise [physics](physics-split.md) (pre [quantum computing](quantum-computing-split.md), see also [quantum chemistry](quantum-chemistry.md)), but not too hard for some maths like [social sciences](social-science.md).

And it underpins [biology](biology-split.md).

<a id="video-100-greatest-discoveries-chemistry-by-the-discovery-channel-2005"></a>
**[Video 1](#video-100-greatest-discoveries-chemistry-by-the-discovery-channel-2005). 100 Greatest Discoveries - Chemistry by the Discovery Channel (2005)** [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oSBM2SXlGC). Pretty good within what you can expect from [popular science](popular-science.md). The discovery selection is solid, and he interviews 3 [Nobel Prize](nobel-prize-split.md) laureates, only one about stuff they invented, so you can see their faces. The short non-precise scenes of epoch are also pleasing. Part of [100 Greatest Discoveries by the Discovery Channel (2004-2005)](100-greatest-discoveries-by-the-discovery-channel-2004-2005.md).
