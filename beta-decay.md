# Beta decay

↑ **Parent:** [Type of radioactive decay](type-of-radioactive-decay.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beta_decay)

[Uranium](uranium.md) emits them, you can see their mass to charge ratio under magnetic field and so deduce that they are [electrons](electron.md).

Caused by [weak interaction](weak-interaction.md) TODO why/how.

The emitted electron kinetic energy is random from zero to a maximum value. The rest goes into a [neutrino](neutrino.md). This is how the neutrino was first discovered/observed indirectly. This is well illustrated in a [decay scheme](decay-scheme.md) such as [Figure "caesium-137 decay scheme"](caesium-137.md#image-caesium-137-decay-scheme).
