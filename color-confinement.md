# Color confinement

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum chromodynamics](quantum-chromodynamics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Color_confinement)

Can be thought as being produced from [gluon](gluon.md)-gluon lines of the [Feynman diagrams](feynman-diagram.md) of [quantum chromodynamics](quantum-chromodynamics.md). This is in contrast to [quantum electrodynamics](quantum-electrodynamics.md), in which there are no [photon](photon-split.md)-photon vertices, because the photon does not have charge unlike gluons.

This phenomena makes the strong force be very very different from electromagnetism.
