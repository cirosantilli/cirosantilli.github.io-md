# Mitochondrial phosphate carrier protein

↑ **Parent:** [Mitochondrial carrier](mitochondrial-carrier.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Transmembrane protein](transmembrane-protein.md)

Just like the [adenine nucleotide translocator](adenine-nucleotide-translocator.md) moves [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md)/[ADP](adenosine-diphosphate.md) in and out, this one moves loose [phosphate](phosphate.md) in.

Both of those together recycle the cellular respiration carriers from/to the mitochondria.
