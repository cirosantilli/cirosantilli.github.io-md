# Big companies manage to publish white papers in peer reviewed journals

↑ **Parent:** [Journals must require source code and data sets to publish](journals-must-require-source-code-and-data-sets-to-publish.md)

Big [companies](company-split.md) like [Google](google-split.md) are able to publish [white papers](white-paper.md) as [peer reviewed papers](peer-review.md) just due to their reputation, e.g. without giving any [source code](source-code.md) that is central for the article.

It is insane.

E.g.: [AlphaGo](alphago.md) is closed source but published as [https://www.nature.com/articles/natnure16961](https://www.nature.com/articles/natnure16961) in 2016 on [Nature](nature-journal.md).
