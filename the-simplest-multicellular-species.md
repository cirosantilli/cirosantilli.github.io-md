# The simplest multicellular species

↑ **Parent:** [Multicellular organism](multicellular-organism.md)

One of the simplest known seems to be: [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trichoplax](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trichoplax)

[https://www.u-tokyo.ac.jp/focus/en/articles/a_00220.html](https://www.u-tokyo.ac.jp/focus/en/articles/a_00220.html) "The simplest multicellular organism unveiled" from 2013 mentions Tetrabaena socialis.

Then of course: [Caenorhabditis elegans](caenorhabditis-elegans.md) is a relatively simple and widely studied [model organism](model-organism.md).
