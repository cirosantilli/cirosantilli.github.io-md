# Depth of a quantum circuit

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum computer benchmark](quantum-computer-benchmark.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Depth_of_a_quantum_circuit)

This is an important metric, because it takes some time for the quantum operations to propagate, and so the depth of a circuit gives you an idea of how long the [coherence time](coherence-time.md) a hardware needs to support a given circuit.
