# Einstein solid

↑ **Parent:** [Specific heat capacity](specific-heat-capacity.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Quantum mechanics experiment](quantum-mechanics-experiment.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Einstein_solid)

One important [quantum mechanics experiment](quantum-mechanics-experiment.md), which using quantum effects explain the dependency of [specific heat capacity](specific-heat-capacity.md) on temperature, an effect which is not present in the [Dulong-Petit law](dulong-petit-law.md).

This is the [solid-state](solid-state-physics.md) analogue to the [black-body radiation](black-body-radiation.md) problem. It is also therefore a [quantum mechanics](quantum-mechanics-split.md)-specific phenomenon.
