# Telephone

↑ **Parent:** [Telecommunication system](telecommunication-system.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telephone)

We are at a point in history where the [electrical telegraph](electrical-telegraph.md) is well established.

But people don't want to press letters one by one on a switch. They want to talk!

The first phones appear to have used telegraph lines directly.

Also wired phones don't require [modulation](modulation.md), which likely made their development much easier than wireless voice transmission. You just send the signal as a voltage differential directly obtained from the air pressure: [how the telephone works](how-the-telephone-works.md).
