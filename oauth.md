# OAuth

↑ **Parent:** [Computer security](computer-security.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Good](good.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OAuth)

The fatal flaw of OAuth is that websites have to enable specific providers, they can't just automatically select the correct OAuth for a given email domain. This means that the vast majority of websites will only provide the most widely popular providers such as [Google](google-split.md), and the like, which means people won't have decent privacy.

So you are just better off with password logins and a decent [password manager](password-manager.md).
