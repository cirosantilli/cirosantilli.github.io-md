# Josephson effect

↑ **Parent:** [Superconductivity](superconductivity.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [1973 Nobel Prize in Physics](1973-nobel-prize-in-physics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josephson_effect)

[Discrete](discrete.md) [quantum](quantum-mechanics-split.md) effect observed in [superconductors](superconductivity.md) with a small insulating layer, a device known as a [Josephson junction](josephson-junction.md).

To understand the behaviour effect, it is important to look at the [Josephson equations](josephson-equations.md) consider the following [Josephson effect regimes](josephson-effect-regime.md) separately:
- [DC Josephson effect](dc-josephson-effect.md)
- [AC Josephson effect](ac-josephson-effect.md)
- [Inverse AC Josephson effect](inverse-ac-josephson-effect.md)

A good summary from Wikipedia by physicist Andrew Whitaker:

> at a junction of two superconductors, a current will flow even if there is no drop in voltage; that when there is a voltage drop, the current should oscillate at a frequency related to the drop in voltage; and that there is a dependence on any magnetic field
