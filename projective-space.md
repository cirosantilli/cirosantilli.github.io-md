# Projective space

↑ **Parent:** [Projective geometry](projective-geometry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Projective_space)

A [unique](uniqueness.md) projective space can be defined for any [vector space](vector-space.md).

The projective space associated with a given [vector space](vector-space.md) $V$ is denoted $\projectiveSpace(V)$.

The definition is to take the vector space, remove the zero element, and identify all elements that lie on the same line, i.e. $\vec{v} = \lambda \vec{w}$

The most important initial example to study is the [real projective plane](real-projective-plane.md).
