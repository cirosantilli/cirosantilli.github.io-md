# Synthetic geometry

↑ **Parent:** [Euclid's Elements](euclid-s-elements.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Synthetic_geometry)

A way to defined geometry without talking about coordinates, i.e. like [Euclid's Elements](euclid-s-elements.md), notably [Euclid's postulates](euclid-s-postulates.md), as opposed to [Descartes](rene-descartes.md)'s [Real coordinate space](real-coordinate-space.md).
