# The direct product of two cyclic groups of coprime order is another cyclic group

↑ **Parent:** [Important discrete mathematical group](important-discrete-mathematical-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_direct_product_of_two_cyclic_groups_of_coprime_order_is_another_cyclic_group)

You just map the value (1, 1) $C_m \times C_n$ to the value 1 of $C_{mn}$, and it works out. E.g. for $C_2 \times C_3$, the [group generated by](generating-set-of-a-group.md) of (1, 1) is:
```
0 = (0, 0)
1 = (1, 1)
2 = (0, 2)
3 = (1, 0)
4 = (0, 1)
5 = (1, 2)
6 = (0, 0) = 0
```
