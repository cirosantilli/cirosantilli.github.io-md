# Spectroscopic notation

↑ **Parent:** [Spin quantum number](spin-quantum-number.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spectroscopic_notation)

This notation is cool as it gives the [spin quantum number](spin-quantum-number.md), which is important e.g. when talking about [hyperfine structure](hyperfine-structure.md).

But it is a bit crap that the spin is not given simply as $\pm 1/2$ but rather mixes up both the [azimuthal quantum number](azimuthal-quantum-number.md) and spin. What is the reason?
