# NP-hard cryptosystem

↑ **Parent:** [Integer factorization](integer-factorization.md)

This is natural question because both [integer factorization](integer-factorization.md) and [discrete logarithm](discrete-logarithm.md) are the basis for the most popular [public-key cryptography](public-key-cryptography.md) systems as of 2020 ([RSA](rsa-cryptosystem.md) and [Diffie-Hellman key exchange](diffie-hellman-key-exchange.md) respectively), and both are [NP-intermediate](np-intermediate.md). Why not use something more provenly hard?
