# Fashion [MNIST](mnist-database.md)

↑ **Parent:** [MNIST database](mnist-database.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fashion_MNIST)

Same style as [MNIST](mnist-database.md): 28x28 grayscale images, but with clothes rather than hand written digits.

It was designed to be much harder than [MNIST](mnist-database.md), and more representative of modern applications, while still retaining the low resolution of [MNIST](mnist-database.md) for simplicity of training.
