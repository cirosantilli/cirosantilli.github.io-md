# Two photon interference experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Single photon production and detection](single-photon-production-and-detection.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Two_photon_interference_experiment)

The basic experiment for a [photonic quantum computer](photonic-quantum-computer.md).

Can be achieved in two ways it seems:
- macroscopic beam splitter and [optical table](optical-table.md)
- [photolithography](photolithography.md)

Animation of Hong-Ou-Mandel Effect on a silicon like structure by Quantum Light University of Sheffield (2014): [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ld2r2IMt4vg](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ld2r2IMt4vg) No maths, but gives the result clear: the photons are always on the same side.
