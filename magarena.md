# Magarena

↑ **Parent:** [Magic: The Gathering](magic-the-gathering-split.md)

Open source MtG engine implementation written in [Java](java-programming-language.md).

Seems to have an option to download art from internet as well.

[Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) wonders how [legal](law-split.md) it is. They very explicitly do not mention the words Magic: The Gathering anywhere.

Their UI does a good job at being self explanatory. Space is the shortcut to skip phases.

No online play.

[https://github.com/magarena/magarena](https://github.com/magarena/magarena)
