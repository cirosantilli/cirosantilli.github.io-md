# eGroups

↑ **Parent:** [Scott Hassan](scott-hassan.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EGroups)

Company co-founded by [Scott Hassan](scott-hassan.md), early [Google](google-split.md) programmer at [Stanford University](stanford-university.md), and [Carl Victor Page, Jr.](carl-victor-page-jr.md), [Larry Page](larry-page.md)'s older brother.

They were an email list management website, and became [Yahoo! Groups](yahoo-groups.md) after the acquisition.

The company was sold to [Yahoo!](yahoo.md) in August 2000 for $432m and became [Yahoo! Groups](yahoo-groups.md). They managed to miraculously dodge the [Dot-com bubble](dot-com-bubble.md), which mostly poppet in 2021. After the acquisition, Yahoo started to redirect them to: [http://groups.yahoo.com](http://groups.yahoo.com) as can be seen on the [Wayback Machine](wayback-machine.md): [http://web.archive.org/web/20000401000000*/egroups.com](http://web.archive.org/web/20000401000000*/egroups.com) The first archive of [https://groups.yahoo.com](https://groups.yahoo.com) is from February 2001: [http://web.archive.org/web/20010202055100/http://groups.yahoo.com/](http://web.archive.org/web/20010202055100/http://groups.yahoo.com/) and it unsurprisingly looks basically exactly like eGroups.
