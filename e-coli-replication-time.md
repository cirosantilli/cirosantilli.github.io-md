# E. Coli replication time

↑ **Parent:** [Escherichia coli](escherichia-coli.md)

20 minutes in optimal conditions, with a crazy multiple start sites mechanism: [E. Coli starts DNA replication before the previous one finished](e-coli-starts-dna-replication-before-the-previous-one-finished.md).

Otherwise, naively, would take 60-90 minutes just to replicate and segregate the full DNA otherwise. So it starts copying multiple times.
