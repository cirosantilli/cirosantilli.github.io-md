# LLM benchmark

↑ **Parent:** [Large language model](large-language-model.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Computer benchmark](computer-benchmark.md)

Benchmarking LLMs is an extremely difficult issue.

LLMs are the type of [GenAI](generative-ai.md) that comes most obviously close to [AGI](artificial-general-intelligence.md) depending on the question asked.

Therefore, there is is a difficult gap between what is easy, what a human can always do, and what [AGI](artificial-general-intelligence.md) will do one day.

Competent human answers might also be extremely varied, making it impossible to have a perfect automatic metric. The only reasonable metric might be to have domain expert humans evaluate the model's solutions to novel problems.
