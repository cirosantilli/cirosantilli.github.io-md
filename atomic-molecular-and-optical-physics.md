# Atomic, Molecular and Optical Physics

↑ **Parent:** [Condensed matter physics](condensed-matter-physics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atomic,_Molecular_and_Optical_Physics)

AMO is a slightly more general area than [condensed matter physics](condensed-matter-physics-split.md), including related phenomena with smaller numbers atoms and optics. The two terms are however sometimes used as synonyms. The term AMO has gained wide usage and acceptability, see e.g.:
- [https://www.sussex.ac.uk/amo/](https://www.sussex.ac.uk/amo/) at [University of Sussex](university-of-sussex.md)
