<a id="video-how-an-atomic-clock-works-and-its-use-in-the-global-positioning-system-gps-by-engineerguy-2012"></a>
**[Video 6](#video-how-an-atomic-clock-works-and-its-use-in-the-global-positioning-system-gps-by-engineerguy-2012). How an atomic clock works, and its use in the global positioning system (GPS) by EngineerGuy (2012)** [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p2BxAu6WZI8). Shows how conceptually an atomic clock is based on a [feedback loop](feedback.md) of two [hyperfine structure](hyperfine-structure.md) states of [caesium](caesium.md) atoms (non-[radioactive](radioactive-decay.md) [caesium-133](caesium-133.md) as clarified by the [Wikipedia](wikipedia.md) page). Like a [quartz clock](quartz-clock.md), it also relies on the [piezoelectricity](piezoelectricity.md) of quartz, but unlike the [quartz clock](quartz-clock.md), the [quartz](quartz.md) is not shaped like a [tuning fork](tuning-fork.md), and has a much larger resonating frequency of about 7 [MHz](megahertz.md). The feedback is completed by producing [photons](photon-split.md) that [resonate](resonance.md) at the right frequency to excite the [caesium](caesium.md).

<a id="video-inside-the-hp-5061a-cesium-clock-by-curiousmarc-2020"></a>
**[Video 7](#video-inside-the-hp-5061a-cesium-clock-by-curiousmarc-2020). Inside the HP 5061A Cesium Clock by CuriousMarc (2020)** [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eOti3kKWX-c). A similar model was used in the [Hafele-Keating experiment](hafele-keating-experiment.md) to test [special relativity](special-relativity.md) on two planes flying in opposite directions. Miniaturization was key.

Contains a disposable tube with 6g of [Caesium](caesium.md). You boil it, so when it runs out, you change the tube, 40k USD. Their tube is made by [Agilent Technologies](agilent-technologies.md), so a replacement since that opened in 1999, and the original machine is from the 60s.

Detection is done with an [electron multiplier](electron-multiplier.md).

[https://youtu.be/eOti3kKWX-c?t=1166](https://youtu.be/eOti3kKWX-c?t=1166) They compare it with their 100 dollar [GPS](global-positioning-system.md) disciplined oscillator, since [GPS](global-positioning-system.md) [satellites](satellite.md) have [atomic clocks](atomic-clock.md) in them.
