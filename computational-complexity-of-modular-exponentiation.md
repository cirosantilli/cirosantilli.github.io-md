# Computational complexity of modular exponentiation

↑ **Parent:** [Modular exponentiation](modular-exponentiation.md)

[https://math.stackexchange.com/questions/2382011/computational-complexity-of-modular-exponentiation-from-rosens-discrete-mathem](https://math.stackexchange.com/questions/2382011/computational-complexity-of-modular-exponentiation-from-rosens-discrete-mathem) mentions:

$$
b^n mod m
$$

can be calculated in:

$$
log(m)^2 log(n)
$$

Remember that $log(m)$ and $log(n)$ are the lengths in bits of $m$ and $n$, so in terms of the length in bits $M$ and $N$ we'd get:

$$
M^2 N
$$
