<h1 id="ciro-santilli-s-drug-experiences">Ciro Santilli's drug experiences</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md)

[Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) never did any [illegal](law-split.md) drugs, because he:
- doesn't want to help fund [organized crime](organized-crime.md). Notably, Ciro is for complete [legalization of drugs](drug-liberalization.md) of all drugs
- already has better more sustainable drugs like [love](ciro-santilli-s-wife.md), [cycling](ciro-santilli-s-cycling.md), learning the [natural sciences](natural-science.md) and [fame](ciro-santilli-s-selfish-desires.md). He (or more realistically, the world) [chose life for him](trainspotting-1996.md).
so don't expect any amazing stories here.

Like [LDS believers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Word_of_Wisdom), Ciro never drinks coffee nor smokes, and only drinks alcohol and tea sparingly, because they are all addictive [drugs](drug.md) and bring no net increase of energy and concentration.

Ciro prefers to only enjoy a glass of tea [when going out cycling on a cold day](ciro-santilli-s-sport-practice.md) ([Earl gray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earl_Grey_tea), with milk, no sugar), or get a half pint of beer when going out with friends to a pub.

Ciro only got reasonably drunk twice on his life:
- once when he was quite young, likely pre-10 years old, while visiting an uncle's home, and adults were having a very nice sweet and thick type of alcoholic cocktail, and Ciro drank a bit too much and that made him really really stupid
- once while studying at [University of São Paulo](university-of-sao-paulo.md), somehow someone was giving free beers at one of the parties (at which Ciro practiced [Cirodance](cirodance.md)). And since Ciro had always been a [cheap-ass](ciro-santilli-s-cheapness.md), he thought, hey, this is a good chance to try it out. Ciro remembers that this made him a bit euphoric, active, very stupid, and a bit [horny](sexual-arousal.md) (though of course, he got no pussy as usual).

Later in life, around the time of [his wedding](ciro-santilli-s-wedding.md), there were guests around all the time, and he was drinking beer with them all the time. Then one day, during lunch, Ciro felt a weirdly strong desire to drink one more pint. It was at this point that Ciro realized first-hand what mild, but real, alcohol addiction felt like, and he didn't get that drink, and swore from then on to never drink more than one glass a week, and only with friends at a bar after work. [Richard Feynman](richard-feynman-split.md) tells a very similar story on his book [Surely You're Joking, Mr. Feynman chapter O Americano, Outra Vez!](surely-you-re-joking-mr-feynman-chapter-o-americano-outra-vez.md), see: [Section "Richard Feynman's drug use"](richard-feynman-s-drug-use.md).
