Vectors with the index on top such as $x^i$ are the "regular vectors", they are called [covariant vectors](covariant-vector.md).

Those in indices on bottom are called [contravariant vectors](contravariant-vector.md).

It is possible to change between them by [Raising and lowering indices](raising-and-lowering-indices.md).

The values are different only when the [metric signature matrix](metric-signature-matrix.md) is different from the [identity matrix](identity-matrix.md).
