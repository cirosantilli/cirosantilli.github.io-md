# One-time pad

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetric encryption](symmetric-encryption.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One-time_pad)

The only perfect cryptosystem!

The problem is that you need a shared [key](key-cryptography.md) as large as the message.

Systems like [advanced Encryption Standard](advanced-encryption-standard.md) allow us to encrypt things larger than the key, but the tradeoff is that they could be possibly broken, as don't have any [provably secure symmetric-key algorithms](provably-secure-symmetric-key-algorithm.md) as of 2020.
