# Spontaneous emission

↑ **Parent:** [Absorption, spontaneous and stimulated emission](absorption-spontaneous-and-stimulated-emission.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spontaneous_emission)

Do electrons spontaneously jump from high orbitals to lower ones emitting photons?

Explaining this was was one of the key initial achievements of the [Dirac equation](dirac-equation.md).

Yes, but this is not predicted by the [Schrödinger equation](schrodinger-equation.md), you need to go to the [Dirac equation](dirac-equation.md).

A critical application of this phenomena is [laser](laser.md).
