# Stimulated emission

↑ **Parent:** [Absorption, spontaneous and stimulated emission](absorption-spontaneous-and-stimulated-emission.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stimulated_emission)

Photon hits excited electron, makes that electron go down, and generates a new identical photon in the process, with the exact same:
- [frequency](frequency.md)
- [polarization](photon-polarization.md)
- direction
This is the basis of [lasers](laser.md).
