# Second-order phase transition

↑ **Parent:** [Phase transition](phase-transition.md)

Mentioned at: [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phase_transition#Modern_classifications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phase_transition#Modern_classifications)

The more familiar transitions we are familiar with like liquid [water](water.md) into solid water happen at constant temperature.

However, other types of phase transitions we are less familiar in our daily lives happen across a continuum of such "state variables", notably:
- [superfluidity](superfluidity.md) and its related manifestation, [superconductivity](superconductivity.md)
- [ferromagnetism](ferromagnetism.md)
