# Elliptic curve group

↑ **Parent:** [Elliptic curve](elliptic-curve.md)

The [elliptic curve group](elliptic-curve-group.md) of an [elliptic curve](elliptic-curve.md) is a group in which the elements of the group are points on an [elliptic curve](elliptic-curve.md).

The [group operation](group-operation.md) is called [elliptic curve point addition](elliptic-curve-point-addition.md).
