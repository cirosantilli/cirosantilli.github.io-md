<h1 id="waring-s-problem-for-squares">Waring's problem for squares</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Waring's problem](waring-s-problem.md)

4 squares are sufficient by [Lagrange's four-square theorem](lagrange-s-four-square-theorem.md).

3 is not enough by [Legendre's three-square theorem](legendre-s-three-square-theorem.md).

The subsets reachable with 2 and 3 squares are fully characterized by [Legendre's three-square theorem](legendre-s-three-square-theorem.md).
