# Caetano Veloso

↑ **Parent:** [The Holy Trinity of popular Brazilian music](the-holy-trinity-of-popular-brazilian-music.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caetano_Veloso)

Within the [The Holy Trinity of popular Brazilian music](the-holy-trinity-of-popular-brazilian-music.md), Caetano has the most [New Age](new-age.md) religious feel to him. He is also perhaps the most varied of the trinity however, also covering heavier topics at times.
