# Molecular beam

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic, Molecular and Optical Physics](atomic-molecular-and-optical-physics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Molecular_beam)

[Molecular beams](molecular-beam.md) are cool because they create a one dimensional flow of [molecules](molecule.md), which makes it easier to observe certain single-molecule effects, as it removes the multi-particle issues from experiments.

Key [molecular beam](molecular-beam.md) experiments include:
- [Stern-Gerlach experiment](stern-gerlach-experiment.md), which confirmed the existence of [spin](spin-physics.md)
- [Rabi's NMR experiment](rabi-s-nmr-experiment.md), which confirmed the existence of [nuclear spin](nuclear-magnetic-moment.md)

The center piece of the control system of [atomic clocks](atomic-clock.md) is a [molecular beam](molecular-beam.md).
