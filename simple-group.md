# Simple group

↑ **Parent:** [Normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simple_group)

Does not have any non-trivial [normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md).

And therefore, going back to our intuition that due to the [fundamental theorem on homomorphisms](fundamental-theorem-on-homomorphisms.md) there is one normal group per homomorphism, a simple group is one that has no non-trivial homomorphisms.
