# Sun Erniang

↑ **Parent:** [Water Margin character](water-margin-character.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [108 Stars of Destiny](108-stars-of-destiny.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sun_Erniang)

Erniang is way more popular than her ranking in the [108 Stars of Destiny](108-stars-of-destiny.md).

This is of course in part because she is one of the only 3 [108 Stars of Destiny women](108-stars-of-destiny-women.md). But her story about making human mantou is particularly fun!

She's also quite seductive, which gives directors a good opportunity for a bit of [fan service](fan-service.md). See e.g. [https://kknews.cc/history/6epp2pp.html](https://kknews.cc/history/6epp2pp.html) for a still from [The Water Margin](the-water-margin-1998-tv-series.md) showing some good [cleavage](cleavage.md) for example.
