# BEHAVIOR Benchmark

↑ **Parent:** [AI training robot simulation](ai-training-robot-simulation.md)

Homepage: [https://behavior.stanford.edu/behavior-1k](https://behavior.stanford.edu/behavior-1k)

Quite impressive.

Focuses on daily human tasks around the house.

Models [soft-body dynamics](soft-body-dynamics.md), [fluid dynamics](fluid-dynamics.md) and object states such as heat/wetness.
