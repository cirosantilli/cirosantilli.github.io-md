# Mind uploading

↑ **Parent:** [Braindumping](braindumping.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mind_uploading)

[Wikipedia](wikipedia.md) defines [Mind uploading](mind-uploading.md) as a synonym for [whole brain emulation](mind-uploading.md). This sounds really weird, as "mind uploading" suggests much more simply [brain dumping](braindumping.md), or perhaps reuploading a brain dump to a brain.

[Superintelligence by Nick Bostrom (2014)](superintelligence-by-nick-bostrom-2014.md) section "Whole brain emulation" provides a reasonable setup: post mortem, take a brain, freeze it, then cut it into fine slices with a [Microtome](microtome.md), and then inspect slices with an [electron microscope](electron-microscope.md) after some kind of staining to determine all the synapses.

Likely implies [AGI](artificial-general-intelligence.md).
