# Los Alamos From Below by Richard Feynman (1975)

↑ **Parent:** [Works about Richard Feynman](works-about-richard-feynman.md)

Amazing talk by [Richard Feynman](richard-feynman-split.md) that describes his experiences at [Los Alamos National Laboratory](los-alamos-national-laboratory.md) while developing the first [nuclear weapons](nuclear-weapon-split.md).

Transcript: [http://calteches.library.caltech.edu/34/3/FeynmanLosAlamos.htm](http://calteches.library.caltech.edu/34/3/FeynmanLosAlamos.htm) Also included full text into [Surely You're Joking, Mr. Feynman](surely-you-re-joking-mr-feynman.md).

- [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uY-u1qyRM5w&t=2881s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uY-u1qyRM5w&t=2881s) describes the computing aspects. Particularly interesting is the quote about how they used the typist secretary pool to [emulate](emulator.md) the [IBM](ibm.md) machines and debug their programs before the machines had arrived. This is exactly analogous to what is done in 2020 in the [semiconductor](semiconductor.md) industry, where slower models are used to estimate how future algorithms will run in future hardware.
