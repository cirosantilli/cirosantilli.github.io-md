# Plancherel theorem

↑ **Parent:** [$\LTwo$](l2.md)

Some sources say that this is just the part that says that the [norm](norm-mathematics.md) of a [$\LTwo$](l2.md) function is the same as the norm of its [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md).

Others say that this theorem actually says that the [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) is [bijective](bijection.md).

The comment at [https://math.stackexchange.com/questions/446870/bijectiveness-injectiveness-and-surjectiveness-of-fourier-transformation-define/1235725#1235725](https://math.stackexchange.com/questions/446870/bijectiveness-injectiveness-and-surjectiveness-of-fourier-transformation-define/1235725#1235725) may be of interest, it says that the [bijection](bijection.md) statement is an easy consequence from the [norm](norm-mathematics.md) one, thus the confusion.
