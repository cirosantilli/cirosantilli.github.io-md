# Jackdaw Playing With Water

↑ **Parent:** [Guzheng piece](guzheng-piece.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [The best Chinese traditional instrumental music](the-best-chinese-traditional-instrumental-music.md)

A Jackdaw is a type of crow: [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_jackdaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_jackdaw)

Considered one of the 10 famous Chaozhou music pieces TODO list.

Was also adapted by Liu Baoshan (1937-1997) as a [pipa](pipa.md) song. [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) prefers the pipa version.
