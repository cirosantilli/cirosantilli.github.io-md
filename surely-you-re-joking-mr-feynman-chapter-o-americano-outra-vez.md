# Surely You're Joking, Mr. Feynman chapter O Americano, Outra Vez!

↑ **Parent:** [Surely You're Joking, Mr. Feynman](surely-you-re-joking-mr-feynman.md)

In this chapter [Richard Feynman](richard-feynman-split.md) talks about his experiences in [Brazil](brazil-split.md).

"O Americano, Outra Vez!" means "The American, once again!" in [Portuguese](portuguese-language.md), which is what one of the samba school boss exclaimed when Feynman was not playing well his instrument, the frigideira, during a rehearsal.

Feynman really enjoyed Brazil's (and notably [Rio](rio-de-janeiro-state.md)'s) stereotypical "take it easy and enjoy life" attitude.
