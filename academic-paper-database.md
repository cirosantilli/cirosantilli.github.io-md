# Academic paper database

↑ **Parent:** [Academic paper](academic-paper.md)

By "Academic paper database" we mean a database that collects paper metadata such as authors and [citations](citation.md), but not necessarily the full article content.

[Academia is so broken](academia-is-broken.md) that there isn't even one be-all and end-all database of:
- all papers by a given author
- all [citations](citation.md) of a given paper
It's [closed access academic journals are evil](closed-access-academic-journals-are-evil.md) to the extreme.
