# Isometry group

↑ **Parent:** [Important Lie group](important-lie-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isometry_group)

The [group](group-split.md) of all transformations that preserve some [bilinear form](bilinear-form.md), notable examples:
- [orthogonal group](orthogonal-group.md) preserves the [inner product](inner-product.md)
- [unitary group](unitary-group.md) preserves a [Hermitian form](hermitian-form.md)
- [Lorentz group](lorentz-group.md) preserves the [Minkowski inner product](minkowski-inner-product.md)
