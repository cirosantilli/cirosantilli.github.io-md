# Distributive property

↑ **Parent:** [Field (mathematics)](field-mathematics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distributive_property)

One of the defining properties of [algebraic structure](algebraic-structure.md) with two operations such as [ring](ring-mathematics.md) and [field](field-mathematics.md):

$$
a(b + c) = ab + ac
$$

This property shows how the two operations interact.
