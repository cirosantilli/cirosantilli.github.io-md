# ATP synthase

↑ **Parent:** [ATP synthesis mechanism](atp-synthesis-mechanism.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Cellular respiration protein](cellular-respiration-protein.md), [Protein complex](protein-complex.md), [Transmembrane protein](transmembrane-protein.md)

One of the most beautiful [molecular machines](molecular-machine.md) known!

The first one with such complexity that was uncovered.

The thing rotates like a water wheel for God's sake, except it uses [protons](proton.md) instead of water.

The ATP synthase complex is so large that [Power, Sex, Suicide by Nick Lane (2006)](power-sex-suicide-by-nick-lane-2006.md) page 123 contains a [cryoEM](cryogenic-electron-microscopy.md) image of several [ATP synthases](atp-synthase.md) on small membrane vesicles, this is the paper: [https://pubs.acs.org/doi/abs/10.1021/bi00437a031#](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/abs/10.1021/bi00437a031#) under [a fucking paywall](closed-access-academic-journals-are-evil.md).
