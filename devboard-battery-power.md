# Devboard battery power

↑ **Parent:** [Microprocessor development board](microprocessor-development-board.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Devboard_battery_power)

Many devborads require a 5V power supply.

This is common on wall transformers and [USB](usb.md), but not in [batteries](electric-battery.md).

For battery power you need a [transformer](transformer.md).
