[Whole organism simulation](whole-organism-simulation.md) of [C. elegans](caenorhabditis-elegans.md).

High level simulation only, no way to get from [DNA](dna-split.md) to worm! :-) Includes:
- [nervous system](nervous-system.md)
- muscle system

3D body viewer at: [http://browser.openworm.org/](http://browser.openworm.org/) TODO can you click on a cell to get its name?
