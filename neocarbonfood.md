# NeoCarbonFood

↑ **Parent:** [Food without photosynthesis](food-without-photosynthesis.md)

[http://neocarbonfood.fi/](http://neocarbonfood.fi/)

Previously known as "Food From Electricity", "NeoCarbonFood" sounds like a more commercializable version of it.

Uses electricity to [electrolyse](electrolysis.md) water into [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) and [oxygen](oxygen.md) molecules, and then use [bacteria](bacteria.md) that do [hydrogen chemosynthesis](hydrogen-chemosynthesis.md) to convert it into food.
