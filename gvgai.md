The project kind of died circa 2020 it seems, a shame. Likely they funding ran out. The domain is dead as of 2023, last archive from 2022: [https://web.archive.org/web/20220331022932/http://gvgai.net/](https://web.archive.org/web/20220331022932/http://gvgai.net/) is marked as funded by [DeepMind](deepmind.md). Researchers really should use university/GitHub domain names!

Similar goals to [Ciro's 2D reinforcement learning games](ciro-s-2d-reinforcement-learning-games.md), but they were focusing mostly on discrete games.
