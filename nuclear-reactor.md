# Nuclear reactor

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear chain reaction](nuclear-chain-reaction.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_reactor)

Some of the most notable ones:
- 1942: [Chicago Pile-1](chicago-pile-1.md): the first human-made [nuclear chain reaction](nuclear-chain-reaction.md).
- 1943: [X-10 Graphite Reactor](x-10-graphite-reactor.md): an intermediate step between the [nuclear chain reaction](nuclear-chain-reaction.md) prototype [Chicago Pile-1](chicago-pile-1.md) and the full blown mass production at [Hanford site](hanford-site.md). Located in the [Oak Ridge National Laboratory](oak-ridge-national-laboratory.md).
- 1944: [B Reactor](b-reactor.md) at the [Hanford site](hanford-site.md) produced the [plutonium](plutonium.md) used for [Trinity](trinity-nuclear-test.md) and [Fat Man](fat-man.md)
