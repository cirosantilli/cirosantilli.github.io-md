[https://discogs.com](https://discogs.com)

The [IMDb](imdb.md) of [music](music-split.md)! They actually have a reputation system apparently. And sneaked in a vinyl marketplace as well.

The website name sounds like play on words: disc + hog, with hog in the sense "memory-hog", i.e. something that consumes all your computer's memory.
