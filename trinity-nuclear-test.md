**[Video 10](#video-trinity-getting-the-job-done). Trinity: Getting The Job Done.** [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7QxsehUI0rY). Good video, clarifies several interesting technical points:
- [Gun-type fission weapon](gun-type-fission-weapon.md) were much easier to build as you don't need super synchronized charges as in [implosion-type fission weapon](implosion-type-fission-weapon.md). But they are less efficient.
- [Plutonium](plutonium.md) make much more efficient usage of [uranium](uranium.md), because you don't need to highly enrich a bunch of [Uranium-235](uranium-235.md) in the first place, but rather just use way less enriched [Uranium-235](uranium-235.md) to produce a bunch of [Plutonium](plutonium.md) by converting [Uranium-238](uranium-238.md)
