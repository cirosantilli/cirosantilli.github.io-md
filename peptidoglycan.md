# Peptidoglycan

↑ **Parent:** [Glycoprotein](glycoprotein.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Polymer](polymer.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peptidoglycan)

Forms the [bacterial cell wall](bacterial-cell-wall.md).

From the Wikipedia image we can see clearly the [polymer](polymer.md) structure formed: it is a mesh with:
- [sugar](sugar.md) [covalent bond](covalent-bond.md) chains in one direction. These have two types of [monosaccharide](monosaccharide.md), NAM and NAG
- [peptide](peptide.md) chains on the other, and only coming off from NAM
