# History of stimulated emission

↑ **Parent:** [Stimulated emission](stimulated-emission.md)

First postulated by [Einstein](albert-einstein.md) in 1917 on his paper [Zur Quantentheorie der Strahlung](on-the-quantum-theory-of-radiation.md)" ("[On the Quantum Theory of Radiation](on-the-quantum-theory-of-radiation.md)") as a more elegant way to rederive [Planck's law](planck-s-law.md) as part of the [Einstein coefficients](einstein-coefficients.md) framework.

At that time there was no other physical evidence supporting the existence of the concept except that it looked more elegant.
