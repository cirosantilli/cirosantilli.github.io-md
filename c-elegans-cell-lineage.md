# C. elegans cell lineage

↑ **Parent:** [Caenorhabditis elegans](caenorhabditis-elegans.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Cell lineage](cell-lineage.md)

Exactly 1033 [somatic cells](somatic-cell.md) on male, 959 on hermaphrodite, every time, counted as of 2020. A beauty.

Exactly 131 commit [apoptosis](apoptosis.md) in the hermaphrodite.

[https://www.wormatlas.org/celllineages.html](https://www.wormatlas.org/celllineages.html) contains the full lineage as some huge and impossible to view images. This image was taken directly from [The embryonic cell lineage of the nematode Caenorhabditis elegans](the-embryonic-cell-lineage-of-the-nematode-caenorhabditis-elegans.md) where it is split across many pages, it is a thing of beauty on the PDF.

[https://www.wormatlas.org/celllistsulston.htm](https://www.wormatlas.org/celllistsulston.htm) contains a non-hierarchical table with the cells and their names.
