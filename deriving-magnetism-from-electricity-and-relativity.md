# Deriving magnetism from electricity and relativity

↑ **Parent:** [Maxwell's equations require special relativity](maxwell-s-equations-require-special-relativity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deriving_magnetism_from_electricity_and_relativity)

It appears that [Maxwell's equations](maxwell-s-equations.md) can be derived directly from Coulomb's law + [special relativity](special-relativity.md):
- [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classical_electromagnetism_and_special_relativity#Deriving_magnetism_from_electrostatics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classical_electromagnetism_and_special_relativity#Deriving_magnetism_from_electrostatics)
- [https://physics.stackexchange.com/questions/3618/can-maxwells-equations-be-derived-from-coulombs-law-and-special-relativity](https://physics.stackexchange.com/questions/3618/can-maxwells-equations-be-derived-from-coulombs-law-and-special-relativity)
This idea is suggested by the [charged particle moving at the same speed of electrons thought experiment](moving-magnet-and-conductor-problem.md), which indicates that [magnetism](magnetism.md) is just a consenquence of [special relativity](special-relativity.md).
