# WhoisXMLAPI

↑ **Parent:** [DNS database](dns-database.md)

[https://dns-history.whoisxmlapi.com/api](https://dns-history.whoisxmlapi.com/api)

They do have [historic reverse IP search](historical-whois-database.md) at [https://dns-history.whoisxmlapi.com/api](https://dns-history.whoisxmlapi.com/api) but their data is not obviously more complete than [viewdns.info](viewdns-info.md), e.g.: as of March 2025:
- 62.22.60.56
  - [https://viewdns.info/reverseip/?t=1&host=62.22.60.56](https://viewdns.info/reverseip/?t=1&host=62.22.60.56) has traveltimenews.com from 2011
  - [https://dns-history.whoisxmlapi.com/api](https://dns-history.whoisxmlapi.com/api) is empty
- 66.175.106.158
  - [https://viewdns.info/reverseip/?host=66.175.106.158&t=1](https://viewdns.info/reverseip/?host=66.175.106.158&t=1) has a hit from 2011
  - [https://dns-history.whoisxmlapi.com/api](https://dns-history.whoisxmlapi.com/api) is empty
Their whois data seems much better quality however.

As of 2025, you can do historical whois for free on the API demo under [https://whois-history.whoisxmlapi.com/](https://whois-history.whoisxmlapi.com/) but it only shows the 3 newest records.

To unlock that, you have to create an account, which gives you 500 credits, and then:
- [https://tools.whoisxmlapi.com/whois-history-search](https://tools.whoisxmlapi.com/whois-history-search): each full historical whois report for a domain costs 50 points
- [https://tools.whoisxmlapi.com/reverse-whois-search](https://tools.whoisxmlapi.com/reverse-whois-search): each historical reverse whois search search costs 1 point
They do normalize [Gmail dot trick](dots-in-gmail-address.md), but not for the `googlemail` trick.
