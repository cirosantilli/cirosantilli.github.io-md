# Path to AGI

↑ **Parent:** [Artificial intelligence](artificial-intelligence-split.md)

There are two main ways to try and reach AGI:
- [AI training robot](ai-training-robot.md): expensive, slow, but realistic world
- [AI training game](ai-game.md): faster, less expensive, but possibly non-realistic-enough realistic
Which one of them to take is of of the most important technological questions of humanity according to [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md)

There is also an intermediate area of research/engineering where people try to first simulate the robot and its world realistically, use the simulation for training, and then transfer the simulated training to real robots, see e.g.: [realistic robotics simulation](realistic-robotics-simulation.md).
