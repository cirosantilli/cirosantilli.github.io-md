  **[Video 59](#video-expresso-2222-by-gilberto-gil-1972). Expresso 2222 by Gilberto Gil (1972)** [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nOaAoa1bWA). [https://youtu.be/aSFahdu5ga8?t=4584](https://youtu.be/aSFahdu5ga8?t=4584) In the [2002 documentary Tempo Rei](tempo-rei-documentary.md) he explains the origin of the song as being based on seeing trains come by in Bahia, when they still existed before [the automobile decimation](street-reclamation.md).

  <a id="video-oriente-by-gilberto-gil"></a>
  **[Video 60](#video-oriente-by-gilberto-gil). Oriente by Gilberto Gil.** [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3sMZmXXx0Rg). This song seems to be about a parent giving a mixture of good and weird advice. Perhaps it is what we all get, or at least the lucky ones of us.

  <a id="video-tenho-sede-by-gilberto-gil-1975"></a>
  **[Video 63](#video-tenho-sede-by-gilberto-gil-1975). Tenho sede by Gilberto Gil (1975)** [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aCOO57oWv3Y).
- <a id="video-sitio-do-picapau-amarelo-by-gilberto-gil-1977"></a>
  **[Video 64](#video-sitio-do-picapau-amarelo-by-gilberto-gil-1977). Sítio Do Picapau Amarelo by Gilberto Gil (1977)** [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kwVvpA9vlJM). This song is the opening theme of the [1977 children's television series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C3%ADtio_do_Picapau_Amarelo_(1977_TV_series)) based on the renowned children books of the same name by Brazilian author Monteiro Lobato.

  The same song was also used on the [2001 series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sítio_do_Picapau_Amarelo_(2001_TV_series)), e.g.: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i9RbBcDTRAI](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i9RbBcDTRAI).

  The choice of Gilberto Gil as a singer and composer was a stroke of genius, as his natural quirkiness perfectly suits the subject matter of the books.
