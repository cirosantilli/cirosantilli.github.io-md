# Authentication (cryptography)

↑ **Parent:** [Man-in-the-middle attack](man-in-the-middle-attack.md)

In the context of cryptography, authentication means "ensuring that the message you got comes from who you think it did".

Authentication is how we prevent the [man-in-the-middle attack](man-in-the-middle-attack.md).

Authentication is one of the hardest parts of cryptography, because the only truly secure way to do it is by driving to the other party yourself to establish a [pre-shared key](pre-shared-key.md) so you can do [message authentication code](message-authentication-code.md). Or to share your [public key](public-key-cryptography.md) with them if you are satisfied with the safety of [post-quantum cryptography](post-quantum-cryptography.md).
