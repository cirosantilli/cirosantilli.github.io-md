# Chinese musical instrument

↑ **Parent:** [Chinese traditional music](chinese-traditional-music-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Chinese_musical_instruments)

The main four instruments are undoubtedly:
- [guqin](guqin.md)
- [pipa](pipa.md)
- [guzheng](guzheng.md)
- [erhu](erhu.md)
but there is also amazing content on others which must not be missed, including:
- [suona](suona.md)
- [Xiao flute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xiao_(flute))

Infinite Wikipedia instrument list: [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Chinese_musical_instruments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Chinese_musical_instruments)
