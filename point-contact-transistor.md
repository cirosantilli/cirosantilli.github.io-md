# Point-contact transistor

↑ **Parent:** [Transistor](transistor.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Point-contact_transistor)

The first working one in 1947 by [John Bardeen](john-bardeen.md) and [walter Brattain](walter-houser-brattain.md) in [Bell Labs Murray Hill](bell-labs-murray-hill.md).

People had already [patented](patent.md) a lot of stuff before without being able to make them work. Nonsense.

As the name suggests, this is not very sturdy, and was quickly replaced by [bipolar junction transistor](bipolar-junction-transistor.md).
