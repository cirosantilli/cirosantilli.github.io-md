# How to store data in the Bitcoin blockchain

↑ **Parent:** [Bitcoin](bitcoin.md)

There are apparently two methods:
- in the script, e.g. as in the [Genesis block message](genesis-block-message.md)
- in output addresses

Specific implementations:
