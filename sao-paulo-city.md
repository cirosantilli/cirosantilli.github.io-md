Although [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) lived in São Paulo City nominally [during his studies](ciro-santilli-s-formal-education.md), it would be more precise to say that he lived in [University of São Paulo](university-of-sao-paulo.md)-land, because Ciro was cheap, didn't have a car, and did nothing but study, stay at home, go back to [Santos](santos-sao-paulo-brazil.md) to see his parents and the beach.

But the little he saw of the city made a deep impression on him.

The unreasonable size.

The unbearable contrasts.

[Caetano's Sampa](the-best-caetano-veloso-songs.md#video-sampa-by-caetano-veloso-1978) is the ultimate description of the city!
