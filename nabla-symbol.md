# Nabla symbol

ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nabla_symbol)

As if [Greek letters](greek-alphabet.md) weren't enough, [physicists](physicist-split.md) and [mathematicians](mathematician.md) also like to make up tons of symbols, [some of which look like the could actually be Greek letters](mathematical-symbol-that-looks-like-a-greek-letter-but-isn-t.md)!

Nabla is one of those: it was completely made up in modern times, and just happens to look like an inverted upper case [delta](delta-letter.md) to make things even more confusing!

Nabla means "harp" in Greek, which looks like the symbol.
