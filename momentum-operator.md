# Momentum operator

↑ **Parent:** [Position and momentum space](position-and-momentum-space.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Momentum_operator)

One dimension in [position representation](position-representation.md):

$$
\hat{p} = - i \hbar \pdv{}{x}
$$

In three dimensions In position representation, we define it by using the [gradient](gradient.md), and so we see that 

$$
\hat{p} = - i \hbar \pdv{}{x}
$$
