# Savannah River site

↑ **Parent:** [American nuclear weapon facility](american-nuclear-weapon-facility.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [American plutonium production](american-plutonium-production.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savannah_River_site)

This was one of the two [American plutonium production](american-plutonium-production.md) sites, together with the [Hanford site](hanford-site.md). [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qwKhz7BPBLY](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qwKhz7BPBLY) mentions it produced about 1/3 of the total American plutonium.

[https://www.reuters.com/article/world/americas-nuclear-headache-old-plutonium-with-nowhere-to-go-idUSKBN1HR1JT/](https://www.reuters.com/article/world/americas-nuclear-headache-old-plutonium-with-nowhere-to-go-idUSKBN1HR1JT/) claims that as of 2018 it stored the majority of the plutonium stockpile of the country.
