# Bilinear form

↑ **Parent:** [Multilinear map](multilinear-map.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bilinear_form)

Analogous to a [linear form](linear-form.md), a bilinear form is a [Bilinear map](bilinear-map.md) where the [image](image-mathematics.md) is the [underlying field of the vector space](underlying-field-of-a-vector-space.md), e.g. $\R^n \times \R^m \to \R$.

Some definitions require both of the input spaces to be the same, e.g. $\R^n \times \R^n \to \R$, but it doesn't make much different in general.

The most important example of a bilinear form is the [dot product](dot-product.md). It is only defined if both the input spaces are the same.
