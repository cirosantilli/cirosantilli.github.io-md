# Military-industrial complex

↑ **Parent:** [Society](society.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military–industrial_complex)

This is notably what the [United States](united-states-split.md) emerged to be after [World War II](world-war-ii.md). But it was likely what [Nazi Germany](nazi-germany.md) also was, and many other superpowers.

[Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) feels that much more relevant would be to also include academia as in "military-industrial-academic" complex, the [Wikipedia](wikipedia.md) page actually mentions precedents to this idea.

The addition of congress/[politicians](politician.md) is also relevant.

But hey, the name wouldn't sound so slick with three parts.

It is basically in this context that [American](united-states-split.md) science and technology flourished after [World War II](world-war-ii.md), including notably the development of [quantum electrodynamics](quantum-electrodynamics.md), [Richard Feynman](richard-feynman-split.md) being a prototypical example, having previously worked on the [Manhattan Project](manhattan-project.md).
