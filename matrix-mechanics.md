# Matrix mechanics

↑ **Parent:** [Equivalent alternatives to the Schrodinger equation](equivalent-alternatives-to-the-schrodinger-equation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matrix_mechanics)

Published by [Werner Heisenberg](werner-heisenberg.md) in 1925-07-25 as [quantum mechanical re-interpretation of kinematic and mechanical relations by Heisenberg (1925)](quantum-mechanical-re-interpretation-of-kinematic-and-mechanical-relations-by-heisenberg-1925.md), it offered the first general formulation of quantum mechanics.

It is apparently more closely related to the [ladder operator](ladder-operator.md) method, which is a more [algebraic](algebra-split.md) than the more [analytical](mathematical-analysis.md) [Schrödinger equation](schrodinger-equation.md).

It appears that this formulation makes the importance of the [Poisson bracket](poisson-bracket.md) clear, and explains why [physicists](physicist-split.md) are so obsessed with talking about [position and momentum space](position-and-momentum-space.md). This point of view also apparently makes it clearer that [quantum mechanics](quantum-mechanics-split.md) can be seen as a generalization of [classical mechanics](classical-mechanics.md) through the [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian-mechanics.md).

[QED and the men who made it: Dyson, Feynman, Schwinger, and Tomonaga by Silvan Schweber (1994)](qed-and-the-men-who-made-it-dyson-feynman-schwinger-and-tomonaga-by-silvan-schweber-1994.md) mentions however that [relativistic quantum mechanics](relativistic-quantum-mechanics-split.md) broke that analogy, because some 2x2 matrix had a different form, TODO find that again.

[Inward Bound by Abraham Pais (1988)](inward-bound-by-abraham-pais-1988.md) chapter 12 "Quantum mechanics, an essay" part (c) "A chronology" has some ultra brief, but worthwhile mentions of matrix mechanics and the [commutator](commutator.md).
