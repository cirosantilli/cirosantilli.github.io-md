# Freeman Dyson

↑ **Parent:** [Physicist](physicist-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [God](god.md), [Good](good.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freeman_Dyson)

[Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md)'s admiration for Dyson goes beyond his "unify all the things approach", which [Ciro loves](high-flying-bird-vs-gophers.md), but also extends to the way he talks and the things he says. Dyson is one of Ciro's favorite physicist.

Besides this, he was also very [idealistic](idealism.md) compassionate, and supported a peaceful resolution until [World War II](world-war-ii.md) with [United Kingdom](united-kingdom-split.md) was basically inevitable. Note that this was a strategic mistake.

Dyson is "hawk nosed" as mentioned in [Genius: Richard Feynman and Modern Physics by James Gleick (1994)](genius-richard-feynman-and-modern-physics-by-james-gleick-1994.md) chapter "Dyson". But he wasn't when he was young, see e.g. [https://i2.wp.com/www.brainpickings.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/03/freemandyson_child-1.jpg?resize=768%2C1064&ssl=1](https://i2.wp.com/www.brainpickings.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/03/freemandyson_child-1.jpg?resize=768%2C1064&ssl=1) It seems that his nose just never stopped growing after puberty.

He also has some fun stories, like him practicing [night climbing](night-climbing.md) while at [Cambridge University](university-of-cambridge.md), and having walked from [Cambridge](cambridge.md) to [London](london.md) (~86km!) in a day with his wheelchair bound friend.

[Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) feels that the label [child prodigy](child-prodigy.md) applies even more so to him than to [Feynman](richard-feynman-split.md) and [Julian Schwinger](julian-schwinger.md).
