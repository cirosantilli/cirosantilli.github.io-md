# Term of the University of Oxford

↑ **Parent:** [University of Oxford](university-of-oxford-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Academic term](academic-term.md)

Each term has 8 weeks, and the week number is often used to denote the time at which something happens.

Week 0 is also often used to denote the week before classes officially start. This is especially important in the first term of the year ([Michaelmas term](michaelmas-term.md)) where people are coming back to school and meeting old and new friends.

At the end of the year, after [Trinity term](trinity-term.md), students have exams. These basically account for all of the grades. In certain courses such as the [Physics course of the University of Oxford](physics-course-of-the-university-of-oxford.md), there is only new material on [Michaelmas term](michaelmas-term.md) and [Hilary term](hilary-term.md), [Trinity term](trinity-term.md) being revision-only. So you can imagine that during [Trinity term](trinity-term.md), students are going to be on edge.
