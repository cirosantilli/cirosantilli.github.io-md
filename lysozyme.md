# Lysozyme

↑ **Parent:** [List of proteins](list-of-proteins.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Enzyme](enzyme.md), [Inate immune system](innate-immune-system.md), [Protein](protein-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lysozyme)

The [second protein to have its structure determined](lysozyme-structure-resolution-1965.md), [after myoglobin](myoglobin-structure-resolution-1958.md), by [X-ray crystallography](x-ray-crystallography.md), in 1965.

Breaks up [peptidoglycan](peptidoglycan.md) present in the [bacterial cell wall](bacterial-cell-wall.md), which is thicker in [Gram-positive bacteria](gram-positive-bacteria.md), which is what this [enzyme](enzyme.md) seems to target.

Part of the [inate immune system](innate-immune-system.md).

It is present on basically everything that [mammals](mammal.md) and [birds](bird.md) [excrete](excretion.md), and it kills [bacteria](bacteria.md), both of which are reasons why it was discovered relatively early on.
