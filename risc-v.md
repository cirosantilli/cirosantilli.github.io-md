# RISC-V

↑ **Parent:** [List of instruction set architectures](list-of-instruction-set-architectures.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RISC-V)

The leading no-royalties options as of 2020.

[China](china-split.md) has been a major [RISC-V](risc-v.md) potential user in the late 2010s, since the country is trying to increase its [semiconductor industry](semiconductor-industry.md) independence, especially given economic sanctions imposed by the [USA](united-states-split.md).

E.g. a result of this, the [RISC-V Foundation](risc-v-international.md) moved its legal headquarters to [Switzerland](switzerland.md) in 2019 to try and overcome some of the sanctions.
