# Hewlett-Packard

↑ **Parent:** [Electronics vendor](electronics-vendor.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hewlett-Packard)

They do seem to have been very innovative, and have had a very good work culture. They also had a huge impact on the [Silicon Valley](silicon-valley.md) startup scene.

Some products they are known for:
- oscilloscopes
- [Atomic clocks](atomic-clock.md), notably highly portable ones, see e.g. [Video "Inside the HP 5061A Cesium Clock by CuriousMarc (2020)"](atomic-clock.md#video-inside-the-hp-5061a-cesium-clock-by-curiousmarc-2020)
- pocket calculator
