Some courses at least allow you to see material for free, e.g.: [https://www.coursera.org/learn/quantum-optics-single-photon/lecture/UYjLu/1-1-canonical-quantization](https://www.coursera.org/learn/quantum-optics-single-photon/lecture/UYjLu/1-1-canonical-quantization). Lots of video focus as usual for [MOOCs](massive-open-online-course.md).

Some are paywalled: [https://www.coursera.org/learn/theory-of-angular-momentum?specialization=quantum-mechanics-for-engineers](https://www.coursera.org/learn/theory-of-angular-momentum?specialization=quantum-mechanics-for-engineers)

It is extremely hard to find the course materials without enrolling, even if enrolling for free! By trying to make money, they make their website shit.

The comment section does have a lot of activity: [https://www.coursera.org/learn/statistical-mechanics/discussions/weeks/2](https://www.coursera.org/learn/statistical-mechanics/discussions/weeks/2)! Nice. And works like a proper issue tracker. But it is also very hidden.
