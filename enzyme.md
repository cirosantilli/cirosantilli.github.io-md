# Enzyme

↑ **Parent:** [Type of protein](type-of-protein.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enzyme)

A [protein](protein-split.md) that is a [catalyst](catalysis.md) for some [chemical reaction](chemical-reaction.md).

For an initial concrete example, consider [e. Coli K-12 MG1655 gene thrA](e-coli-k-12-mg1655-gene-thra.md).

Shows in detail how [aconitase](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aconitase) catalyses the citrate to isocitrate reaction in the [citric acid cycle](citric-acid-cycle.md).
