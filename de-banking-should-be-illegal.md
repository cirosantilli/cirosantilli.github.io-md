# De-banking should be illegal

↑ **Parent:** [De-banking](de-banking.md)

Courts of law should decide if your money is legal or not. Not private entities such as banks. This is actually a case for [cryptocurrencies](cryptocurrency-split.md) and [central bank digital currencies](central-bank-digital-currency.md).

[Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) had a fun mini-case of this with his [Barclays](barclays.md) account frozen for a few days in 2024 in the [UK](united-kingdom-split.md) after [receiving a large anonymous cryptocurrency donatio](sponsor/1000-monero-donation.md): [Barclays regulation](sponsor/1000-monero-donation/barclays-regulation.md).
