<h1 id="ciro-santilli-s-bicycle-wisdom">Ciro Santilli's bicycle wisdom</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ciro Santilli's cycling](ciro-santilli-s-cycling.md)

Sometimes, these are more than just mechanics, but also have deeper life analogues. The title of [Zen and the Art of Motorcycle Maintenance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zen_and_the_Art_of_Motorcycle_Maintenance) comes to mind. Sometimes they are just mechanics.

With more philosophical metaphors:
- when your bike breaks, that provides an unique opportunity to learn how to fix it: [try to fix your own bike before taking it to the shop](try-to-fix-your-own-bike-before-taking-it-to-the-shop.md)
- if the wind is blowing against you on the way out, it will likely blow behind you on the way back. But remember that the other way around also applies.
- always take one extra clothing layer than what you think you will need in your back pocket or sport bag, especially when time is changing fast in Sprint and Autumn. The weather on the road outside of town can change very quickly!
- if you took a turn, and it feels wrong, stop to check the map, and possibly backtrack to safety. When it feels wrong, it usually is a bad idea, e.g. roads where cars are too fast/too many. But if you take a wrong turn and it feels right, then follow it without fear and see what it leads to!
- don't carry a speedometer on your bike. Analysis can be done afterwards on [Strava](strava.md). The only measurement that matters is "how awesome am I feeling right now?". [Live in the moment](buddhism.md) instead of checking your speedometer every 10 seconds.
- cycle when you body calls it out of addiction, not out a goal that you've made up that must be reached

With less philosophical metaphors:
- learn how much water and food you need to take for a trip. Otherwise, you _will_ bonk at some time, when you least expect, it happens very suddenly.

  And then you better hope to God that you can find a food shop nearby. Luckily this was the case for Ciro's first and only bonk so far.

  And besides bonking all out, being tired and hungry makes you make stupid decision, especially where traffic is involved!

  Food is safety. Light is safety. Time is safety. Calm is safety. Chocolate bars and candy cannot count as lunch food, only delay lunch. A sandwich with ham cheese and salad is food. A bag of M&M's with a can of soda can bring you back from the dead.

  When you are not in familiar grounds, take twice as much as you think you might possibly ever need. [Hofstadter's law](hofstadter-s-law.md).

  You will also learn that, surprise surprise, [carbohydrates](carbohydrate.md) that you ate one or two days before a ride stay stored in your liver and muscles, and also greatly affect how quickly you will bonk, thus the concept of [carbohydrate loading](carbohydrate-loading.md).

  And surprise surprise: heat can also make you bonk! Who would have thought!
- correct saddle hight is fundamental, your legs must be almost fully stretched at the bottom position
- it is impossible to reach the correct tire pressure with (cheap?) hand pumps, their only purpose is to fill up a flat tire so you can get home after a long ride. But a track pump.
- clean and lube your chain. The speed benefit is instantaneous and [mind blowing](mind-blown.md). It also greatly improves gear shifting.

  This also prevents the chain from rusting, because the lube takes up the place where water would stay, and the muck makes it harder for water to evaporate.

  This is the most common bike maintenance mistake you see on the streets: people with that high pitched overly dry chain noise.

  **[Video 11](#video-how-to-get-a-perfectly-clean-chain-by-gcn-2016). How To Get A Perfectly Clean Chain by GCN (2016)** [Source](http://youtube.com/watch?v=KM6mzE5lQ0w).
- when a piece on your bike breaks and has no clear name written on it, you can try to identify it Google images
- the more you watch [YouTube](youtube.md) maintenance videos without haste, the more you end up learn random new stuff that unexpectedly saves you later
- if you took a turn, and it feels wrong, stop to check the map, and possibly backtrack to safety. When it feels wrong, it usually is a bad idea, e.g. roads where cars are too fast/too many
- public place with lots of people are bicycle parking [Hell](hell.md), because due to [anonymity](anonymity.md) and the large number of distractions, it becomes exponentially more likely that someone will [fuck](sexual-intercourse.md) you bike somehow, e.g. by dropping it on the ground. Always search a bit for a reasonable place to park, and avoid overcrowded parking spaces at all costs.
- gear change matters
  - when you get on your bike to start riding, start riding slowly and gradually switch up pedal forces and gears. Things may have shifted in a weird position as it gets kicked around in parking. Ciro managed to bend his derailleur like that!
  - [spin to win](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=43ci68I23hA), AKA learn to user your gears
  - it is not shameful to ride on your lower gears on a hill. You can actually go surprisingly fast with them, and conserve energy for later. Learn when to use each gear ratio.
- learn to identify your suppliers:
  - [https://www.wiggle.co.uk/](https://www.wiggle.co.uk/): in [Europe](europe.md), this is best place to buy clothing from, and also good for some bike parts. It is the most organized website, and contains non-generic [shit](feces.md) which [Amazon](amazon-split.md) is full of.

    For bike parts [Amazon](amazon-split.md) is also worth looking into however. Bike parts a bit different from clothing because you have to make sure that stuff fits, so you hopefully know exactly the part name before before buying it, and therefore website organization is not as crucial.

    Wiggle is however guilty of shameless: [discounts that happen more often than not](discounts-that-happen-more-often-than-not.md)
- always take your lights off the bike into your bag when you park, anywhere, and for any amount of time, even if a quick stop. [Drug addicts](drug-addict.md) are everywhere, always ready love to steal and resell them.
- sometimes you do something stupid like going into a really muddy path, and it is really fun, because you've never been there in your life. But then your bike gets really dirty, and your feet are wet and freezing, and you promise yourself you will never do something that stupid again. But then you do it again in a different location, because it was too much fun. [Once more unto the breach](once-more-unto-the-breach.md) just comes to Ciro's mind every such time. Embrace this.
