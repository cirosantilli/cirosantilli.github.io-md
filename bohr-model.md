# Bohr model

↑ **Parent:** [Model of the atom](model-of-the-atom.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bohr_model)

Was the first model to explain the [Balmer series](balmer-series.md), notably linking atomic spectra to the [Planck constant](planck-constant.md) and therefore to other initial [quantum mechanical](quantum-mechanics-split.md) observations.

This was one of the first major models that just said:

> I give up, I can't tie this to [classical physics](classical-physics.md) in any way, let's just roll with it, OK?

It still treats [electrons](electron.md) as little points spinning around the nucleus, but it makes the non-[classical](classical-physics.md) postulate that only certain angular momentums (and therefore energies) are allowed.
