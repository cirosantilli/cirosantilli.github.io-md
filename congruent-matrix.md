# Congruent matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Sylvester's law of inertia](sylvester-s-law-of-inertia.md)

Two [symmetric matrices](symmetric-matrix.md) $A$ and $B$ are defined to be congruent if there exists an $S$ in [$GL(n)$](general-linear-group.md) such that:

$$
A = S B S^T
$$
