# Prime k-tuple conjecture

↑ **Parent:** [Admissible prime k-tuple](admissible-prime-k-tuple.md)

There are infinitely many [prime k-tuples](prime-k-tuple.md) for every admissible tuple.

Generalization of the [Twin prime conjecture](twin-prime-conjecture.md).

As of 2023, there was no specific admissible tuple for which it had been proven that there infinite of, only bounds of type:

> there are infinitely 2-tuple instances with at most a finite bound

But these do not specify which specific tuple, e.g. [Yitang Zhang's theorem](yitang-zhang-s-theorem.md).
