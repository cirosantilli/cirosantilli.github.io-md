# Specific values of the Busy beaver function

↑ **Parent:** [Busy beaver function](busy-beaver-function.md)

The following things come to mind when you look into research in this area, especially the search for [BB(5)](bb-5.md) which was hard but doable:
- it is largely [recreational mathematics](recreational-mathematics.md), i.e. done by non-professionals, a bit like the [aperiodic tiling](aperiodic-tiling.md). Humbly, they tend to call their results [lemmas](lemma-mathematics.md)
- complex structure emerges from simple rules, leading to a complex [classification](classification-mathematics.md) with a few edge cases, much like the [classification of finite simple groups](classification-of-finite-simple-groups.md)
