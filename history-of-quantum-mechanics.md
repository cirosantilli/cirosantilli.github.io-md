# History of quantum mechanics

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum mechanics](quantum-mechanics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_quantum_mechanics)

The discovery of the [photon](photon-split.md) was one of the major initiators of quantum mechanics.

Light was very well known to be a wave through [diffraction](diffraction.md) experiments. So how could it also be a particle???

This was a key development for people to eventually notice that the [electron](electron.md) is also a wave.

This process "started" in 1900 with [Planck's law](planck-s-law.md) which was based on discrete energy packets being exchanged as exposed at [On the Theory of the Energy Distribution Law of the Normal Spectrum by Max Planck (1900)](on-the-law-of-distribution-of-energy-in-the-normal-spectrum.md).

This ideas was reinforced by [Einstein](albert-einstein.md)'s explanation of the [photoelectric effect](photoelectric-effect.md) in 1905 in terms of [photon](photon-split.md).

In the next big development was the [Bohr model](bohr-model.md) in 1913, which supposed non-[classical physics](classical-physics.md) new quantization rules for the [electron](electron.md) which explained the [hydrogen emission spectrum](hydrogen-emission-spectrum.md). The quantization rule used made use of the [Planck constant](planck-constant.md), and so served an initial link between the emerging quantized nature of [light](light.md), and that of the [electron](electron.md).

The final phase started in 1923, when [Louis de Broglie](louis-de-broglie.md) proposed that in analogy to photons, [electrons](electron.md) might also be waves, a statement made more precise through the [de Broglie relations](de-broglie-relations.md).

This event opened the floodgates, and soon [matrix mechanics](matrix-mechanics.md) was published in [quantum mechanical re-interpretation of kinematic and mechanical relations by Heisenberg (1925)](quantum-mechanical-re-interpretation-of-kinematic-and-mechanical-relations-by-heisenberg-1925.md), as the first coherent formulation of [quantum mechanics](quantum-mechanics-split.md).

It was followed by the [Schrödinger equation](schrodinger-equation.md) in 1926, which proposed an equivalent [partial differential equation](partial-differential-equation.md) formulation to [matrix mechanics](matrix-mechanics.md), a mathematical formulation that was more familiar to [physicists](physicist-split.md) than the matrix ideas of Heisenberg.

[Inward Bound by Abraham Pais (1988)](inward-bound-by-abraham-pais-1988.md) summarizes his views of the main developments of the subjectit:

> 
> - Planck's on the discovery of the quantum theory (1900);
> - Einstein's on the light-quantum (1905);
> - [Bohr's on the hydrogen atom](bohr-model.md) (1913);
> - Bose's on what came to be called quantum statistics (1924);
> - Heisenberg's on what came to be known as [matrix mechanics](matrix-mechanics.md) (1925);
> - and Schroedinger's on wave mechanics (1926).
