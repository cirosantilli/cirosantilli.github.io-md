# Special unitary group

↑ **Parent:** [Unitary group](unitary-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_unitary_group)

The complex analogue of the [special orthogonal group](special-orthogonal-group.md), i.e. the subgroup of the [unitary group](unitary-group.md) with determinant equals exactly 1 instead of an arbitrary complex number with absolute value equal 1 as is the case for the unitary group.
