# Parallel light

↑ **Parent:** [Collimated beam](collimated-beam.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parallel_light)

Often just called collimated light due to the [collimator](collimator.md) being the main procedure to obtain it.

However, you move very far away from the source, e.g. the [Sun](sun.md), you also get essentially parallel light.
