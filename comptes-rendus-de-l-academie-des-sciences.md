Apparently there were biweekly reports, that were grouped and published biannually on January and July, each one with a sequential tome number. 

For example, both [Marie Curie's Polonium paper](on-a-new-radioactive-substance-contained-in-pitchblende.md) and [Marie Curie's Radium paper](on-a-new-strongly-radioactive-substance-contained-in-pitchblende.md) were published in the second half of 1898 and fell in [tome 127](comptes-rendus-de-l-academie-des-sciences-tome-127.md).
