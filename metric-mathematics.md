# Metric (mathematics)

↑ **Parent:** [Topology](topology.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metric_(mathematics))

A metric is a function that give the distance, i.e. a [real number](real-number.md), between any two elements of a space.

A metric may be induced from a [norm](norm-mathematics.md) as shown at: [Section "Metric induced by a norm"](metric-induced-by-a-norm.md).

Because a [norm can be induced by an inner product](norm-induced-by-an-inner-product.md), and the [inner product](inner-product.md) given by the [matrix representation of a positive definite symmetric bilinear form](matrix-representation-of-a-positive-definite-symmetric-bilinear-form.md), in simple cases metrics can also be represented by a [matrix](matrix.md).
