Where most of [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md)'s ancestors came from, and why Ciro has the [Italian](italy.md) nationality as well as [Brazilian](brazil-split.md).

More specifically his paternal line comes from [Gissi in the Abruzzo region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gissi).

Ciro feels really bad by the fact that he does not speak Italian and has never visited Gissi as of 2020.

He would likely be able to learn Italian in like 3 months because it is so similar to [Portuguese and French which he already speaks](ciro-santilli-s-natural-languages-skills.md).
