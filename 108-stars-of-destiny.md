# 108 Stars of Destiny

↑ **Parent:** [Water Margin](water-margin.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Chinese numbered list](chinese-numbered-list.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/108_Stars_of_Destiny)

The concept is a strong part of [Water Margin](water-margin.md).

The very first chapter opens with [Grand Commander Hou](grand-commander-hou.md) disrespecting spirits by opening up a sealed tomb and unintentionally releasing 108 demons who presumably reincarnate as humans, a backstory to the events that follow years later.

This is well shown in the Japanese [The Water Margin](the-water-margin-1973-tv-series.md), but is skipped in the Chinese [The Water Margin](the-water-margin-1998-tv-series.md) unfortunately.

The concept is also central to the [Suikoden](suikoden.md) video game.

Actually, now that [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) thinks about it, these were already likely meant as a "[collectible](collecting.md)" element by the author of the original book, as is strongly suggested by all the little illustrations of each character present on the [Wikipedia](wikipedia.md) page. Just like e.g. [Catholic](catholic-church.md) [saints](saint.md). It's [Pokemon](pokemon.md), but 2000 years earlier.

Ciro would notably feel many years later, that as he [met random people online](ciro-santilli-s-e-soulmates.md) who were interested in [improving eduction](ourbigbook-com-split.md), or was otherwise networking to reach his goals, as if he were actually building up his 108 stars of destiny.

A central part, and perhaps the most interesting part of the novel, is how each character has been motivated by injustice to join the rebellion. This reminds Ciro of [Final Fantasy VI](final-fantasy-vi.md)
