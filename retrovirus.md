# Retrovirus

↑ **Parent:** [Virus](virus.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Positive-strand RNA virus](positive-strand-rna-virus.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Retrovirus)

Integrates its [RNA](rna.md) genome into the host genome.
- first RNA to DNA with [reverse transcriptase](reverse-transcriptase.md)
- then injects DNA into host genome with [integrase](integrase.md)

Sounds complicated! The advantage is likely as in [HIV](hiv.md): once inside the cell, it can remain hidden far away from the cell surface, but still infections.
