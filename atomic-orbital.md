# Atomic orbital

↑ **Parent:** [Schrödinger equation solution for the hydrogen atom](schrodinger-equation-solution-for-the-hydrogen-atom.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atomic_orbital)

In the case of the [Schrödinger equation solution for the hydrogen atom](schrodinger-equation-solution-for-the-hydrogen-atom.md), each orbital is one [eigenvector](eigenvector.md) of the solution.

Remember from [time-independent Schrödinger equation](time-independent-schrodinger-equation.md) that the final solution is just the weighted sum of the eigenvector decomposition of the initial state, analogously to [solving partial differential equations with the Fourier series](solving-partial-differential-equations-with-the-fourier-series.md).
