# Fiber optic coupling

↑ **Parent:** [Optical fiber equipment](optical-fiber-equipment.md)

This section is about stuff efficiently getting light into or out of [optical fibers](optical-fiber.md), or joining two optical fibers together end to end so that light goes through.

Historically this has been an important development, as it is much harder than with wires since [optical fiber](optical-fiber.md) has to be very narrow to work properly, e.g. this is mentioned a lot in [City of Light: The Story of Fiber Optics](city-of-light-the-story-of-fiber-optics.md).
