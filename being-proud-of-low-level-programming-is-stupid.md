# Being proud of low level programming is stupid

↑ **Parent:** [Systems programming](systems-programming-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Essays by Ciro Santilli](essays-by-ciro-santilli.md)

Ciro's word of caution for 2019 aspiring system programmers: [Should you waste your life with systems programming?](https://cirosantilli.com/linux-kernel-module-cheat/#should-you-waste-your-life-with-systems-programming)

This is basically a direct consequence of [backward design](backward-design.md).

The higher the level you can operate at, the better.

[C](c-programming-language.md) is better than [assembly](assembly-language.md), [userland](user-space.md) better than [kernelland](kernel-space.md).

The ideal level to operate at, and one of humankind's greatest ambitions is "[AGI](artificial-general-intelligence.md), make me money", the highest possible level.

Only go down a level when it seems necessary.
