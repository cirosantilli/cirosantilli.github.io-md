# Max Planck Society

↑ **Parent:** [German physics research institute](german-physics-research-institute.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Planck_Society)

A massive group of research institutes scattered across [Germany](germany.md). The constituent institutes are known as "[Max Planck Institutes](max-planck-institute.md) (for|of) X". They are primarily known for their incredible [natural science](natural-science.md) research, but they also cover [social sciences](social-science.md).
