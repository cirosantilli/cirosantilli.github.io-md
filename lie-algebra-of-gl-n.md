Is the [set of all n-by-y square matrices](matrix-ring.md).

Because [$GL(n)$](general-linear-group.md) is a [Lie group](lie-group.md) we can use [Section "Lie algebra of a matrix Lie group"](lie-algebra-of-a-matrix-lie-group.md).

For every matrix $x$ in the [set of all n-by-y square matrices](matrix-ring.md) $M_n$, $e^x$ has inverse $e^-x$.

Note that this works even if $x$ is not [invertible](invertible.md), and therefore not in [$GL(n)$](general-linear-group.md)!

Therefore, the Lie algebra of [$GL(n)$](general-linear-group.md) is the entire [$M_n$](matrix-ring.md).
