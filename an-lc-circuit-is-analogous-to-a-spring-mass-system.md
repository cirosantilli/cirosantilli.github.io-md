# An LC circuit is analogous to a spring-mass system

↑ **Parent:** [LC circuit](lc-circuit.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Spring-mass system](spring-mass-system.md)

Both are [harmonic oscillators](harmonic-oscillator.md).

In the [LC circuit](lc-circuit.md):
- the current current may be seen as the velocity and containing the [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md)
- the charge stored in the capacitor as the [potential energy](potential-energy.md)

You can kickstart motion in either of those systems in two ways:
- charge the capacitor, i.e. pull the string, and then let it go, i.e. close the circuit. This is the simpler one to realise. Shown concretely at: [Video 25. "LC circuit dampened oscillations on an oscilloscope by Queuerious Guy (2014)"](lc-circuit.md#video-lc-circuit-dampened-oscillations-on-an-oscilloscope-by-queuerious-guy-2014)
- give speed to the mass, i.e. make a current pass through the inductor
