# ARM architecture family

↑ **Parent:** [List of instruction set architectures](list-of-instruction-set-architectures.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Arm (company)](arm-company.md)

This [ISA](instruction-set-architecture.md) basically completely dominated the [smartphone](smartphone.md) market of the 2010s and beyond, but it started appearing in other areas as the end of [Moore's law](moore-s-law.md) made it more economical logical for large companies to start developing their own semiconductor, e.g. [Google custom silicon](google-custom-silicon.md), [Amazon custom silicon](amazon-custom-silicon.md).

It is exciting to see ARM entering the [server](server-computing.md), [desktop](desktop-computer.md) and [supercomputer](supercomputer.md) market circa 2020, beyond its dominant mobile position and roots.

[Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) likes [to see the underdogs rise](ciro-santilli-s-self-perceived-creative-personality.md), and bite off dominant ones.

The excitement also applies to [RISC-V](risc-v.md) possibly over ARM mobile market one day conversely however.

Basically, as long as were a huge company seeking to develop a [CPU](central-processing-unit.md) and able to control your own ecosystem independently of [Windows](microsoft-windows.md)' desktop domination (held by the need for backward compatibility with a billion end user programs), ARM would be a possibility on your mind.

- in 2020, the Fugaku supercomputer, which uses an ARM-based [Fujitsu](fujitsu.md) designed chip, because the number 1 fastest supercomputer in [TOP500](top500.md): [https://www.top500.org/lists/top500/2021/11/](https://www.top500.org/lists/top500/2021/11/)

  It was later beaten by another [x86](x86.md) supercomputer [https://www.top500.org/lists/top500/2022/06/](https://www.top500.org/lists/top500/2022/06/), but the message was clearly heard.
- 2012 [https://hackaday.com/2012/07/09/pedal-powered-32-core-arm-linux-server/](https://hackaday.com/2012/07/09/pedal-powered-32-core-arm-linux-server/) pedal-powered 32-core Arm Linux server. A [publicity stunt](publicity-stunt.md), but still, cool.
