# General relativity

↑ **Parent:** [Relativity](relativity-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_relativity)

Unifies both [special relativity](special-relativity.md) and [gravity](gravity.md).

Not compatible with the [Standard Model](standard-model-split.md), and the 2020 unification attempts are called [theory of everything](theory-of-everything.md).

One of the main motivations for it was likely having forces not be instantaneous, but rather mediated by [field](field-physics.md) to maintain the [principle of locality](principle-of-locality.md), just like [electromagnetism](electromagnetism-split.md) did earlier.
