It should be mentioned that when you start [Googling](google-split.md) for [PDE](partial-differential-equation.md) stuff, you will reach Han's writings a lot under his [GitHub Pages](github-pages.md): [http://hplgit.github.io/](http://hplgit.github.io/), and he is one of the main authors of the [FEniCS Project](fenics-project.md).

Unfortunately he died of [cancer](cancer.md) in 2016, shame, he seemed like a good educator.

He also published to [GitHub](github.md) pages with his own crazy [markdown](markdown.md)-like multi-output [markup language](markup-language.md): [https://github.com/hplgit/doconce](https://github.com/hplgit/doconce).

Rest in peace, Hans.
