# Leanpub

↑ **Parent:** [Publishing](publishing.md)

[https://leanpub.com](https://leanpub.com)

Founder: Peter Armstrong

The general idea is publishing entire books with usual copyright, but with gradual updates.

[http://ruboss.com/](http://ruboss.com/) documents their stack, a somewhat similar choice to [OurBigBook.com](ourbigbook-com-split.md) as of 2021, notably [Next.js](next-js.md). But backend in [Ruby on Rails](ruby-on-rails.md). They actually managed Apollo/[GraphQL](graphql.md), which [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) would have liked, but din't have the patience for.

The founder/CEO Peter Armstrong [https://www.linkedin.com/in/peterburtonarmstrong/](https://www.linkedin.com/in/peterburtonarmstrong/) He looks like a nice guy.
