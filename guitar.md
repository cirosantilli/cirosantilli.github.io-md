# Guitar

↑ **Parent:** [Musical instrument](musical-instrument.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guitar)

The [guitar](guitar.md) is a highly imperfect instrument if compared to something like a [piano](piano.md), which is much more [mathematically](mathematics-split.md) elegant.

However, [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) just loves this imperfection for some reason, especially in the case of the [electric guitar](electric-guitar.md).

Bending, sliding and strumming just feel to good to not have.

And Ciro sucks are doing things in parallel, so the more single threaded approach of the guitar fits his brain/abilities better.

For those reasons, the [electric guitar](electric-guitar.md) is Ciro's favorite [musical instrument](musical-instrument.md).
