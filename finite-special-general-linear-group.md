# Finite special general linear group

↑ **Parent:** [Special linear group](special-linear-group.md)

Just like for the [finite general linear group](finite-general-linear-group.md), the definition of special also works for finite fields, where 1 is the multiplicative identity!

Note that the definition of [orthogonal group](orthogonal-group.md) may not have such a clear finite analogue on the other hand.
