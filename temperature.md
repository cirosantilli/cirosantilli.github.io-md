# Temperature

↑ **Parent:** [Thermodynamics](thermodynamics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temperature)

For scales from absolute 0 like [Kelvin](kelvin.md), is proportional to the total kinetic energy of the material.

The [Boltzmann constant](boltzmann-constant.md) tells us how much energy that is, i.e. gives the slope.
