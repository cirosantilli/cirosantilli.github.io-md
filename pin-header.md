# Pin header

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical connector](electrical-connector.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pin_header)

These often come pre-soldered on [devboards](microprocessor-development-board.md), e.g. and allow for easy access to [GPIO](general-purpose-input-output.md) pins. E.g. they're present on the [Raspberry Pi 2](raspberry-pi-2.md).

Why would someone ever sell a devboard without them pre-soldered!
