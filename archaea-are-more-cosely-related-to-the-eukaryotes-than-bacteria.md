# Archaea are more cosely related to the eukaryotes than bacteria

↑ **Parent:** [Archaea](archaea.md)

[Archaea](archaea.md) are much more closely related to the [eukaryotes](eukaryote.md) than [bacteria](bacteria.md), see e.g. [Figure 1. "Coral of life by János Podani (2019)"](coral-of-life.md#image-coral-of-life-by-janos-podani-2019) which shows how [archaea](archaea.md) diverged from eukarya almost 2 By after [LUCA](last-universal-common-ancestor.md)!

It therefore appears that the [mitochondrial endosymbiosis](mitochondrial-endosymbiosis.md) happened when a [bacteria](bacteria.md) like cell joined up with an [archaea](archaea.md).

Some notable points in which [archaea](archaea.md) look more like [eukaryotes](eukaryote.md) than [bacteria](bacteria.md):
- although they don't have a [cell nucleus](cell-nucleus.md), they do have [histones](histone.md)! Mentioned at:
  - [Power, Sex, Suicide by Nick Lane (2006)](power-sex-suicide-by-nick-lane-2006.md)
  - [https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.94.23.12633](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.94.23.12633)
