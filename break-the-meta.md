# Break the meta

↑ **Parent:** [Meta (game)](meta-game.md)

To break the meta means to find a new strategy that offers a significant advantage over the existing [meta](meta-game.md).

For the specific of [video game](video-game-split.md) [glitch](glitch.md) breaks see: [Section "Meta breaking glitch"](meta-breaking-glitch.md).

Due to [Ciro Santilli's self perceived creative personality](ciro-santilli-s-self-perceived-creative-personality.md), [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) is very attracted to meta breaks.

[Dick Fosbury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dick_Fosbury) created and implemented the Fosbury Flop jump style in 1968.

Akiyo Noguchi asks the rules while climbing! | Beta Break Ep.1 by Albert Ok (2020)** [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sa46ZBDRT1E). Happened at the 2015 IFSC Climbing World Cup during the Haiyang, [China](china-split.md), bouldering event. The author has [a playlist](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=07dmwivNcBk&list=PLsfWe31L6Quop7WDY7ky711o_Jhp4LKaS) of such [climbing](climbing.md) meta breaks. In climbing, the [meta](meta-game.md) is called "the [beta](beta-climbing.md)". Climbing competitions are perhaps the sport in which the meta is broken the most often, since each stage is unique.
