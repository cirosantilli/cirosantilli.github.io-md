# Publication by Marie Curie

↑ **Parent:** [Marie Curie](marie-curie.md)

God, how terrible the current systems are! E.g. looking for the [Polonium](polonium.md) publication from 1898 and can't find it easily! Presumably would be in the [Comptes rendus de l'Académie des Sciences](comptes-rendus-de-l-academie-des-sciences.md) 1898. There are two per year, and from searches would be tome 127. Finally found at: [https://archive.org/details/ComptesRendusAcademieDesSciences0127/page/n5/mode/2up](https://archive.org/details/ComptesRendusAcademieDesSciences0127/page/n5/mode/2up)

A decent manual list of publications from the [Comptes rendus de l'Académie des Sciences](comptes-rendus-de-l-academie-des-sciences.md): [https://comptes-rendus.academie-sciences.fr/page/marie-skodowska-curie_fr/](https://comptes-rendus.academie-sciences.fr/page/marie-skodowska-curie_fr/) They group [Marie Curie's Polonium paper](on-a-new-radioactive-substance-contained-in-pitchblende.md) and [Marie Curie's Radium paper](on-a-new-strongly-radioactive-substance-contained-in-pitchblende.md) together without giving the title of the second one though, but the page is given.
