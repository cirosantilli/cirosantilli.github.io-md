# Scalar (mathematics)

↑ **Parent:** [Vector (mathematics)](vector-mathematics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scalar_(mathematics))

A member of the [underlying field of a vector space](underlying-field-of-a-vector-space.md). E.g. in $\R^3$, the underlying field is $\R$, and a scalar is a member of $\R$, i.e. a [real number](real-number.md).
