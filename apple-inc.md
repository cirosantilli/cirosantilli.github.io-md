## Think different

↑ **Parent:** [Apple Inc.](apple-inc.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [It's Popular Now It Sucks](literature.md#it-s-popular-now-it-sucks)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Think_different)

Of course, this only made sense when Apple was more of an underdog to [IBM](computer.md#ibm), and [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli.md) greatly [admires their defiance of the norm](ciro-santilli-s-psychology-and-physiology.md#ciro-santilli-s-self-perceived-creative-personality).

As of 2020 however, [Apple](apple-inc.md) is kind of on the top of the mobile world, and Think different simply makes no sense anymore, notably because it relies on [closed source offline software used by millions](software.md#closed-source-offline-software-used-by-millions).

[it's Popular Now It Sucks](literature.md#it-s-popular-now-it-sucks) comes to mind.

This is a trap every company that prides itself on it's "alternative culture" sets for itself. If they succeed, they could become the norm.

### MacOS

↑ **Parent:** [Apple Inc product](#apple-inc-product)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Closed source offline software used by millions](software.md#closed-source-offline-software-used-by-millions)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MacOS)

- if you want to be ripped off, just use [Microsoft Windows](microsoft.md#microsoft-windows) which has more software available
