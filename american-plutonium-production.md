# American plutonium production

↑ **Parent:** [American nuclear weapons program](american-nuclear-weapons-program.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Weapons-grade nuclear material](weapons-grade-nuclear-material.md)

[https://sgp.fas.org/othergov/doe/pu50yc.html](https://sgp.fas.org/othergov/doe/pu50yc.html) mentions:

> The United States Government has used 14 [plutonium](plutonium.md) production reactors at the [Hanford](hanford-site.md) and [Savannah River](savannah-river-site.md) sites to produce plutonium for the U.S. nuclear weapons stockpile and DOE research and development programs. From 1944 to 1994, these reactors produced 103.4 metric tons of plutonium; 67.4 MT at Hanford, and 36.1 at Savannah River.

That site also contains a good summary of the closed shutdown reactors in each site. These are publicly disclosed e.g. at: [https://www.hanford.gov/page.cfm/ProjectsFacilities](https://www.hanford.gov/page.cfm/ProjectsFacilities)

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qwKhz7BPBLY](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qwKhz7BPBLY) mentions that before they stopped production at the end of the [Cold War](cold-war.md), [Hanford site](hanford-site.md) produced 2/3 of the total American stockpile, and [Savannah River site](savannah-river-site.md) produced 1/3.

[https://www.reuters.com/article/world/americas-nuclear-headache-old-plutonium-with-nowhere-to-go-idUSKBN1HR1JT/](https://www.reuters.com/article/world/americas-nuclear-headache-old-plutonium-with-nowhere-to-go-idUSKBN1HR1JT/) claims that as of 2018 [Savannah River site](savannah-river-site.md) stored the majority of the stockpile.
