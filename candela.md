# Candela

↑ **Parent:** [Luminous intensity](luminous-intensity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candela)

Candela is [Lumen](lumen-unit.md) density per [solid angle](https://ourbigbook.com/go/topic/solid-angle). A sphere emitting 1 Candela uniformly in all directions produces 4π [Lumen](lumen-unit.md) total power.
