# Natural sciences course of the University of Cambridge

↑ **Parent:** [Course of the University of Cambridge](course-of-the-university-of-cambridge.md)

Home page: [https://www.natsci.tripos.cam.ac.uk/](https://www.natsci.tripos.cam.ac.uk/)

Their organization is interesting, and allows students to take broad courses on year 1 and 2 and only pick a specialization in year 3 without having to decide upfront when the join the university. This is the correct way of doing it pre-Web.

As per [https://www.natsci.tripos.cam.ac.uk/course-structure](https://www.natsci.tripos.cam.ac.uk/course-structure) as of 2026, the way the course works is:

- Year 1 and 2: you pick 3 random subjects, e.g. mathematics, physics
- Year 2: pick 3 random subjects
- Year 3 and 4: 1 major subject, but then within that there are multiple possible module choices
  - physics: [https://www.phy.cam.ac.uk/study/undergraduate/undergraduate-courses](https://www.phy.cam.ac.uk/study/undergraduate/undergraduate-courses)
    - year 3 has a few mandatory subjects (core) and a few optional ones
    - year 4 has major and minor subjects
  - chemistry: [https://teaching.ch.cam.ac.uk/course-guides](https://teaching.ch.cam.ac.uk/course-guides)
