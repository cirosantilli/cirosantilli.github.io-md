# Gao Qiu

↑ **Parent:** [Water Margin character](water-margin-character.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gao_Qiu)

The ball game is named [cuju](cuju.md).

He is extremely despicable and petty, enjoys [only useless things](art-split.md), and is vengeful and disloyal. [Rooting for sport teams is stupid](rooting-for-sport-teams-is-stupid.md).

Surely his family name 俅 is a pun on the same sounding character 求 for ball.
