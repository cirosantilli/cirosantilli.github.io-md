# Quantum chemistry

↑ **Parent:** [Computational chemistry](computational-chemistry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_chemistry)

Ah, the jewel of [computational physics](computational-physics.md).

Also known as an [ab initio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ab_initio_quantum_chemistry_methods) method: no experimental measurement is taken as input, [QED is all you need](quantum-electrodynamics.md).

But since QED is thought to fully describe all relevant aspects [molecules](molecule.md), it could be called "the" ab initio method.

For one, if we were able to predict protein molecule interactions, our understanding of [molecular biology technologies](molecular-biology-technologies.md) would be solved.

No more ultra expensive and complicated [X-ray crystallography](x-ray-crystallography.md) or [cryogenic electron microscopy](cryogenic-electron-microscopy.md).

And the fact that [quantum computers](quantum-computing-split.md) are one of the most promising advances to this field, is also very very exciting: [Section "Quantum algorithm"](quantum-algorithm.md).
