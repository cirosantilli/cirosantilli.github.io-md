### Amazon anticompetitive acquisition

↑ **Parent:** [Amazon acquisition](#amazon-acquisition)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Antitrust](law.md#competition-law)

[Amazon](amazon.md) is apparently notorious for having bought off many competitors, many of them just to kill off the competition and clear the way, not to actually reuse them.

[https://youtu.be/tfAhTtBlb2Q?t=849](https://youtu.be/tfAhTtBlb2Q?t=849) from [Video 8. "Jeff Bezos Revealed by Bloomberg (2015)"](#video-jeff-bezos-revealed-by-bloomberg-2015) clearly shows [Tim O'Reilly](software.md#tim-o-reilly) saying that very clearly about Bezos.

> I do know of a number of cases in which he \[Bezos\] has acquired companies in order to take out competitors, potential future competitors. Rather than because he actually wants that business to continue.

Perhaps O'Reilly who is the bookselling business is not the greatest fan of Jeff. But still. My God.

[https://www.yalelawjournal.org/pdf/e.710.Khan.805_zuvfyyeh.pdf](https://www.yalelawjournal.org/pdf/e.710.Khan.805_zuvfyyeh.pdf) Amazon's Antitrust Paradox by Lina M . Khan from The Yale Law Journal raises this incredible issue.

### Amazon custom silicon

↑ **Parent:** [Amazon infrastructure](#amazon-infrastructure)

Like [Google custom silicon](google.md#google-custom-silicon), [Amazon](amazon.md) server operations are so large that with the slowdown of [Moore's law](computer-hardware.md#moore-s-law), it started being worth it for them to develop custom in-house silicon to serve as a competitive advantage, not to be sold for external companies. Can you imagine the scale required to justify silicon development investment that is not sold externally!
