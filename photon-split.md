# Photon

↑ **Parent:** [Elementary particle](elementary-particle.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Elementary particle](elementary-particle.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photon)

Initially light was though of as a wave because it experienced interference as shown by experiments such as:
- [double-slit experiment](double-slit-experiment.md)
- [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newton%27s_rings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newton%27s_rings)

But then, some key experiments also start suggesting that [light](light.md) is made up of [discrete](discrete.md) packets:
- [Compton scattering](compton-scattering.md), also suggests that photons carry momentum
- [photoelectric effect](photoelectric-effect.md)
- [single photon production and detection experiments](single-photon-production-and-detection.md)
and in the understanding of the 2020 [Standard Model](standard-model-split.md) the photon is one of the [elementary particles](elementary-particle.md).

This duality is fully described mathematically by [quantum electrodynamics](quantum-electrodynamics.md), where the photon is modelled as a quantized excitation of the photon field.
