# WordNet

↑ **Parent:** [List of ontologies](list-of-ontologies.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WordNet)

Groups concepts by [hyponymy and hypernymy](hyponymy-and-hypernymy.md) and [meronymy and holonymy](meronymy-and-holonymy.md). That actually makes a lot of sense! TODO: is there a clear separation between hyponymy and meronymy?

Browse: [http://wordnetweb.princeton.edu/perl/webwn](http://wordnetweb.princeton.edu/perl/webwn) Appears dead as of 2025 lol.

> The online version of WordNet has been deprecated and is no longer available.

Does not contain intermediat scientific terms, only very common ones, e.g. no mention, of "[Josephson effect](josephson-effect.md)", "[photoelectric effect](photoelectric-effect.md)"
