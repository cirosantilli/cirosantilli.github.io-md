# Silicon Graphics

↑ **Parent:** [Semiconductor company](semiconductor-company.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silicon_Graphics)

This company is a bit like [Sun Microsystems](sun-microsystems.md), you can hear a note of awe in the voice of those who knew it at its peak. This was a bit before [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md)'s awakening.

Those people created [OpenGL](opengl.md) for [God](god.md)'s sake! Venerable.

Both of them and Sun kind of died in the same way, unable to move from the [workstation](workstation.md) to the [personal computer](personal-computer.md) fast enough, and just got killed by the scale of competitors who did, notably [Nvidia](nvidia.md) for graphics cards.

Some/all [Nintendo 64 games](nintendo-64-game.md) were developed on it, e.g. it is well known that this was the case for [Super Mario 64](super-mario-64.md).

Also they were a big [UNIX](unix.md) vendor, which is another kudos to the company.
