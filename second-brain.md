# Second brain

↑ **Parent:** [Personal knowledge base](personal-knowledge-base.md)

In the 2020's, this refers to writing down everything you know, usually in some graph structured way.

This is somewhat the centerpiece of [Ciro Santilli's documentation superpowers](ciro-santilli-s-documentation-superpowers.md): dumping your brain into text form, which he has been doing through [Ciro Santilli's website](cirosantilli-com-split.md).

This is also the closest one can get to immortality pre full blown [transhumanism](transhumanism.md).

Ciro's still looking for the restore this plaintext backup on a new body though.

It is a good question, how much of your knowledge you would be able to give to others with text and images. It is likely almost all of it, except for coordination/signal processing tasks.

His passion for [braindumping](braindumping.md) like this is a big motivation behind [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md)'s [OurBigBook.com](ourbigbook-com-split.md) work.
