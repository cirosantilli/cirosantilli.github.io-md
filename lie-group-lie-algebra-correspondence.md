# Lie group-Lie algebra correspondence

↑ **Parent:** [Lie algebra](lie-algebra.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lie_group–Lie_algebra_correspondence)

Every [Lie algebra](lie-algebra.md) corresponds to a single [simply connected](simply-connected-space.md) [Lie group](lie-group.md).

The [Baker-Campbell-Hausdorff formula](baker-campbell-hausdorff-formula.md) basically defines how to map an algebra to the group.
