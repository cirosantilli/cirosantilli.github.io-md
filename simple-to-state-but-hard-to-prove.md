# Simple to state but hard to prove

↑ **Parent:** [The beauty of mathematics](the-beauty-of-mathematics.md)

One of the most beautiful things in mathematics are theorems of conjectures that are very simple to state and understand (e.g. for [K-12](k-12.md), lower [undergrad](undergraduate-education.md) levels), but extremely hard to prove.

This is in contrast to conjectures in certain areas where you'd have to study for a few months just to precisely understand all the definitions and the interest of the problem statement.
