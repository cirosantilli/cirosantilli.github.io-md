# Gauge theory

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical formulation of quantum field theory](mathematical-formulation-of-quantum-field-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gauge_theory)

The term and idea was first introduced initialized by [Hermann Weyl](hermann-weyl.md) when he was working on combining [electromagnetism](electromagnetism-split.md) and [general relativity](general-relativity.md) to formulate [Maxwell's equations in curved spacetime](maxwell-s-equations-in-curved-spacetime.md) in 1918 and published as [Gravity and electricity by Hermann Weyl (1918)](gravity-and-electricity-by-hermann-weyl-1918.md). Based on perception that [$U(1)$](unitary-group-of-degree-1.md) symmetry implies [charge conservation](charge-conservation.md). The same idea was later adapted for [quantum electrodynamics](quantum-electrodynamics.md), a context in which is has even more impact.
