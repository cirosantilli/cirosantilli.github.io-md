# University IP ownership

↑ **Parent:** [University policy](university-policy.md)

[Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md)'s general feeling is that university should not own IP, it should belong to the researchers. Instead, university should help researchers make their startups, so they can become big, and then we can tax them and reinvest in the universities.

Of course, this goes through the [nonprofit impact measurement](nonprofit-impact-measurement.md) difficulty. Maybe we could instead limit the IP to some reasonably small percentage, like 10%?

But still, as of 2020, if feels like universities are way too greedy.

- [https://youtu.be/ji5_MqicxSo?t=1406](https://youtu.be/ji5_MqicxSo?t=1406) Achieving Your Childhood Dreams by Randy Pausch (2007). At this timestamp he tells a story about how university IP issues almost ruined a collaboration he was passionate about.
