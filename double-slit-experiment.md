# Double-slit experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum mechanics experiment](quantum-mechanics-experiment.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Double-slit_experiment)

Amazingly confirms the wave particle duality of [quantum mechanics](quantum-mechanics-split.md).

The effect is even more remarkable when done with individual particles such individual [photons](photon-split.md) or [electrons](electron.md).

[Richard Feynman](richard-feynman-split.md) liked to stress how this experiment can illustrate the core ideas of [quantum mechanics](quantum-mechanics-split.md). Notably, he night have created the [infinitely many slits thought experiment](infinitely-many-slits-thought-experiment.md) which illustrates the [path integral formulation](path-integral-formulation.md).
