This section is about [Nvidia](nvidia.md) GPUs that are focused on compute rather than rendering.

Until 2020 these were branded as [Nvidia Tesla](nvidia-tesla.md), but then [Nvidia](nvidia.md) dropped that brand due to confusion with the [Tesla Inc.](https://ourbigbook.com/go/topic/tesla-inc) the car maker.[https://wccftech.com/nvidia-drops-tesla-brand-to-avoid-confusion-with-tesla/](https://wccftech.com/nvidia-drops-tesla-brand-to-avoid-confusion-with-tesla/).
