# Piano

↑ **Parent:** [Musical instrument](musical-instrument.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piano)

The piano is the most elegant non-electronic instrument. But it requires way too many strings, so expensive and not portable.

Also it allows for no [legato](legato.md) or [vibrato](vibrato.md).

The [guitar](guitar.md) is kind of the opposite.

After computer [sound synthesis](sound-synthesis.md) however, all of these distinctions become meaningless.
