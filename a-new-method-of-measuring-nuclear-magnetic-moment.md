# A New Method of Measuring Nuclear Magnetic Moment

↑ **Parent:** [Work by Isidor Rabi](work-by-isidor-rabi.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Paper published on Physical Review ](paper-published-on-physical-review.md)

[https://journals.aps.org/pr/abstract/10.1103/PhysRev.53.318](https://journals.aps.org/pr/abstract/10.1103/PhysRev.53.318) 

[Physical Review](physical-review.md) Volume 53, page 318.

Not [paywalled](paywall.md) as of 2024! A miracle! It is barely one page long.

This is the paper that contains the first successful report of experimental [nuclear magnetic moment](nuclear-magnetic-moment.md) observation.

They promise more at the end:

> We have tried this experiment with LiC1 and observed the resonance peaks of Li and Cl. The effects are very striking and the resonances sharp (Fig. 1). A full account of this experiment, together with the values of the nuclear moments, will be published when the homogeneous field is recalibrated.

and this promise was fulfilled on the later [The Molecular Beam Resonance Method for Measuring Nuclear Magnetic Moments](the-molecular-beam-resonance-method-for-measuring-nuclear-magnetic-moments.md).

**[Figure 12](#image-figure-1-of-a-new-method-of-measuring-nuclear-magnetic-moment). Figure 1 of A New Method of Measuring Nuclear Magnetic Moment**. This figure sums it all up: they were measuring the intensity of one side of a [molecular beam](molecular-beam.md) after a [Stern-Gerlach experiment](stern-gerlach-experiment.md).

Then, they vary the constant [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) before the splitting, and at the same time apply a fixed radio frequency to the beam.

When the constant magnetic field makes the energy gap match the radio frequency input, [nuclear spin](nuclear-magnetic-moment.md) of many atoms goes to the anti-aligned direction, the beam gets diverted, so the previously detected beam gets weaker.
