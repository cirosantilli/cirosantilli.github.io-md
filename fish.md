# Fish

↑ **Parent:** [Vertebrate](vertebrate.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Paraphyletic subgroup](paraphyletic-subgroup.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fish)

[Vertebrates](vertebrate.md) minus [tetrapods](tetrapod.md).

This paraphyletic subgroup is easy to form the "acquatic only" (fishes) vs "things that come out of water" ([tetrapods](tetrapod.md)). Though [mudfishes](mudfish.md) make that distinction harder.

Which kind of makes sense, why would you want for limbs unless you are going to stay out of water!
