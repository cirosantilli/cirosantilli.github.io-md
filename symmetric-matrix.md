# Symmetric matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Named matrix](named-matrix.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symmetric_matrix)

A [matrix](matrix.md) that equals its [transpose](transpose.md):

$$
M = M^T
$$

Can represent a [symmetric bilinear form](symmetric-bilinear-form.md) as shown at [matrix representation of a symmetric bilinear form](matrix-representation-of-a-symmetric-bilinear-form.md), or a [quadratic form](quadratic-form.md).
