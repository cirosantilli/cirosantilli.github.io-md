# Cancer is natural selection gone wrong

↑ **Parent:** [Cancer](cancer.md)

A cool thought about cancer expressed at [Power, Sex, Suicide by Nick Lane (2006)](power-sex-suicide-by-nick-lane-2006.md) is that [cancer](cancer.md) it is the direct product of [natural selection](natural-selection.md) gone wrong!

Cancer cells are obviously selected against [anti-cancer mechanism](anti-cancer-mechanism.md), which when they manage to evade, they reproduce uncontrollably, gaining more and more momentum.
