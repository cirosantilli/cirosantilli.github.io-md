# Vienna

↑ **Parent:** [City in Austria](city-in-austria.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vienna)

Impressions of Vienna in 2026:

- they have heavy industry, unlike the [UK](united-kingdom-split.md)
- lots of free water taps
- lots of playgrounds
- Vespa motorcycles
- the cold water is very cold, from mountain ice. The hot water is scalding hot, I burnt myself twice.
