# Great doubt

↑ **Parent:** [Koan](koan.md)

The type of feeling of confusion and distrut for your sense that some [Koans](koan.md) attempt to instill.

[Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md)'s preferred version of it is [physics and the illusion of life](physics-and-the-illusion-of-life.md).
