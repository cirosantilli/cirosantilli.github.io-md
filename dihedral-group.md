# Dihedral group

↑ **Parent:** [Important discrete mathematical group](important-discrete-mathematical-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dihedral_group)

Our notation: $D_n$, called "dihedral group of degree n", means the dihedral group of the [regular polygon](regular-polygon.md) with $n$ sides, and therefore has order $2n$ (all rotations + flips), called the "dihedral group of [order](order-algebra.md) 2n".
