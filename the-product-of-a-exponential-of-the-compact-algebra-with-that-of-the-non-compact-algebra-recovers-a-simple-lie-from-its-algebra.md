# The product of a exponential of the compact algebra with that of the non-compact algebra recovers a simple Lie from its algebra

Example at: [Lie Groups, Physics, and Geometry by Robert Gilmore (2008)](lie-groups-physics-and-geometry-by-robert-gilmore-2008.md) Chapter 7 "EXPonentiation".
