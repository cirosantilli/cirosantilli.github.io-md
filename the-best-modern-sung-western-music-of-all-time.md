# The best modern sung Western music of all time

↑ **Parent:** [Music](music-split.md)

You have to know the language to appreciate them.

The 60's and 70's were the days, those great [proxy wars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proxy_war) and [CIA dictatorships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Condor) allowed hippies to make awesome freedom music without too imminent a fear of death.

Songs making fun of things or that are pure [Brazil](brazil-split.md) nostalgia are also accepted. No love songs, ever. Except [some by Caetano](brazilian-pop.md), but that's it!

English:
- 1967 [Red Telephone](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EtYS3EYjVyk) track from the [Forever Changes (1967)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forever_Changes) album by [Love](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Love_(band))
- 1967 [White Rabbit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EUY2kJE0AZE) track from the [Surrealistic Pillow (1967)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surrealistic_Pillow) album by [Jefferson Airplane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jefferson_Airplane)

French:
- [Emmenez-moi](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zKbXUcJawUI) ([wiki](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emmenez-moi_(chanson))) from the album Entre Deux Rêves (1967) by [Charles Aznavour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Aznavour)
