# Hadamard gate

↑ **Parent:** [Single-qubit gate](single-qubit-gate.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_logic_gate#Hadamard_gate)

The [Hadamard gate](hadamard-gate.md) takes $\ket{0}$ or $\ket{1}$ ([quantum states](quantum-state.md) with probability 1.0 of measuring either 0 or 1), and produces states that have equal probability of 0 or 1.

<a id="equation-hadamard-gate-matrix"></a>
$$
H = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & -1 \end{bmatrix}
$$
