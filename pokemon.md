One of the main [children cartoons Ciro Santilli liked to watch](children-cartoons-ciro-santilli-liked-to-watch.md). Part of the [Pokemon Mania](pokemon-mania.md) of the 90s of course.

Ciro could not understand why Nintendo won't make a proper 3D [MMORPG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massively_multiplayer_online_role-playing_game) Pokemon with actually 3D Pokemon roaming the land, which is obviously what everyone wants. There are even fan games getting there!
- [https://pokemon-mmo-3d.com](https://pokemon-mmo-3d.com) Interesting concept here, where when you deploy a Pokemon, you start controlling the Pokemon rather than the main character
- [https://www.quora.com/Why-won-t-Nintendo-make-an-open-world-3D-Pokémon-RPG/answer/Ciro-Santilli](https://www.quora.com/Why-won-t-Nintendo-make-an-open-world-3D-Pokémon-RPG/answer/Ciro-Santilli)
until this explaiend it beautifully [Video 5. "The Downfall Of Mainline Pokemon Games by GONZ media (2020)"](#video-the-downfall-of-mainline-pokemon-games-by-gonz-media-2020):
- Pokemon became an [industrialized](industry.md) yearly game spitting machine, and therefore any new feature is just extra risk
- Pokemon was kept mostly as a handheld exclusive to sell handlhelds, with a few early exceptions such as [Pokemon Snap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pokémon_Snap)
