# Controlled quantum gate

↑ **Parent:** [Multi-qubit gate](multi-qubit-gate.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_logic_gate#Controlled_gates)

[Controlled quantum gates](controlled-quantum-gate.md) are gates that have two types of input qubits:
- control [qubits](qubit.md)
- operand [qubits](qubit.md) (terminology made up by [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) just now)
These gates can be understood as doing a certain [unitary operation](unitary-matrix.md) only if the control qubits are enabled or disabled.

The first example to look at is the [CNOT gate](cnot-gate.md).

<a id="image-generic-controlled-quantum-gate-symbol"></a>
![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/d/dc/Controlled_gate.svg)

**[Figure 3](#image-generic-controlled-quantum-gate-symbol). Generic controlled quantum gate symbol**. [Source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Controlled_gate.svg). The black dot means "control qubit", and "U" means an arbitrary [Unitary operation](unitary-matrix.md).

When the operand has a conventional symbol, e.g. the [Figure 2. "Quantum NOT gate symbol"](pauli-x-gate.md#image-quantum-not-gate-symbol) for the [quantum NOT gate](pauli-x-gate.md) to form the [CNOT gate](cnot-gate.md), that symbol is used in the operand instead.
