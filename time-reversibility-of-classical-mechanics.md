# Time reversibility of classical mechanics

↑ **Parent:** [Time reversibility](time-reversibility.md)

Considering e.g. [Newton's laws of motion](newton-s-laws-of-motion.md), you take a system that is a function of time $f(t)$, e.g. the position of many point particles, and then you reverse the speeds of all particles, then $f(-t)$ is a solution to that.
