# Looking Glass Universe

↑ **Parent:** [Physics YouTube channel](physics-youtube-channel.md)

[https://www.youtube.com/user/LookingGlassUniverse](https://www.youtube.com/user/LookingGlassUniverse)

Cute simple paper-cut stop motion animations videos by Mithuna Yoganathan, a PhD in theoretical physics at the [University of Cambridge](university-of-cambridge.md): [http://www.damtp.cam.ac.uk/person/my332](http://www.damtp.cam.ac.uk/person/my332).

This has the seeds of direct good intuition, but often stops a bit too short. Worth a look though, there is value in them for beginners.
