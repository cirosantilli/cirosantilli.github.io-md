<h1 id="hamilton-s-equations">Hamilton's equations</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Hamiltonian mechanics](hamiltonian-mechanics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamilton's_equations)

Analogous to what the [Euler-Lagrange equation](euler-lagrange-equation.md) is to [Lagrangian mechanics](lagrangian-mechanics.md), Hamilton's equations give the [equations of motion](equations-of-motion.md) from a given input [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian-mechanics.md):

$$
\frac{\partial H}{\partial q_j} = -\dot{p}_j \\
\frac{\partial H}{\partial p_j} =  \dot{q}_j
$$

So once you have the Hamiltonian, you can write down this system of [partial differential equations](partial-differential-equation.md) which can then be numerically solved.
