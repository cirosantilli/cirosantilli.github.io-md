# Fiat currency

↑ **Parent:** [Currency](currency.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fiat_currency)

A useless piece of paper (or [digital version](digital-currency.md) of it) that you can pay [taxes](tax.md) with :)

As opposed to:
- 2020 [cryptocurrencies](cryptocurrency-split.md), while [governments](government.md) still don't accept them for taxes, as well as other [assets](asset.md) that are also not accepted for taxes (i.e. most assets)
- physical currencies that have intrinsic material value, e.g. [gold](gold.md) coins
