# Topology

↑ **Parent:** [Calculus](calculus-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topology)

Topology is the plumbing of [calculus](calculus-split.md).

The key concept of topology is a [neighbourhood](neighbourhood-mathematics.md).

Just by havin the notion of neighbourhood, concepts such as [limit](limit-mathematics.md) and [continuity](continuous-function.md) can be defined without the need to specify a precise numerical value to the distance between two points with a [metric](metric-mathematics.md).

As an example. consider the [orthogonal group](orthogonal-group.md), which is also naturally a [topological space](topological-space.md). That group does not usually have a notion of distance defined for it by default. However, we can still talk about certain properties of it, e.g. that [the orthogonal group is compact](the-orthogonal-group-is-compact.md), and that [the orthogonal group has two connected components](connected-components-of-the-orthogonal-group.md).
