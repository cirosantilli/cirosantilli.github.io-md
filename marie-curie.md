# Marie Curie

↑ **Parent:** [Physicist](physicist-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marie_Curie)

Like all big names in science, she was at the right place at the right time and with the right interest and passion.

Notably, the man she married, [Pierre Curie](pierre-curie.md), happened to be a the world master at precisely the technique that she needed to carefully measure [radioactivity](radioactive-decay.md): he had the most precise [electrometers](electrometer.md) in the world, which allowed to detect small amounts of [radioactivity](radioactive-decay.md) via the [ionization of air by radiation ](ionization-of-air-by-radiation.md). These used [piezoelectricity](piezoelectricity.md), which [Pierre Curie](pierre-curie.md) co-discovered with his brother.
