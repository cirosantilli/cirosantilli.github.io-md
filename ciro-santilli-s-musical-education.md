<h1 id="ciro-santilli-s-musical-education">Ciro Santilli's musical education</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ciro Santilli's biography](ciro-santilli-s-biography.md)

Ciro's parents put him to play the piano. This is partly influenced by Ciro's paternal grandfather, an energetic Italian descendant who liked music

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cirosantilli/media/master/Six_year_old_Ciro_Santilli_when_his_grandfather_offered_him_an_electronic_keyboard.jpg" alt="" height="600">

The piano was fine, but a bit boring due to how it was taught.

The teachers were nice old ladies who followed a very traditional and methodic approach which was just like [regular school](ourbigbook-com/motivation.md), instead of doing what actually needed to be done: inspire kids into becoming creative musical geniuses that can compose their own stuff.

While in [Santos](santos-sao-paulo-brazil.md), before going to university, Ciro somehow got into acoustic and [electric guitar](electric-guitar.md).

The [electric guitar](electric-guitar.md) environment was much less formalized in general, and he took courses with [an awesome teacher](http://www.maurohector.com.br/) ([archive](https://web.archive.org/web/20190107231749/http://www.maurohector.com.br/)), who actually tried to inspire his students to create their own music and improvisation.

And so a young teenage Ciro once seriously considered becoming a professional [guitar player](the-best-modern-instrumental-western-music.md).

In his early teens, Ciro listened to the usual canned music his friends listened to: [music teenager Ciro Santilli liked to listen to](music-teenager-ciro-santilli-liked-to-listen-to.md), until he started to stumble upon [jazz](jazz.md).

Ciro remembers clearly rainy [weekend](weekend.md) days where he would go to a run down second hand shop near his home in someone's garage (Sebo do Alfaiate, R. Frei Francisco de Sampaio, 183 - Embaré, Santos - SP, 11040-220, Brazil :-)), and buy amazing second hand Jazz CDs. It was just a matter of time until he would start scouring the web for "the best jazz albums of all time" and start listening to all of them, see e.g. [the best modern instrumental Western music](the-best-modern-instrumental-western-music.md). [https://digitaldreamdoor.com/index.html](https://digitaldreamdoor.com/index.html) was a good resource from those times!

Ciro ultimately decided [his bad memory](ciro-santilli-s-bad-old-event-memory.md) and overwhelming [passion for the natural sciences](ourbigbook-com/motivation.md) would better suit a scientific carrier.

He also learnt that the [computer](computer-split.md) is also an extremely satisfying artistic instrument.

Also, with a computer, boring dexterity limitations are no more: you can just record perfect played segments or program things note by note to achieve whatever music or action you want!

Although Ciro quit playing musical instruments, his [passion for the music has remained](music-split.md), and who knows how it has influenced his life.
