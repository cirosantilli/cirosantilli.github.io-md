# One parameter subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Lie algebra of a matrix Lie group](lie-algebra-of-a-matrix-lie-group.md)

The one parameter subgroup of a [Lie group](lie-group.md) for a given element $M$ of its [Lie algebra](lie-algebra.md) is a [subgroup](subgroup.md) of $G$ given by:

$$
{ e^{tM} \in G | t \in \R }
$$

Intuitively, $M$ is a direction, and $t$ is how far we move along a given direction. This intuition is especially vivid in for example in the case of the [Lie algebra of $SO(3)$](lie-algebra-of-so-3.md), the [rotation group](special-orthogonal-group.md).

One parameter subgroups can be seen as the continuous analogue to the [cycle of an element of a group](cycle-of-an-element-of-a-group.md).
