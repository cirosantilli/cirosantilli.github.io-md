# Pion

↑ **Parent:** [Meson](meson.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Particle discovered with cosmic rays](particle-discovered-with-cosmic-rays.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pion)

Conceptually the simplest [mesons](meson.md). All of them have neutral [color charge](color-charge.md):
- charged: down + anti-up or up + anti-down, therefore with net electrical charge $\pm1$ [electron charge](elementary-charge.md)
- neutral: down + anti-down or up + anti-up, therefore with net electrical charge 0
