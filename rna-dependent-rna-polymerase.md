# RNA-dependent RNA polymerase

↑ **Parent:** [RNA polymerase](rna-polymerase.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RNA-dependent_RNA_polymerase)

Makes [RNA](rna.md) from [RNA](rna.md).

Used in [Positive-strand RNA virus](positive-strand-rna-virus.md) to replicate.

I don't think it's present outside viruses. Well regulated organisms just [transcribe](transcription-biology.md) more [DNA](dna-split.md) instead.
