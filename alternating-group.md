# Alternating group

↑ **Parent:** [Permutation group](permutation-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alternating_group)

Note that [odd permutations](odd-permutation.md) don't form a [subgroup](subgroup.md) of the [symmetric group](symmetric-group.md) like the even permutations do, because the composition of two odd permutations is an even permutation.
