# Minkowski space

↑ **Parent:** [Spacetime](spacetime.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Pseudometric space](pseudometric-space.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minkowski_space)

[$\R^4$](real-coordinate-space-of-dimension-four.md) with a weird [dot product](dot-product.md)-like operation called the [Minkowski inner product](minkowski-inner-product.md).

Because the [Minkowski inner product](minkowski-inner-product.md) product is not [positive definite](positive-definite-matrix.md), the [norm induced by an inner product](norm-induced-by-an-inner-product.md) is a [norm](norm-mathematics.md), and the space is not a [metric space](metric-space.md) strictly speaking.

The name given to this type of space is a [pseudometric space](pseudometric-space.md).
