# It must be easy to change your area of study

↑ **Parent:** [Students must have a flexible choice of what to learn](students-must-have-a-flexible-choice-of-what-to-learn.md)

This is a pre-requisite of [Section "Students must have a flexible choice of what to learn"](students-must-have-a-flexible-choice-of-what-to-learn.md).

If the choice of what to learn depend on a years long [dependency graph](dependency-graph.md) of other obligations, which currently are the increasingly interlinked:
- passing the [University entry exam](university-entry-exam.md)
- getting your [undergrad](undergraduate-education.md) degree
- getting your [PhD](doctor-of-philosophy.md)
you end up without much choice at all.

The lock-in periods must be much more fluid and shorter term than those, otherwise it makes the almost inevitable [pivots](pivot-strategy.md) to success impossible.

This is something that [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) has heard from several people at the end of their [undergrad](undergraduate-education.md)/[PhD](doctor-of-philosophy.md) degrees. Some online mentions:

> When I realized the biggest reason to continue my pdh was to be dr helps, that's when decided I should probably leave.
