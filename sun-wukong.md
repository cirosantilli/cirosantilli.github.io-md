# Sun Wukong

↑ **Parent:** [Journey to the West character](journey-to-the-west-character.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sun_Wukong)

Only after Ciro became an adult did he finally understand that [Sun Wukong](sun-wukong.md) was the basis for [Dragon Ball](children-cartoons-ciro-santilli-liked-to-watch.md) as mentioned at: [Figure "19th century illustration of the Journey to the West protagonist Sun Wukong"](ciro-santilli-split.md#image-19th-century-illustration-of-the-journey-to-the-west-protagonist-sun-wukong). And that Sun Wukong was a million times more famous overall. [Mind blown](mind-blown.md).

His given name "Wukong" literally means "the one who mastered the void" (Wu = Comprehend, Kong = void), so clearly a [Dharma name](dharma-name.md). Edit: it is actually given in the novel, he was born without name. They seem to be Taoist however.

The family name sun1 孙 is the same character as "grandson", but most educated Chinese people seem to be able to recognize it as a reference to "monkey" from some archaic context not anymore in current usage.
