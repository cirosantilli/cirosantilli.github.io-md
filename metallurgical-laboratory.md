# Metallurgical Laboratory

↑ **Parent:** [Chicago Pile-1](chicago-pile-1.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metallurgical_Laboratory)

The lab that made [Chicago Pile-1](chicago-pile-1.md), located in the [University of Chicago](university-of-chicago.md). Metallurgical in this context basically as in "working with the metals [uranium](uranium.md) and [plutonium](plutonium.md)".

Given their experience, they also designed the important [X-10 Graphite Reactor](x-10-graphite-reactor.md) and the [B Reactor](b-reactor.md) which were built in other locations.
