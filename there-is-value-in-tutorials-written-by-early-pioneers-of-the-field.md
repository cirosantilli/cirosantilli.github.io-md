# There is value in tutorials written by early pioneers of the field

↑ **Parent:** [Physics education needs more focus on understanding experiments and their history](physics-education-needs-more-focus-on-understanding-experiments-and-their-history.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Cirism](cirism-split.md)

Everyone is beginner when the field is new, and [there is value in tutorials written by beginners](there-is-value-in-tutorials-written-by-beginners.md).

For example, [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) felt it shocking how direct and satisfying [Richard Feynman](richard-feynman-split.md)'s [scientific vulgarization](popular-science.md) of [quantum electrodynamics](quantum-electrodynamics.md) were, e.g. at: [Richard Feynman Quantum Electrodynamics Lecture at University of Auckland (1979)](richard-feynman-quantum-electrodynamics-lecture-at-university-of-auckland-1979.md), and that if he had just assumed minimal knowledge of [mathematics](mathematics-split.md), he was about to give a full satisfactory picture in just a few hours.

Other supporters of this:
- [Ron Maimon](ron-maimon.md): the same also applies to early original papers of the field, not just tutorials
- [Dean Kamen](dean-kamen.md): quick mention at: [https://fi.edu/en/awards/laureates/dean-kamen](https://fi.edu/en/awards/laureates/dean-kamen), but a better longer mention on Dreamer (2020), nearby section from trailer: [https://youtu.be/Cj2VKVJKf1I?t=16](https://youtu.be/Cj2VKVJKf1I?t=16)
