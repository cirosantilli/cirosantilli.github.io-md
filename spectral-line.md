# Spectral line

↑ **Parent:** [Emission spectrum](emission-spectrum.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spectral_line)

A single line in the [emission spectrum](emission-spectrum.md).

So precise, so [discrete](discrete.md), which makes no sense in [classical mechanics](classical-mechanics.md)!

Has been the leading motivation of the development of [quantum mechanics](quantum-mechanics-split.md), all the way from the:
- [Schrödinger equation](schrodinger-equation.md): major lines predicted, including [Zeeman effect](zeeman-effect.md), but not finer line splits like [fine structure](fine-structure.md)
- [Dirac equation](dirac-equation.md): explains [fine structure](fine-structure.md) 2p spin split due to electron spin/orbit interactions, but not [Lamb shift](lamb-shift.md)
- [quantum electrodynamics](quantum-electrodynamics.md): explains [Lamb shift](lamb-shift.md)
- [hyperfine structure](hyperfine-structure.md): due to electron/nucleus spin interactions, offers a window into [nuclear spin](nuclear-magnetic-moment.md)
