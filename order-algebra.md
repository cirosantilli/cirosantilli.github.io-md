# Order (algebra)

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic structure](algebraic-structure.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_(algebra))

The order of a [algebraic structure](algebraic-structure.md) is just its [cardinality](cardinality.md).

Sometimes, especially in the case of structures with an [infinite](infinity.md) number of elements, it is often more convenient to talk in terms of some parameter that characterizes the structure, and that parameter is usually called the [degree](degree-algebra.md).
