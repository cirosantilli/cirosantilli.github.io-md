# Shelter Island Conference

↑ **Parent:** [Physics conference](physics-conference.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shelter_Island_Conference)

Sponsored by [National Academy of Sciences](national-academy-of-sciences.md), located in [Long Island](long-island.md).

Some photos at: [http://www.nasonline.org/about-nas/history/archives/milestones-in-NAS-history/shelter-island-conference-photos.html](http://www.nasonline.org/about-nas/history/archives/milestones-in-NAS-history/shelter-island-conference-photos.html) on the website of [National Academy of Sciences](national-academy-of-sciences.md), therefore canon. 

This is where [Isidor Rabi](isidor-isaac-rabi.md) exposed experiments carried out on the [anomalous magnetic dipole moment](anomalous-magnetic-dipole-moment.md) and [Willis Lamb](willis-lamb.md) presented his work on the [Lamb shift](lamb-shift.md).

It was a very private and intimate conference, that gathered the best physicists of the area, one is reminded of the style of the [Solvay Conference](solvay-conference.md).

[QED and the men who made it: Dyson, Feynman, Schwinger, and Tomonaga by Silvan Schweber (1994)](qed-and-the-men-who-made-it-dyson-feynman-schwinger-and-tomonaga-by-silvan-schweber-1994.md) chapter 4.1 this conference was soon compared to the [First Solvay Conference (1911)](first-solvay-conference-1911.md), which set in motion the development of [non-relativistic quantum mechanics](non-relativistic-quantum-mechanics.md).
