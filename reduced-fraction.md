# Reduced fraction

↑ **Parent:** [Fraction](fraction.md)

A "reduced fraction" is a fraction that has the smallest possible [integer](integer.md) [numerator](numerator.md) and [denominator](denominator.md) for its value.

For example:

$$
\frac{3}{6}
$$

is not a reduced fraction, because there is another fraction equal to it but with smaller [numerator](numerator.md) and [denominator](denominator.md):

$$
\frac{1}{2}
$$
