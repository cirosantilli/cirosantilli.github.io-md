**[Video 1](#video-testing-and-circuit-for-a-condenser-microphone-by-rsd-academy-2018). Testing and Circuit for a Condenser microphone by RSD Academy (2018)** [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y3KSEteq3fU). Not very numerical, but shows a simple working [breadboard](breadboard.md) circuit and an oscilloscope. He whistles with his mouth to get a pretty pure frequency.

That type of microphone requires a [bias voltage](biasing.md). The circuit is in [Ciro's ASCII art circuit diagram notation](ciro-s-ascii-art-circuit-diagram-notation.md):
```
DC_9---R_10k--+--MICROPHONE--+--G
              |              |
              +-------V------+
```
