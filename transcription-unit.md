# Transcription unit

↑ **Parent:** [Operon](operon.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transcription_unit)

A sequence of [mRNA](messenger-rna.md) that can actually be [transcribed](transcription-biology.md).

For an example, see [E. Coli K-12 MG1655 operon thrLABC](e-coli-k-12-mg1655-operon-thrlabc.md).

Multiple different transcription units can be produced by a single [operon](operon.md), see: [operon vs transcription unit](operon-vs-transcription-unit.md).
