# Classification of finite groups

↑ **Parent:** [Finite group](finite-group.md)

As shown in [Video 1. "Simple Groups - Abstract Algebra by Socratica (2018)"](classification-of-finite-simple-groups.md#video-simple-groups-abstract-algebra-by-socratica-2018), this can be split up into two steps:
- [classification of finite simple groups](classification-of-finite-simple-groups.md): done
- [group extension problem](group-extension-problem.md)
This split is sometimes called the "Jordan-Hölder program" in reference to the authors of the [jordan-Holder Theorem](jordan-holder-theorem.md).

Good lists to start playing with:

History: [https://math.stackexchange.com/questions/1587387/historical-notes-on-the-jordan-h%C3%B6lder-program](https://math.stackexchange.com/questions/1587387/historical-notes-on-the-jordan-h%C3%B6lder-program)

It is generally believed that no such classification is possible in general beyond the [simple groups](simple-group.md).
