<h1 id="maxwell-s-equations-are-lorentz-invariant">Maxwell's equations are Lorentz invariant</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Covariant formulation of classical electromagnetism](covariant-formulation-of-classical-electromagnetism.md)

[Subtle is the Lord by Abraham Pais (1982)](subtle-is-the-lord-by-abraham-pais-1982.md) chapter III "Relativity, the special theory" mentions that this fact and its importance (we want the laws of physics to look the same on all [inertial frames](inertial-frame-of-reference.md), AKA [Lorentz covariance](lorentz-covariance.md)) was first fully relized by [poincaré](henri-poincare.md) in 1905.

And at that same time [poincaré](henri-poincare.md) also immediately started to think about the other [fundamental force](fundamental-interaction.md) then known: [gravity](gravity.md), and off the bat realized that [gravitational waves](gravitational-wave.md) must exist. [general relativities](general-relativity.md) is probably just "the simplest way to make gravity [Lorentz covariant](lorentz-covariance.md)".
