# The real projective plane is not simply connected

↑ **Parent:** [Real projective plane](real-projective-plane.md)

To see that the [real projective plane](real-projective-plane.md) is not [simply connected space](simply-connected-space.md), considering the [lines through origin model of the real projective plane](lines-through-origin-model-of-the-real-projective-plane.md), take a [loop](loop-topology.md) that starts at $(1, 0, 0)$ and moves along the $y=0$ [great circle](great-circle.md) ends at $(-1, 0, 0)$.

Note that both of those points are the same, so we have a loop.

Now try to shrink it to a point.

There's just no way!
