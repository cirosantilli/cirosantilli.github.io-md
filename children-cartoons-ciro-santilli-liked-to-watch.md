# Children cartoons Ciro Santilli liked to watch

↑ **Parent:** [Art young Ciro Santilli consumed](art-young-ciro-santilli-consumed.md)

These did not stand the test of time however.

When Ciro was ten years old, he was addicted to 2 cartoons: [Pokemon](pokemon.md) and [Dragon Ball Z](dragon-ball-z.md)!

Pokemon had just launched in Brazil in 1999, 2 years after the Japanese launch: [https://br.historyplay.tv/hoje-na-historia/comeca-exibicao-original-do-anime-pokemon](https://br.historyplay.tv/hoje-na-historia/comeca-exibicao-original-do-anime-pokemon) ([archive](http://web.archive.org/web/20191221091947/https://br.historyplay.tv/hoje-na-historia/comeca-exibicao-original-do-anime-pokemon)) And Dragon Ball, was first aired in 1989 in Japan! My God, those translations took forever back then!

And _everyone_ was playing Pokemon on their [Game Boy Color](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Game_Boy_Color). Ciro was [already cheap](ciro-santilli-s-psychology-and-physiology-split.md) however, and didn't buy the console despite wanting it, and just played it through his friends handhelds. But maybe this is a good thing. Playing alone sucks.
