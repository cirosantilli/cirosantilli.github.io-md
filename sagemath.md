# SageMath

↑ **Parent:** [Python scientific library](python-scientific-library.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SageMath)

A [Python](python-programming-language.md) wrapper over a bunch of [numeric](numerical-software.md) and [computer algebra system](computer-algebra-system.md) packages to try and fully replace [MATLAB](matlab.md) et. al.

For example, their 

Quickstart tutorial at: [https://www.sagemath.org/tour-quickstart.html](https://www.sagemath.org/tour-quickstart.html) From this we see that they are very opinionated, you don't need to import anything, everything has a pre-defined global name, which is convenient, e.g.:

$$
QQ^3
$$

is the [3D](real-coordinate-space-of-dimension-three.md) [vector space](vector-space.md) over the [rationals](rational-number.md). This also suggests that they are quite focused on [computer algebra](computer-algebra-system.md) as opposed to numerical.
