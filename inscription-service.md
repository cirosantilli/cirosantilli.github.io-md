# Inscription service

↑ **Parent:** [Inscription (blockchain)](inscription-blockchain.md)

We define an "Inscription service" as a website that easily allows you to pay to have arbitrary data inscribed on a blockchain.

The most notable early example of such a service on the [Bitcoin blockchain](bitcoin.md) was [cryptograffiti.info](cool-data-embedded-in-the-bitcoin-blockchain/cryptograffiti-info.md).
