# Mathematical formulation of quantum mechanics

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum mechanics](quantum-mechanics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_formulation_of_quantum_mechanics)

These are the key mathematical ideas to understand!!

There are actually a few formulations out there. By far the dominant one as of 2020 has been the [Schrödinger picture](schrodinger-picture.md), which contrasts notably with the [Heisenberg picture](heisenberg-picture.md).

Another well known one is the [de Broglie-Bohm theory](de-broglie-bohm-theory.md), which is deterministic, but [non-local](principle-of-locality.md).
