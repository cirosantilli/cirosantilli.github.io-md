# Gel electrophoresis alelle determination

↑ **Parent:** [Gel electrophoresis](gel-electrophoresis.md)

In the case of [indel](indel.md) mutations (see [limits of gel electrophoresis](limits-of-gel-electrophoresis.md) for minimal size difference issues), it is possible to determine the [allele](allele.md) with gel electrophoresis. You can just read out the alleles right in the gel. It is a thing of beauty.

As of 2020, this method appears to be much cheaper than [DNA sequencing](dna-sequencing.md) approaches.
