Contains formulas such as the [Schrödinger equation solution for the hydrogen atom](schrodinger-equation-solution-for-the-hydrogen-atom.md) (1''):

$$
\left( \pdv{\psi}{x} \right) ^ 2 + \left( \pdv{\psi}{y} \right) ^ 2 + \left( \pdv{\psi}{z} \right) ^ 2 + \frac{2m}{K} \left( E + \frac{2}{r} \right) \psi^2 = 0
$$

where:
- $r = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2 + z^2}$
- > In order for there to be numerical agreement, $K$ must have the value $h/2\pi$
- > $e$, $m$ are the charge and mass of the electron
