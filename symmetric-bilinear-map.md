# Symmetric bilinear map

↑ **Parent:** [Multilinear map](multilinear-map.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symmetric_bilinear_map)

Subcase of [symmetric multilinear map](symmetric-multilinear-map.md):

$$
f(x, y) = f(y, x)
$$

Requires the two inputs $x$ and $y$ to be in the same [vector space](vector-space.md) of course.

The most important example is the [dot product](dot-product.md), which is also a [positive definite symmetric bilinear form](positive-definite-symmetric-bilinear-form.md).
