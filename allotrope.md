# Allotrope

↑ **Parent:** [Homonuclear molecule](homonuclear-molecule.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allotrope)

Single [chemical element](chemical-element.md), single phase (usually solid), but different 3D structures.

The prototypical examples are the [allotropes of carbon](allotrope-of-carbon.md) such as [diamond](diamond.md) vs [graphite](graphite.md).
