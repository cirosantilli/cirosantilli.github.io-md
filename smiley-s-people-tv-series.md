This is perhaps slightly worse than the [Tinker Tailor Soldier Spy](tinker-tailor-soldier-spy-tv-series.md), but still amazing.

Some difficult points:
- how did the general deduce that the old woman's daughter had a link to Karla? It must be linked to the fact that the Russian agent who made the offer was a Karla-man.
- some things are hard to understand without having seen the previous [Tinker Tailor Soldier Spy](tinker-tailor-soldier-spy-tv-series.md), e.g. they say nothing clearly who Toby Esterhase is, he now works on art sales
- but others are inconsistent, e.g. they changed the actor for Peter Guillam...
