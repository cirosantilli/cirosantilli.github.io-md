# Lorentz gauge condition

↑ **Parent:** [Electromagnetic four-potential](electromagnetic-four-potential.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lorentz_gauge_condition)

There are several choices of [electromagnetic four-potential](electromagnetic-four-potential.md) that lead to the same physics.

E.g. thinking about the [electric potential](electric-potential.md) alone, you could set the zero anywhere, and everything would remain be the same.

The Lorentz gauge is just one such choice. It is however a very popular one, because it is also manifestly [Lorentz invariant](lorentz-invariant.md).
