# David Tong

↑ **Parent:** [Physicist](physicist-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Tong_(physicist))

A charismatic, perfect-[English](english-language.md)-accent ([Received Pronunciation](received-pronunciation.md)) [physicist](physicist-split.md) from [University of Cambridge](university-of-cambridge.md), specializing in [quantum field theory](quantum-field-theory-split.md).

He has done several "[vulgarization](popular-science.md)" lectures, some of which could be better called undergrad appetizers rather, a notable example being [Video "Quantum Fields: The Real Building Blocks of the Universe by David Tong (2017)"](quantum-field-theory-split.md#video-quantum-fields-the-real-building-blocks-of-the-universe-by-david-tong-2017) for the prestigious [Royal Institution](royal-institution.md), but remains a hardcore researcher: [https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=felFiY4AAAAJ&view_op=list_works&sortby=pubdate](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=felFiY4AAAAJ&view_op=list_works&sortby=pubdate). Lots of [open access](open-access.md) publications BTW, so [kudos](kudos.md).

The amount of lecture notes on his website looks really impressive: [http://www.damtp.cam.ac.uk/user/tong/teaching.html](http://www.damtp.cam.ac.uk/user/tong/teaching.html), he looks like a good educator.

David has also shown some interest in applications of high energy mathematical ideas to [condensed matter](condensed-matter-physics-split.md), e.g. links between the [renormalization group](renormalization-group.md) and [phase transition](phase-transition.md) phenomena. [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) [wonders](physics-gossip.md) if his family is of [East Asian](east-asian.md), origin and if he can still speak any east asian languages. "Tong" is of course a transcription of [several major Chinese surnames](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tong_(surname)) and from looks he could be mixed blood, but as mentioned at [https://www.ancestry.co.uk/name-origin?surname=tong](https://www.ancestry.co.uk/name-origin?surname=tong) it can also be an English "metonymic occupational name for a maker or user of [tongs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tongs)". After staring at his picture for a while Ciro is going with the maker of tongs theory initially.
