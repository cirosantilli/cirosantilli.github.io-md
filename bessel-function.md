# Bessel function

↑ **Parent:** [2D wave equation on a circular domain](2d-wave-equation-on-a-circular-domain.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Complete basis](complete-basis.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bessel_function)

Shows up when trying to solve [2D wave equation on a circular domain](2d-wave-equation-on-a-circular-domain.md) in [polar coordinates](polar-coordinate-system.md) with [separation of variables](separation-of-variables.md), where we have to decompose the initial condition in termes of a [fourier-Bessel series](fourier-bessel-series.md), exactly like the [Fourier series](fourier-series.md) appears when solving the wave equation in linear coordinates.

For the same fundamental reasons, also appears when calculating the [Schrödinger equation solution for the hydrogen atom](schrodinger-equation-solution-for-the-hydrogen-atom.md).
