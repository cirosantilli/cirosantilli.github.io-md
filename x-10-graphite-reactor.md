# X-10 Graphite Reactor

↑ **Parent:** [Oak Ridge National Laboratory](oak-ridge-national-laboratory.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Breeder reactor](breeder-reactor.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/X-10_Graphite_Reactor)

This was an intermediate step between the [nuclear chain reaction](nuclear-chain-reaction.md) prototype [Chicago Pile-1](chicago-pile-1.md) and the full blown [plutonium](plutonium.md) mass production at [Hanford site](hanford-site.md). Located in the [Oak Ridge National Laboratory](oak-ridge-national-laboratory.md).
