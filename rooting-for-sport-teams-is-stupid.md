# Rooting for sport teams is stupid

↑ **Parent:** [Cirism](cirism-split.md)

Since [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) is [Brazilian](brazil-split.md), this is understandably a common conversation opener.

And rightly so, since [soccer](football.md) in particular is truly ridiculously popular in [Brazil](brazil-split.md), where "what is your local soccer team?" is just as valid a conversation starter as "Which city are you from?".

So here goes Ciro's 2020 cynic answer:

> I currently root actively against Brazil.

The ironic reason is simple: maybe is Brazil loses more on this [useless art](art-split.md), then maybe people will get tired of it, and instead [invest on more useful and beautiful arts](physics-split.md).

Notably, what Ciro really wants people to root for are:
- the number of [Brazilian](brazil-split.md) [Nobel Prizes](nobel-prize-split.md), which is zero, yes, zero, as of 2020, despite a population of 210 million people. But thank [God](god.md) for our one [Field Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artur_Avila), what an epic start, even though [Mathematics is useless](art-split.md).
- the number of high tech companies that have a global impact, which is likely extremely low as of 2020, and must contain only a few mammoths that dominate some local [commodity](commodity.md) market and therefore got enough money from that to expand a bit of technology worldwide. But they were mostly not classic tech [startups](startup-company.md) that did world innovation from the start.
- how low your country's [Gini coefficient is](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gini_coefficient)

Don't get Ciro wrong.

[Playing sports yourself is amazing](ciro-santilli-s-sport-practice.md).

Observing professionals who do it amazingly can be beautiful.

But why the F do you have to root for a team unless your wife or children are playing in it (and even then..., how will that help?)?

What will you get from that?

Even if it is your national team, why does it matter if they win or lose?

[Hooliganism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_hooliganism) just takes that uselessness to a hole new level.

Now some confessions.

A five year old Ciro will never forget when the feeling of Brazil won the [1994 World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1994_FIFA_World_Cup) on the penalties and everyone went mad that evening.

A nine year old Ciro stopped watching the [1998 World Cup Final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1998_FIFA_World_Cup) of Brazil vs France half way during the 3-0 massacre and went to his front garden to kick his [soccer](football.md) ball on the metallic fence gate which represented a goal.

After that, Ciro went through puberty he guesses, and noticed that the [natural sciences](natural-science.md) are just cooler than this soccer watching bullshit.

**[Video 8](#video-why-football-is-the-most-popular-sport-in-the-world-by-desmond-morris). Why football is the most popular sport in the world by Desmond Morris.** [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fjjTF5BkUoA). Interview by [Web of Stories](web-of-stories.md). His thesis is that football is the sport that looks most like a [hunt](hunt.md), the scoring of a goal being the kill of the prey, and thus appeals to people's [Paleolithic](paleolithic.md) hunting habits the most. He feels it is more like hunting than war, and that the opposing team is just there to add some difficulty to scoring. He mentions that soccer has all the fundamental aspects of hunting: running after something, aiming, and hunting in a pack of allies. Ciro agrees with this dude. Ciro also adds that the fact that each soccer match has few goals, e.g. as opposed to basketball, makes it much more like a hunt, where you score few large kills per hunt.
