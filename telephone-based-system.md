# Telephone-based system

↑ **Parent:** [Telecommunication system](telecommunication-system.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telephone-based_system)

This section is about [telecommunication systems](telecommunication-system.md) that are based on top of telephone lines.

Telephone lines were ubiquitous from early on, and many technologies used them to send data, including much after regular phone calls became obsolete with [VoIP](voice-over-ip.md).

These market forces tended to eventually crush non-telephone-based systems such as [telex](telex.md). Maybe in that case it was just that the name sounded like a thing of the 50's. But still. Dead.
