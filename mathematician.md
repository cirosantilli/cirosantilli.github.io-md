# Mathematician

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematics](mathematics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematician)

Poet, scientists and warriors all in one? [Conquerors of the useless](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/180_Degrees_South:_Conquerors_of_the_Useless).

A wise teacher from [University of São Paulo](university-of-sao-paulo.md) once told the class [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) attended an anecdote about his life:

> I used to want to learn Mathematics.
> 
> But it was very hard.
> 
> So in the end, I became an engineer, and found an engineering solution to the problem, and married a Mathematician instead.

It turned out that, about 10 years later, Ciro [ended up following this advice](ciro-santilli-s-wife.md), unwittingly.
