# XP School

↑ **Parent:** [List of innovative high schools](list-of-innovative-high-schools.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/XP_School)

[https://www.thetimes.co.uk/article/no-uniforms-no-detentions-no-maths-lessons-is-xp-in-doncaster-the-school-of-the-future-jnc6n80kq](https://www.thetimes.co.uk/article/no-uniforms-no-detentions-no-maths-lessons-is-xp-in-doncaster-the-school-of-the-future-jnc6n80kq)

Amazing [self-directed learning](self-directed-learning.md) direction:

> The pupils have a parents' evening coming up but instead of their teachers giving an account of their progress, it is a "student-led conference" at which they must present a portfolio of their work, explain what they are most proud of and discuss where they need to put in more effort.

[https://world.hey.com/gwyn/no-excuses-bc4152fb](https://world.hey.com/gwyn/no-excuses-bc4152fb) mentions that the founder was inspired by other schools: High Tech High and Expeditionary Learning.

Lots of focus on [showcase student work](how-to-teach/showcase-student-work.md).

The founder [Gwyn ap Harri](gwyn-ap-harri.md) is quite dirty mouthed, which is also cool.

[Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) tried to contact them in 2021 at: [https://twitter.com/cirosantilli/status/1448924419016036353](https://twitter.com/cirosantilli/status/1448924419016036353) and on website contact form to see if we could do some project together, but no reply.
