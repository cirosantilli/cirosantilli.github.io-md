# Lab vs cyclotron X-ray crystallography

↑ **Parent:** [X-ray crystallography](x-ray-crystallography.md)

[cyclotrons](cyclotron.md) produce the better images, but they are expensive/you have to move to them and order a timeslot.

Lab-based just use some X-ray source from the lab, so it is much move convenient e.g. for a [pharmaceutical company](pharmaceutical-company.md) doing a bunch of images. The Wikipedia image shows such a self-contained lab system: [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Freezed_XRD.jpg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Freezed_XRD.jpg)
