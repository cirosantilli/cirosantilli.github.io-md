# Suikoden

↑ **Parent:** [Playstation games](playstation-games.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Role-playing video game](role-playing-video-game.md), [Water Margin adaptation](water-margin-adaptation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suikoden)

This game (1 or 2, can't remember) made an impression on [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) for some reason.

Only many many years after playing it, after Ciro [started getting more interested](ciro-santilli-s-campaign-for-freedom-of-speech-in-china.md), did he learn that it was actually an adaptation of the [Chinese](china-split.md) mega-classic [Water Margin](water-margin.md).

"Suikoden" is the actual Japenese [transliteration](transliteration.md) for the Chinese name of the original [Water Margin](water-margin.md) novel.

Yet another case of [Chinese thing better known in the West as Japanese](chinese-thing-better-known-in-the-west-as-japanese.md).

The game puts great emphasis on the concept of the [108 Stars of Destiny](108-stars-of-destiny.md), which never left Ciro's mind: making 108 allies, the main [collectible](collectible-video-game-design.md) of the game, allows you to make a more powerful alliance, and unlock better endings.
