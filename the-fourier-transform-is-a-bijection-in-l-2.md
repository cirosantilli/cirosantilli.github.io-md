As mentioned at [Section "Plancherel theorem"](plancherel-theorem.md), some people call this part of [Plancherel theorem](plancherel-theorem.md), while others say it is just a corollary.

This is an important fact in [quantum mechanics](quantum-mechanics-split.md), since it is because of this that it makes sense to talk about [position and momentum space](position-and-momentum-space.md) as two dual representations of the [wave function](wave-function.md) that contain the exact same amount of information.
