# SQUID device

↑ **Parent:** [Applications of Josephson Junctions](applications-of-josephson-junctions.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Electronic component](electronic-component.md), [Magnetometer](magnetometer.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SQUID)

Can be used as a very precise [magnetometer](magnetometer.md).

There are high temperature [yttrium barium copper oxide](yttrium-barium-copper-oxide.md) ones that work on [liquid nitrogen](liquid-nitrogen.md).

**[Video 11](#video-superconducting-quantum-interference-device-by-felipe-contipelli-2019). Superconducting Quantum Interference Device by Felipe Contipelli (2019)** [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d_vrhzX3VcE). Good intuiotionistic video. Some points deserved a bit more detail.

<a id="video-mishmash-of-squid-interviews-and-talks-by-bartek-glowaki"></a>
**[Video 12](#video-mishmash-of-squid-interviews-and-talks-by-bartek-glowaki). Mishmash of SQUID interviews and talks by Bartek Glowaki.** [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0kl3ucjh2Uw). The videos come from: [https://www.ascg.msm.cam.ac.uk/lectures/](https://www.ascg.msm.cam.ac.uk/lectures/). Vintage.

Mentions that the [SQUID device](squid-device.md) is analogous to a [double-slit experiment](double-slit-experiment.md).

One of the segments is by John Clarke.

<a id="video-superconducting-quantum-interference-devices-by-unsw-physics-2020"></a>
**[Video 13](#video-superconducting-quantum-interference-devices-by-unsw-physics-2020). Superconducting Quantum Interference Devices by UNSW Physics (2020)** [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ql2Yo5LgU8M). An experimental lab video for [COVID-19](covid-19.md) lockdown. Thanks, [COVID-19](covid-19.md). Presented by a cute and awkward Adam Stewart.

Uses a [SQUID device](squid-device.md) and control system made by [STAR Cryoelectronics](star-cryoelectronics.md). We can see [Mr. SQUID](mr-squid.md) EB-03 written on the probe and control box, that is their educational product.

As mentioned on the Mr. SQUID specs, it is a [high-temperature superconductor](high-temperature-superconductivity.md), so [liquid nitrogen](liquid-nitrogen.md) is used.

He then measures the [I-V curve](current-voltage-characteristic.md) on an [Agilent Technologies oscilloscope](agilent-technologies-oscilloscope.md).

Unfortunately, the video doesn't explain very well what is happening behind the scenes, e.g. with a [circuit diagram](circuit-diagram.md). That is the curse of university laboratory videos: some of them assume that students will have material from other internal sources.


- [https://youtu.be/ql2Yo5LgU8M?t=211](https://youtu.be/ql2Yo5LgU8M?t=211) shows the classic voltage oscillations, presumably on a magnetic field sweep, and then he puts a [magnet](magnet.md) next to the device from outside the [Dewar](vacuum-flask.md)
- [https://youtu.be/ql2Yo5LgU8M?t=253](https://youtu.be/ql2Yo5LgU8M?t=253) demonstrates the formation of [Shapiro steps](shapiro-steps.md). Inserts a [Rohde & Schwarz](rohde-and-schwarz.md) signal generator into the Dewar to vary the flux. The result is not amazing, but they are visible somewhat.
