The thing about projects is that they are [illiquid](../liquidity.md): it is not easy to immediately compare them.

And that is the whole point.

The outcome of that however is that you have to learn how to explain what you've achieved to others and why it is awesome.

Just like in the real world.

You have to create portfolio, and do some [public relations](../public-relations.md).
