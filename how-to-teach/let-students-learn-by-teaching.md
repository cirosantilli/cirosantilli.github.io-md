# Let students learn by teaching

↑ **Parent:** [How to teach](../how-to-teach-split.md)

<a id="_24"></a>
Tell students to:<a id="_25"></a>

<a id="_26"></a>
- make suggestions to the course material themselves, since you have [used text](text-is-cheaper-than-video.md) and [published your source](publish-your-source.md).Review their suggestions, and accept the best ones.
<a id="_27"></a>
- answer the questions of other students on your [online forum](publish-somewhere-people-can-comment.md). Let them work instead of you.

<a id="_28"></a>
Praise those that do this very highly, and give them better grades if you have that superpower.

<a id="_29"></a>
This is part of a larger concept [Ciro Santilli](../ciro-santilli-split.md) holds dear: don't just consume, but also produce.

<a id="_30"></a>
Whatever you do, even if it is playing [video games](../video-game-split.md): if you manage to produce related content that will interest other people, and possibly allow you to get paid, it will much much fun to do that thing.
