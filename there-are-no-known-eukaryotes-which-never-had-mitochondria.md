# There are no known eukaryotes which never had mitochondria

↑ **Parent:** [Eukaryote without mitochondria](eukaryote-without-mitochondria.md)

As of 2020, there are no known [eukaryotes which have never had mitochondria](eukaryote-without-mitochondria.md).

Known [eukaryotes without mitochondria](eukaryote-without-mitochondria.md), which are very rare, have lost mitochondria they previously had.

Having mitochondria appears to be a requisite for being an eukaryote. This is one of the central thesis of [Power, Sex, Suicide by Nick Lane (2006)](power-sex-suicide-by-nick-lane-2006.md).
