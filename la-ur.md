# LA-UR

↑ **Parent:** [Los Alamos National Laboratory](los-alamos-national-laboratory.md)

Publicly released documents from the [Los Alamos National Laboratory](los-alamos-national-laboratory.md) are marked with this identifier. This is for example the case of each video on ther [YouTube channel](youtube-channel.md): [https://www.youtube.com/@LosAlamosNationalLab](https://www.youtube.com/@LosAlamosNationalLab). E.g. [Video "Historic, unique Manhattan Project footage from Los Alamos by Los Alamos National Lab"](manhattan-project.md#video-historic-unique-manhattan-project-footage-from-los-alamos-by-los-alamos-national-lab) is marked with "LA-UR 11-4449".

[https://www.osti.gov/biblio/1372821](https://www.osti.gov/biblio/1372821) contains "How to Get an LA-UR: Using RASSTI to Release Your Work" which is of interest: [https://permalink.lanl.gov/object/tr?what=info:lanl-repo/lareport/LA-UR-17-26023](https://permalink.lanl.gov/object/tr?what=info:lanl-repo/lareport/LA-UR-17-26023). That document documents the acronym's expansion, plus it leaks some internal-only URLs such as [http://lasearch.lanl.gov/oppie/service](http://lasearch.lanl.gov/oppie/service).
