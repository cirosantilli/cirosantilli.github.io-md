# Possibility of a pivot

↑ **Parent:** [Sponsor Ciro Santilli's work on OurBigBook.com](../sponsor-split.md)

When doing "innovative" things that seem "smart", you often end up noticing that they were actually "old" and "dumb", and that you should instead be doing another "innovative smart" thing.

Therefore, there is always a possibility that at some point [Ciro Santilli](../ciro-santilli-split.md)'s intended project for donation money will change halfway.

Ciro however makes the following pledge: everything that comes out of donation money work will be:
- [openly licensed](../open-license.md)
- amazingly documented
- [STEM](../science-technology-engineering-and-mathematics.md) focused
as always.

This does not apply to contract work obviously, only donations.
