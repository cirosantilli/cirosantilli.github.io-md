# Alpha particles have low penetration depth

↑ **Parent:** [Alpha particle](alpha-particle.md)

They are stopped by:
- by a few centimeters of air
- a sheet of paper
- the skin
Therefore, [alpha emitters](alpha-decay.md) are not too dangerous unless ingested.
