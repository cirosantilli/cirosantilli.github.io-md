Covers some specific hardcore subjects, notably [quantum electrodynamics](quantum-electrodynamics.md), in full [mathematical](mathematics-split.md) detail, e.g.: "Quantum Field Theory Lecture Series" playlist: [https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLSpklniGdSfSsk7BSZjONcfhRGKNa2uou](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLSpklniGdSfSsk7BSZjONcfhRGKNa2uou)

As of 2020 Dietterich was a [condensed matter](condensed-matter-physics-split.md) [PhD](doctor-of-philosophy.md) candidate or post-doc at the University of Minnesota Twin Cities, and he lives in [Minnesota](minnesota.md), sources:
- [https://web.archive.org/web/20220112060801/https://cse.umn.edu/physics/graduate-students](https://web.archive.org/web/20220112060801/https://cse.umn.edu/physics/graduate-students)
- [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fs9O1PZDtag](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fs9O1PZDtag)
- [https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Samuel-Dietterich-2](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Samuel-Dietterich-2)

Unfortunately the channel is too obsessed with mathematical detail (which it does amazingly), and does not give enough examples/application/intuition, which is what would be useful to most people, thus falling too much on the hardcore side of [the missing link between basic and advanced](the-missing-link-between-basic-and-advanced.md).

This channel does have on merit however: compared to other university courses, it is much more direct, which might mean that you get to something interesting before you got bored to death, [Section "You can learn more from older students than from faculty"](you-can-learn-more-from-older-students-than-from-faculty.md) comes to mind.

Videos generally involves short talks + a detailed read-through of a pre-prepared [PDF](pdf.md). Dietterich has refused however giving the PDF or [LaTeX](latex.md) source as of 2020 on comments unfortunately... what a [wasted opportunity](open-educational-resources.md) for society.
