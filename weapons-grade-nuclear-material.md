# Weapons-grade nuclear material

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear weapon](nuclear-weapon-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weapons-grade_nuclear_material)

For [nuclear weapons](nuclear-weapon-split.md) you need a certain level of [isotope](isotope.md) purity of either [plutonium-239](plutonium-239.md) or [uranium-235](uranium-235.md).

And the easiest way by far to achieve this purity is to produce [plutonium-239](plutonium-239.md) in a [breeder reactor](breeder-reactor.md), which allows you to get it out with much cheaper chemical processes rather than costly [isotope separation](isotope-separation.md) methods.

[https://fissilematerials.org/](https://fissilematerials.org/) summarizes stockpiles and production status. [20224 Archive](https://web.archive.org/web/20240815234449/https://fissilematerials.org/).
