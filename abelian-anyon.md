# Abelian anyon

↑ **Parent:** [Abelian an non abelian anyons](abelian-an-non-abelian-anyons.md)

On particle exchange:

$$
\psi = e^{\theta i} \psi
$$

so it is a generalization of [bosons](boson.md) and [fermions](fermion.md) which have $\theta = 0$ and $\theta = \pi$ respectively.

Key physical experiment: [fractional quantum Hall effect](fractional-quantum-hall-effect.md).
