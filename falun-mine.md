# Falun mine

↑ **Parent:** [List of mines](list-of-mines.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Sweden](sweden.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Falun_mine)

A hugely important [copper](copper.md) mine in Sweden.

[Selenium](selenium.md) was initially discovered from ore from this mine.
