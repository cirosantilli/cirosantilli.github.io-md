The good:
- a bit of [Students must be allowed to progress as fast as they want](students-must-be-allowed-to-progress-as-fast-as-they-want.md). Though not fully as once you're in your stuck for 6 weeks in their rythm. Though there are research projects going on too which helps.
- [peer tutoring](peer-tutoring.md)-like: they seem to pick [undergraduate](undergraduate-education.md) students to serve as supervisors

The bad: everything else. [Closed source](closed-source-software.md) learning materials + a university-like selection program. Such a waste of efforts that could benefit way more people with more open resources.
