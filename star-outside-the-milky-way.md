# Star outside the Milky Way

↑ **Parent:** [Brightest natural objects in the sky](brightest-natural-objects-in-the-sky.md)

[It is not possible to see stars outside of the Milky Way by naked eye](it-is-not-possible-to-see-stars-outside-of-the-milky-way-by-naked-eye.md).

With [telescopes](telescope.md) however, it is possible. [https://www.quora.com/Can-we-distinguish-individual-stars-in-other-galaxies-or-would-it-be-equivalent-to-say-we-know-there-are-other-forests-of-stars-galaxies-but-we-cant-tell-the-individual-trees-stars-What-is-the-farthest-individual/answer/Jerzy-Micha%C5%82-Pawlak](https://www.quora.com/Can-we-distinguish-individual-stars-in-other-galaxies-or-would-it-be-equivalent-to-say-we-know-there-are-other-forests-of-stars-galaxies-but-we-cant-tell-the-individual-trees-stars-What-is-the-farthest-individual/answer/Jerzy-Micha%C5%82-Pawlak) contains an amazing answer that mentions two special cases of the furthest ones:
- [gravitational lensing](https://ourbigbook.com/go/topic/gravitational-lensing) observation
- a star that is far but visible because its light is reflected by a nearby [nebulae](https://ourbigbook.com/go/topic/nebulae)

But what we can definitely see are [globular clusters](https://ourbigbook.com/go/topic/globular-clusters) of galaxies. E.g. the article [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Messier_87](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Messier_87) basically gauges the size of galaxies by the number of [globular clusters](https://ourbigbook.com/go/topic/globular-clusters) that they contain.
