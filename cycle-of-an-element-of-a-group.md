# Cycle of an element of a group

↑ **Parent:** [Generating set of a group](generating-set-of-a-group.md)

Take the element and apply it to itself. Then again. And so on.

In the case of a [finite group](finite-group.md), you have to eventually reach the [identity element](identity-element.md) again sooner or later, giving you the [order of an element of a group](order-of-an-element-of-a-group.md).

The continuous analogue for the cycle of a group are the [one parameter subgroups](one-parameter-subgroup.md). In the continuous case, you sometimes reach identity again and to around infinitely many times (which always happens in the finite case), but sometimes you don't.
