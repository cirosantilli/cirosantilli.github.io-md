# The orthogonal group is the group of all matrices that preserve the dot product

↑ **Parent:** [Definition of the orthogonal group](definition-of-the-orthogonal-group.md)

When viewed as matrices, it is the group of all matrices that preserve the [dot product](dot-product.md), i.e.:

$$
O(n) = { O \in M(n) | \forall x, y, x^Ty = (Ox)^T (Oy) }
$$

This implies that it also preserves important geometric notions such as [norm](norm-mathematics.md) (intuitively: distance between two points) and [angles](angle.md).

This is perhaps the best "default definition".
