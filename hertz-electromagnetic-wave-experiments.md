# Hertz electromagnetic wave experiments

↑ **Parent:** [History of radio](history-of-radio.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hertz_electromagnetic_wave_experiments)

[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heinrich_Hertz#Electromagnetic_waves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heinrich_Hertz#Electromagnetic_waves)

[Heinrich Hertz](heinrich-hertz.md)'s main initial experiment used a [spark-gap transmitter](spark-gap-transmitter.md). It is not something that transmits recorded sounds like voice: it only transmits noisy beeps. And as such was used for [wireless telegraphy](wireless-telegraphy.md).

Hertz Experiment on Electromagnetic Waves by Ludic Science (2015)** [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9gDFll6Ge7g). Simplified recreation with cheap modern equipment. Uses as [transmitter](transmitter.md) power source both:
- a [piezo igniter](piezo-ignition.md) from a [barbequeue lighter](barbequeue-lighter.md)
- a more powerful home-made [transformer](transformer.md)

and the signal is observed on the [receiver](radio-receiver.md) with a [neon lamp](neon-lamp.md)
