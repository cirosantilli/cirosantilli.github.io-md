# Think different

↑ **Parent:** [Apple Inc.](apple-inc-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [It's Popular Now It Sucks](it-s-popular-now-it-sucks.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Think_different)

Of course, this only made sense when Apple was more of an underdog to [IBM](ibm.md), and [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) greatly [admires their defiance of the norm](ciro-santilli-s-self-perceived-creative-personality.md).

As of 2020 however, [Apple](apple-inc-split.md) is kind of on the top of the mobile world, and Think different simply makes no sense anymore, notably because it relies on [closed source offline software used by millions](closed-source-offline-software-used-by-millions.md).

[it's Popular Now It Sucks](it-s-popular-now-it-sucks.md) comes to mind.

This is a trap every company that prides itself on it's "alternative culture" sets for itself. If they succeed, they could become the norm.
