# Physical Review Letters

↑ **Parent:** [Journal by the American Physical Society](journal-by-the-american-physical-society.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physical_Review_Letters)

As indicated by its name, the journal contains mostly short letters sent to the editor, often 2 or 3 pages long, which allows for a faster publication cycle and dissemination of new results. This is notably useful for [experimental physics](experimental-physics.md).
