# A Structure for Deoxyribose Nucleic Acid

↑ **Parent:** [Paper by Watson and Crick](paper-by-watson-and-crick.md)

[Watson and Crick](francis-crick-and-james-watson.md)'s "[Nobel Prize](nobel-prize-split.md) paper.

[Nature](nature-journal.md) paywall: [https://www.nature.com/articles/171737a0](https://www.nature.com/articles/171737a0)

Starting line:

> We wish to suggest a structure for the [salt](salt-chemistry.md) of deoxyribose nucleic acid (D.N.A,). This structure has novel features which are of considerable biological interest.

[The Eighth Day of Creation](the-eighth-day-of-creation.md) explains the "[salt](salt-chemistry.md)" part as that was the usual way to prepare DNA for [X-ray crystallography](x-ray-crystallography.md), where something binds with the [phosphate](phosphate.md) groups of [DNA](dna-split.md)

The paper then shoots down other previously devised helical structures, notably some containing 3 strands or phosphate on the inside.

Then they briefly describe their structure, and promise more details on future articles. This was mostly a short one-page priority note.

Then they drop their shell bomb conclusion:

> It has not es~aped our notice that the specific pairing we have postulated immediately suggests a possible copying mechanism for the genetic material.

Both Wilkins and Rosalind Franklin are acknowledged at the end.
