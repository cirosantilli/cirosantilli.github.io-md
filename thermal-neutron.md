# Thermal neutron

↑ **Parent:** [Neutron temperature](neutron-temperature.md)

These are [neutrons](neutron.md) that have reached the thermal equilibrium according to the [Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution](maxwell-boltzmann-distribution.md) after having bounced around many times without undergoing [neutron capture](neutron-capture.md).

Good [fissile material](fissile-material.md) is material that is able to absorb [thermal neutrons](thermal-neutron.md) and continue the reaction, because that's the type of neutron you end up getting the most of.
