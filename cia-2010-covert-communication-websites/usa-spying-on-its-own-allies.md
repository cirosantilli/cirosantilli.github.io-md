<a id="_200"></a>
It is worth noting that democracies represent just a small minority of the websites found. The Middle East, and Spanish language sites (presumably for Venezuela + war on drugs countries?) were the huge majority. But [Americans](../united-states-split.md) have to understand that [democracies have to work together and build mutual trust, and not spy on one another](https://cirosantilli.com/china-dictatorship/dictatorships-tend-to-work-together). Even some of the enlightened people from [Hacker News](../hacker-news.md) seem [to not grasp this point](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=36279768). The [USA](../united-states-split.md) cannot single handedly maintain world order as it once could. Collaboration based on trust is the only way.

<a id="_201"></a>
[Snowden](../edward-snowden.md)'s 2013 revelations particularly shocked USA "allies" with the fact that they were being spied upon, and as of the 2020's, everybody knows this and has "stopped caring", and or moved to [end-to-end encryption](../end-to-end-encryption.md) by default. This is beautifully illustrated in the [2016 film "Snowden"](../snowden-film.md) when [Snowden](../edward-snowden.md) talks about his time in [Japan](../japan-split.md) working for [Dell](../dell.md) as an undercover [NSA](../national-security-agency.md) operative:<a id="_202"></a>


> <a id="_203"></a>
> [NSA](../national-security-agency.md) wanted to impress the [Japanese](../japan-split.md). Show them our reach. They loved the live video from drones. This is [Pakistan](../pakistan.md) right now \[video shows American agents demonstrating drone footage to Japanese officials\]. They were not as excited about that we wanted their help to spy on the Japanese population. They said it was against their laws.
> 
> <a id="_204"></a>
> Of course we tapped the entire country anyway.
> 
> <a id="_205"></a>
> And we did not stop there. Once we owned their communications systems, we started going after the physical infrastructure.
> 
> <a id="_206"></a>
> We'd slip these little sleeper programs into power grids, dams, hospitals. The idea was that if the day came when Japan was no longer an ally, it would be "lights out".
> 
> <a id="_207"></a>
> And it wasn't just the Japanese. We were planting malware in [Mexico](../mexico.md), [Germany](../germany.md), [Brazil](../brazil-split.md), Austria.
> 
> <a id="_208"></a>
> I mean, [China](../china-split.md), I can understand. [Russia](../russia.md). [Iran](../iran.md). Venezuela, okay.
> 
> <a id="_209"></a>
> But Austria?!
> 
> <a id="_210"></a>
> \[shows footage of [cow](../cow.md) on an idyllic [Alpine](../alps.md) mountain grazing field, suggesting that there is nothing in Austria to spy on\]
