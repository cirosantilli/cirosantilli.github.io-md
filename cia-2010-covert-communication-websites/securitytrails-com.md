<h1 id="securitytrails-com">securitytrails.com</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Data sources](data-sources.md)

<a id="_6138"></a>
They appear to piece together data from various sources. This is the most complete historical domain -\> IP database we have so far. They don't have hugely more data than [viewdns.info](viewdns-info.md), but many times do offer something new. It feels like the key difference is that their data goes further back in the critical time period a bit.
