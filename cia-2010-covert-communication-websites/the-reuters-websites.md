# The Reuters websites

↑ **Parent:** [Methodology](methodology.md)

The [Reuters article](reuters-article.md) directly reported only two domains in writing:
- [http://iraniangoals.com](http://iraniangoals.com). Iranian language [football](../football.md) website. As of 2023, the domain had been bought by Reuters and redirects to their website.

  2009 archive: [https://web.archive.org/web/20090411072857/http://iraniangoals.com/](https://web.archive.org/web/20090411072857/http://iraniangoals.com/)

  [JavaScript](../javascript.md) file: [https://web.archive.org/web/20110202091909/http://iraniangoals.com/journal.js](https://web.archive.org/web/20110202091909/http://iraniangoals.com/journal.js) [Reverse engineering](reverse-engineering.md) information: [iraniangoals.com JavaScript reverse engineering](iraniangoals-com-javascript-reverse-engineering.md).
- [http://iraniangoalkicks.com](http://iraniangoalkicks.com). Iranian language [football](../football.md) website. Available in GoDaddy as of 2023.

  2008 archive: [https://web.archive.org/web/20080211111124/http://iraniangoalkicks.com/](https://web.archive.org/web/20080211111124/http://iraniangoalkicks.com/)

  [Java](../java-programming-language.md) [JAR](../jar-file-format.md) file: [https://web.archive.org/web/20110202091917/http://iraniangoalkicks.com/clamping.jar](https://web.archive.org/web/20110202091917/http://iraniangoalkicks.com/clamping.jar)

But by looking at the URLs of the screenshots they provided from other websites we can easily uncover all others that had screenshots, [except for the Johnny Carson one](searching-for-carson.md), which is just generically named. E.g. the image for the Chinese one is [https://www.reuters.com/investigates/special-report/assets/usa-spies-iran/screencap-activegaminginfo.com.jpg?v=192516290922](https://www.reuters.com/investigates/special-report/assets/usa-spies-iran/screencap-activegaminginfo.com.jpg?v=192516290922) which leads us to domain [http://activegaminginfo.com](http://activegaminginfo.com).

[Oleg Shakirov](oleg-shakirov-s-findings.md) later discovered that the Carson one had its domain written right on the screenshot, as part of a watermark present on the original website itself. Therefore the URLs of all the websites were in one way or another essentially given on the article.

The full list of domains from screenshots is:
- `activegaminginfo.com`: [Chinese](../chinese-language.md) gaming information website.

  2011 archive: [https://web.archive.org/web/20110208113503/http://activegaminginfo.com/](https://web.archive.org/web/20110208113503/http://activegaminginfo.com/). Contains mentions of 2010.

  Domain available in GoDaddy as of 2023.

  [Java](../java-programming-language.md) JAR file: [https://web.archive.org/web/20110202130304/http://activegaminginfo.com/gaming.jar](https://web.archive.org/web/20110202130304/http://activegaminginfo.com/gaming.jar)
- `capture-nature.com`: [English](../english-language.md) [photography](../photography.md) website.

  2011 archive: [https://web.archive.org/web/20110201104659/http://capture-nature.com/](https://web.archive.org/web/20110201104659/http://capture-nature.com/).

  As of 2023, it seemed to be an actual legit [photography](../photography.md) website by German (amateur?) photographer Klaus Wägele. Archive: [https://web.archive.org/web/20230323102504/https://www.capture-nature.com/](https://web.archive.org/web/20230323102504/https://www.capture-nature.com/)

  [Ciro Santilli](../ciro-santilli-split.md) actually sent him a message to let him know about the [CIA](../central-intelligence-agency.md) thing in case he didn't, and he replied that he wasn't aware of it.

  [Java](../java-programming-language.md) JAR file: [https://web.archive.org/web/20110201104851/http://capture-nature.com/Scenes.jar](https://web.archive.org/web/20110201104851/http://capture-nature.com/Scenes.jar).
- `www.headlines2day.com`: Iranian language news website.

  2011 archive: [https://web.archive.org/web/20110201164741/https://www.headlines2day.com/](https://web.archive.org/web/20110201164741/https://www.headlines2day.com/). Dated "Copyright 2009".

  As of 2023, this was a completly broken-looking news website but in English entitled:

  > Today's Headlines

  2023 archive: [https://web.archive.org/web/20230121191348/https://www.headlines2day.com/](https://web.archive.org/web/20230121191348/https://www.headlines2day.com/). It makes one wonder if the [CIA](../central-intelligence-agency.md) still operates it!

  [Java](../java-programming-language.md) JAR file: [https://web.archive.org/web/20110201165347/http://www.headlines2day.com/today.jar](https://web.archive.org/web/20110201165347/http://www.headlines2day.com/today.jar).
- `fitness-dawg.com`: English fitness website.

  2021 archive: [https://web.archive.org/web/20110207104044/http://fitness-dawg.com/](https://web.archive.org/web/20110207104044/http://fitness-dawg.com/).

  Domain available as of 2023.

  [Java](../java-programming-language.md) JAR file: [https://web.archive.org/web/20110207104457/http://fitness-dawg.com/Routines.jar](https://web.archive.org/web/20110207104457/http://fitness-dawg.com/Routines.jar).
- `rastadirect.net`: English Rastafari culture website.

  2010 archive: [https://web.archive.org/web/20100429002010/http://rastadirect.net/](https://web.archive.org/web/20100429002010/http://rastadirect.net/) dated as "Copyright 2008".

  Domain available as of 2023.

  [Java](../java-programming-language.md) JAR file: [https://web.archive.org/web/20110106225504/http://rastadirect.net/africa.jar](https://web.archive.org/web/20110106225504/http://rastadirect.net/africa.jar).
- `fightwithoutrules.com`: [Russian](../russia.md) fighting website.

  2011 archive: [https://web.archive.org/web/20110203021315/http://fightwithoutrules.com/](https://web.archive.org/web/20110203021315/http://fightwithoutrules.com/). Contains mentions of 2009 news.
