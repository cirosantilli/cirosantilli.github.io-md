# Globalization reduces the power of governments

↑ **Parent:** [Globalization](globalization.md)

While [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) is a big fan of having "one global country" ([and language](having-more-than-one-natural-language-is-bad-for-the-world.md)), which is somewhat approximated by globalization, he has come to believe that there is one serious downside to globalization as it stands in 2020: it allows companies to pressure [governments](government.md) to reduce [taxes](tax.md), and thus [reduces the power of government](governments-have-lost-all-power-to-companies.md), which in turn increases [social inequality](social-inequality.md). This idea is very well highlighted in [Can't get you out of my head by Adam Curtis (2021)](can-t-get-you-out-of-my-head-by-adam-curtis-2021.md).

The only solution seems to be for governments to get together, and make deals to have fair taxation across each other. Which might never happen.
