# E. Coli K-12 MG1655 gene thrL

↑ **Parent:** [E. Coli K-12 MG1655 gene](e-coli-k-12-mg1655-gene.md)

[UniProt](uniprot.md) entry: [https://www.uniprot.org/uniprot/P0AD86](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprot/P0AD86).

[NCBI](national-center-for-biotechnology-information.md) gene entry: [https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gene/944742](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gene/944742).

The first [gene](gene.md) in the [E. Coli K-12 MG1655](e-coli-k-12-mg1655.md) genome. Remember however that [bacterial chromosome is circular](bacterial-chromosome-is-circular.md), so being the first doesn't mean much, how the choice was made: [Section "E. Coli genome starting point"](e-coli-genome-starting-point.md).

Part of [E. Coli K-12 MG1655 operon thrLABC](e-coli-k-12-mg1655-operon-thrlabc.md).

At only 65 bp, this gene is quite small and boring. For a more interesting gene, have a look at the next gene, [e. Coli K-12 MG1655 gene thrA](e-coli-k-12-mg1655-gene-thra.md).

Does something to do with [threonine](threonine.md).

This is the first in the sequence thrL, thrA, thrB, thrC. This type of naming convention is quite common on related adjacent proteins, all of which must be getting [transcribed](transcription-biology.md) into a single [RNA](rna.md) by the same [promoter](promoter-genetics.md). As mentioned in the analysis of the [KEGG](kegg.md) entry for [e. Coli K-12 MG1655 gene thrA](e-coli-k-12-mg1655-gene-thra.md), those A, B and C are actually directly functionally linked in a direct [metabolic pathway](metabolic-pathway.md).

We can see that thrL, A, B, and C are in the same [transcription unit](transcription-unit.md) by browsing the list of [promoter](promoter-genetics.md) at: [https://biocyc.org/group?id=:ALL-PROMOTERS&orgid=ECOLI](https://biocyc.org/group?id=:ALL-PROMOTERS&orgid=ECOLI). By finding the first one by position we reach; [https://biocyc.org/ECOLI/NEW-IMAGE?object=TU0-42486](https://biocyc.org/ECOLI/NEW-IMAGE?object=TU0-42486).
