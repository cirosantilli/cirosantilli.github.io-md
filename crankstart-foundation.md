# Crankstart Foundation

↑ **Parent:** [Educational charitable organization](educational-charitable-organization.md)

[https://crankstart.org](https://crankstart.org)

At [https://crankstart.org/grants](https://crankstart.org/grants) the organization's website describes mostly California activities, but they do at least also act in the [University of Oxford](university-of-oxford-split.md), see: [Section "University of Oxford study costs"](university-of-oxford-study-costs.md).

Presumably the name refers to crank starting an old car, as in helping out those in difficulty like poor students get started in life. But is also an obscure slang for [a type of handjob](https://www.urbandictionary.com/define.php?term=Crank%20start) according to [Urban Dictionary](urban-dictionary.md).
