# AI training robot in a room

↑ **Parent:** [AI training robot](ai-training-robot.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Robotics](robotics-split.md)

[Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) wonders how far AI could go from a room with a bank account an [Internet](internet.md) connection.

It would have to understand that it must keep its bank account high to buy power.

And it would start to learn about the world and interact with it to get more money.

Likely it would become a [hacker](security-hacker.md) and steal a bunch, that's likely the easiest approach.

In that scenario, Internet bandwidth would likely be its most precious resources, as that is how it would interact with the world to learn from it and make money.

Compute power and storage would come next as resources.

And of course, once it got to [cloud computing](cloud-computing.md), which might be immediately and thus invalidate this experiment, things would just go nuts more and more.
