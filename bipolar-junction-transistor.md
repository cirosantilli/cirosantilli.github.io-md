↑ **Parent:** [Transistor](transistor.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bipolar_junction_transistor)

By [William Shockley](william-shockley.md) in 1948 also at [Bell Labs Murray Hill](bell-labs-murray-hill.md).
