# System of units

↑ **Parent:** [Physics](physics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/System_of_units)

The key thing in a good system of units is to define units in a way that depends only on physical properties of nature.

Ideally (or basically necessarily?) the starting point generally has to be discrete phenomena, e.g.
- number of times some light oscillates per second
- number of steps in a [quantum Hall effect](quantum-hall-effect.md) or [Josephson junction](josephson-junction.md)

What we don't want is to have macroscopic measurement artifacts, (or even worse, the size of body parts! Inset [dick](penis.md) joke) as you can always make a bar slightly more or less wide. And even metals evaporate over time! Though the mad people of the [Avogadro project](avogadro-project.md) still attempted otherwise well into the 2010s!

Standards of measure that don't depend on artifacts are known as [intrinsic standards](intrinsic-standards.md).
