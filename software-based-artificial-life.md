# Software-based artificial life

↑ **Parent:** [Artificial life](artificial-life.md)

Some of the [software-based artificial life](software-based-artificial-life.md) simulators can be used as [AI training game](ai-game.md).

[Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) just always feels that what can be classified as "artificial life" simulators have too much focus on beating more continuous population mechanics, and lack the discrete elements which he feels could be important to [AGI](artificial-general-intelligence.md): [Section "The missing link between continuous and discrete AI"](the-missing-link-between-continuous-and-discrete-ai.md).

There is great interest in this direction of research however quite clearly.
