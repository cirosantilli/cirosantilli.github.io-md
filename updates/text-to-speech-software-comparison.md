I tried to use every single free offline [text-to-speech](../text-to-speech.md) engine that would run on [Ubuntu 24.04](../ubuntu-24-04.md) without too much hassle to see if any of them sounded natural. [pico2wave](https://ourbigbook.com/-/topic/pico2wave) was the overall winner so far, but it is not perfect.

I've been noticing a gap between the "[AI](../artificial-intelligence-split.md)" [SOTA](../state-of-the-art.md) and what is actually packaged well enough to be usable by a general audience.

Also played a bit more with [OpenAI Whisper](https://ourbigbook.com/-/topic/openai-whisper): [https://askubuntu.com/questions/24059/automatically-generate-subtitles-close-caption-from-a-video-using-speech-to-text/1522895#1522895](https://askubuntu.com/questions/24059/automatically-generate-subtitles-close-caption-from-a-video-using-speech-to-text/1522895#1522895) Mind blowing performance and perfect packaging as well, kudos.
