Whenever a user creates an issue or comment on [China Dictatorship](../ciro-santilli-s-campaign-for-freedom-of-speech-in-china.md), the bot now automatically creates a new issue with one of the latest news from Duty Machine: [https://github.com/duty-machine/duty-machine](https://github.com/duty-machine/duty-machine)

<a id="_604"></a>
Duty Machine is a bot repo that automatically scrapes Chinese language news from major news outlets such as the [New York Times](../new-york-times.md) or [Radio Free Asia](https://ourbigbook.com/go/topic/radio-free-asia) which ensures that China Dictatorship news will always be new.

<a id="_605"></a>
It's the war of the anonymous bots against the little pinks, part of asymmetric information warfare: [https://cirosantilli.com/china-dictatorship/asymmetric-information-warfare](https://cirosantilli.com/china-dictatorship/asymmetric-information-warfare)
