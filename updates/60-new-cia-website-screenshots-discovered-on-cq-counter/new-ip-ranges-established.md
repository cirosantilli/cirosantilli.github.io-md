<h1 id="60-new-cia-website-screenshots-discovered-on-cq-counter/new-ip-ranges-established">New IP ranges established</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [60 new CIA website screenshots discovered on CQ Counter](../60-new-cia-website-screenshots-discovered-on-cq-counter.md)

I have come to realize that a few of the websites do seem to use virtual hosting, i.e. multiple domains per IP, and I put a bit more manual effort into looking at known possible IPs that had a relatively small number of domains in them.

This led either to finding a few new domains, or placing existing domains in the same IP as another domains.

From now on I'll consider any IP with more than two hits to be an "IP range".

The new finds around other pre-existing domains are:
- 199.19.110.7 ([theworldnewsfeeds.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20110209183352/http://theworldnewsfeeds.com/)):
  - [classymotors.net](https://web.archive.org/web/20110106000954/http://classymotors.net/)
  - [russiansportsworld.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20100331193337/http://www.russiansportsworld.com/)
  - [urbestbod.com](https://cqcounter.com/whois/www/urbestbod.com.html)
- 207.150.191.68 ([technologypresstoday.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20110201212849/http://technologypresstoday.com/))
  - [kickofffootballnews.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20100512232600/http://kickofffootballnews.com/)
- 216.93.248.194 ([esmundonoticias.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20110201124007/http://esmundonoticias.com/))
  - [tech-geek-news.com](https://cqcounter.com/whois/www/tech-geek-news.com.html)
- 216.104.38.110 ([all-sport-headlines.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20110207144440/http://all-sport-headlines.com/))
  - [wahidfutbol.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20110202203450/http://wahidfutbol.com/)
  - [wildbirds-seasia.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20110207183336/http://wildbirds-seasia.com/)
