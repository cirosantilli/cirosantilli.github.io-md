# OurBigBook Project Update March 2025

↑ **Parent:** [Updates](../updates-split.md)

<a id="ourbigbook-project-update-march-2025/_356"></a>
This is a summary of the status of the [OurBigBook Project](../ourbigbook.md), focusing notably on the past 9 months that I've been able to devote fully to it starting June 2024 notably due to the anonymous [1000 Monero donation](../sponsor/1000-monero-donation.md) and other supporters.

<a id="ourbigbook-project-update-march-2025/_357"></a>
I have 3 months left and after unless some crazy person gives more money, I'll go back to some generic programming job that could be done by many other people so that my wife won't kill me. Hopefully I'll find something in [quantum computing](../quantum-computing-split.md) or [AGI research](../agi-research.md) this time that is not too boring, but we'll see.

<a id="ourbigbook-project-update-march-2025/_358"></a>
I should also note that I have raised my requirement for a second year full time from 100k USD to 200k USD, such that there are about only **144k USD** missing as of writing, a bargain. See also [Section "Sponsor Ciro Santilli's work on OurBigBook.com"](../sponsor-split.md). I have also set a **2M USD retirement goal** in case someone wants to free me to lurk after university students for the rest of my life. Creepy.

<a id="ourbigbook-project-update-march-2025/_359"></a>
The reason for this increase is partly because I'm jealous watching my university peers getting relatively richer and richer than me. More seriously though, as I'm likely going to be looking for a job soon, I don't want to scare employers off too much thinking that it is likely that I'll be leaving in a few months too easily. Plus inflation and the natural lack of security that such endeavour brings.
