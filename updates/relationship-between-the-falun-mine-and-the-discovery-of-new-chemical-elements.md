# Relationship between the Falun Mine and the discovery of new chemical elements

↑ **Parent:** [Updates](../updates-split.md)

<a id="_594"></a>
I like the [Falun Mine](https://ourbigbook.com/go/topic/falun-mine) for two reasons:

<a id="_595"></a>
<a id="_596"></a>
- some cool chemical discoveries have been made with a relation to the mine, notably [tantalum](https://ourbigbook.com/go/topic/tantalum) and [selenium](https://ourbigbook.com/go/topic/selenium), added a section to [Wikipedia](../wikipedia.md): [https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Falun_Mine&oldid=1245374294#Discovery_of_new_elements](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Falun_Mine&oldid=1245374294#Discovery_of_new_elements) I used the book [discovery Of The Elements by Mary Elvira Weeks](../discovery-of-the-elements-by-mary-elvira-weeks.md) as my primary reference.
<a id="_597"></a>
- it is the [Chinese](../china-split.md) version of the [Scunthorpe problem](https://ourbigbook.com/go/topic/scunthorpe-problem) due to a naming conflict with [Falun Gong](../falun-gong.md), a censored new religion that was banned in [China](../china-split.md)
