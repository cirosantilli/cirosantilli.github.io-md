# Zatoichi effect

↑ **Parent:** [Zatoichi](zatoichi.md)

This is a [neologism](neologism.md) by [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md), it refers to the fact that [Zatoichi](zatoichi.md) was not fully blind, but extremely hard of sight, which makes him:
- too capable for the blind people, who did not trust him
- too incapable for non-blind people, who despised him
and metaphorically refers to similar situations where a person or group of people are in the middle of two groups and not part of either of them.

A related thing that comes to mind is [Aum Shinrikyo](aum-shinrikyo.md)'s [Prophet](prophet.md) [Shoko Asahara](shoko-asahara.md), who was semi blind, and would bully the fully blind people of his school for blind people.
