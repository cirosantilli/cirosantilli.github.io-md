# Superfluid helium-3

↑ **Parent:** [Superfluid helium](superfluid-helium.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [1996 Nobel Prize in Physics](1996-nobel-prize-in-physics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Superfluid_helium-3)

Surprisingly, it can also become a [superfluid](superfluidity.md) even though each atom is a [fermion](fermion.md)! This is because of [Cooper pair](cooper-pair.md) formation, just like in [superconductors](superconductivity.md), but the transition happens at lower temperatures than [superfluid helium-4](superfluid-helium-4.md), which is a [boson](boson.md).

[https://aps.org/publications/apsnews/202110/history.cfm](https://aps.org/publications/apsnews/202110/history.cfm): October 1972: Publication of Discovery of Superfluid Helium-3 contains comments on the seminal paper and a graph which we must steal.
