# Argonne National Laboratory

↑ **Parent:** [United States Department of Energy national laboratory](united-states-department-of-energy-national-laboratory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argonne_National_Laboratory)

This is where they moved the [Chicago Pile-1](chicago-pile-1.md) after they decided it might be a bad idea to run [highly experimental nuclear reactions](chicago-pile-1.md) right in the middle of one of the most populous cities of the [United States](united-states-split.md).

After it was reassembled, the [Chicago Pile-1](chicago-pile-1.md) was renamed as Chicago Pile 2 (CP2).

So more precisely, it is a continuation of the [Metallurgical Laboratory](metallurgical-laboratory.md).

It's still not that far though, only about 20 kilometers, and today is also a populated area.

[Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) maintains that they chose the site because the name is so cool. Wikipedia says it is derived from the [Forest of Argonne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forest_of_Argonne), maybe it even shared etymology with the element [argon](argon.md).
