# Feynman was a huge womanizer during a certain period of his life

↑ **Parent:** [Personal life of Richard Feynman](personal-life-of-richard-feynman.md)

[Feynman](richard-feynman-split.md) became a terrible [womanizer](promiscuity.md) after his first wife [Arline Greenbaum](arline-greenbaum.md) died, involving himself with several married women, and leading to at least two [abortions](abortion.md) according to [Genius: Richard Feynman and Modern Physics by James Gleick (1994)](genius-richard-feynman-and-modern-physics-by-james-gleick-1994.md).

[Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) likes to think that he is quite liberal and not a strict follower of [Christian](christianism.md) morals, but this one shocked him slightly even. Feynman could be a [God](god.md), but he could also be a [dick](penis.md) sometimes.

One particular case that stuck to [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md)'s mind, partly because he is [Brazilian](brazil-split.md), is when [Feynman](richard-feynman-split.md) was in [Brazil](brazil-split.md), he had a girlfriend called Clotilde that called him "Ricardinho", which means "Little Richard"; -inho is a [diminutive](diminutive.md) suffix in [Portuguese](portuguese-language.md), and also indicates affection. At some point he even promised to take her back to the [United States](united-states-split.md), but didn't in the end, and instead came back and married his second wife in marriage that soon failed.

Richard's third and final wife, Gweneth Howarth, seemed a good match for him though. When they started [courting](courtship.md), she made it very clear that Feynman should decide if he wanted her or not soon, because she had other options available and being actively tested.
