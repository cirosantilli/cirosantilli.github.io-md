# DNA sequencing milestone

↑ **Parent:** [DNA sequencing](dna-sequencing.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DNA_sequencing_milestone)

Most of these are going to be [Whole-genome sequencing](whole-genome-sequencing.md) of some [model organism](model-organism.md):
- 1975 by [Sanger](frederick-sanger.md) et al.: 5 kbp of the single-stranded [bacteriophage ΦX174](phi-x-174.md) using Sanger's radiolabelling method
- 1981 by [Sanger](frederick-sanger.md) et al.: 17 kbp of human mitochondrial DNA via [Sanger method](sanger-method.md), known as the [Cambridge Reference Sequence](cambridge-reference-sequence.md)
- 2003: [Human Genome Project](human-genome-project.md) (3 [Gbp](base-pair.md))
[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whole_genome_sequencing#History](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whole_genome_sequencing#History) lists them all. Basically th big "firsts" all happened in the 1990s and early 2000s.
