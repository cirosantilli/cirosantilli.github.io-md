ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magic:_The_Gathering)

Magic is the best card game of all time. [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) agrees with this fact, and this [has nothing to do with the nostalgia factor of having played it while being a teenager](games-young-ciro-santilli-played.md).

It is also the one with the most cumbersome name possible, containing even a [bloody](blood.md) colon punctuation in it!

However, besides that, Magic has another major flaw: the cards of old formats (Legacy and Modern), which are the only really interesting ones, are [fucking](sexual-intercourse.md) expensive: [Section "Magic: The Gathering is too expensive"](magic-the-gathering-is-too-expensive.md).

Like in [mathematics](mathematics-split.md), the most beautiful decks are those that do [crazy things](ciro-santilli-s-self-perceived-creative-personality.md):
- infinite combos
- semi-infinite combos that allow you to likely draw your entire deck or deal 20 damage
- all-in decks that either win or lose on turn two
- and lands
All of this comes to a certain extent from the deep [asymmetry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symmetric_game) that permeates the game.

It is also really interesting to watch as new sets as spoiled and try to guess if certain cards will have any impact on the Modern or Legacy metagame.

If Ciro were to ever overcome [his cheapness](ciro-santilli-s-cheapness.md) and play the legacy forma (which will never happen), he would likely play one of the following decks when trying to be able to win at all:
- [turbo depths](https://www.mtggoldfish.com/archetype/turbo-depths)
- [sneak and show](https://www.mtggoldfish.com/archetype/sneak-and-show)
Both of this decks focus on cheating a huge creature into play in one go, and both have combo protection methods (discard for turbo depths, and counterspells for sneak and show). Ciro believes that those decks reflect his personality well, notably [Ciro Santilli's self perceived creative personality](ciro-santilli-s-self-perceived-creative-personality.md). Related decks that don't appeal as much to Ciro:
- [reanimator](https://www.mtggoldfish.com/archetype/reanimator): you have to worry about graveyard hate all the time, worrying is bad
- [storm](https://www.mtggoldfish.com/deck/2888239): you have to play too many spells, it's tiring. Ciro would rather put a fattie into play and swing once.
And above all, Ciro would never play a fair deck. Grinding victories is not for him. He'd rather quickly decide win/lose status and move on.

Competitive commander is also interesting, although matches tend to be much more random so the format is harder to digest, see for example this channel; [Playing With Power MTG channel](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCum8N4KUUC0l_NK_mybvilg).

In Ciro's mind, [Urza's block](https://mtg.gamepedia.com/Urza%27s_block) is the most epic of all, followed by the [masques block](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Masques_block). Those sets had a ridiculous power level and epic art, and they happened just before [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) started playing during [Invasion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invasion_(Magic:_The_Gathering)), which had an extremely low power level in comparison. So Ciro saw some cards from those slightly older formats floating around, but not many, and they felt so mystical and awesome.

Magic's competitive play became a mess in the late 2010's. They had a clear tournament structure, but they decided to start changing things every 6 months, and give tournaments meaningless names like "Mythic championship", and it just became impossible to follow what is what.
- [https://www.mtggoldfish.com/articles/disorganized-play-and-the-magic-pro-league](https://www.mtggoldfish.com/articles/disorganized-play-and-the-magic-pro-league)
- [https://mtg.fandom.com/wiki/List_of_PT_events](https://mtg.fandom.com/wiki/List_of_PT_events)
- [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nXUHaXiZ0HI](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nXUHaXiZ0HI) The Moment Professional Magic Came to an End by Nikachu MTG (2021) has a good perspective. Magic is never going to have enough viewers to actually allow pros to make consistent ammounts money. And Magic's variability is just way too high for anyone to consistently win few large prizes on annual contests. Therefore they just killed such contests.
