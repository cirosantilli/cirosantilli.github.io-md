<h1 id="jachymov">Jáchymov</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [List of mines](list-of-mines.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Czech Republic](czech-republic.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jáchymov)

"Joachimsthal" is the [German](germany.md) for it. Note how it is just near the modern frontier between [Germany](germany.md) and the [Czech Republic](czech-republic.md).

It is from [ore](ore.md) of this mine that [Uranium](uranium.md) and [Radium](radium.md) were discovered.

[https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Uranium&oldid=1243907294#Pre-discovery_use](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Uranium&oldid=1243907294#Pre-discovery_use):

> In the early 19th century, the world's only known sources of [uranium ore](uranium-ore.md) were these mines.

Apparently the region was a [silver](silver.md) mining center:

> Starting in the late Middle Ages, [pitchblende](uraninite.md) was extracted from the Habsburg silver mines in Joachimsthal, Bohemia (now Jáchymov in the Czech Republic), and was used as a coloring agent in the local glassmaking industry
