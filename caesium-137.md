# Caesium-137

↑ **Parent:** [Caesium](caesium.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caesium-137)

Highly [radioactive](radioactive-decay.md) [isotope](isotope.md) of [caesium](caesium.md)  with [half-life](half-life.md) of 30.17 [y](year.md). Produced from the [nuclear fission](nuclear-fission.md) of [uranium](uranium.md), TODO exact reaction, not found in nature.

The [fucked](sexual-intercourse.md) thing about this byproduct is that it is in the same chemical family as [sodium](sodium.md), and therefore forms a salt that looks like regular [table salt](sodium-chloride.md), and dissolves in water and therefore easily enters your body and sticks to things.

Another problem is that its [half-life](half-life.md) is long enough that it doesn't lose radioactivity very quickly compared to the life of a human person, although it is short enough to make it highly toxic, making it a terrible pollutant when released.

This is why for example in the [goiânia accident](goiania-accident.md) a girl ended up ingesting Caesium-137 after eating an egg after touching the Caesium with her hands.
