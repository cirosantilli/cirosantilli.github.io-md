# Dialog between Fisherman and Woodcutter

↑ **Parent:** [Guqin piece](guqin-piece.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Ten ancient famous songs of China](ten-ancient-famous-songs-of-china.md)

Lit: fish timber question answer.

On [Baidu Baike](baidu-baike.md): [https://baike.baidu.com/item/渔樵问答/336905](https://baike.baidu.com/item/渔樵问答/336905)

The dialog is also known as [allegory](allegory.md) for an incredibly deep philosophical discussion between an idealized wise woodcutter and a fisherman, e.g. mentioned at: [https://www2.kenyon.edu/Depts/Religion/Fac/Adler/Writings/Fisherman%20and%20Woodcutter.pdf](https://www2.kenyon.edu/Depts/Religion/Fac/Adler/Writings/Fisherman%20and%20Woodcutter.pdf)

This song is just too slow for [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) to make much out of it.
