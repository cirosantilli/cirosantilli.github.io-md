# Available battery voltages

↑ **Parent:** [Electric battery](electric-battery.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Available_battery_voltages)

Only certain battery voltages exist, because this voltage depends intrinsically on the battery's chemical composition.

[https://learn.sparkfun.com/tutorials/battery-technologies/all](https://learn.sparkfun.com/tutorials/battery-technologies/all) ([CC BY-SA](cc-by-sa.md)) has a very good summary list, reordered from lowest to highest voltage:

| Battery Shape | Chemistry | Nominal Voltage | Rechargeable? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AA, AAA, C, D (Rechargeable) | NiMH or NiCd | 1.2V | Yes |
| AA, AAA, C, and D | Alkaline or Zinc-carbon | 1.5V | No |
| Coin Cell | Lithium | 3V | No |
| Silver Flat Pack | Lithium Polymer (LiPo) | 3.7V | Yes |
| [9V](nine-volt-battery.md) | Alkaline or Zinc-carbon | 9V | No |
| Car Battery | Six-cell lead acid | 12.6V | Yes |
