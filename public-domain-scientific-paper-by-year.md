# Public domain scientific paper by year

↑ **Parent:** [Public domain by year](public-domain-by-year.md)

Here we list public domain [academic papers](academic-paper.md). They must be public domain in the country of origin, not just the [US](united-states-split.md), which had generally less stringent timings with the 95 year after publication rule rather than life + 70, which often ends up being publication + 110/120. Once these are reached, they may be upload to [Wikimedia Commons](wikimedia-commons.md)!
