# Miner message

↑ **Parent:** [Inscription (blockchain)](inscription-blockchain.md)

A "miner message" is an [inscription](inscription-blockchain.md) message left by a [miner](cryptocurrency-mining.md) on a [blockchain](blockchain.md).

This is opposed to messages that may be left by non miners during transactions.

Miner messages are therefore of course much harder to control on established blockchains, as they basically require consensus in a [mining pool](mining-pool.md) to set. Most of them are just ads for the [mining pool](mining-pool.md) itself.
