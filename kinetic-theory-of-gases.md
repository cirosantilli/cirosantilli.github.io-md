# Kinetic theory of gases

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical mechanics](statistical-mechanics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kinetic_theory_of_gases)

Theory that gases are made up of a bunch of small billiard balls that don't interact with each other.

This theory attempts to deduce/explain properties of matter such as the [equation of state](equation-of-state.md) in terms of [classical mechanics](classical-mechanics.md).
