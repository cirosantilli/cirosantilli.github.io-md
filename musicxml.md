# MusicXML

↑ **Parent:** [Computer music file format](computer-music-file-format.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MusicXML)

Higher level than [Csound](csound.md): describes the notes only, not the exact waveforms it seems.

Therefore also a bit harder to convert to actual sound: [https://stackoverflow.com/questions/33775336/convert-musicxml-to-wav](https://stackoverflow.com/questions/33775336/convert-musicxml-to-wav) but possibly easier to convert to [LilyPond](lilypond.md).

Now they need to create a "MusicCSS" that gives the waveforms! :-)

The usual "let's make a standard without a reference implementation" W3C approach.
