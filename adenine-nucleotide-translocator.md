# Adenine nucleotide translocator

↑ **Parent:** [Mitochondrial carrier](mitochondrial-carrier.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Transmembrane protein](transmembrane-protein.md)

A single [transmembrane protein](transmembrane-protein.md) that moves [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) out and [ADP](adenosine-diphosphate.md) in of the [mitochondrion](mitochondrion.md). So crucial.

Present in chormosome 4.
