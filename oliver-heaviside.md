# Oliver Heaviside

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical engineer](electrical-engineer.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Autodidact](autodidacticism.md), [Idealist](idealist.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oliver_Heaviside)

He participated in the development of the [electrical telegraph](electrical-telegraph.md), and he did some good modeling work that improved the foundations of the field, notably creating the [telegrapher's equations](telegrapher-s-equations.md).

He was one of those [idealists](idealist.md) who just want to do some cool work even if they have to starve for it, people had to get a state pension for him for his contributions. Nice guy. [https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Oliver_Heaviside&oldid=1230097796#Later_years_and_views](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Oliver_Heaviside&oldid=1230097796#Later_years_and_views):

> In 1896, FitzGerald and John Perry obtained a civil list pension of £120 per year for Heaviside, who was now living in Devon, and persuaded him to accept it, after he had rejected other charitable offers from the [Royal Society](royal-society.md).

He also never married: [https://www.nndb.com/people/627/000204015/](https://www.nndb.com/people/627/000204015/)
