# Infrastructure as a service

↑ **Parent:** [Type of cloud computing](type-of-cloud-computing.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infrastructure_as_a_service)

You [SSH](secure-shell.md) into a an OS like [Ubuntu](ubuntu.md) and do whatever you want from there. E.g. [Amazon EC2](amazon-elastic-compute-cloud.md).

The OS is usually virualized, and you get only a certain share of the CPU by default.
