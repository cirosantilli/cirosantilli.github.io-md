# High level quantum synthesis

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum compilation](quantum-compilation.md)

This is a term "invented" by [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) to refer to quantum compilers that are able to convert non-specifically-quantum ([functional](functional-programming.md), since there is no state in quantum software) programs into [quantum circuit](quantum-circuit.md).

The term is made by adding "quantum" to the more "classical" concept of "[high-level synthesis](high-level-synthesis.md)", which refers to software that converts an [imperative program](imperative-programming.md) into [register transfer level](register-transfer-level.md) hardware, typicially for [FPGA](field-programmable-gate-array.md) applications.
