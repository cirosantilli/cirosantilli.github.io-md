# Insulin

↑ **Parent:** [Peptide hormone](peptide-hormone.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Model protein](model-protein.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Insulin)

Studying insulin reveals some really cool protein motifs of life:
- both the [A chain](a-chain-of-insulin.md) and [B chain](b-chain-of-insulin.md) are [transcribed](transcription-biology.md) from a single gene. The resulting peptide also contain:
  - the [C-peptide](c-peptide.md) in the middle which gets removed
  - a signaling sequence telling the cell to export the substance out as it is a hormone
