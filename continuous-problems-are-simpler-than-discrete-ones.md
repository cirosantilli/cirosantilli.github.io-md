# Continuous problems are simpler than discrete ones

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous function](continuous-function.md)

This is a general philosophy that [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md), and likely others, observes over and over.

Basically, [continuity](continuous-function.md), or higher order conditions like [differentiability](differentiable-function.md) seem to impose greater constraints on problems, which make them more solvable.

Some good examples of that:
- complex [discrete](discrete.md) problems:
  - [classification of finite groups](classification-of-finite-groups.md)
- simple [continuous](continuous-function.md) problems:
  - characterization of [Lie groups](lie-group.md)
