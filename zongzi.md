# Zongzi

↑ **Parent:** [Chinese food](chinese-food.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zongzi)

Eaten during [Dragon Boat Festival](dragon-boat-festival.md), but can be bought year round.

Buy frozen. Pick the ones with meat and eggs!!! Boil.

Cost about 4 dollars for 2.

You can't eat them every day because too heavy, but they are so amazingly tasty!!!
