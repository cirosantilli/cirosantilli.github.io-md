# Implicit metric signature in Einstein notation

↑ **Parent:** [Einstein notation](einstein-notation.md)

Then a specific [metric](metric-mathematics.md) is involved, sometimes we want to automatically add it to products.

E.g., in a context considering the common [Minkowski inner product matrix](minkowski-inner-product-matrix.md) where the $\eta$ 4x4 matrix and $\mu$ is a vector in [$\R^4$](real-coordinate-space-of-dimension-four.md)

$$
x^{\mu} x_{\mu} = x^{\mu} \eta_{\mu \nu} x^{\nu} = -x_0^2 + x_1^2 + x_2^2 + x_3^2;
$$

which leads to the change of sign of some terms.
