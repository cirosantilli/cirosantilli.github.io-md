# Greek alphabet

↑ **Parent:** [Alphabet](alphabet.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_alphabet)

Unfortunately, [physicists](physicist-split.md) and [mathematicians](mathematician.md) keep using Greek letters in their formulas, so we just have to learn them.

A helpful way to remember is to learn a bit of their history/pronunciation: [Section "Historical correspondence between Latin and Greek"](historical-correspondence-between-latin-and-greek.md).

To learn the greek letters if you have a base latin alphabet, you _must_ learn the sound of each letter, and which [Latin letters](latin-letter.md) they correspond to.

Symbols that look like Greek letters but are not Greek letters:
- [nabla symbol](nabla-symbol.md)

Is [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) crazy (he is, but for this point specifically), or do many/most [Greek letters](greek-alphabet.md) represent the mouth position used in the pronunciation of the letter?
