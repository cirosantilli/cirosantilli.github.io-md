# Lattice Microbes

↑ **Parent:** [M. genitalium whole cell simulation](m-genitalium-whole-cell-simulation.md)

[GPU](graphics-processing-unit.md) accelerated, simulates the Craig's minimized [M. genitalium](mycoplasma-genitalium.md), [JCVI-syn3A](jcvi-syn3a.md) at a particle basis of some kind.

Lab head is the cutest-looking lady ever: [https://chemistry.illinois.edu/zan](https://chemistry.illinois.edu/zan), Zaida (Zan) Luthey-Schulten.

- 2022 paper: [https://www.cell.com/cell/fulltext/S0092-8674(21)01488-4](https://www.cell.com/cell/fulltext/S0092-8674(21)01488-4) Fundamental behaviors emerge from simulations of a living minimal cell by Thornburg et al. (2022) published on [Cell](cell-journal.md)
- [http://faculty.scs.illinois.edu/schulten/lm/](http://faculty.scs.illinois.edu/schulten/lm/) actual source code. No [Version control](version-control.md) and non-[code drop](code-drop.md) release, openess and best practices haven't reached such far obscure reaches of academia yet. One day.
- [https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/2022/01/20/living-cell-simulation/](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/2022/01/20/living-cell-simulation/) [Nvidia](nvidia.md) announcement. That's how they do business, it is quite interesting how they highlight this kind of research.
