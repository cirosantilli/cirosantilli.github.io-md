# University has become a luxury item

↑ **Parent:** [University is broken](university-is-broken.md)

[https://medium.com/left-right/the-education-system-is-about-to-implode-5c95f774b523](https://medium.com/left-right/the-education-system-is-about-to-implode-5c95f774b523) The Education System is About to Implode by Nicholas Budny (2020)

> [Stanford](stanford-university.md), [MIT](massachusetts-institute-of-technology.md), [Harvard](harvard-university.md) and Oxford are luxury goods in the same sense that a Mercedes-Benz might be, or the latest [iPhone](iphone.md). They sell an aesthetic experience that provides the consumer with social status, similar to acquiring expensive clothes or a fancy house.
