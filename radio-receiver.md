# Radio receiver

↑ **Parent:** [Radio transmitter and receiver](radio-transmitter-and-receiver.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radio_receiver)

As well put by Wikipedia, a radio receiver has to perform three functions on the signal from the antenna:
- filtering, so you can tune the station you care about. This filters based on the frequency of the [carrier wave](carrier-wave.md) you want. I.e. you use a [bandpass filter](bandpass-filter.md).
- [amplification](amplifier.md): otherwise you won't be able to hear anything if the emitter is too far away
- [demodulation](modulation.md): this means decoding the signal based on whatever way it was encoded, notably e.g. [AM](amplitude-modulation.md)/[FM](frequency-modulation.md)
