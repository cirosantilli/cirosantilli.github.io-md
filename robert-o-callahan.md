<h1 id="robert-o-callahan">Robert O'Callahan</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [List of systems programmers](list-of-systems-programmers.md)

Creator of [Mozilla rr](mozilla-rr.md), of which [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) is a huge fan of!

He quit Mozilla in 2016 to try and commercialize an `rr` extension called [Pernosco](pernosco.md).

But as of 2022, he advertised himself as part of "[Google](google-split.md) Research", so maybe that went under, sample source: [https://archive.ph/o9622](https://archive.ph/o9622).
