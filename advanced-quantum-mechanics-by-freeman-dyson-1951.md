# Advanced quantum mechanics by Freeman Dyson (1951)

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum electrodynamics bibliography](quantum-electrodynamics-bibliography.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by Freeman Dyson](work-by-freeman-dyson.md)

[https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0608140v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0608140v1)

Lecture notes that were apparently very popular at [Cornell University](cornell-university.md). In this period he was actively synthesizing the revolutionary bullshit [Richard Feynman](richard-feynman-split.md) and [Julian Schwinger](julian-schwinger.md) were writing and making it understandable to the more general [physicist](physicist-split.md) audience, so it might be a good reading.



> We shall not develop straightaway a correct theory including many particles. Instead we follow the historical development. We try to make a relativistic quantum theory of one particle, find out how far we can go and where we get into trouble.
