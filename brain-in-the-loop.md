# Brain-in-the-loop

↑ **Parent:** [Brain simulation](brain-simulation.md)

[Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) invented this term, derived from "hardware in the loop" to refer to simulations in which both the brain and the body and physical world of [organism models](organism-model.md) are modelled.

E.g. just imagine running:
- [NeuroMechFly](neuromechfly.md)
- a [Drosophila connectome](drosophila-connectome.md) simulation
