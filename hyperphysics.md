# HyperPhysics

↑ **Parent:** [The best personal webpages of all time](the-best-personal-webpages-of-all-time.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HyperPhysics)

[http://hyperphysics.phy-astr.gsu.edu/hbase/hframe.html](http://hyperphysics.phy-astr.gsu.edu/hbase/hframe.html)

Created by Dr. Rod Nave from Georgia State University, where he worked from 1968 after his post-doc in North Wales on molecular [spectroscopy](spectroscopy.md).

While there is value to that website, it always feels like it [falls a bit too short as too "encyclopedic" and too little "tutorial-like"](ourbigbook-com/wikipedia.md). Most notably, it has very little on the [history of physics](history-of-physics.md)/experiments.

[Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) likes this Rod, he really practices some good [braindumping](braindumping.md), just look at how he documented his life in the pre-[social media](social-media.md) [Internet](internet.md) dark ages: [http://hyperphysics.phy-astr.gsu.edu/Nave-html/nave.html](http://hyperphysics.phy-astr.gsu.edu/Nave-html/nave.html)

The website evolved from a [HyperCard](hypercard.md) stack, as suggested by the website name, mentioned at: [http://hyperphysics.phy-astr.gsu.edu/hbase/index.html](http://hyperphysics.phy-astr.gsu.edu/hbase/index.html).

Shame he was too old for [CC BY-SA](cc-by-sa.md), see "Please respect the Copyright" at [http://hyperphysics.phy-astr.gsu.edu/hbase/index.html](http://hyperphysics.phy-astr.gsu.edu/hbase/index.html).

[https://exhibits.library.gsu.edu/kell/exhibits/show/nave-kell-hall/capturing-a-career](https://exhibits.library.gsu.edu/kell/exhibits/show/nave-kell-hall/capturing-a-career) has some good photo selection focused on showing the department, and has an interview.

Kell hall is a building of GSU that was demolished in 2019: [https://atlanta.curbed.com/2020/1/31/21115980/gsu-georgia-state-atlanta-kell-hall-demolition-park-library-north](https://atlanta.curbed.com/2020/1/31/21115980/gsu-georgia-state-atlanta-kell-hall-demolition-park-library-north)
