# DNA methylation

↑ **Parent:** [Epigenetics](epigenetics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DNA_methylation)

The first found and most important known [epigenetic](epigenetics.md) marker.

Happens only on [adenine](adenine.md) and [cytosine](cytosine.md). [Adenine methylation](adenine-methylation.md) is much less common in [mammal](mammal.md) than [cytosine](cytosine.md) methylation, when people say "methylation" they often mean just cytosine methylation.

It often happens on [promoters](promoter-genetics.md), where it inhibits [transcription](transcription-biology.md).
