[domain-specific language](domain-specific-language.md) unfortunately, but at least it's on [GitHub](github.md), looks promising.

How to play scores and save them to files is discussed at: [https://doc.sccode.org/Guides/Non-Realtime-Synthesis.html](https://doc.sccode.org/Guides/Non-Realtime-Synthesis.html)

They have a nice looking [IDE](integrated-development-environment.md), but running anything from the [command-line interface](command-line-interface.md) is super hard, much unlike [Csound](csound.md). How to get a decent [hello world](hello-world-program.md): [https://stackoverflow.com/questions/65360414/how-to-play-a-supercollider-file-non-interactively-from-the-terminal-command-lin](https://stackoverflow.com/questions/65360414/how-to-play-a-supercollider-file-non-interactively-from-the-terminal-command-lin)

Sample composition with custom synths + notes: [http://sccode.org/1-5cl](http://sccode.org/1-5cl)

[https://leanpub.com/ScoringSound](https://leanpub.com/ScoringSound) looks like a decent tutorial, it is basically the [Csound FLOSS manual](csound-floss-manual.md) for [SuperCollider](supercollider.md).
