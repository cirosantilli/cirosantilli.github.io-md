# Suwu herding sheep

↑ **Parent:** [Erhu piece](erhu-piece.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [The best Chinese traditional instrumental music](the-best-chinese-traditional-instrumental-music.md)

[https://www.ibiblio.org/chinese-music/html/traditional.html](https://www.ibiblio.org/chinese-music/html/traditional.html) contains an amazing orchestral version for [di flute](dizi-instrument.md) TODO identify! When attempting to upload to [YouTube](youtube.md), it identifies as "Su Wu Tending the Sheep" and give a name "Chen Tao", but no further information. Chen Tao is presumably this dude: [https://www.barduschinamusic.org/chen-tao-dizi](https://www.barduschinamusic.org/chen-tao-dizi) | [https://www.melodyofdragon.org/chentao.html](https://www.melodyofdragon.org/chentao.html) 陈涛

[https://baike.baidu.com/item/苏武牧羊/5532#11_2](https://baike.baidu.com/item/苏武牧羊/5532#11_2) mentions that it comes from an erhu concerto composed by Peng Xiuwen

The sadness of the [erhu](erhu.md) perfectly fits the role and mood of the story! Brilliant!
