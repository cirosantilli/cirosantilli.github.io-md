# Mediocre Amateur

↑ **Parent:** [Endurance sport show](endurance-sport-show.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [The best YouTube channels](the-best-youtube-channels.md)

[https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC-04mJDJUYHEyE8JPIEa0-w](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC-04mJDJUYHEyE8JPIEa0-w)

About 50k subscribers on 2021, which feels way too little for the video quality and quantity.

[Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) believes that this channel will go very far, certainly achieving 1M subscribers in they keep it for one or two more years. Update: it didn't, as of 2024, shame.

They are [Utah](utah.md)-based, and they do many many amazing weekend trips. They mostly drive from home to some trailhead, and then climb up and down it the entire day.

No technical [rock climbing](rock-climbing.md), only [bouldering](bouldering.md), but they still manage to reach many amazing places, and there is a level of danger in many of their ascents.

They also often [ski](skiing.md) down the mountains when there is [snow](snow.md).

The cool thing about this channel is that as the name suggests, they are not professionals, and what they do can be done by anyone without working full time on it, as long as you have adequate preparation.
