# Gun-type fission weapon

↑ **Parent:** [Fission weapon by geometry](fission-weapon-by-geometry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gun-type_fission_weapon)

[Gun-type fission weapons](gun-type-fission-weapon.md) are the simplest approach and they work with [Uranium-235](uranium-235.md) bombs as you can ignite it with just one explosion.

But [Gun-type fission weapons don't work with plutonium](gun-type-fission-weapons-don-t-work-with-plutonium.md), and [weapon grade Plutonium is cheaper than weapon grade Uranium](weapon-grade-plutonium-is-cheaper-than-weapon-grade-uranium.md), so it wasn't much used.
