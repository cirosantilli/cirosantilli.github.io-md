# Artificial general intelligence

↑ **Parent:** [AI by capability](ai-by-capability.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_general_intelligence)

Given enough computational power per dollar, AGI is inevitable, but it is not sure certain ever happen given the end of [end of Moore's Law](moore-s-law.md).

Alternatively, it could also be achieved genetically modified biological brains + [brain in a vat](brain-in-a-vat.md).

Imagine a brain the size of a building, perfectly engineered to solve certain engineering problems, and giving hints to human operators + taking feedback from cameras and audio attached to the operators.

This likely implies [transhumanism](transhumanism.md), and [mind uploading](mind-uploading.md).

[Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) joined the silicon industry at one point to help increase our computational capacity and reach AGI.

Ciro believes that the easiest route to full AI, if any, could involve [Ciro's 2D reinforcement learning games](ciro-s-2d-reinforcement-learning-games.md).
