↑ **Parent:** [Video sharing website](video-sharing-website.md)

ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YouTube)

[Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) publishes videos of this not-so-common visual programming experiments on his YouTube channel occasionally: [https://www.youtube.com/c/CiroSantilli](https://www.youtube.com/c/CiroSantilli). Ciro should however not be lazy and also upload each video produced to [Wikimedia Commons](wikimedia-commons.md), since YouTube does not offer a download option even for videos marked with a [Creative Commons license](creative-commons-license.md): [https://www.quora.com/Can-I-download-Creative-Commons-licensed-YouTube-videos-to-edit-them-and-use-them/answer/Tarmo-Toikkanen](https://www.quora.com/Can-I-download-Creative-Commons-licensed-YouTube-videos-to-edit-them-and-use-them/answer/Tarmo-Toikkanen)!

This is also where Ciro's downtime converged to in his early 30's, since he long lost patience for stupid [video games](video-game-split.md) and [television series](television-series-split.md).

Ciro developed one interesting technique: while scrolling through YouTube's useless recommendations, when he understands what a channel is about, he either immediately:
- subscribes if it is amazing and then "Don't recommend channel"
- otherwise just "Don't recommend channel" immediately
This helps to keep this feed clean of boring stuff he already knows about. There is unfortunately an _infinite_ amount of useless videos out there however on the topics of:
- sports
- music, mostly idiotic top of the charts
- news and political commentary
- food
- programming tutorials. [Meh](meh.md), got [Stack Overflow](stack-overflow-split.md).
- stuff that is [not in English](having-more-than-one-natural-language-is-bad-for-the-world.md), and notably languages that Ciro does not even speak!
- motorcycles
- [ASMR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ASMR)
- cute animals
- gaming and movie commentary. Ciro is interested only in a very specific number of [video games](video-game-split.md)
- nature life, e.g. hiking, cycling, or living in isolation, this Ciro enjoys
- science for kids ([popular science](popular-science.md))
and no matter how much you say you don't want to hear about them, YouTube juts keeps on sending more.

Things Ciro hates about YouTube:
- you can't follow or ignore a subject, only indirectly tell the algorithm about that. Once you click a popular cat video, you will be forced to watch cat videos for all eternity.

Likely [FFmpeg is the backend of YouTube](ffmpeg-is-the-backend-of-youtube.md).

Bought by [Google](google-split.md) in 2006.
