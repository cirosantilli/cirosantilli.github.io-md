# Fabless manufacturing

↑ **Parent:** [Register transfer level](register-transfer-level.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fabless_manufacturing)

In the past, most computer designers would have their own [fabs](semiconductor-fabrication-plant.md).

But once designs started getting very complicated, it started to make sense to separate concerns between designers and [fabs](semiconductor-fabrication-plant.md).

What this means is that design companies would primarily write [register transfer level](register-transfer-level.md), then use [electronic design automation](electronic-design-automation.md) tools to get a final manufacturable chip, and then send that to the [fab](semiconductor-fabrication-plant.md).

It is in this point of time that [TSMC](tsmc.md) came along, and benefied and helped establish this trend.

The term "Fabless" could in theory refer to other areas of industry besides the [semiconductor industry](semiconductor-industry.md), but it is mostly used in that context.
