# Arm on tracks

↑ **Parent:** [Robotic arm](robotic-arm.md)

"Arm on tracks" refers to a variant of the [robotic arm](robotic-arm.md) [robot form factor](robot-form-factor.md) in which the position of attachment of the [robotic arm](robotic-arm.md) can also be moved around linearly or with more degrees of freedom.

E.g.:
- [Everyday Robots](everyday-robots.md) robot
- [Transcendence (2014)](transcendence-2014.md) robots
