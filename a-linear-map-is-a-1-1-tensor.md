<h1 id="a-linear-map-is-a-1-1-tensor">A linear map is a (1,1) tensor</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor](tensor.md)

A linear map $A$ can be seen as a (1,1) [tensor](tensor.md) because:

$$
T(w, v*) = v* A w
$$

is a number, $v*$. is a [dual vector](dual-vector.md), and [W](w.md) is a [vector](vector-mathematics.md). Furthermoe, $T$ is linear in both $v*$ and $w$. All of this makes $T$ fullfill the definition of a (1,1) tensor.
