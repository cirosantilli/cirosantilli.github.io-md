# Gilberto Gil

↑ **Parent:** [The Holy Trinity of popular Brazilian music](the-holy-trinity-of-popular-brazilian-music.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gilberto_Gil)

Gilberto is definitely the most psychedelic/tribal one of the [The Holy Trinity of popular Brazilian music](the-holy-trinity-of-popular-brazilian-music.md), though he also has a boyish quality to his soul.

He is also perhaps the one that impresses [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) the most, at times he can't help but feel:

> OMG how the hell did he come up with that?!
