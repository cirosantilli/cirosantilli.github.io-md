# Exponential function differential equation

↑ **Parent:** [Exponential function](exponential-function.md)

The [differential equation](differential-equation.md) that is solved by the [exponential function](exponential-function.md):

$$
y'(x) = y(x)
$$

with [initial condition](initial-condition.md):

$$
y(0) = 1
$$
