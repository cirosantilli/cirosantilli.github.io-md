# Spacelike-separated event

↑ **Parent:** [Light cone](light-cone.md)

Mathematically, we can decide if two events are [timelike-separated](timelike-separated-event.md) or [spacelike-separated](spacelike-separated-event.md) by just looking at the sign of the [spacetime interval](spacetime-interval.md) between them.

On the [light cone](light-cone.md), these are events on the left/right part of the cone.

Different observers might not agree on the order of two spacelike-separated events.

Further discussion at [Section "Light cone"](light-cone.md).

The opposite of those events are [timelike-separated events](timelike-separated-event.md).
