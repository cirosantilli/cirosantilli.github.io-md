# Photomultiplier tube

↑ **Parent:** [Photomultiplier](photomultiplier.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Photonics equipment](photonics-equipment.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photomultiplier_tube)

Can be used to [detect single photons](single-photon-production-and-detection.md).

[Richard Feynman](richard-feynman-split.md) likes them, he describes the tube at [Richard Feynman Quantum Electrodynamics Lecture at University of Auckland (1979)](richard-feynman-quantum-electrodynamics-lecture-at-university-of-auckland-1979.md) at one point.

It uses the [photoelectric effect](photoelectric-effect.md) multiple times to produce a chain reaction. In particular, as mentioned at [https://youtu.be/5V8VCFkAd0A?t=74](https://youtu.be/5V8VCFkAd0A?t=74) from [Video 23. "Using a Photomultiplier to Detect single photons by Huygens Optics"](#video-using-a-photomultiplier-to-detect-single-photons-by-huygens-optics) this means that the device has a lowest sensitive light frequency, beyond which [photons](photon-split.md) don't have enough energy to eject any [electrons](electron.md).
