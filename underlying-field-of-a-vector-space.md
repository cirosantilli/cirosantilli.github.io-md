# Underlying field of a vector space

↑ **Parent:** [Vector space](vector-space.md)

Every vector space is defined over a [field](field-mathematics.md).

E.g. in $\R^3$, the underlying [field](field-mathematics.md) is $\R$, the [real numbers](real-number.md). And in $\C^2$ the underlying field is $\C$, the [complex numbers](complex-number.md).

Any field can be used, including [finite field](finite-field.md). But the underlying thing has to be a field, because the definitions of a vector need all field properties to hold to make sense.

Elements of the underlying field of a vector space are known as [scalar](scalar-mathematics.md).
