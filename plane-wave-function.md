# Plane wave function

↑ **Parent:** [Schrödinger equation for a free one dimensional particle](schrodinger-equation-for-a-free-one-dimensional-particle.md)

In this solution of the [Schrödinger equation](schrodinger-equation.md), by the [uncertainty principle](uncertainty-principle.md), position is completely unknown (the particle could be anywhere in space), and momentum (and therefore, [energy](energy.md)) is perfectly known.

The [plane wave function](plane-wave-function.md) appears for example in the solution of the [Schrödinger equation for a free one dimensional particle](schrodinger-equation-for-a-free-one-dimensional-particle.md). This makes sense, because when solving with the [time-independent Schrödinger equation](time-independent-schrodinger-equation.md), we do separation of variable on fixed energy levels explicitly, and the plane wave solutions are exactly fixed energy level ones.
