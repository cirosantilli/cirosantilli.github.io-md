# Funding

↑ **Parent:** [OurBigBook.com](../ourbigbook-com-split.md)

<a id="_388"></a>
Ciro is looking for:<a id="_389"></a>

<a id="_390"></a>
- university teachers who might be interested in trying it out as described at [Section "Action plan"](action-plan.md), especially [those who already use open licenses for their lecture notes](how-to-convince-teachers-to-use-cc-by-sa.md)
<a id="_391"></a>
- [funding possibilities](funding.md) for this project, including donations as mentioned at [Section "Sponsor Ciro Santilli's work on OurBigBook.com"](../sponsor-split.md) and contracts

<a id="_392"></a>
The initial incentive for the creators is to make them famous and allow them to get more fulfilling jobs more easily, although Ciro also wants to add [money transfer](knowledge-market.md) mechanisms to it later on.

<a id="_393"></a>
We [can't rely on teachers writing materials, because they simply don't have enough incentive](how-to-convince-teachers-to-use-cc-by-sa.md): publication count is all that matters to their careers. The students however, are desperate to prove themselves to the world, and becoming famous for amazing educational content is something that some of them might want to spend their times on, besides grinding for useless [grade](../grade-exam.md).
