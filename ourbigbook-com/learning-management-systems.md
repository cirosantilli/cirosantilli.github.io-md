# Learning management systems

↑ **Parent:** [Alternatives](alternatives.md)

<a id="_225"></a>
This website basically aims to be a [learning management system](../learning-management-system.md), allowing in particular a teacher to focus his help on students that he is legally obliged to help due to their job. But it will have the following unusual characteristics in current LMS solutions:<a id="_226"></a>

<a id="_227"></a>
- public first, to allow reuse across universities, rather than paywalled as is the case for most top universities
<a id="_228"></a>
- students can create material just like teachers, both are on equal footing. Students/teachers will see an indicator "this is your teacher"/"this is your student for this/past semester", but that is the only difference between their interfaces.
