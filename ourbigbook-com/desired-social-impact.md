# Desired social impact

↑ **Parent:** [Philosophy](philosophy.md)

Crush the current grossly inefficient educational system, replace today's students + teachers + researchers with unified "online content creators/consumers".

Gamify them, and pay the best creators so they can work it full time, until some company hires for more them since they are so provenly good.

Destroy useless [exams](../exam.md), the only metrics of society are either:
- how much money you make
- how high is your educational content creator reputation score

Reduce the entry barrier to education, like Uber has done for taxis.

Help create much greater [equal opportunity](../equal-opportunity.md) to talented poor students as described at [free gifted education](../free-gifted-education.md).

Give the students [a flexible choice of what to learn](../students-must-have-a-flexible-choice-of-what-to-learn.md), which basically implies that a much large proportion of students get a de-facto [gifted education](../gifted-education.md).

In some ways, Ciro wants the website to feel like a [video game](../video-game-split.md), where you fluidly interact with headers, comments and their metadata. If game developers can achieve impressively complicated game engines, why can't we achieve a decent amazing elearning website? :-)
