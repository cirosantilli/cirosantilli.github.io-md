<a id="_221"></a>
- [Roam Research](../roam-research.md) and its [open source](../open-source-software.md) clone [Foam](../foam-personal-knowledge-base.md)
<a id="_222"></a>
- [Forester](../forester.md)

<a id="_223"></a>
While we believe that [OurBigBook](../ourbigbook.md) can hold its own against most of them as a [personal knowledge base](../personal-knowledge-base.md), there is one feature which we believe truly distinguishes OurBigBook from all others in a big way: trustless mind meld with the [OurBigBook topic feature](../ourbigbook-topic-feature.md), which no other system seems to have.

<a id="_224"></a>
Many such systems are also no publishing focused enough, and are more focused only in maintaining people's private knowledge bases. Some of them don't even have publishing at all, or its complicated. While publishing is optional in OurBigBook, it is a crucial feature and extremely well supported.
