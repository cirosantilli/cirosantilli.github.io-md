# Cross polytope

↑ **Parent:** [Classification of regular polytopes](classification-of-regular-polytopes.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cross_polytope)

Examples: [square](square.md), [octahedron](octahedron.md).

Take $(0, 0, 0, \dots, 0)$ and flip one of 0's to $\pm 1$. Therefore has $2 \times D$ [vertices](vertex.md).

Each edge E is linked to every other edge, except it's opposite -E.
