# Timeline of quantum mechanics

↑ **Parent:** [History of quantum mechanics](history-of-quantum-mechanics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timeline_of_quantum_mechanics)

- 1859-1900: see [Section "Black-body radiation experiment"](black-body-radiation-experiment.md). Continuously improving  culminating in [Planck's law](planck-s-law.md) [black-body radiation](black-body-radiation.md) and [Planck's law](planck-s-law.md)
- 1905 [photoelectric effect](photoelectric-effect.md) and the [photon](photon-split.md)
  - TODO experiments
  - 1905 Einstein's photoelectric effect paper. Planck was intially thinking that light was continuous, but the atoms vibrated in a discrete way. Einstein's explanation of the photoelectric effect throws that out of the window, and considers the photon discrete.
- 1913 [atomic spectra](emission-spectrum.md) and the [Bohr model](bohr-model.md)
  - 1885 [Balmer series](balmer-series.md), an [empirical formula](empirical-formula.md) describes some of the lines of the [hydrogen emission spectrum](hydrogen-emission-spectrum.md)
  - 1888 [Rydberg formula](rydberg-formula.md) generalizes the [Balmer series](balmer-series.md)
  - 1896 [Pickering series](pickering-series.md) makes it look like a star has some new kind of hydrogen that produces half-integer entries in the [Pickering series](pickering-series.md)
  - 1911 Bohr visits [J. J. Thomson](j-j-thomson.md) in the [University of Cambridge](university-of-cambridge.md) for his postdoc, but they don't get along well
    - Bohr visits [Rutherford](ernest-rutherford.md) at the [University of Manchester](university-of-manchester.md) and decides to transfer there. During this stay he becomes interested in problems of the electronic structure of the atom.

      Bohr was forced into a quantization postulate because spinning electrons must radiate energy and collapse, so he postulated that electrons must somehow magically stay in orbits without classically spinning.
  - 1913 february: young physics professor Hans Hansen tells Bohr about the [Balmer series](balmer-series.md). This is one of the final elements Bohr needed.
  - 1913 [Bohr model](bohr-model.md) published predicts atomic spectral lines in terms of the [Planck constant](planck-constant.md) and other [physical constant](physical-constant.md).
    - explains the [Pickering series](pickering-series.md) as belonging to inoized [helium](helium.md) that has a single [electron](electron.md). The half term in the spectral lines of this species come from the nucleus having twice the charge of hydrogen.
    - 1913 March: during review before publication, Rutherford points out that [instantaneous quantum jumps don't seem to play well with causality](causality-and-quantum-jumps-are-incompatible.md).
  - 1916 [Bohr-Sommerfeld model](bohr-sommerfeld-model.md) introduces [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) to explain why some lines are not observed, as they would violate the conservation of angular momentum.
