# Marc Verdiell

↑ **Parent:** [CuriousMarc](curiousmarc.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [École Polytechnique alumnus of 1983](ecole-polytechnique-alumnus-of-1983.md), [Human](human-split.md), [Intel fellow](intel-fellow.md)

[Marc Verdiell](marc-verdiell.md) is a [French](france.md) [electrical engineer](electrical-engineer.md) born in 1963 or 1964[https://www.intel.com/pressroom/archive/releases/2001/20011129corp.htm](https://www.intel.com/pressroom/archive/releases/2001/20011129corp.htm) and best known for being the creator and host of the [CuriousMarc](curiousmarc.md) YouTube channel where he does [mind blowing](mind-blown.md) repairs and [reverse engineering](reverse-engineering.md) of vintage [computers](computer-split.md) and other [electronic](electronics-split.md) equipment.

Marc sold his company [LightLogic](lightlogic.md), an [optoelectronics](optoelectronics.md) company he founded, to [Intel](intel.md) in April 2001. This was just after the [dot-com crash](dot-com-bubble.md), but [Intel](intel.md) apparently still correctly believed that the networking and the [Internet](internet.md) would continue to grow and was investing in the area. His associate [Frank Shum](https://ourbigbook.com/go/topic/frank-shum) sued claiming he should be credited for some of the inventions sold but lost and Marc got it all.[https://www.courthousenews.com/inventor-barred-from-proceeds-of-intel-buyout/](https://www.courthousenews.com/inventor-barred-from-proceeds-of-intel-buyout/)[https://mergr.com/intel-acquires-lightlogic](https://mergr.com/intel-acquires-lightlogic)[https://www.lightreading.com/business-management/lightlogic-bulks-up-under-intel](https://www.lightreading.com/business-management/lightlogic-bulks-up-under-intel). Marc was then almost immediately appointed an [Intel fellow](intel-fellow.md) at the extremelly early age of 37, and then stayed for a few years at [Intel](intel.md) until 2006 according to his LinkedIn.[https://www.intel.com/pressroom/archive/releases/2001/20011129corp.htm](https://www.intel.com/pressroom/archive/releases/2001/20011129corp.htm)[https://web.archive.org/web/20030213010020/http://www.intel.com/pressroom/kits/bios/verdiell.htm](https://web.archive.org/web/20030213010020/http://www.intel.com/pressroom/kits/bios/verdiell.htm)

but the full text is not available online.
