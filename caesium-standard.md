# Caesium standard

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic clock](atomic-clock.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Caesium](caesium.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caesium_standard)

Uses the [frequency](frequency.md) of the [hyperfine structure](hyperfine-structure.md) of [caesium-133](caesium-133.md) ground state, i.e spin up vs spin down of its valence electron $6s^1$, to define the [second](second.md).

[International System of Units](international-system-of-units.md) definition of the second since 1967, because this is what [atomic clocks](atomic-clock.md) use.
