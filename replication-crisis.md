# Replication crisis

↑ **Parent:** [Reproducibility](reproducibility.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Replication_crisis)

Of course, if [academic journals](academic-journal.md) require greater reproducibility for publication, then the cost per paper increases.

However, the total cost has to be smaller than the cost everyone who reads the paper spends to reproduce, no?

The truth is, part of the replication crisis is also due to [research groups](research-group.md) not wanting to share their precious secrets with others, so they can keep ahead of the publication curve, or maybe spin off a [startup](startup-company.md).

And when it comes to papers, things are even crazier: [big companies manage to publish white papers in peer reviewed journals](big-companies-manage-to-publish-white-papers-in-peer-reviewed-journals.md).

[Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) wants to help in this area with his [videos of all key physics experiments](videos-of-all-key-physics-experiments.md) project idea.

Cool initiative. Papers that do not share source code should be banned from peer reviewed [academic journals](academic-journal.md).
