# Planck-Einstein relation

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum mechanics](quantum-mechanics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planck–Einstein_relation)

Photon energy is proportional to its frequency:

$$
energy = (plancks \space constant) * (frequency)
$$

or with common weird variables:

$$
E = h * \nu
$$

This only makes sense if the [photon](photon-split.md) exists, there is no classical analogue, because the energy of classical waves depends only on their amplitude, not frequency.

Experiments that suggest this:
- [photoelectric effect](photoelectric-effect.md)
- [Compton scattering](compton-scattering.md)
