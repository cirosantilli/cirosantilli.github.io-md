#### Arm on tracks

↑ **Parent:** [Robotic arm](#robotic-arm)

"Arm on tracks" refers to a variant of the [robotic arm](#robotic-arm) [robot form factor](#robot-form-factor) in which the position of attachment of the [robotic arm](#robotic-arm) can also be moved around linearly or with more degrees of freedom.

- [Everyday Robots](#everyday-robots) robot
- [Transcendence (2014)](film.md#transcendence-2014) robots
