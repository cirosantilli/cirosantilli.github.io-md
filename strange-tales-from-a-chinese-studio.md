# Strange Tales from a Chinese Studio

↑ **Parent:** [Chinese book](chinese-book.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strange_Tales_from_a_Chinese_Studio)

Big collection of a bunch of ghost stories. 90% of them involve some dude dating a beautiful ghost woman or animal that can shapeshift as a woman.

Organized into 12 chapeters as seen on the Chinese wiki: [https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/聊齋志異#章節](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/聊齋志異#章節)
