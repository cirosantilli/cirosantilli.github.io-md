# Optical table

↑ **Parent:** [Photonics equipment](photonics-equipment.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optical_table)

The [breadboard](breadboard.md) of [photonics](photonics.md)!

For example, that is how most modern [microscopes](microscopy-split.md) are prototyped, see for example [Video "Two Photon Microscopy by Nemonic NeuroNex (2019)"](two-photon-excitation-microscopy.md#video-two-photon-microscopy-by-nemonic-neuronex-2019).

This is kind of why they are also sometimes called "optical breadboarbds", since [breadboards](breadboard.md) are what we use for early prototyping in [electronics](electronics-split.md). Wikipedia however says "optical breadboard" is a simpler and cheaper type of optical table with less/no stabilization.
