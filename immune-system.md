# Immune system

↑ **Parent:** [List of anatomical systems](list-of-anatomical-systems.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Immune_system)

A cool thought: [bacteria](bacteria.md) like [E. Coli](escherichia-coli.md) replicate every 20 minutes. A human replicates every 15 years. So how can [multicellular](multicellular-organism.md) beings possibly cope with the speed of evolution of parasites?

The answer is that within us, the [adaptive immune system](adaptive-immune-system.md) is a population of cells that [evolves](evolution.md) very quickly. So in a sense, within our bodies there is fast cell-level non-inheritable evolution happening daily!
