# What poor countries have to do to get richer

↑ **Parent:** [Essays by Ciro Santilli](essays-by-ciro-santilli.md)

<a id="_1"></a>
Like all poor countries, [Brazil](brazil-split.md)'s lack of money and scientific culture severely limit its ability to make [technological](technology-split.md) and [scientific](science-split.md) advances.

<a id="_2"></a>
While this sounds obvious, [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) has felt it first hand since he moved from Brazil to [Europe](europe.md), and it is just shocking.

<a id="_3"></a>
In the city of [Santos](santos-sao-paulo-brazil.md) for example, despite being a [dream place from the natural point of view](ciro-santilli-s-ideal-city-to-live-in.md), it would be completely impossible to achieve any [deep tech](deep-tech.md) technical advance that impacts the world. In Europe however, there are several several places where this can happen.
