# Eigendecomposition of a real symmetric matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Eigendecomposition of a matrix](eigendecomposition-of-a-matrix.md)

The general result from [eigendecomposition of a matrix](eigendecomposition-of-a-matrix.md):

$$
M = QDQ^{-1}
$$

becomes:

$$
M = ODO^T
$$

where $O$ is an [orthogonal matrix](orthogonal-matrix.md), and therefore has $O^{-1} = O^T$.
