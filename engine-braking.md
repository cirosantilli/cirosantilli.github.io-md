# Engine braking

↑ **Parent:** [Driving](driving.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Good](good.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engine_braking)

This is one of the most important driving techniques that you have likely not learned in driving school. [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) had only learnt it from YouTube.

It is understandable why. It only matters in higher speeds/gears and mostly in emergency or when going downhill. But by God, in those cases, it is a life saving skill!
