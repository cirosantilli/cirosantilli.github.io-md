# Radium

↑ **Parent:** [List of chemical elements](list-of-chemical-elements.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radium)

Discovered by [Marie Curie](marie-curie.md) when she noticed that there was some yet unknown more radioactive element in their raw samples, after [uranium](uranium.md) and [polonium](polonium.md), which she published 6 months prior, had already been separated. Published on December 1989 as: [Section "Sur une nouvelle substance fortement radio-active, contenue dans la pechblende"](on-a-new-strongly-radioactive-substance-contained-in-pitchblende.md).

The [uranium 238 decay chain](uranium-238-decay-chain.md) is the main source of naturally occurring [radium](radium.md).
