# Decision problem

↑ **Parent:** [Computational problem](computational-problem.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decision_problem)

[Computational problem](computational-problem.md) where the solution is either yes or no.

When there are more than two possible answers, it is called a [function problem](function-problem.md).

Decision problems come up often in [computer science](computer-science-split.md) because many important problems are often stated in terms of "decide if a given string belongs to given [formal language](formal-language.md)".
