# Universities are obsessed by their images

↑ **Parent:** [University is broken](university-is-broken.md)

The image of an university is basically their only real asset, which they extract money out of via undergraduate students in some countries like the [USA](united-states-split.md), and by getting funding.

For this reason, universities will sacrifice basically any principle in order to give themselves a good image.

Although for profit companies also do this, it is simply on another level for universities.
