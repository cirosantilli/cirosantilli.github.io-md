# Theory of everything

↑ **Parent:** [Standard Model](standard-model-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Unsolved physics problem](unsolved-physics-problem.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theory_of_everything)

As of 2019, the [Standard Model](standard-model-split.md) and [general relativity](general-relativity.md) are incompatible. Once those are unified, we will have one equation to describe the entirety of [physics](physics-split.md).

There are also however also unsolved problems in [electroweak interaction](electroweak-interaction.md) + [strong interaction](strong-interaction.md), which if achieved is referred to as a [Grand Unified Theory](grand-unified-theory.md). Reaching a GUT is considered a sensible intermediate step before TOE.

The current state of Physics has been the result of several previous unifications as shown at: [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theory_of_everything#Conventional_sequence_of_theories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theory_of_everything#Conventional_sequence_of_theories) so it is expected that this last missing unification is likely to happen one day, potentially conditional on humanity having enough energy to observe new phenomena.
