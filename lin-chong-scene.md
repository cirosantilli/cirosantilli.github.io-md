# Lin Chong scene

↑ **Parent:** [Lin Chong](lin-chong.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Water Margin scene](water-margin-scene.md)

Lance instructor of the 800,000 Imperial Guards (八十万禁军枪棒教头). TODO understand the "枪棒" part: [https://zhidao.baidu.com/question/206659649.html](https://zhidao.baidu.com/question/206659649.html).

The adopted son of [Gao Qiu](gao-qiu.md) wanted to [fuck](sexual-intercourse.md) his wife, and because of this they frame him of planning to take revenge by killing Go Qiu, even though Lin Chong had decided not to take revenge to avoid harming his wife further.

They just keep trying to kill him, until at one point he just gives up and becomes a fugitive.

His story is well told in [The Water Margin](the-water-margin-1973-tv-series.md).

Usually called by others as 林教头 (lin jiaotou, literally Head Instructor Lin, but usually translated as just Instructor Lin).
