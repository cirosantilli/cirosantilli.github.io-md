# Matrix representation of a bilinear form

↑ **Parent:** [Bilinear form](bilinear-form.md)

As usual, it is useful to think about how a [bilinear form](bilinear-form.md) looks like in terms of [vectors](vector-mathematics.md) and [matrices](matrix.md).

Unlike a [linear form](linear-form.md), which [was a vector](matrix-representation-of-a-linear-form.md), because it has two inputs, the bilinear form is represented by a matrix $M$ which encodes the value for each possible pair of [basis vectors](basis-linear-algebra.md).

In terms of that [matrix](matrix.md), the form $B(x,y)$ is then given by:

$$
B(x,y) = x^T M y
$$
