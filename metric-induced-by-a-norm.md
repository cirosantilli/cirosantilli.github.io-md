# Metric induced by a norm

↑ **Parent:** [Norm (mathematics)](norm-mathematics.md)

In a [vector space](vector-space.md), a [metric](metric-mathematics.md) may be induced from a norm by using [subtraction](subtraction.md):

$$
d(x, y) = |x - y|
$$
