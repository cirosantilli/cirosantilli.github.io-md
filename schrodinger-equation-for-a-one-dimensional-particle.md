<h1 id="schrodinger-equation-for-a-one-dimensional-particle">Schrödinger equation for a one dimensional particle</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Solutions of the Schrodinger equation](solutions-of-the-schrodinger-equation.md)

We select for the general [Equation 7. "Schrodinger equation"](schrodinger-equation.md#equation-schrodinger-equation):
- $\vv{x} = x$, the linear [cartesian coordinate](cartesian-coordinate-system.md) in the x direction
- $\hat{H} = -\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\pdv{^2}{x^2} + V(x, t)$, which analogous to the sum of [kinetic](kinetic-energy.md) and [potential energy](potential-energy.md) in [classical mechanics](classical-mechanics.md)
giving the full explicit [partial differential equation](partial-differential-equation.md):<a id="equation-schrodinger-equation-for-a-one-dimensional-particle"></a>


$$
i\hbar\pdv{\psi(x, t)}{t} = \left[ -\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\pdv{^2}{x^2} + V(x, t) \right]\psi(x, t)
$$

The corresponding [time-independent Schrödinger equation](time-independent-schrodinger-equation.md) for this equation is:<a id="equation-time-independent-schrodinger-equation-for-a-one-dimensional-particle"></a>


$$
\left[-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m} \pdv{^2}{x^2} + V(x)\right]\psi(x) = E \psi(x)
$$
