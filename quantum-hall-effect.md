Quantum version of the [Hall effect](hall-effect.md).

As you increase the [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md), you can see the [Hall resistance](hall-resistance.md) increase, but it does so in discrete steps.

The effect can be separated into two modes:
- [Integer quantum Hall effect](integer-quantum-hall-effect.md): easier to explain from first principles
- [Fractional quantum Hall effect](fractional-quantum-hall-effect.md): harder to explain from first principles
  - [Fractional quantum Hall effect for $\nu = 1/m$](fractional-quantum-hall-effect-for-nu-1-m.md): [1998 Nobel Prize in Physics](1998-nobel-prize-in-physics.md)
  - [Fractional quantum Hall effect for $\nu \ne 1/m$](fractional-quantum-hall-effect-for-nu-ne-1-m.md): one of the most important [unsolved physics problems](unsolved-physics-problem.md) as of 2023
