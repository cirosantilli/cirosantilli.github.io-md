# Maxima and minima

↑ **Parent:** [Derivative](derivative.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maxima_and_minima)

Given a [function](function-mathematics.md) $f$:
- from some space. For beginners the [real numbers](real-number.md) but more generally [topological spaces](topological-space.md) should work in general
- to the [real numbers](real-number.md)
we want to find the points $x$ of the [domain](domain-of-a-function.md) of $f$ where the value of $f$ is smaller (for minima, or larger for maxima) than all other points in some [neighbourhood](neighbourhood-mathematics.md) of $x$.

In the case of [Functionals](functional.md), this problem is treated under the theory of the [calculus of variations](calculus-of-variations.md).
