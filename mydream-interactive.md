# MyDream Interactive

↑ **Parent:** [Allison Huynh](allison-huynh.md)

[Allison Huynh](allison-huynh.md)'s zombie gaming company.

Their URL was: [http://www.mydreaminteractive.com](http://www.mydreaminteractive.com), with several evolving captures: [https://web.archive.org/web/20160127075812/http://mydreaminteractive.com/](https://web.archive.org/web/20160127075812/http://mydreaminteractive.com/)

Their flagship seems to have been this game: "MyDream" [https://store.steampowered.com/app/348860/MyDream/](https://store.steampowered.com/app/348860/MyDream/), a [Roblox](https://ourbigbook.com/go/topic/roblox)-like. This is seen in 2015 at [on web archive](https://web.archive.org/web/20160127075812/http://mydreaminteractive.com/).

They had a child educational focus and also made some attempts in [cryptocurrency](cryptocurrency-split.md).
