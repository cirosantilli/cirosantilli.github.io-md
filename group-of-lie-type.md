# Group of Lie type

↑ **Parent:** [Classification of finite simple groups](classification-of-finite-simple-groups.md)

In the [classification of finite simple groups](classification-of-finite-simple-groups.md), groups of Lie type are a set of infinite families of simple lie groups. These are the other infinite families besides te [cyclic groups](cyclic-group.md) and [alternating groups](alternating-group.md).
