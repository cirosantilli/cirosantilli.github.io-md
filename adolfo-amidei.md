# Adolfo Amidei

↑ **Parent:** [Enrico Fermi](enrico-fermi.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Amazing mentor](amazing-mentor.md)

This dude mentored [Enrico Fermi](enrico-fermi.md) in [high school](secondary-school.md). [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) added some info to [Fermi](enrico-fermi.md)'s [Wikipedia](wikipedia.md) page at: [https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Enrico_Fermi&type=revision&diff=1050919447&oldid=1049187703](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Enrico_Fermi&type=revision&diff=1050919447&oldid=1049187703) from [Enrico Fermi: physicist by Emilio Segrè (1970)](enrico-fermi-physicist-by-emilio-segre-1970.md):

> In 1914, [Fermi](enrico-fermi.md), who used to often meet with his father in front of the office after work, met a colleague of his father called [Adolfo Amidei](adolfo-amidei.md), who would walk part of the way home with [Alberto](alberto-fermi.md) \[Enrico's father\]. Enrico had learned that Adolfo was interested in [mathematics](mathematics-split.md) and [physics](physics-split.md) and took the opportunity to ask Adolfo a question about [geometry](geometry-split.md). Adolfo understood that the young Fermi was referring to [projective geometry](projective-geometry.md) and then proceeded to give him a book on the subject written by [Theodor Reye](https://ourbigbook.com/go/topic/theodor-reye). Two months later, Fermi returned the book, having solved all the problems proposed at the end of the book, some of which Adolfo considered difficult. Upon verifying this, Adolfo felt that Fermi was "a prodigy, at least with respect to geometry", and further mentored the boy, providing him with more books on physics and mathematics. Adolfo noted that Fermi had a very good memory and thus could return the books after having read them because he could remember their content very well.
