# Dense and sparse matrices

↑ **Parent:** [Named matrix](named-matrix.md)

A good definition is that the [sparse matrix](sparse-matrix.md) has non-zero entries proportional the number of rows. Therefore this is [Big O notation](big-o-notation.md) less than something that has $N^2$ non zero entries. Of course, this only makes sense when generalizing to larger and larger matrices, otherwise we could take the constant of proportionality very high for one specific matrix.
