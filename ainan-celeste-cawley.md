# Ainan Celeste Cawley

↑ **Parent:** [Child prodigy](child-prodigy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ainan_Celeste_Cawley)

His father fought a lot with the stupid [educational system](education-split.md) to try and move his son to his full potential and move to more advanced subjects early.

A crime of society to try and prevent it. They actually moved the family from Singapore to Malaysia for a learning opportunity for the son. Amazing.

This is the perfect illustration of one of [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md)'s most important complaints about the 2020 educational system:
- [students must have a flexible choice of what to learn](students-must-have-a-flexible-choice-of-what-to-learn.md)
- [the missing link between basic and advanced](the-missing-link-between-basic-and-advanced.md)
and why Ciro created [OurBigBook.com](ourbigbook-com-split.md) to try and help fix the issue.
