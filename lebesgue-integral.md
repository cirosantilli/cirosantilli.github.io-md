# Lebesgue integral

↑ **Parent:** [Integral](integral.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebesgue_integration)

"More complex and general" integral. Matches the [Riemann integral](riemann-integral.md) for "simple functions", but also [works for some "funkier" functions that Riemann does not work for](lebesgue-integral-vs-riemann-integral.md).

[Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) sometimes wonders how much someone can gain from learning this besides [the beauty of mathematics](the-beauty-of-mathematics.md), since we can hand-wave a [Lebesgue integral](lebesgue-integral.md) on almost anything that is of practical use. The beauty is good reason enough though.
