# Which boundary conditions lead to existence and uniqueness of a second order PDE

↑ **Parent:** [Classification of second order partial differential equations into elliptic, parabolic and hyperbolic](classification-of-second-order-partial-differential-equations-into-elliptic-parabolic-and-hyperbolic.md)

[http://www.cns.gatech.edu/~predrag/courses/PHYS-6124-12/StGoChap6.pdf](http://www.cns.gatech.edu/~predrag/courses/PHYS-6124-12/StGoChap6.pdf) 6.1 "Classification of PDE's" clarifies which boundary conditions are needed for existence and uniqueness of each [type of second order of PDE](classification-of-second-order-partial-differential-equations-into-elliptic-parabolic-and-hyperbolic.md):
- [elliptic partial differential equation](elliptic-partial-differential-equation.md) and [parabolic partial differential equation](parabolic-partial-differential-equation.md): [Dirichlet boundary condition](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) or [Neumann boundary condition](neumann-boundary-condition.md)
- [hyperbolic partial differential equation](hyperbolic-partial-differential-equation.md): [Cauchy boundary condition](cauchy-boundary-condition.md)
