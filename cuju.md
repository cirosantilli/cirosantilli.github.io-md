# Cuju

↑ **Parent:** [Chinese game](chinese-game.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cuju)

Lit. "to kick (leather) ball".

Figures notbaly in [Water Margin](water-margin.md), where it is played by [Gao Qiu](gao-qiu.md). The novel also suggests that it was considered a lesser art, e.g. as opposed to the scholarly [four arts](four-arts.md) and the "proper" martial arts.
