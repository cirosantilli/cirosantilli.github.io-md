On Wikipedia we only find the term [Scholar-official](#scholar-official). But the idea of the ancient Chinese scholar is a bit wider as a concept, and even people who were not trying to be officials could thrive to follow certain aspects of the scholar way of life.

##### Scholar-official

↑ **Parent:** [Chinese scholar](#chinese-scholar)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scholar-official)

The "Chinese scholar" is one of the traditional male ideals of [Chinese culture](#chinese-culture).

Particular attributes the ideal Chinese scholar would have include:
- mastery of the [Four arts](#four-arts), which also implies the [Four Treasures of the Study](#four-treasures-of-the-study) for caligraphy and painting
- live in a place that includes
  - a [Chinese garden](#chinese-garden)
It is in a sense sad (or awesome) that the [computer](computer.md) has essentially replaced, or one may argue, enhanced beyong recognition, all of the traditional four arts, to the point that Ciro can't touch anything that is not a computer. Sometimes the old arts live on particularly closely though, e.g. in [computer music](music.md#computer-music), [computer graphics](computer.md#computer-graphics) and [computer Go](art.md#computer-go).

###### Water Margin

↑ **Parent:** [Four Great Classic Novels](#four-great-classic-novels)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Good book](literature.md#good-book), [Water](chemistry.md#water)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Water_Margin)

Talks about rebellion of the oppressed (and bandits), and therefore has been controversial throughout the many [Chinese dictatorships](the-most-important-projects-done-by-ciro-santilli.md#ciro-santilli-s-campaign-for-freedom-of-speech-in-china).

The book is based on real events surrounding 12th century rebel leader [Song Jiang](#song-jiang) during the [Song dynasty](#song-dynasty).

It is also interesting that [Mao Zedong](#mao-zedong) was apparently a fan of the novel, although he had to hide that to some extent due to the controversial nature of the material, which could be said to instigate rebellion.

The incredible popularity of the novel can also be seen by the large number of paintings of it found in the [Summer Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Summer_Palace).

This is a [good novel](literature.md#good-book). It appeals to [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli.md)'s sensibilities [of rebelling against unfairness](the-most-important-projects-done-by-ciro-santilli.md#ciro-santilli-s-campaign-for-freedom-of-speech-in-china), and in particular about people who are at the margin of society (at the river margin) doing so. [Tax the rich](social-technology.md#tax-the-rich) BTW.

It also has always made Ciro quite curious how such novels are not used as a way to inspire people to rebel against the [Chinese Communist Party](#chinese-communist-party).

The good part basically ends with the end of Wu Song's story at episode 22.

One of the most amazing things about this series is learning about how people lived in the past. It feels very realistic. Some amazing details:

###### 108 Stars of Destiny

↑ **Parent:** [Water Margin](#water-margin)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Chinese numbered list](#chinese-numbered-list)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/108_Stars_of_Destiny)

The concept is a strong part of [Water Margin](#water-margin).

The very first chapter opens with [Grand Commander Hou](#grand-commander-hou) disrespecting spirits by opening up a sealed tomb and unintentionally releasing 108 demons who presumably reincarnate as humans, a backstory to the events that follow years later.

This is well shown in the Japanese [The Water Margin](#the-water-margin-1973-tv-series), but is skipped in the Chinese [The Water Margin](#the-water-margin-1998-tv-series) unfortunately.

The concept is also central to the [Suikoden](video-game.md#suikoden) video game.

Actually, now that [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli.md) thinks about it, these were already likely meant as a "[collectible](art.md#collecting)" element by the author of the original book, as is strongly suggested by all the little illustrations of each character present on the [Wikipedia](website.md#wikipedia) page. Just like e.g. [Catholic](religion.md#catholic-church) [saints](religion.md#saint). It's [Pokemon](video-game.md#pokemon), but 2000 years earlier.

Ciro would notably feel many years later, that as he [met random people online](ciro-santilli-s-psychology-and-physiology.md#ciro-santilli-s-e-soulmates) who were interested in [improving eduction](ourbigbook-com.md), or was otherwise networking to reach his goals, as if he were actually building up his 108 stars of destiny.

A central part, and perhaps the most interesting part of the novel, is how each character has been motivated by injustice to join the rebellion. This reminds Ciro of [Final Fantasy VI](video-game.md#final-fantasy-vi)

### Stone garden

↑ **Parent:** [Chinese garden](#chinese-garden)

[https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/假山](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/假山)

The literal Chinese name says it all: "Fake Mountain". The stones evoke the feeling of the beautiful rock mountains of China.

The term "奇石假山" (qi2 shi2 jia3 shan1, lit. "weird shaped stone fake mountain") is also used, almost as a synonym by many people, since the stones are often chose in interesting shapes. Choosing the right stone is basically an art form in itself.

The stones used are generally [limestone](astronomy.md#limestone), which as a [sedimentary rock](astronomy.md#sedimentary-rock) is weaker, and more likely to be eroded into interesting shapes.
