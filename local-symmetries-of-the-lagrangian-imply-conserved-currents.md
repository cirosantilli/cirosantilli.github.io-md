# Local symmetries of the Lagrangian imply conserved currents

↑ **Parent:** [Local symmetry](local-symmetry.md)

TODO. I think this is the key point. Notably, [$U(1)$](unitary-group-of-degree-1.md) symmetry implies [charge conservation](charge-conservation.md).

More precisely, each [generator of the corresponding Lie algebra](generator-of-a-lie-algebra.md) leads to one separate conserved current, such that a single symmetry can lead to multiple conserved currents.

This is basically the [local symmetry](local-symmetry.md) version of [Noether's theorem](noether-s-theorem.md).

Then to maintain charge conservation, we have to maintain [local symmetry](local-symmetry.md), which in turn means we have to add a [gauge field](gauge-field.md) as shown at [Video "Deriving the qED Lagrangian by Dietterich Labs (2018)"](derivation-of-the-quantum-electrodynamics-lagrangian.md#video-deriving-the-qed-lagrangian-by-dietterich-labs-2018).

Forces can then be seen as kind of a side effect of this.
