# Model protein

↑ **Parent:** [Protein](protein-split.md)

[Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) defines a "[model protein](model-protein.md)" as a protein which has been significantly used in the history of protein science, in analogy to the term [model organism](model-organism.md).

Key characteristics of [model proteins](model-protein.md) include:
- they are easy to obtain and are stable
- they are important to medical applications
- they are small and easier to understand for early studies

Important model proteins include:
- [insulin](insulin.md): as a [peptide hormone](peptide-hormone.md), this was small. Also it was useful and widely available even at pharmacies, [The Eighth Day of Creation](the-eighth-day-of-creation.md) says you could get it a Boots, a major British pharmacy chain, and as such was a natural choice for the first sequencing by [Frederick Sanger](frederick-sanger.md) published in 1951
- [hemoglobin](hemoglobin.md)
- keratin
