# Linear form

↑ **Parent:** [Linear map](linear-map.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_form)

A [Linear map](linear-map.md) where the [image](image-mathematics.md) is the [underlying field of the vector space](underlying-field-of-a-vector-space.md), e.g. $\R^n \to \R$.

The set of all [linear forms](linear-form.md) over a [vector space](vector-space.md) forms another vector space called the [dual space](dual-space.md).
