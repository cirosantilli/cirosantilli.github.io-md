# Half-life

↑ **Parent:** [Radioactive decay](radioactive-decay.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Half-life)

The [half-life](half-life.md) of [radioactive decay](radioactive-decay.md), which as discovered a few years before [quantum mechanics](quantum-mechanics-split.md) was discovered and matured, was a major mystery. Why do some nuclei fission in apparently random fashion, while others don't? How is the state of different nuclei different from one another? This is mentioned in [Inward Bound by Abraham Pais (1988)](inward-bound-by-abraham-pais-1988.md) Chapter 6.e Why a half-life?

The term also sees use in other areas, notably [biology](biology-split.md), where e.g. [RNAs](rna.md) spontaneously decay as part of the [cell](cell-split.md)'s [control system](control-system.md), see e.g. mentions in [E. Coli Whole Cell Model by Covert Lab](e-coli-whole-cell-model-by-covert-lab-split.md).
