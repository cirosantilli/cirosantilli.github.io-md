# Tree traversal

↑ **Parent:** [Tree (data structure)](tree-data-structure.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tree_traversal)

The summary from [https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/tree-traversals-inorder-preorder-and-postorder/](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/tree-traversals-inorder-preorder-and-postorder/) is a winner:
```
    1
   / \
  2   3
 / \
4   5
```

- [inorder DFS](in-order-depth-first-search.md): 4 2 5 1 3
- [preorder DFS](pre-order-depth-first-search.md): 1 2 4 5 3
- [postorder DFS](post-order-depth-first-search.md): 4 5 2 3 1
- [breadth-first search](breadth-first-search.md): 1 2 3 4 5

In principle one could talk about tree traversal of [unordered trees](unordered-tree.md) as a number of possible traversals without a fixed order. But we won't consider that under this section, only deterministic [ordered tree](ordered-tree.md) traversals.
