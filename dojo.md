# Dojo

↑ **Parent:** [Martial art](martial-art.md)

"Dojo" is the [japanese](japanese-language.md) version of the word that unfortunately came to dominate in the West, the original is of course [Chinese](chinese-language.md) Dao4 chang3 (道場) which means:
- dao4 (道): [Taoism](taoism.md), the Enlightned Path to something
- chang3 (場): suffix indicating "a place where you do something"
