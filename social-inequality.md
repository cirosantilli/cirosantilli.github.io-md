# Social inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Economy](economy-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_inequality)

[Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) is extremely passionate about this issue, partly due to [Ciro Santilli's self perceived compassionate personality](ciro-santilli-s-self-perceived-compassionate-personality.md).

[Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md)'s main approaches to reduce it:
- [OurBigBook.com](ourbigbook-com-split.md) to make education more efficient
- [free gifted education](free-gifted-education.md)
- more granular [electronic voting](electronic-voting.md)

One of Ciro's most direct experiences with social inequality is described at: [São Remo, the favela next to USP](sao-remo-the-favela-next-to-usp.md).

We have to be careful not to make everyone poorer when trying to reduce inequality.

But as things stand as of 2020, [increasing taxes on the very richest](tax-the-rich.md), and notably [wealth tax](wealth-tax.md), and investing it in [free gifted education](free-gifted-education.md), seems like a safe bet to achieve any meaningful level of [equal opportunity](equal-opportunity.md) and [meritocracy](meritocracy.md).
