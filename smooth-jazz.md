# Smooth jazz

↑ **Parent:** [Jazz subgenre](jazz-subgenre.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smooth_jazz)

[Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) defines "Smooth Jazz" simply as jazz that he can leave on the background to work without being particularly distracted.

It does not mean that the music is bad in any way. Just not particularly distracting.
