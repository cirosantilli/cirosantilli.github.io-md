# Lysozyme structure resolution (1965)

↑ **Parent:** [Lysozyme](lysozyme.md)

With [X-ray crystallography](x-ray-crystallography.md) by David Chilton Phillips. The second [protein](protein-split.md) to be resolved fter [after myoglobin](myoglobin-structure-resolution-1958.md), and the first [enzyme](enzyme.md).

Published at: [Structure of Hen Egg-White Lysozyme: A Three-dimensional Fourier Synthesis at 2 Å Resolution (1965)](structure-of-hen-egg-white-lysozyme-a-three-dimensional-fourier-synthesis-at-2-a-resolution-1965.md). The work was done while at the [Davy Faraday Research Laboratory](davy-faraday-research-laboratory.md) of the [Royal Institution](royal-institution.md).

Phillips also published a lower resolution (6[angstrom](angstrom.md)) of the enzyme-[inhibitor](enzyme-inhibitor.md) complexes at about the same time: [Structure of Some Crystalline Lysozyme-Inhibitor Complexes Determined by X-Ray Analysis At 6 Å Resolution (1965)](structure-of-some-crystalline-lysozyme-inhibitor-complexes-determined-by-x-ray-analysis-at-6-a-resolution-1965.md). The point of doing this is that it points out the [active site](active-site.md) of the [enzyme](enzyme.md).
