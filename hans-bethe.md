# Hans Bethe

↑ **Parent:** [Physicist](physicist-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hans_Bethe)

Head of the theoretical division at the [Los Alamos Laboratory](los-alamos-laboratory.md) during the [Manhattan Project](manhattan-project.md).

[Richard Feynman](richard-feynman-split.md) was working under him there, and was promoted to team lead by him because Richard impressed Hans.

He was also the person under which [Freeman Dyson](freeman-dyson.md) was originally under when he moved from the [United Kingdom](united-kingdom-split.md) to the [United States](united-states-split.md).

And Hans also impressed Feynman, both were problem solvers, and liked solving mental arithmetic and [numerical analysis](numerical-analysis.md).

This relationship is what brought Feynman to [Cornell University](cornell-university.md) after [World War II](world-war-ii.md), Hans' institution, which is where Feynman did the main part of his [Nobel prize winning](1965-nobel-prize-in-physics-laureate.md) work on [quantum electrodynamics](quantum-electrodynamics.md).

Hans must have been the perfect [PhD](doctor-of-philosophy.md) advisor. He's always smiling, and he seemed so approachable. And he was incredibly capable, notably in his calculation skills, which were much more important in those pre-[computer](computer-split.md) days.
