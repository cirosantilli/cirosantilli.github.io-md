# The book binding broke three times

↑ **Parent:** [Guqin piece](guqin-piece.md)

韦编三绝 is a [chengyu](chengyu.md) that means "to study diligently", i.e. to read so much to the point that your book starts to wear down.

There is a Chinese Wiki page for this song: [https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/韦编三绝](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/韦编三绝) which says it dates from the early [Qing dynasty](qing-dynasty.md)
