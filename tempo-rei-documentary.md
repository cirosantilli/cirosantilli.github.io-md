# Tempo Rei (documentary)

↑ **Parent:** [Gilberto Gil bibliography](gilberto-gil-bibliography.md)

Very good documentary!

[IMDb](imdb.md) entry: [https://www.imdb.com/title/tt1252389/](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt1252389/)

This is a documentary about [Gilberto Gil](gilberto-gil.md) going back to his homelnd for the first time since he emigrated to Salvador/Rio, with some of his most popular songs intermixed into it. Beautiful!

"Tempo Rei" is the name of a [Song composed by Gilberto Gil](song-composed-by-gilberto-gil.md).

Of particular note is the [Video 68. "Lamento Sertanejo scene from the 2002 documentary Tempo Rei"](lamento-sertanejo.md#video-lamento-sertanejo-scene-from-the-2002-documentary-tempo-rei).
