**[Figure 1](#image-19th-century-illustration-of-the-journey-to-the-west-protagonist-sun-wukong). 19th century illustration of the Journey to the West protagonist Sun Wukong**. [Source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Xiyou.PNG). [Sun Wukong](sun-wukong.md) (孙悟空) is a playful and obscenely powerful monkey Journey to the West. He protects [Buddhist](buddhism.md) monk [Tang Sanzang](tang-sanzang.md), and likes [eating fruit, just like Ciro](ciro-santilli-s-psychology-and-physiology-split.md). Oh, and [Goku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goku) from [Dragon Ball](dragon-ball.md) is based on him. His [japanese](japanese-language.md) name _is_ "Sun Wukong" (same [Chinese characters](chinese-character.md) with different Japanese pronunciation) for the love. His given name "Wukong" means literally "the one who mastered the void", which is clearly a [Dharma name](dharma-name.md) and [fucking awesome](physics-and-the-illusion-of-life.md) in [multiple ways](the-correlation-between-software-engineers-and-buddhism.md). This is another sad instance of a [Chinese thing better known in the West as Japanese](chinese-thing-better-known-in-the-west-as-japanese.md).

It is worth noting however that although Wukong is extremely charming, Ciro's favorite novel of the [Four Great Classic Novels](four-great-classic-novels.md) is [Water Margin](water-margin.md). [Journey to the West](journey-to-the-west.md) is just a [monster of the week](monster-of-the-week.md) for kids, but [Water Margin](water-margin.md) is a fight for justice saga. Sorry Wukong!

**[Figure 3](#image-scribe-jean-mielot-15th-century). Scribe Jean Miélot, 15th century**. [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) fantasizes that he would have make a good scribe in the middle ages, partly due to his [self diagnosed graphomania](ciro-santilli-s-psychology-and-physiology-split.md), but also [appreciation for foreign languages](having-more-than-one-natural-language-is-bad-for-the-world.md), and his mild obsession with the [natural sciences](natural-science.md).

[OurBigBook.com](ourbigbook-com-split.md) is Ciro's view of a modern day [scriptorium](scriptorium.md), except that now the [illuminations](illuminated-manuscript.md) are [YouTube](youtube.md) videos.

[Chill and eat your bread in peace](chill-and-eat-your-bread-in-peace.md) comes to mind. A scribe, in a library, reading and writing the entire day in peace and quiet. The life!

The job of a Internet-age scribe is basically that of making knowledge more open, legally extracting it from closed copyrighted sources, and explaining your understanding of it to the wider world under [Creative Commons licenses](creative-commons-license.md) on the web. And in the process of greater openness, given a [well organized system](ourbigbook-com-split.md), we are able combine the knowledge of many different people, and thus make things more understandable than any single/few creator closed source source could ever achieve.

Ciro once saw some cartoon on [Wikipedia](wikipedia.md) help pages of a turtle with a book in one hand, and typing into Wikipedia on its computer, TODO find it. That cartoon summarizes well the modern scribe life.

Another analogous version of this fantasy more in touch with Ciro's [sinophily](sinophile.md) is the ideal of the [Chinese scholar](chinese-scholar.md), notably including their stereotypical attributes such as mastery of the [Four arts](four-arts.md).
