# Yttrium barium copper oxide

↑ **Parent:** [List of High-temperature superconductors](list-of-high-temperature-superconductors.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Barium compound](barium-compound.md), [Copper compound](copper-compound.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yttrium_barium_copper_oxide)

Upside: superconducting above 92K, which is above the 77K of [liquid nitrogen](liquid-nitrogen.md), and therefore much much cheaper to obtain and maintain than liquid helium.

Downside: it is brittle, so how do you make wires out of it? Still, can already be used in certain circuits, e.g. high temperature [SQUID devices](squid-device.md).
