# Hermite polynomials

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hermite_polynomials)

Show up in the solution of the [quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator.md) after [separation of variables](separation-of-variables.md) leading into the [time-independent Schrödinger equation](time-independent-schrodinger-equation.md), much like [solving partial differential equations with the Fourier series](solving-partial-differential-equations-with-the-fourier-series.md).

I.e.: they are both:
- solutions to the [time-independent Schrödinger equation](time-independent-schrodinger-equation.md) for the [quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator.md)
- a complete basis of that space
