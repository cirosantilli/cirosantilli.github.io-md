# The Amino-acid Sequence in the [Phenylalanyl](phenylalanine.md) Chain of Insulin

↑ **Parent:** [Paper by Fred Sanger](paper-by-fred-sanger.md)

This is where he started publishing the sequence of [insulin](insulin.md). The paper gives the full [B-chain](b-chain-of-insulin.md) sequence, which it tentatively calls the "[Phenylalanyl](phenylalanine.md) Chain" because it starts with a [Phenylalanyl](phenylalanine.md).

The official link seems to be: [https://portlandpress.com/biochemj/article/49/4/463/47212/The-amino-acid-sequence-in-the-phenylalanyl-chain](https://portlandpress.com/biochemj/article/49/4/463/47212/The-amino-acid-sequence-in-the-phenylalanyl-chain) It seems to explain the methods very well at first glance, with lots of schematics.
