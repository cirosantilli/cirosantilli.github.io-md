# Breadboard

↑ **Parent:** [Circuit board](circuit-board.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breadboard)

This is how [electronic circuits](electronic-circuit.md) are normally prototyped!

Once you validate them like this, the next step is usually to move on to [printed circuit boards](printed-circuit-board.md) for more reliable production setups.

Breadboards are a thing of beauty and wonder.

<a id="image-point-to-point-constructions-on-woden-boards"></a>
![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/56/Wooden_Breadboard_Circuits.jpg/960px-Wooden_Breadboard_Circuits.jpg)

**[Figure 21](#image-point-to-point-constructions-on-woden-boards). Point-to-point constructions on woden boards**. [Source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Wooden_Breadboard_Circuits.jpg). Predecessors to [breadboards](breadboard.md) from where the name came. A thing of beauty, so vintage. You could actually write stuff on those with a pencil!
