# Monotreme

↑ **Parent:** [Mammal subclade](mammal-subclade.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monotreme)

The weirdest [mammal](mammal.md) [clade](clade.md): they lay fucking [eggs](egg.md). Only 5 known species alive as of 2020.

Eggs are [basal](basal-phylogenetics.md): they simply didn't evolve out of what other [reptiles](reptile.md) do. From which we conclude that [milk](milk.md) came before [eggs](egg.md) stopped.

So this is the most basal subclade of mammals.

Etymology: means "single hole" in [Greek](greek-language.md), because like other [reptiles](reptile.md) it has a single hole for [shit](feces.md), [pee](urine.md) and [fucking](sexual-intercourse.md): the [cloaca](cloaca.md).
