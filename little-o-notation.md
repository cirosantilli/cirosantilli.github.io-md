# Little-o notation

↑ **Parent:** [Big O notation family](big-o-notation-family.md)

Stronger version of the [big O notation](big-o-notation.md), basically means that ratio goes to zero. In [big O notation](big-o-notation.md), the ratio does not need to go to zero.

So in informal terms, [big O notation](big-o-notation.md) means $\leq$, and [little-o notation](little-o-notation.md) means $<$.

E.g.:
- $x = O(x)$
- $x \ne o(x)$K does not tend to zero
- $x = O(x^2)$
- $x = o(x^2)$
