# Lumen (unit)

↑ **Parent:** [Candela](candela.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lumen_(unit))

1 [Watt](watt.md) equals 683 Lumens of light power at [wavelength](wavelength.md) 555 nm. At other wavelengths 1 Watt is less Lumens as it takes into account the sensitivity of the average human eye.
