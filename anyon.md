# Anyon

↑ **Parent:** [Fermions, bosons and anyons](fermions-bosons-and-anyons.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anyon)

The name actually comes from "any". Amazing.

Can only exist in [2D](real-plane.md) surfaces, not 3D, where [fermions](fermion.md) and [bosons](boson.md) are the only options.

All known [anyons](anyon.md) are [quasiparticles](quasiparticle.md).
