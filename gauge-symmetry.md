While most of this is useless as you would expect from the channel, it does give one key idea: you can change charge locally, but things somehow still work out.

And this has something to do with the general intuition of [special relativity](special-relativity.md) that only local measures make much sense, as evidenced by [Einstein synchronization](einstein-synchronization.md).
