# Students must be allowed to progress as fast as they want

↑ **Parent:** [Students must have a flexible choice of what to learn](students-must-have-a-flexible-choice-of-what-to-learn.md)

One of the main reasons for [Section "Students must have a flexible choice of what to learn"](students-must-have-a-flexible-choice-of-what-to-learn.md).

We've created a system where people just wait, and wait, and wait, never really doing what they really want.

They wait through school to get into university.

They wait through university to get to masters.

They wait through masters to get to [PhD](doctor-of-philosophy.md).

They wait through [PhD](doctor-of-philosophy.md) to become a PI.

And for the minuscule fraction of those that make it, [they become fund proposal writers](academia-is-broken.md).

And if you make any wrong choice along the, it's all over, you can't continue anymore, the cost would be too great.

So you just become [software engineer](software-engineer.md) or a consultant until you die.

Is this the society that we really want?
