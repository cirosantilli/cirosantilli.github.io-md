# Polynomial over a commutative ring

↑ **Parent:** [Domain of a polynomial](domain-of-a-polynomial.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polynomial_over_a_commutative_ring)

Unlike [over non-commutative rings](polynomial-over-a-ring.md), polynomials do look like proper [polynomials](polynomial.md) over [commutative ring](commutative-ring.md).

In particular, [Hilbert's tenth problem](hilbert-s-tenth-problem.md) is about [polynomials](polynomial.md) over the [integers](integer.md), which is a [commutative ring](commutative-ring.md), and therefore brings mindshare to this definition.
