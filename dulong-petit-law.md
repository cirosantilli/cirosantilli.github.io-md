# Dulong-Petit law

↑ **Parent:** [Einstein solid](einstein-solid.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dulong-Petit_law)

Observation that all solids appear to have the same constant heat capacity per [mole](mole-unit.md).

It can be seen as the limit case of an [Einstein solid](einstein-solid.md) at high temperatures. At lower temperatures, the heat capacity depends on temperature.
