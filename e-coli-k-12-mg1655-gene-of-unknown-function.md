# E. Coli K-12 MG1655 gene of unknown function

↑ **Parent:** [E. Coli K-12 MG1655](e-coli-k-12-mg1655.md)

All [gene](gene.md) names that start with an [Y](y.md) such as:
- [e. Coli K-12 MG1655 gene ytdX](e-coli-k-12-mg1655-gene-ytdx.md)
- [e. Coli K-12 MG1655 gene yaaX](e-coli-k-12-mg1655-gene-yaax.md)
appear to be [proteins of unknown function](protein-of-unknown-function.md).

[UniProt](uniprot.md) for example describes YaaX as "Uncharacterized protein YaaX".

As function is discovered, they then change it to a better name, e.g. to names such as the [E. Coli K-12 MG1655 transcription unit thrLABC](e-coli-k-12-mg1655-transcription-unit-thrlabc.md) proteins all of which have a clear name due to [threonine](threonine.md).

There are many other `y???` as of 2021! Though they do tend to be smaller [molecules](molecule.md).
