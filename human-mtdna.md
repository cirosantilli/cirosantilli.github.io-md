# Human mtDNA

↑ **Parent:** [Human genome](human-genome.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Human mitochondrion](human-mitochondrion.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_mitochondrial_genetics)

This was the first large part of the genome that was sequenced, in 1981: [Cambridge Reference Sequence](cambridge-reference-sequence.md). Presumably they picked it because it is short and does not undergo [crossover](chromosomal-crossover.md).

About 16.6 [kbp](base-pair.md):
- 13 coding genes
- 24 [non-coding genes](non-coding-dna.md)
