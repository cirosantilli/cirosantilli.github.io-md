[Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) also things of those people as being part of his [108 Stars of Destiny](108-stars-of-destiny.md) troupe.

Ciro sometimes ponders why is it so hard to find people online that you truly love and admire. Maybe it is for similar reasons why it is also hard in the real world: the great variety of human interest, and the great limitation of our attention spans. But online, where we have access to "everyone", shouldn't it should be easier? Not naturally finding such people is perhaps [one of the greatest failings of our education system](how-to-teach/group-students-by-interest-not-by-age.md).
