It is interesting to note how one talks about [single cell analysis](single-cell-analysis.md), in contrast to [whole cell simulation](whole-cell-simulation.md): experimentally it is hard to analyse a single cell. But theoretically, it is hard to simulate a single cell. This mismatch is perhaps the ultimate frontier of [molecular biology](molecular-biology-split.md).
