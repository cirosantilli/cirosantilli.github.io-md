# Microwave source

↑ **Parent:** [Microwave](microwave.md)

Microwave only found applications into the 1940s and 1950s, much later than radio, because good enough sources were harder to develop.

One notable development was the [cavity magnetron](cavity-magnetron.md) in 1940, which was the basis for the original radar systems of [World War II](world-war-ii.md).
