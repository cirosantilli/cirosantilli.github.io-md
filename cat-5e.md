# Cat 5e

↑ **Parent:** [Category 5 cable](category-5-cable.md)

This is the most common home "ethernet cable" as of 2024. It is essentially ubiquitous. According to the existing [Ethernet physical layer](ethernet-physical-layer.md), the maximum speed supported is 2.5 Gbit/s.
