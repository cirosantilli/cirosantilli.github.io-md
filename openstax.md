# OpenStax

↑ **Parent:** [List of e-learning websites](list-of-e-learning-websites.md)

- [https://openstax.org/](https://openstax.org/)
- [https://cnx.org/](https://cnx.org/)

These people have good intentions.

The problem is that they don't manage to go critical because there's to way for students to create content, everything is manually curated.

You can't even publicly comment on the textbooks. Or at least [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) hasn't found a way to do so. There is just a "submit suggestion" box.

This massive lost opportunity is even shown graphically at: [https://cnx.org/about](https://cnx.org/about) ([archive](https://web.archive.org/web/20201127013553/https://cnx.org/about)) where there is a clear separation between:
- "authors", who can create content
- "students", who can consume content
Maybe this wasn't the case in their legacy website, [https://legacy.cnx.org/content?legacy=true](https://legacy.cnx.org/content?legacy=true), but not sure, and they are retiring that now.

Thus, [OurBigBook.com](ourbigbook-com-split.md). License: [CC BY](cc-by.md)! So we could re-use their stuff!
