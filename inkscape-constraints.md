# Inkscape constraints

↑ **Parent:** [Inkscape HOWTO](inkscape-howto.md)

What would be really amazing is if they had constraints like proper [CAD](computer-aided-design.md) software, it would make it possible to not have to redo entire diagrams when you want to change a small part of them.
