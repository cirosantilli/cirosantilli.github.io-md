# Nuclear magnetic resonance spectroscopy

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear magnetic resonance](nuclear-magnetic-resonance.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_magnetic_resonance_spectroscopy)

Used to identify [organic compounds](organic-compound.md).

Seems to be based on the effects that electrons around the nuclei (shielding electrons) have on the outcome of [NMR](nuclear-magnetic-resonance.md).

So it is a bit unlike [MRI](magnetic-resonance-imaging.md) where you are interested in the position of certain nuclei in space (of course, these being atoms, you can't see their positions in space).
