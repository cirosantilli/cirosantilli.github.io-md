# Classification (mathematics)

↑ **Parent:** [The beauty of mathematics](the-beauty-of-mathematics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classification_(mathematics))

In [mathematics](mathematics-split.md), a "classification" means making a list of all possible objects of a given type.

Classification results are some of [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md)'s favorite: [Section "The beauty of mathematics"](the-beauty-of-mathematics.md).

Examples:
- [classification of finite simple groups](classification-of-finite-simple-groups.md)
- [classification of regular polytopes](classification-of-regular-polytopes.md)
- [classification of closed surfaces](classification-of-closed-surfaces.md), and more generalized [generalized Poincaré conjectures](generalized-poincare-conjecture.md)
- [classification of associative real division algebras](frobenius-theorem-real-division-algebras.md)
- [classification of finite fields](classification-of-finite-fields.md)
- [classification of simple Lie groups](classification-of-simple-lie-groups.md)
- classification of the [wallpaper groups](wallpaper-group.md) and the [space groups](space-group.md)
