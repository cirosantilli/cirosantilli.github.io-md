# The best popular Brazilian music (MPB) of all time

↑ **Parent:** [Brazilian pop](brazilian-pop.md)

It is such a huge shame that you have to understand [Portuguese](portuguese-language.md) to appreciate those songs... this is yet another great evil outcome of [having more than one natural language is bad for the world](having-more-than-one-natural-language-is-bad-for-the-world.md).

The good songs stopped before of just after [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) was born, they were originally heard by his parent's generation. Those young new kids are boring.

The place to start is definitely [the Holy Trinity of popular Brazilian music](the-holy-trinity-of-popular-brazilian-music.md):
- [Caetano Veloso](caetano-veloso.md) is arguably [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md)'s favorite MPB artist, he has just too many amazing songs, best ones at: [Section "The best Caetano Veloso songs"](the-best-caetano-veloso-songs.md)
- [Chico Buarque](chico-buarque.md). Ciro's second favorite.
- [Gilberto Gil](gilberto-gil.md). Perhaps Ciro likes him third because he is the most lighthearted one, although not always: [Section "The best Gilberto Gil songs"](the-best-gilberto-gil-songs.md)
