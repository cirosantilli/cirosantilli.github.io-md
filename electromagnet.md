# Electromagnet

↑ **Parent:** [Magnet](magnet.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electromagnet)

[Electromagnets](electromagnet.md) allow us to create controllable [magnetic fields](magnetic-field.md), i.e.: they act as [magnets](magnet.md) that we can turn on and off as we please but controlling an input [voltage](voltage.md).

Compare them to [permanent magnet](permanent-magnet.md): on a magnet, you always have a fixed generated magnetic field. But with an [electromagnet](electromagnet.md) you can control the field, and even turn it off entirely.

This type of "useful looking thing that can be controlled by a voltage" tends to be of huge importance in [electrical engineering](electrical-engineer.md), the [transistor](transistor.md) being another example.
