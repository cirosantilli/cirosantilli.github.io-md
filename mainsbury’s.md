# Mainsbury’s

↑ **Parent:** [University of Cambridge slang](university-of-cambridge-slang.md)

The relatively large Sainsbury's located at 42-45 Sidney St, Cambridge CB2 3HX. Existed in late 2010's and early 2020's [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) witnessed.

Being the only relatively large grocery shop in central Cambridge near several [colleges](college-of-the-university-of-cambridge.md), where manu students live, makes this one of the most popular grocery shopping location for many of the students.

The [Oxford](university-of-oxford-split.md) equivalent has to be the Tesco Express on Magdalen St, Oxford OX1 3AD, but not sure if there's a name for it.
