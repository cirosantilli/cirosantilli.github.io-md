A weapons-grade ring of electrorefined plutonium, typical of the rings refined at Los Alamos and sent to Rocky Flats for fabrication**. [Source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Plutonium_ring.jpg). The ring has a purity of 99.96%, weighs 5.3 kg, and is approx 11 cm in diameter. It is enough plutonium for one bomb core. Which city shall we blow up today?

[Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) is mildly obsessed by nuclear reactions, because they are so [quirky](ciro-santilli-s-self-perceived-creative-personality.md). How can a little ball destroy a city? How can putting too much of it together produce criticality and kill people like in the [Slotin accident](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demon_core) or the [Tokaimura criticality accident](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZWomuWd7-to). It is [mind blowing](mind-blown.md) really.
