# Spark-gap transmitter

↑ **Parent:** [Transmitter](transmitter.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spark-gap_transmitter)

The first type of device that allowed sending [Morse code](morse-code.md) without wires, as opposed to the wired [electrical telegraph](electrical-telegraph.md) that previously existed.

Naval communications was one of the first major applications, as you can't have wires on boats!

Wireless voice transmission came about with [modulation](modulation.md).
