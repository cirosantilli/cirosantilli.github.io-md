# The Bibites

↑ **Parent:** [Software-based artificial life](software-based-artificial-life.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [AI training game](ai-game.md), [Closed source software](closed-source-software.md), [Unity-based software](unity-based-software.md)

Unknown real developer name, claims to be from [Canada](canada.md) on YouTube channel about: [https://www.youtube.com/@TheBibitesDigitalLife/about](https://www.youtube.com/@TheBibitesDigitalLife/about), likely because he's a software developer and wants to keep his employer's claws away from his side project.

Appears to be [closed source](closed-source-software.md) unfortunately, so not suitable for research.

[Video 5. "What will happen after 100h of evolution? by The Bibites (2022)"](#video-what-will-happen-after-100h-of-evolution-by-the-bibites-2022) mentions it was started five years ago, so circa 2017.

Appears to be [Unity](unity-game-engine.md)-based, if you download and extract for [Linux](linux.md) you get files named `UnityPlayer.so`.

Author is named Leo Caussan in game credits at startup: [https://www.linkedin.com/in/l%C3%A9o-caussan-560350136/](https://www.linkedin.com/in/l%C3%A9o-caussan-560350136/), a [Canadian](canada.md) [software engineer](software-engineer.md).

Was not very [Linux](linux.md) compatible: [https://www.reddit.com/r/TheBibites/comments/vqk6ac/program_stalls_at_a_blue_screen/](https://www.reddit.com/r/TheBibites/comments/vqk6ac/program_stalls_at_a_blue_screen/) Trying to run 0.5.0 leads to a blank screen after you click "start simulation".
