# Art

↑ **Parent:** [Ciro Santilli's Homepage](split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Art)

Stuff that is beautiful but useless because it does not make food or houses cheaper.

Or from [Ciro Santilli's best random thoughts](quote-by-ciro-santilli.md):

> Without technology, one cannot survive. Without art, one cannot live.

But that sure enough has a [Jesus](jesus.md) semi-precursor, and likely many others: [man shall not live by bread alone](man-shall-not-live-by-bread-alone.md).

There is some art however that lives in the fine intersection between beauty and usefulness:
- [mathematics](mathematics-split.md)
- [physics](physics-split.md)
- [computer](computer-split.md)
