# Every Lie algebra has a unique single corresponding simply connected Lie group

↑ **Parent:** [Two different Lie groups can have the same Lie algebra](two-different-lie-groups-can-have-the-same-lie-algebra.md)

This [simply connected](simply-connected-space.md) is called the [universal covering group](universal-covering-group.md).

E.g. in the case of [$SO(3)$](3d-rotation-group.md) and [$SU(2)$](special-unitary-of-degree-2.md), [$SU(2)$](special-unitary-of-degree-2.md) is [simply connected](simply-connected-space.md), but [$SO(3)$](3d-rotation-group.md) is not.
