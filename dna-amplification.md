# DNA amplification

↑ **Parent:** [DNA](dna-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DNA_amplification)

DNA amplification is one of the key DNA technologies:
- it is one of the main ways in which [DNA detection](dna-detection.md) can be done.
- it is the first step of [Illumina sequencing](illumina.md), since you need multiple copies of several parts of the genome for the method to work
