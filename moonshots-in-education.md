# Moonshots in Education

↑ **Parent:** [Innovative school](innovative-school.md)

[https://www.moonshots.org/](https://www.moonshots.org/)

By [Esther Wojcicki](esther-wojcicki.md), [Sergey Brin](sergey-brin.md)'s ex-mother-in-law:

> A moonshot classroom is a fundamental shift to give students more autonomy and agency in the classroom and entrusts them with greater ownership of their learning outcomes. 

So basically strong focus on [self-directed learning](self-directed-learning.md).

Also:

> Real World Work. Students must produce learning projects with real world applications and an authentic audience.

so along the lines of [project-based learning](project-based-learning.md). [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) specifically likes the "authentic audience" part, related: [Section "Projects must aim for novelty"](how-to-teach/projects-must-aim-for-novelty.md)
