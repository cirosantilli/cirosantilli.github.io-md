# Hydrogen 1-2 spectral line

↑ **Parent:** [Gross hydrogen emission spectrum](gross-hydrogen-emission-spectrum.md)

[Equation 2. "Hydrogen spectral series mnemonic"](gross-hydrogen-emission-spectrum.md#equation-hydrogen-spectral-series-mnemonic) gives for example from [principal quantum number](principal-quantum-number.md) 1 to 2 a difference:

$$
E_n = -13.6eV\left[\frac{1}{2^2} - \frac{1}{1^2}\right] = 10.2 eV
$$

which with [Planck-Einstein relation](planck-einstein-relation.md) gives about 121.6 [nm](nanometer.md) ($2.47 \times 10^15$ [Hz](hertz.md)), which is a reasonable match with the value of 121.567... from the [NIST Atomic Spectra Database](nist-atomic-spectra-database.md).
