# Mass cytometry

↑ **Parent:** [Flow cytometry](flow-cytometry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mass_cytometry)

You label cells with [isotopes](isotope.md) rather than [fluorescent](fluorescence.md) substances. Vendors claim that this allows much wider N-way sorts, e.g. 2022 Fluidigm claims around 40/50, because the fluorecent spectrum is too wide to do much more than 7/8 way splits.
