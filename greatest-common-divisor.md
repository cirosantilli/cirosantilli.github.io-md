# Greatest common divisor

↑ **Parent:** [Prime number](prime-number.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greatest_common_divisor)

The "greatest common divisor" of two [integers](integer.md) $x$ and $y$, denoted $\gdc(x, y)$ is the largest [natural number](natural-number.md) that divides both of the integers.

For example, $\gdc(8, 12)$ is 4, because:
- 4 divides both 8 and 12
- and this is not the case for any number larger than 4. E.g.:
  - 5 divides neither one
  - 6 divides 12
  - 7 divides neither
  - 8 divides only 8

  and so on.
