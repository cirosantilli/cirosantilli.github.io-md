# Wave-particle duality

↑ **Parent:** [Light](light.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wave-particle_duality)

The history of light if funny.

First people thought it was a particle, as per [corpuscular theory of light](corpuscular-theory-of-light.md), notably [Newton supported the corpuscular theory of light](newton-supported-the-corpuscular-theory-of-light.md).

But then evidence of the [diffraction](diffraction.md) of light start to become unbearably strong, culminating in the [Arago spot](arago-spot.md).

And finally it was understood from [Maxwell's equations](maxwell-s-equations.md) that light is a form of [electromagnetic radiation](electromagnetic-radiation.md), as its speed was perfectly predicted by the theory.

But then evidence of particle nature started to surface once again with the [photoelectric effect](photoelectric-effect.md). Physicists must have been driven mad by all these changes.
