<h1 id="ciro-santilli-s-biography">Ciro Santilli's biography</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md)

Maybe [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) should do something useful and remarkable so that someone might actually want to read his biography in the first place. But hey, [procrastination](effortless-effort.md).

[Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) was born in [Brazil](brazil-split.md) in the small/medium city of [Rio Claro, São Paulo](rio-claro-sao-paulo.md) (~200k people in 2020) in the [State of São Paulo](sao-paulo-state.md) in 1989 [AD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anno_Domini).

When he was 3, the family moved within Rio Claro to [Av. 44 - Parque Universitario, Rio Claro - SP, 13504-360](https://maps.app.goo.gl/KKEucvyu4SnEoaQR7).

In 1995 the family moved to [Jundiaí](jundiai.md) ([Rua José Milani, CEP 13207-691](https://maps.app.goo.gl/HoRfLUrVdsxEf3pi7)) for his father's work. He played a lot on the streets. Ciro learned to cycle there on a slight downhill. He was mildly bullied by some kids on the street. He played [NES](nintendo-entertainment-system.md) games that were far too hard for him which made him made and required his parents to call the teenage neighbors to beat. The neighbors had some DOS games which felt like mystery and magic.

In 1997 family finally moved to [Santos, São Paulo, Brazil](santos-sao-paulo-brazil.md) again for his father's work.

At the age of 10, [Ciro Santilli spent 10 months in Coventry, United Kingdom, where he greatly improved his English](ciro-santilli-s-10-month-stay-in-coventry-united-kingdom-in-the-year-2000.md).

After Coventry, Ciro's family went back to [Santos, São Paulo, Brazil, which made a deep impression on Ciro](santos-sao-paulo-brazil.md), until he [Ciro Santilli's undergrad studies at the University of São Paulo](ciro-santilli-s-undergrad-studies-at-the-university-of-sao-paulo.md) in 2007.

In 2010, as mentioned at [Section "Ciro Santilli's formal education"](ciro-santilli-s-formal-education.md), Ciro as admitted in a double degree program at the [École Polytechnique](ecole-polytechnique-split.md), France, where he stayed until 2013. Going to France was a [mind blowing](mind-blown.md), life changing event.
