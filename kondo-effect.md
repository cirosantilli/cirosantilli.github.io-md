# Kondo effect

↑ **Parent:** [Dependence of electrical resistivity on tempreature](dependence-of-electrical-resistivity-on-tempreature.md)

If you adda bit of impurities to certain materials, at low temperatures of a few [Kelvin](kelvin.md) their [resistivity](electrical-resistivity-and-conductivity.md) actually starts increasing if you go below a certain critical temperature.
