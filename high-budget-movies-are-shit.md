# High budget movies are shit

↑ **Parent:** [Ciro Santilli's film tastes](ciro-santilli-s-film-tastes.md)

Movies that are very expensive to make tend to be bad, because they have to make returns and thus appeal to a large amorphous population without any specialization, i.e. the [lowest common denominator](lowest-common-denominator.md) but in [TV Tropes](tv-tropes.md) terminology rather than [mathematics](mathematics-split.md): [https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Main/LowestCommonDenominator](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Main/LowestCommonDenominator).

See e.g.:
- [Primer (2004)](primer-2004.md)
- [The Man From Earth (2007)](the-man-from-earth-2007.md)
- [Pirates (2005)](pirates-2005.md)

Looking down the [largest flops of all time list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_biggest_box-office_bombs) didn't help much, only [Heaven's gate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heaven%27s_Gate_(film)) appears reasonable from the top 20.
