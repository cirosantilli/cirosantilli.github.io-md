Why Wikipedia sucks: [Section "Wikipedia"](ourbigbook-com/wikipedia.md).

Best languages:
- [latin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin_Wikipedia)
- [esperanto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esperanto_Wikipedia). Other constructed languages: [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:List_of_constructed_languages_with_Wikipedias](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:List_of_constructed_languages_with_Wikipedias)

The most important page of Wikipedia is undoubtedly: [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Reliable_sources/Perennial_sources](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Reliable_sources/Perennial_sources) which lists the accepted and non accepted sources. Basically, the decision of what is true in this world.

Wikipedia is incredibly picky about copyright. E.g.: [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Deletion_of_all_fair_use_images_of_living_people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Deletion_of_all_fair_use_images_of_living_people) because "such portrait could be created". Yes, with a time machine, no problem! This does more harm than good... excessive!

Citing in Wikipedia is painful. Partly because of they have a billion different templates that you have to navigate. They should really have a system where you can easily reuse existing sources across articles! [Section "How to use a single source multiple times in a Wikipedia article?"](how-to-use-a-single-source-multiple-times-in-a-wikipedia-article.md)
