# Exascale hypothesis

↑ **Parent:** [Exascale computing](exascale-computing.md)

The "exascale hypothesis" is a name made up by [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) to refer to the hypothesis that the real-time human [brain simulation](brain-simulation.md) becomes possible at [exascale computing](exascale-computing.md).

It is a simple extrapolation from the [number of synapses in the human brain](number-of-synapses-in-the-human-brain.md) ($10^{15}$) times the number of times each neuron fires per second.
