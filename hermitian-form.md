# Hermitian form

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetric bilinear map](symmetric-bilinear-map.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hermitian_form)

The [complex number](complex-number.md) analogue of a [symmetric bilinear form](symmetric-bilinear-form.md).

The prototypical example of it is the [complex dot product](complex-dot-product.md).

Note that this form is neither strictly [symmetric](symmetric-bilinear-map.md), it satisfies:

$$
<x, y> = \overline{<y, x>}
$$

where the over bar indicates the [complex conjugate](complex-conjugate.md), nor is it linear for complex scalar multiplication on the second argument.
