# The correlation between software engineers and Buddhism

↑ **Parent:** [Software engineer stereotype](software-engineer-stereotype.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Essays by Ciro Santilli](essays-by-ciro-santilli.md)

[Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) believes that there is a positive correlation between being a [software engineer](software-engineer.md) and liking [Buddhist](buddhism.md)-like things.

Maybe it is linked to minimalism and [DRY](yet-another.md), which software engineers value so greatly.

Even Ciro had to try an unoriginal Buddhist joke intro in [one of this Stack Overflow answers](https://stackoverflow.com/questions/572897/how-does-javascript-prototype-work/23877420#23877420).

Ciro also feels that his ["minimal reproducible example" scientific language/concept learning method obsession](https://github.com/cirosantilli/linux-kernel-module-cheat/tree/e1d0a2fafbb35c9e65c1a8a0b6d46df3e9161461/userland) of breaking things into tiny sub-problems has a strong link with [Koans](koan.md).

Some notable Buddhism/programmer examples:
- [http://www.catb.org/~esr/writings/unix-koans/](http://www.catb.org/~esr/writings/unix-koans/) "The Unix Koans of Master Foo - Rootless Root (无根的根)" by the legendary [Eric Steven Raymond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eric_S._Raymond) is notable
- [http://thecodelesscode.com/](http://thecodelesscode.com/) "The Codeless Code" by anonymous Qi.
- [http://canonical.org/~kragen/tao-of-programming.html](http://canonical.org/~kragen/tao-of-programming.html)
- [https://wiki.c2.com/?MysticalProgrammingKoans](https://wiki.c2.com/?MysticalProgrammingKoans)
- [http://rubykoans.com/](http://rubykoans.com/) even [evil](evil.md) programming languages adopt them!
- [The Zen of Python](zen-of-python.md)

Another thing that points the correlation out is the existence of [https://wattsalan.github.io/](https://wattsalan.github.io/) on a `github.io` about [Alan Watts](alan-watts.md).
