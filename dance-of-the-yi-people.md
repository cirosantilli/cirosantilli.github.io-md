# Dance of the Yi People

↑ **Parent:** [Pipa piece](pipa-piece.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [The best Chinese traditional instrumental music](the-best-chinese-traditional-instrumental-music.md)

Composed by [Wang Huiran](https://ourbigbook.com/go/topic/wang-huiran) in 1960.

The [Yi people](yi-people.md) are one of the 55 [Chinese ethnic minorities](ethnic-minorities-in-china.md) officially recognized by the [Chinese government](chinese-government.md).
