# Saylor Academy

↑ **Parent:** [Educational charitable organization](educational-charitable-organization.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saylor_Academy)

This is an interesting initiative which has some similarities to [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md)'s [OurBigBook](ourbigbook.md) project.

The fatal flaw of the initiative in [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md)'s opinion is the lack of [user-generated content](https://ourbigbook.com/go/topic/user-generated-content). We will never get there without UGC and algorithms, never.

Also as of 2021, it mostly useless business courses: [https://learn.saylor.org](https://learn.saylor.org) unfortunately.

But it has several redeeming factors which Ciro Santilli aproves of:
- [exam as a service](exam-as-a-service.md)-like
- they have a [GitHub](github.md): [https://github.com/saylordotorgo](https://github.com/saylordotorgo)
