# B3 Oxford physics course

↑ **Parent:** [Year 3 of the physics course of the University of Oxford](year-3-of-the-physics-course-of-the-university-of-oxford.md)

[https://users.physics.ox.ac.uk/~lvovsky/B3/](https://users.physics.ox.ac.uk/~lvovsky/B3/) contain assorted PDFs from between 2015 and 2019

Syllabus reads:
- Multi-electron atoms: central field approximation, electron configurations, shell structure, residual electrostatic interaction, spin orbit coupling ([fine structure](fine-structure.md)).
- Spectra and energy levels: [Term symbols](term-symbol.md), [selection rules](selection-rule.md), X-ray notation, Auger transitions.
- [Hyperfine structure](hyperfine-structure.md); effects of magnetic fields on fine and hyperfine structure. Presumably [Zeeman effect](zeeman-effect.md).
- Two level system in a classical light field: Rabi oscillations and Ramsey fringes, decaying states; Einstein
- [A and B coefficients](einstein-coefficients.md); homogeneous and inhomogeneous broadening of spectral lines; rate equations.
- Optical absorption and gain: [population inversion](population-inversion.md) in [3-](three-level-laser.md) and [4-level](four-level-laser.md) systems; optical gain cross section; saturated absorption and gain.
