# Meta breaking glitch

↑ **Parent:** [Video game](video-game-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Break the meta](break-the-meta.md)

A meta breaking [glitch](glitch.md) of a [video game](video-game-split.md) is a glitch that when discovered significantly [breaks the meta](break-the-meta.md).

In non-[video game](video-game-split.md)-game, it does sometimes happen that a meta is broken as well, but these events tend to be rarer and less dramatic than meta-breaking due to [computer program](computer-program.md) glitches.

In [PvP](tea-bagging.md) games, those glitches are generally forbidden by existing rules, and quickly patched after discovered.

In [speedrunning](speedrun.md) however, they are either incorporated in the existing strategy, or may lead to the creation of a new run category for particularly significant glitches.
