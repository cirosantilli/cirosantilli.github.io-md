# Chain rule

↑ **Parent:** [Derivative](derivative.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chain_rule)

Here's an example of the chain rule. Suppose we want to calculate:

$$
\dv{}{x} e^{2x}
$$

So we have:

$$
f(x) = e^x \\
g(x) = 2x
$$

and so:

$$
f'(x) = e^x \\
g'(x) = 2
$$

Therefore the final result is:

$$
f'(g(x))g'(x) = e^{2x} 2 = 2 e ^{2x}
$$
