# Abugida

↑ **Parent:** [Syllabary](syllabary.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abugida)

Somewhat midway between a [syllabary](syllabary.md) and an [alphabet](alphabet.md): you write out consonants, and vowels are "punctuation-like-modifiers".

E.g.: the main [Hindi](hindi.md) script ([devanagari](devanagari.md)) and most other [Indian](india.md) languages.
