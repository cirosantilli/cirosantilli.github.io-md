# Breeder reactor

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear reactor](nuclear-reactor.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breeder_reactor)

A [nuclear reactor](nuclear-reactor.md) made to produce specific [isotopes](isotope.md) rather than just consume [fissile material](fissile-material.md) to produce [electrical power](https://ourbigbook.com/go/topic/electrical-power). The most notably application being to produce [Plutonium-239](plutonium-239.md) for [nuclear weapons](nuclear-weapon-split.md) from [Uranium-238](uranium-238.md) being irradiated from [Uranium-235](uranium-235.md)-created [fission](nuclear-fission.md).
