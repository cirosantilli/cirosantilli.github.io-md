# Electronic design automation

↑ **Parent:** [Semiconductor device fabrication](semiconductor-device-fabrication.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_design_automation)

A set of software programs that [compile](compiler.md) high level [register transfer level](register-transfer-level.md) languages such as [Verilog](verilog.md) into something that a [fab](semiconductor-fabrication-plant.md) can actually produce. One is reminded of a [compiler toolchain](compiler-toolchain.md) but on a lower level.

The most important steps of that include:
- [logic synthesis](logic-synthesis.md): mapping the [Verilog](verilog.md) to a [standard cell library](standard-cell-library.md)
- [place and route](place-and-route.md): mapping the synthesis output into the 2D surface of the chip
