# Quaternion

↑ **Parent:** [Cayley-Dickson construction](cayley-dickson-construction.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quaternion)

Kind of extends the [complex numbers](complex-number.md).

Some facts that make them stand out:
- one of the only three real [associative](associative-property.md) [division algebras](division-algebra.md) in addition to the [real numbers](real-number.md) and [complex numbers](complex-number.md), according to the [classification of associative real division algebras](frobenius-theorem-real-division-algebras.md)
- the simplest non-[commutative](commutative-property.md) [division algebra](division-algebra.md). Contrast for example with [complex numbers](complex-number.md) where multiplication is [commutative](commutative-property.md)
