# Hanford site

↑ **Parent:** [American nuclear weapon facility](american-nuclear-weapon-facility.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [American plutonium production](american-plutonium-production.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanford_site)

The [B Reactor](b-reactor.md) of the facility produced the [plutonium](plutonium.md) used for [Trinity](trinity-nuclear-test.md) and [Fat Man](fat-man.md), and then for many more thousand bombs during the [Cold War](cold-war.md). More precisely, this was done at 

Located in [Washington](washington-state.md), in a dry place the middle of the mountainous areas of the Western [United States](united-states-split.md), where basically no one lives. The Columbia river is however nearby, that river is quite large, and provided the water needed by their activities, notably for cooling the [nuclear reactors](nuclear-reactor.md). It is worth it having look on [Google Maps](google-maps.md) to get a feel for the region.

Unlike many other such laboratories, this one did not become a [United States Department of Energy national laboratories](united-states-department-of-energy-national-laboratory.md). It was likely just too polluted.
