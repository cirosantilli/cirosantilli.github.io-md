# Bitcoin halving

↑ **Parent:** [Bitcoin mining reward](bitcoin-mining-reward.md)

[https://cointelegraph.com/learn/bitcoin-halving-how-does-the-halving-cycle-work-and-why-does-it-matter](https://cointelegraph.com/learn/bitcoin-halving-how-does-the-halving-cycle-work-and-why-does-it-matter) Happens every 210,000 blocks, aiming approximately at 4 year intervals. The historical dates were:
- 50 [BTC](bitcoin.md) initially
- 1st: 2012: down to 25 [BTC](bitcoin.md)
- 2nd: 2016: down to 12.5 [BTC](bitcoin.md)
- 3rd: 2020: down to 6.25 [BTC](bitcoin.md)

Each of these events prompts some commemorative [inscriptions](inscription-blockchain.md): [Section "Halving messages"](cool-data-embedded-in-the-bitcoin-blockchain/halving-messages.md).
