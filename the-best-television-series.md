# The best television series

↑ **Parent:** [Good television series](good-television-series.md)

The BBC 1979-1982 adaptations of [John Le Carré](john-le-carre.md)'s novels are the best miniseries ever made:
- [Tinker Tailor Soldier Spy](tinker-tailor-soldier-spy-tv-series.md)
- [Smiley's People](smiley-s-people-tv-series.md)
They are the most realistic depiction of [spycraft](spycraft.md) ever made.

Some honorable mentions:
- [Futurama](futurama.md)
  - S02E15 [The Problem With Popplers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Problem_with_Popplers), see also: [animal rights](animal-rights.md). There has to be prior art on this idea, there has to, can someone please point it out?
  - [S06E09 A Clockwork Origin](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q-RUHhCzgxI)
- [Rick and Morty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rick_and_Morty) before it turned to [shit](feces.md) on season 3 had some genius moments:
  - S02E04 Total Rickall
  - [Rick and Morty A Life Well Lived](rick-and-morty-a-life-well-lived.md)
