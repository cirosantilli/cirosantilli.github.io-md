# Magnetic hysteresis

↑ **Parent:** [Ferromagnetism](ferromagnetism.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnetic_hysteresis)

To understand the graph, first learn/remember the difference between the [magnetic B and H field](magnetic-b-and-h-field.md).

The interest of the [magnetic hysteresis](magnetic-hysteresis.md) graph is that it serves as an important characterization of a :
- its area gives you the hysteresis loss of the [transformer](transformer.md), which is a major cause of efficiency loss of the component
- some key points of the curve give important characterizations of the core/material:
  - [Saturation magnetisation](saturation-magnetisation.md)
  - magnetization strength without field
  - how much field you need to demagnetize it
This curve will also tell you how many turns of the coil will be needed to reach the required field.
