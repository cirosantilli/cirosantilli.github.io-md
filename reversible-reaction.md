# Reversible reaction

↑ **Parent:** [Gibbs free energy](gibbs-free-energy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reversible_reaction)

I think these are the ones where $\Delta H \times \Delta S > 0$, i.e. [enthalpy](enthalpy.md) and [entropy](entropy.md) push the reaction in different directions. And so we can use temperature to move the [Chemical equilibrium](chemical-equilibrium.md) back and forward.
