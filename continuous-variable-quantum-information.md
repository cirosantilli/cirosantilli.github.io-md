It is also possible to carry out [quantum computing](quantum-computing-split.md) without [qubits](qubit.md) using processes with a [continuous spectrum](continuous-spectrum-functional-analysis.md) of measurement.

As of 2020, these approaches seem less developed/promising, but who knows.

These computers can be seen as analogous to classical non-quantum [analog computers](analog-computer.md).
