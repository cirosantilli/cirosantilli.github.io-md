# We are 256, We are 1

↑ **Parent:** [Ordinal ASCII art inscription](ordinal-ascii-art-inscription.md)

<a id="_893"></a>
"WE ARE 256, WE ARE 1" is an invitation to a [Discord](../discord-software.md)-based puzzle game with the promise of a money prize of unspecified value in Round 10.
