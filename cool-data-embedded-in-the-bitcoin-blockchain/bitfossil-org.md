<h1 id="bitfossil-org">bitfossil.org</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [AtomSea & EMBII](atomsea-and-embii.md)

<a id="_486"></a>
[https://bitfossil.org/root/](https://bitfossil.org/root/) is an indexer website created by [EMBII](../embii.md) for the [AtomSea & EMBII](atomsea-and-embii.md) inscription format.

<a id="_487"></a>
There was also a semi-mirror at [https://bitfossil.org/root/](https://bitfossil.org/root/), though they were not always in perfect sync for whatever reason.

<a id="_488"></a>
The website shut down by [EMBII](../embii.md) on January 2025 for an undisclosed reason. He mentioned however that after the shutdown he started to like the idea of keeping it down forever due the ideology of not having official centralized services linked to his protocol.[https://x.com/EMBII4U/status/1884792993921601843](https://x.com/EMBII4U/status/1884792993921601843)[https://x.com/EMBII4U/status/1888554371815821634](https://x.com/EMBII4U/status/1888554371815821634)[https://x.com/EMBII4U/status/1888692105180311565](https://x.com/EMBII4U/status/1888692105180311565)

<a id="_489"></a>
Each page has an "abuse report" button to unindex presumably.
