# ImageNet subset

↑ **Parent:** [ImageNet](imagenet.md)

Subset generators:
- [https://github.com/mf1024/ImageNet-datasets-downloader](https://github.com/mf1024/ImageNet-datasets-downloader) generates on download, very good. As per [https://github.com/mf1024/ImageNet-Datasets-Downloader/issues/14](https://github.com/mf1024/ImageNet-Datasets-Downloader/issues/14) counts go over the limit due to bad multithreading. Also unfortunately it does not start with a subset of 1k.
- [https://github.com/BenediktAlkin/ImageNetSubsetGenerator](https://github.com/BenediktAlkin/ImageNetSubsetGenerator)

Unfortunately, since [ImageNet](imagenet.md) is a [closed standard](closed-standard.md) no one can upload such pre-made subsets, forcing everybody to download the full dataset, in [ImageNet1k](imagenet-large-scale-visual-recognition-challenge-dataset.md), which is huge!
