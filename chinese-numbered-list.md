# Chinese numbered list

↑ **Parent:** [Chinese culture](chinese-culture.md)

For some reason Chinese people (and their sphere of influence such as Japan) are obsessed by numbered lists, e.g. stuff like [Four Beauties](four-beauties.md), [Four Treasures of the Study](four-treasures-of-the-study.md) and so on!

The concept does exist in the West (e.g. The Seven Wonders of the World), but clearly the Sinosphere is much more obsesse by it!
