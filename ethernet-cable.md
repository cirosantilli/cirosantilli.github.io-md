# Ethernet cable

↑ **Parent:** [Ethernet physical layer](ethernet-physical-layer.md)

When non-specialists say "Ethernet cable", they usually mean [twisted pair](twisted-pair.md) for [Ethernet over twisted pair](ethernet-over-twisted-pair.md).

But of course, this term is much more generic to a more specialized person, since notably [fiber optics](optical-fiber.md) are also extensively used in [Ethernet over fiber](ethernet-over-fiber.md).
