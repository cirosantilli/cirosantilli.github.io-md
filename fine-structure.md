# Fine structure

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral line](spectral-line.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fine_structure)

Split in energy levels due to interaction between electron up or down [spin](spin-physics.md) and the electron orbitals.

Numerically explained by the [Dirac equation](dirac-equation.md) when [solving it for the hydrogen atom](dirac-equation-solution-for-the-hydrogen-atom.md), and it is one of the main triumphs of the theory.
