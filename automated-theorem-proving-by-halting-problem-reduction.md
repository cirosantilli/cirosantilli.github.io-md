# Automated theorem proving by halting problem reduction

↑ **Parent:** [Busy beaver scale](busy-beaver-scale.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Automated theorem proving](automated-theorem-proving.md)

If you can reduce a mathematical problem to the [Halting problem](halting-problem.md) of a specific turing machine, as in the case of a few machines of the [Busy beaver scale](busy-beaver-scale.md), then using [Turing machine deciders](turing-machine-decider.md) could serve as a method of [automated theorem proving](automated-theorem-proving.md).

That feels like it could be an elegant proof method, as you reduce your problem to one of the most well studied representations that exists: a [Turing machine](turing-machine.md).

However it also appears that certain problems cannot be reduced to a [halting problem](halting-problem.md)... OMG life sucks (or is awesome?): [Section "Turing machine that halts if and only if Collatz conjecture is false"](turing-machine-that-halts-if-and-only-if-collatz-conjecture-is-false.md).
