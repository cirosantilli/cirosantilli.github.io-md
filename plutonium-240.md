# Plutonium-240

↑ **Parent:** [Plutonium isotope](plutonium-isotope.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plutonium-240)

This [isotope](isotope.md) shows up as an inevitable contaminant in [Plutonium-239](plutonium-239.md) for [nuclear weapons](nuclear-weapon-split.md), because it emits [neutrons](neutron.md) too fast and makes it harder to assemble the critical mass without [fizzle](fizzle-nuclear-explosion.md).

It is the presence of this contaminant that made [implosion-type fission weapon](implosion-type-fission-weapon.md) a necessity: [Section "Gun-type fission weapons don't work with plutonium"](gun-type-fission-weapons-don-t-work-with-plutonium.md).

[Wikipedia](wikipedia.md) explains that Pu-240 is formed by Pu-239 [Neutron](neutron.md) capture:

> About 62% to 73% of the time when <sup>239</sup>Pu captures a neutron, it undergoes fission; the remainder of the time, it forms 240Pu.

so its presence is inevitable.
