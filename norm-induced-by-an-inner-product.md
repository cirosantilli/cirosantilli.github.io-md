# Norm induced by an inner product

↑ **Parent:** [Norm (mathematics)](norm-mathematics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norm_induced_by_an_inner_product)

An [inner product](inner-product.md) $x \cdot y$ induces a [norm](norm-mathematics.md) with:

$$
|x| = \sqrt{<x, x>}
$$
