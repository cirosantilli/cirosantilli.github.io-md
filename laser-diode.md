# Laser diode

↑ **Parent:** [Type of laser](type-of-laser.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laser_diode)

This is by far the most important type of [laser](laser.md) commercially, as it can be made relatively cheaply, and it doesn't break easily as it ends up being a single [crystal](crystal.md).

Compare them for example to the earlier [gas lasers](gas-laser.md).

This is the type of laser that you would get in a simple [laser pointer](laser-pointer.md).

But the real mega aplications are:
- [fiber-optic communication](fiber-optic-communication.md), where [laser diodes](laser-diode.md) are one of the most commonly used methods to generate the light that goes in the fiber. This makes [laser diodes](laser-diode.md) one of the most important inventions of the 20th centure without doubt.
- [optical storage](optical-storage.md). But as of the 2020s its usefulness was much diminished by a combination of [solid-state storage](solid-state-storage.md) + faster [Internet](internet.md) due largely to [fiber-optic communication](fiber-optic-communication.md). So it is partly a matter of [laser diodes](laser-diode.md) beating [laser diodes](laser-diode.md)!
