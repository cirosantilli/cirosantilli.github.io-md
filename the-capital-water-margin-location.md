# The Capital (Water Margin location)

↑ **Parent:** [Water Margin location](water-margin-location.md)

"The Capital" is what now is [Kaifeng](kaifeng.md) in [Shandong](shandong.md), and it used to be the capital during the (Northern) [Song dynasty](song-dynasty.md).

In the novel it is simply often simply called as 东京 (capital of the East), which is now highly ambiguous term that can indicate several different historical capitals! Notably it is the name of modern [tokyo](tokyo.md).
