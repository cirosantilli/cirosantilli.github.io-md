**[Video 34](#video-fabry-perot-interferometer-by-jfc-ucl-2016). Fabry Perot Interferometer by JFC UCL (2016)** [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HkjlfCiEqGE). Description only, reasonable animations. Considers the case of two nearby [beam splitters](beam-splitter.md).

<a id="video-fabry-perot-introduction-by-williams-college-physics-2020"></a>
**[Video 35](#video-fabry-perot-introduction-by-williams-college-physics-2020). Fabry-Perot Introduction by Williams College Physics (2020)** [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tx67rXzXYD0). Shows a working device. Confocal [optical cavity](optical-cavity.md), one of the mirrors scans back and forward moved by a [piezoelectric motor](piezoelectric-motor.md), this is called a "scanning Fabry-Perot interferometer".

Does not produce an interference pattern, only an on/off blob, which is then fed into an [oscilloscope](oscilloscope.md) for analysis. The oscilloscope shows both the mirror displacement (which is given by a voltage) and the light detector output.
