# CommonMark

↑ **Parent:** [Markdown](markdown.md)

CommonMark is a good project. But its initial release method was not very nice, they first developed everything behind closed doors with the big adopters like [GitHub](github.md) and [Stack Overflow](stack-overflow-split.md), and only later released the thing read, thus wasting the time of people who were working on alternative in the meanwhile, e.g. [https://github.com/karlcow/markdown-testsuite](https://github.com/karlcow/markdown-testsuite) which Ciro contributed to: [Ciro Santilli's minor projects](ciro-santilli-s-minor-projects.md).
