  **[Video 33](#video-alegria-alegria-by-caetano-veloso-1968). Alegria, Alegria by Caetano Veloso (1968)** [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=he_ghOAXbSM). Critique of [Military dictatorship in Brazil](military-dictatorship-in-brazil.md).

- 1972
  - [Transa (1972)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transa_(album)) album. Literally: "The Fuck", good old seventies. Caetano himself later mentions that this is one of his own favorite albums.[https://youtu.be/o96Q-vXtiEA?t=2482](https://youtu.be/o96Q-vXtiEA?t=2482) The album was composed when he was living in [London](london.md).

    <a id="video-triste-bahia-by-caetano-veloso-1972"></a>
    **[Video 34](#video-triste-bahia-by-caetano-veloso-1972). Triste Bahia by Caetano Veloso (1972)** [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GVVSimWZm0U). Inspired by (or more likely: actually is) [capoeira music](capoeira-music.md).

    <a id="video-mora-na-filosofia-by-caetano-veloso-1972"></a>
    **[Video 35](#video-mora-na-filosofia-by-caetano-veloso-1972). Mora na Filosofia by Caetano Veloso (1972)** [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u18HsCm8W7U).


  - [Caetano e Chico: Juntos e Ao Vivo (1972)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caetano_e_Chico:_Juntos_e_Ao_Vivo) album

    <a id="video-voce-nao-entende-nada-by-caetano-veloso-1970"></a>
    **[Video 36](#video-voce-nao-entende-nada-by-caetano-veloso-1970). Você Não Entende Nada by Caetano Veloso (1970)** [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F4TXkeJYxTE). Amazing performance at Coliseu dos Recreios, Lisbon. 1981. [Discogs](https://www.discogs.com/Gal-Costa-Legal-Voc%C3%AA-N%C3%A3o-Entende-Nada/release/5872459) says original album is Legal (1970), but [wiki page](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legal_(Gal_Costa_album)) and photos of back disagree...

    This song talks about a man's mixed desires to remain with his partner and also escape to adventure. It contains one of the most amazing [sexual innuendo](sexual-innuendo.md) ever recorded: the man describes foods that his partner will serve him, and the he endlessly eats and eats and eats:

    > eu como, eu como, eu como, você \[... huge intentional pause ...\] não está entendendo quase nada do que eu digo.

    translation:

    > I eat, I eat, I eat, you \[... huge intentional pause ...\] don't understand anything that I'm saying.

    which makes it unclear "you" is part of "I eat you" (to eat (comer) is a slang for [fucking](sexual-intercourse.md)), or if "you" is part of the next sentence.

    <a id="video-partido-alto-by-caetano-veloso-1972"></a>
    **[Video 37](#video-partido-alto-by-caetano-veloso-1972). Partido Alto by Caetano Veloso (1972)** [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_p854CGQ8vo). [Atheism](atheism.md) song ([theodicy](theodicy.md))! Lyrics by [Chico Buarque](chico-buarque.md), but [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) prefers this interpretation. From the 1972 "Caetano E Chico Juntos E Ao Vivo" joint album with [Chico Buarque](chico-buarque.md).
- 1975 [Qualquer coisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qualquer_Coisa) album

  <a id="video-qualquer-coisa-by-caetano-veloso-1975"></a>
  **[Video 38](#video-qualquer-coisa-by-caetano-veloso-1975). Qualquer Coisa by Caetano Veloso (1975)** [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=796yP7HX9L0).

  <a id="video-samba-e-amor-by-caetano-veloso-1975"></a>
  **[Video 39](#video-samba-e-amor-by-caetano-veloso-1975). Samba e Amor by Caetano Veloso (1975)** [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HbXembjZ-cE). Cover from original song by [Chico Buarque](chico-buarque.md), original album [Chico Buarque de Hollanda - Nº4](https://pt.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chico_Buarque_de_Hollanda_-_N%C2%BA4). Desperately reminds Ciro of his University day [weekend](weekend.md) nights. Except that there was no Samba. And little Amor. Mostly a silent and wholesome loneliness and [emulation](games-young-ciro-santilli-played.md).

  <a id="video-georde-de-capadocia-by-caetano-veloso-1975"></a>
  **[Video 40](#video-georde-de-capadocia-by-caetano-veloso-1975). Georde de Capadócia by Caetano Veloso (1975)** [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=61DddY9OlbE). Composed by Jorge Ben, but this interpretation is remarkable. [corpo fechado](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/corpo_fechado) (closed body) style song. This idea is much linked to [Capoeira](capoeira.md)/African religion idea. E.g. a more traditional capoeira corpo fechado song: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tfd9j6XFmSg](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tfd9j6XFmSg)

  <a id="video-coricos-prayer-scene-from-black-god-white-devil-1964"></a>
  **[Video 41](#video-coricos-prayer-scene-from-black-god-white-devil-1964). Coricos' prayer scene from Black God, White Devil (1964)** [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H3IH0pnwpl4). This shows an Amazing corpo fechado scene from an amazing old black an white [Brazilian](brazil-split.md) film.

- 1977 [Bicho (1977)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bicho_(album))

  <a id="video-tigresa-by-caetano-veloso-1977"></a>
  **[Video 43](#video-tigresa-by-caetano-veloso-1977). Tigresa by Caetano Veloso (1977)** [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4pRGpUkBI0k). Talks about a strong willed, unapologetic, disenchanted, but also hopeful brown skinned lover: a tigress. Ciro once knew one, but it wasn't meant to be.

- 1978 album [Muito (Dentro da Estrela Azulada)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muito_(Dentro_da_Estrela_Azulada))

  <a id="video-sampa-by-caetano-veloso-1978"></a>
  **[Video 44](#video-sampa-by-caetano-veloso-1978). Sampa by Caetano Veloso (1978)** [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nmxp4XQnBpw). "Sampa" is an affectionate slang for [São Paulo City](sao-paulo-city.md). The song perfectly captures the city, and reminds Ciro so badly of [his University days there](university-of-sao-paulo.md).

  > \[D\]a força da grana que ergue e destroi coisas belas  
  > The power of money that builds and destroy beautiful things

  <a id="video-terra-by-caetano-veloso-1978"></a>
  **[Video 45](#video-terra-by-caetano-veloso-1978). Terra by Caetano Veloso (1978)** [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WyxL_lbo4kM). "Terra" means [Earth](earth.md) in [Portuguese](portuguese-language.md).

  Ciro used to watch a television nature show called "Planeta Terra" in the legendary [TV Cultura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TV_Cultura) with his parents in the couch when he was young, and under a duvet when it was a bit cold. Those days were the best. The narrator's lady voice was particularly soothing, and would easily put you in a kind of sleepy trance, her name is [Valéria Grillo](https://pt.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valéria_Grillo)
