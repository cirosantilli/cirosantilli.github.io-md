# Adenosine triphosphate

↑ **Parent:** [Cellular respiration](cellular-respiration.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adenosine_triphosphate)

The fact that ATP is the universal energy storage mollecule of all life on Earth is such an incredible unifying principle of biology!

It is the direct output of all the major forms of "energy generation" in cells: [ATP synthesis mechanism](atp-synthesis-mechanism.md).

It is just as fundamental as the [genetic code](genetic-code.md) for example.

No wonder dozens of [Nobel Prizes](nobel-prize-split.md) were related to its discovery, given its complexity.
