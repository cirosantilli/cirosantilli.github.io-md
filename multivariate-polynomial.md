# Multivariate polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial](polynomial.md)

A [polynomial](polynomial.md) with multiple input arguments, e.g. with two inputs $x$ and $y$:

$$
f(x, y) = x^2 + 2x + y^3 + 1
$$

as opposed to a [polynomial](polynomial.md) with a single argument e.g. one with just $x$:

$$
f(x) = x^2 + 2x + 1
$$
