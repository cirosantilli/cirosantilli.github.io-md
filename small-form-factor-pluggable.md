# Small Form-factor Pluggable

↑ **Parent:** [Fiber-optic communication](fiber-optic-communication.md)

It's the thing that allows you to connect [fiber optics](optical-fiber.md) into a compter, or the corresponding port for the thing.

Many of them can take two fibers as input/output because [fiber optics cables often come in pairs because it is needed for duplex](fiber-optics-cables-often-come-in-pairs-because-it-is-needed-for-duplex.md).
