**[Video 2](#video-history-of-oxford-university-by-chris-day-2018). History of Oxford University by Chris Day (2018)** [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uol4V1Wa8B0). A large part of the video talks about how the insane system of [colleges of the University of Oxford](college-of-the-university-of-oxford.md) came about organically.
- [https://youtu.be/uol4V1Wa8B0?t=343](https://youtu.be/uol4V1Wa8B0?t=343) at the [University of Bologna](university-of-bologna.md), the original system was for students to decide what they would learn, and hire and fire teachers as they decided. This is opposed to the system of the [university of Paris](university-of-paris.md), in which teachers make the final decisions. He mentions that this is the system that the [University of Oxford](university-of-oxford-split.md) and the [University of Cambridge](university-of-cambridge.md) use: the "congregation". He mentions that [Oxbridge](oxbridge.md) are one of the few universities that maintained this structure (as opposed to having funding sources select the final decision makers)
- [https://youtu.be/uol4V1Wa8B0?t=1327](https://youtu.be/uol4V1Wa8B0?t=1327) mentions the quadrangle architecture which served as the basis of the Colleges: make a closed square with everything students need: Chapel, Hall to eat, classes and accommodation. This is based of course on monastic cloisters.
