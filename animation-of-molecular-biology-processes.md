# Animation of molecular biology processes

↑ **Parent:** [Physics and the illusion of life](physics-and-the-illusion-of-life.md)

Nothing makes the fact that [your life is an illusion](physics-and-the-illusion-of-life.md) clearer than [animations of molecular biology processes](animation-of-molecular-biology-processes.md). You just have no idea what is going on inside your own body right now!

And don't get [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) started on [the brain](brain-split.md) and the impossibility of [free will](free-will.md).

And yet, we live, oblivious to all of it.
