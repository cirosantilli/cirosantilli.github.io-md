# Feynman diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum electrodynamics](quantum-electrodynamics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feynman_diagram)

I think they are a tool to calculate the probability of different types of particle decays and particle collision outcomes. TODO Minimal example of that.

And they can be derived from a more complete [quantum electrodynamics](quantum-electrodynamics.md) formulation via [perturbation theory](perturbation-theory.md).

Can be used for all of [quantum electrodynamics](quantum-electrodynamics.md), [weak interaction](weak-interaction.md) and [quantum chromodynamics](quantum-chromodynamics.md).

At [Richard Feynman Quantum Electrodynamics Lecture at University of Auckland (1979)](richard-feynman-quantum-electrodynamics-lecture-at-university-of-auckland-1979.md), an intuitive explanation of them in termes of sum of products of [propagators](propagator.md) is given.
