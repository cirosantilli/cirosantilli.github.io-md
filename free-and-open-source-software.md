# Free and open-source software

↑ **Parent:** [Open source software](open-source-software.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_and_open-source_software)

A more precise term for those in the know: [open source software](open-source-software.md) that also has a liberal license, for some definition of liberal.

[Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) defines liberal as: "can be commercialized without paying anything back" (but possibly subject to other restrictions).

He therefore does not consider [Creative Commons licenses](creative-commons-license.md) with NC to be FOSS.

For the newbs, the term [open source software](open-source-software.md) is good enough, since most open source software is also FOSS.

But when it's not, it's crucial to know.
