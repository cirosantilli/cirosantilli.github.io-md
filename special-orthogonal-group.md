# Special orthogonal group

↑ **Parent:** [Orthogonal group](orthogonal-group.md)

Group of rotations of a rigid body.

Like [orthogonal group](orthogonal-group.md) but without reflections. So it is a "special case" of the orthogonal group.

This is a subgroup of both the [orthogonal group](orthogonal-group.md) and the [special linear group](special-linear-group.md).
