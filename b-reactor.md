# B Reactor

↑ **Parent:** [Hanford site](hanford-site.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Breeder reactor](breeder-reactor.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B_Reactor)

Reactor of the [Hanford site](hanford-site.md) of the produced the [plutonium](plutonium.md) used for [Trinity](trinity-nuclear-test.md) and [Fat Man](fat-man.md).

This was the first full scale [nuclear reactor](nuclear-reactor.md) in the world, and was brought up slowly to test it out.
