# Causality and quantum jumps are incompatible

↑ **Parent:** [Causality in quantum mechanics](causality-in-quantum-mechanics.md)

If something does a [quantum jump](quantum-jump.md), what causes it to decide doing so at a particular time and not another? It is expected that a continuous cause would have continuous effects.

This concern was raised immediately by [Rutherford](ernest-rutherford.md) while reviewing the [Bohr model](bohr-model.md) in 1913 as mentioned in [The Quantum Story by Jim Baggott (2011)](the-quantum-story-by-jim-baggott-2011.md) page 32.
