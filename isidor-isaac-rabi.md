# Isidor Isaac Rabi

↑ **Parent:** [Physicist](physicist-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [1944 Nobel Prize in Physics](1944-nobel-prize-in-physics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isidor_Isaac_Rabi)

He was a leading figure at the [MIT Radiation Laboratory](mit-radiation-laboratory.md), and later he was head at the [Columbia University](columbia-university.md) laboratory that carried out the crucial [Lamb-Retherford experiment](lamb-retherford-experiment.md) and the [anomalous magnetic dipole moment of the electron](anomalous-magnetic-dipole-moment-of-the-electron.md) published at [The Magnetic Moment of the Electron by Kusch and Foley (1948)](the-magnetic-moment-of-the-electron-by-kusch-and-foley-1948.md) using related techniques.
