But one thing you should really know, as often mentioned in [Power, Sex, Suicide by Nick Lane (2006)](power-sex-suicide-by-nick-lane-2006.md): they are all [eukaryotes](eukaryote.md).

Because [prokaryotes](prokaryote.md) are fundamentally unable to do [phagocytosis](phagocytosis.md), because they have a rigid [cell wall](cell-wall.md). Changing cell shape at will requires a [cytoskeleton](cytoskeleton.md).
