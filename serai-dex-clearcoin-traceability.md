# Serai DEX clearcoin traceability

↑ **Parent:** [Serai DEX](serai-dex.md)

Can anyone know that clearcoins came from [Serai DEX](serai-dex.md)? Because if they can, exchanges could just blacklist anything coming from [Serai DEX](serai-dex.md).

[Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) asked at: [https://x.com/cirosantilli/status/1855332323405009047](https://x.com/cirosantilli/status/1855332323405009047). They replied, and the answer is yes, it is possible to know that clearcois came from Serai: [https://x.com/SeraiDEX/status/1855337686208516523](https://x.com/SeraiDEX/status/1855337686208516523):

> Serai is fully auditable. With that is full transparency into all outputs received, and all outputs sent
> 
> Removing auditability would massively incrase complexity and force users into needing to make fraud proofs if they didn't receive coins expected, or require extreme ZK proofs
