# Direct product

↑ **Parent:** [Set function](set-function.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Direct_product)

A [Cartesian product](cartesian-product.md) that carries over some extra structure of the input groups.

E.g. the [direct product of groups](direct-product-of-groups.md) carries over [group](group-split.md) structure on both sides.
