# Planck constant

↑ **Parent:** [Planck-Einstein relation](planck-einstein-relation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planck_constant)

Proportionality factor in the [Planck-Einstein relation](planck-einstein-relation.md) between light energy and frequency.

And analogously for matter, appears in the [de Broglie relations](de-broglie-relations.md) relating momentum and frequency. Also appears in the [Schrödinger equation](schrodinger-equation.md), basically as a consequence/cause of the de Broglie relations most likely.

Intuitively, the [Planck constant](planck-constant.md) determines at what length scale do quantum effects start to show up for a given energy scale. It is because the Plank constant is very small that we don't perceive quantum effects on everyday energy/length/time scales. On the $\lim_{h \to 0}$, quantum mechanics disappears entirely.

A very direct way of thinking about it is to think about what would happen in a [double-slit experiment](double-slit-experiment.md). TODO think more clearly what happens there.

Defined exactly in the [2019 redefinition of the SI base units](2019-redefinition-of-the-si-base-units.md) to:

$$
6.62607015 \times 10^{-34} J \cdot s
$$
