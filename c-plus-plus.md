<h1 id="c-plus-plus">C++</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [List of programming languages](list-of-programming-languages.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C++)

C plus plus is what you get when you want to have all of:
- ability to write DRY code, which is e.g. impossible in the [C](c-programming-language.md)
- low level control, notably not having [garbage collection](garbage-collection-computer-science.md), as possible in the [C](c-programming-language.md)
- somewhat backwards compatibility with [C](c-programming-language.md)
