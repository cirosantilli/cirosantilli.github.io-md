# Hyperfine structure

↑ **Parent:** [Fine structure](fine-structure.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperfine_structure)

Small splits present in all levels due to interaction between the electron spin and the [nuclear spin](nuclear-magnetic-moment.md) if it is present, i.e. the nucleus has an even number of nucleons.

As the name suggests, this energy split is very small, since the influence of the nucleus spin on the electron spin is relatively small compared to other [fine structure](fine-structure.md).
