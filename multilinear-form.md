# Multilinear form

↑ **Parent:** [Multilinear map](multilinear-map.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multilinear_form)

See [form](form-mathematics.md).

Analogous to a [linear form](linear-form.md), a multilinear form is a [Multilinear map](multilinear-map.md) where the [image](image-mathematics.md) is the [underlying field of the vector space](underlying-field-of-a-vector-space.md), e.g. $\R^{n_1} \times \R^{n_2} \times \R^{n_2} \to \R$.
