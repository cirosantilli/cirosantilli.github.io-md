# Timberland Larchmont Chukka

↑ **Parent:** [Shoes](shoes.md)

<a id="_612"></a>
Bought 2021-11, light brown, size EUR 44.5, 190$, waterproof: [https://www.timberland.co.uk/shop/en/tbl-uk/larchmont-chukka-for-men-in-brown-a12es210](https://www.timberland.co.uk/shop/en/tbl-uk/larchmont-chukka-for-men-in-brown-a12es210)

<a id="_613"></a>
Made four blisters on back of foot on first two days after walking a few hours on them, but then put on some tape on foot, and stopped hurting after that, so the shoe broke quickly.

<a id="_614"></a>
May 2026: sole unlgued from body and water started coming in. Fuck. The pain was all for naught. I'm never buying leather shoes again. The leather itself is good, but it has to last forever to be worth the pain.
