# Vauxhall Mokka

↑ **Parent:** [Car](car.md)

Rented it for one week in 2026.

Compared to my Golf mk6:

Bad:
- trunk is ridiculously tiny, could not fit one suitcase and the child buggy which easily fit into the Golf. God.
- visibility is worse, small window on back, rear mirror blocks front view
- too much digital shit. At one point started showing speed in kmh instead of mph and I had to stop to fix it. Trunk opening very annoying.

Good:
- steering is even more assisted, feels like video game
- 4G in-car as a backup
- engine felt more powerful and efficient
