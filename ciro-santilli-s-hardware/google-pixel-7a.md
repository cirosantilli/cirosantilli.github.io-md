Bought: November 2023.

<a id="_206"></a>
March 2026: charger cords is not fixing in very well. I can get it with with a bit of force, but it comes out very easily. I can't see anything blocking the charger port, maybe the pin it got pushed in somehow? Happens on all USB-C cords, though it is slightly better on some than others. What a pain. Later I cleaned it with my Swiss knife's wooden pick and it worked wonderfully, there was actually some pocket lint stuck in there somehow and got it back to normal: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MtQGhSNBsnU](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MtQGhSNBsnU) Awesome? While taking of the cover however for cleaning I noticed there's a crack behind the phone under the camera. Funny how the screen is bulletproof, even stronger than the body.
