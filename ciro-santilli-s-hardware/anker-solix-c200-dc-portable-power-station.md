# Anker SOLIX C200 DC Portable Power Station

↑ **Parent:** [Mechanical and electrical tools](mechanical-and-electrical-tools.md)

[https://www.amazon.co.uk/dp/B0D62P85ZR](https://www.amazon.co.uk/dp/B0D62P85ZR)

192Wh 200W output DC Power Bank, Portable Power Station.

Can be charged at up to 140W. Uses a USB-C PD (power delivery) to charge it.

[https://www.ankersolix.com/uk/products/c200-dc?variant=49876549435728](https://www.ankersolix.com/uk/products/c200-dc?variant=49876549435728)

Manual: [https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0491/8460/4324/files/A1727_V2_EN_UM_Web_20241031.pdf?v=1730355325](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0491/8460/4324/files/A1727_V2_EN_UM_Web_20241031.pdf?v=1730355325)

Specifies: USB-C3 Input 9V 3A / 15V 3A / 20V 5A / 28V 5A (140W Max)

Should be compatible with a Mac laptop charger, though that only works up to 20.5 V at 4.7 A, i.e. 96.35 W, so it won't reach max charging speed at 28V.

28V is part of PD 3.1 Extended Power Range (EPR). PD 2.0 / 3.0 only went up to 20V.
