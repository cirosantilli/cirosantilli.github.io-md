# Water

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrogen compound](hydrogen-compound.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Chemical substance](chemical-substance.md), [Oxygen compound](oxygen-compound.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Water)

"Water" is the name for both:
- the [chemical compound](chemical-compound.md) with [chemical formula](https://ourbigbook.com/go/topic/chemical-formula) H<sub>2</sub>O
- the [liquid phase](liquid.md) of the [chemical substance](chemical-substance.md) composed of the above [chemical compound](chemical-compound.md)
