# Games young Ciro Santilli played

↑ **Parent:** [Art young Ciro Santilli consumed](art-young-ciro-santilli-consumed.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Game](game.md)

Mostly [video games](video-game-split.md) of course.

First when he was really young, about 5, Ciro played a lot of [NES](nintendo-entertainment-system.md), but he doesn't remember things from that era very well. Contra, Ninja Turtles, Battle Tanks, Duck Hunt, and some modern "real world jet" top to bottom rail shooter (TODO identify) are definitely some of the games he clearly remembers playing, see also: [Figure "Five year old Ciro Santilli playing NES on a joystick"](video-game-split.md#image-five-year-old-ciro-santilli-playing-nes-on-a-joystick). [Nintendo hard](nintendo-hard.md) was truly a thing back then.

As an honorable mention, Ciro remembers his teenage/young adult neighbours in [Jundiaí](jundiai.md) playing some [DOS](dos.md) games on their computer, notably there was a 3D racing one. This must have been around 1995/1997, so using some of the very earliest [GPUs](graphics-processing-unit.md). Those games felt so incredibly advanced, including the required setup to play them, which required some [command-line](command-line-interface.md) commands. It felt like some kind of black magic! But Ciro didn't really play them however.

Ciro then skipped the [SNES](super-nintendo-entertainment-system.md) and handhelds, which he played only through friends because he [was cheap](ciro-santilli-s-cheapness.md) (but also because Brazil is a poor country remember, and imports are pretty expensive). He clearly remembers playing [Super Mario World](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Mario_World) for the SNES and [Pokemon on friends' Gameboys](children-cartoons-ciro-santilli-liked-to-watch.md) of course.

Ciro then went straight to 5th generation with the [Nintendo 64](nintendo-64.md) in 1994 which his parents bought for him during a trip to the [United States](united-states-split.md). Once again, because he was cheap, the only game he bought was [Super Mario 64](super-mario-64.md), which likely came with the console? He played that game to death.

Then came [Ocarina of Time](the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time.md), which blew everyone's minds, and Ciro would go to Blockbuster to rent it for the weekend, and again play to death with his friends. You had to arrive early at Blockbuster to rent it, otherwise other people would rent all copies!!!

The only time Ciro got robbed as of 2020 was when an older teenager stopped his bicycle in front of Ciro and took his rented Golden Eye 64 copy away from his hand, and run off. Poor drug addict.

Ciro always felt that the PS1 had a much uglier aesthetics than the N64, and didn't like the console. Playing a bit of [Final Fantasy VI](final-fantasy-vi.md) on his memory did stick deeply to his mind however. Ciro later played all good PS1 RPGs on emulation during [University of São Paulo](university-of-sao-paulo.md) during amazing solitary nights.

And on the PC, Ciro was particularly touched by [Age of Empires II](age-of-empires-ii.md) and [Diablo II](diablo-ii.md).

As a young teenager Ciro would also play [Counter-Strike](counter-strike.md) with his friends at LAN houses. Playing that game would make Ciro extremely anxious, his hands got all cold, and it was a lot of fun.

After this Ciro grew up and notice that the only fun game is that of becoming [become rich and famous](ciro-santilli-s-selfish-desires.md) in the real world.

This explains however Ciro's [tool-assisted speedrun](tool-assisted-speedrun.md) interests.

Outside of video games, Ciro got mildly addicted to [Magic: The Gathering](magic-the-gathering-split.md) in his early teens.
