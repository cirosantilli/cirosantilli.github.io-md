# Possible new effects in superconductive tunnelling

↑ **Parent:** [History of the Josephson effect](history-of-the-josephson-effect.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Physical Review Letters](physical-review-letters.md)

The inaugural that predicted the [Josephson effect](josephson-effect.md).

Published on [Physics Letters](physics-letters.md), then a new journal, before they split into [Physics Letters A](physics-letters-a.md) and [Physics Letters B](physics-letters-b.md). [True Genius: The Life and Science of John Bardeen](true-genius-the-life-and-science-of-john-bardeen.md) mentions that this choice was made rather than the more prestigious [Physical Review Letters](physical-review-letters.md) because they were not yet so confident about the results.

[Paywalled](closed-access-academic-journals-are-evil.md) by [Elsevier](elsevier.md) as of 2023 at: [https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/0031916362913690](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/0031916362913690)
