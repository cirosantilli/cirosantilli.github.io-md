# Lorentz boost

↑ **Parent:** [Lorentz group](lorentz-group.md)

Two observers travel at fixed speed relative to each other. They synchronize origins at x=0 and t=0, and their spacial axes are perfectly aligned. This is a subset of the [Lorentz group](lorentz-group.md). TODO confirm it does not form a subgroup however.
