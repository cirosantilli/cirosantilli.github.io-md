[http://www.silkqin.com/](http://www.silkqin.com/)

John Thompson's Guqin website.

Holy crap amazing list of Guqin pieces by the guy for MP3 download! [http://www.silkqin.com/06hear.htm](http://www.silkqin.com/06hear.htm) And the explanations are insane. What a dude. [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md)'s hero.

Download all [MP3](mp3.md):
```
wget -r -np -l 1 -A mp3 http://www.silkqin.com/06hear.htm
```

[Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) Contacted John by email in 2019 telling him to put his stuff on [YouTube](youtube.md) and offering help, and he replied, but nothing came of it unfortunately. Edit: he uploaded a bunch of videos of him playing live in 2020! [https://www.youtube.com/user/silkqin/videos](https://www.youtube.com/user/silkqin/videos)

John focuses on playing the tunes in a "historically informed performance", in particular using silk strings rather than metal ones which are used by most modern artists: [http://www.silkqin.com/08anal/hip.htm](http://www.silkqin.com/08anal/hip.htm)
