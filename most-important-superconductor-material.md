# Most important superconductor material

↑ **Parent:** [Applications of superconductivity](applications-of-superconductivity.md)

As of 2023 the most important ones economicaly were:
- [Nb-Ti](niobium-titanium.md): the most widely used one. Used e.g. to create the [magnetic fields](magnetic-field.md) of the [Large Hadron Collider](large-hadron-collider.md) Up to 15 [T](tesla-unit.md).
- [Nb-Sn](niobium-tin.md): more expensive than [Nb-Ti](niobium-titanium.md), but can reach up to 30 [T](tesla-unit.md).
The main application is [magnetic resonance imaging](magnetic-resonance-imaging.md). Both of these are have to be [Liquid helium](liquid-helium.md), i.e. they are not "[high-temperature superconductor](high-temperature-superconductivity.md)" which is a pain. One big strength they have is that they are [metallic](metal.md), and therefore can made into wires, which is crucial to be able to make [electromagnetic coils](electromagnetic-coil.md) out of them.
