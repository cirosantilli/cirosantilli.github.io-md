# Discrete logarithm of the cyclic group

↑ **Parent:** [Discrete logarithm](discrete-logarithm.md)

This is the [discrete logarithm](discrete-logarithm.md) problem where the group is a [cyclic group](cyclic-group.md).

In this case, the problem becomes equivalent to reversing [modular exponentiation](modular-exponentiation.md).

This computational problem forms the basis for [Diffie-Hellman key exchange](diffie-hellman-key-exchange.md), because [modular exponentiation](modular-exponentiation.md) can be efficiently computed, but no known way exists to efficiently compute the reverse function.
