### Tokyo

↑ **Parent:** [City in Japan](#city-in-japan)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo)

Literally: "capital of the East". Note that the [Chinese](linguistics.md#chinese-language) term 东京 (dong1 jing1) is highly ambiguous in historical contexts, as many places throughout history have been known as "the capital of the East".
- [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/東京](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/東京)
- [https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/東京_(消歧義)](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/東京_(消歧義))
Of of those other places is the [Kaifeng](china.md#kaifeng), which is [the Capital](china.md#the-capital-water-margin-location) in [Water Margin](china.md#water-margin) and in real life during the [Song dynasty](china.md#song-dynasty). The novel is also likely highly popular in Japan apparently as well BTW, e.g. it merited a well funded adaptation: [The Water Margin](china.md#the-water-margin-1973-tv-series).
