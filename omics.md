# Omics

↑ **Parent:** [Systems biology](systems-biology.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omics)

Each of the omics studies a subset of [molecular biology](molecular-biology-split.md) with a data intensive and broad point of view that tries to understand global function or organisms, trying to understand what every biologically relevant [molecule](molecule.md) does as part of the hole [metabolism](metabolism.md).

The main omics are:
- [proteomics](proteomics.md)
- [metabolomics](metabolomics.md)

[Omics](omics.md) might be [stamp collecting](stamp-collecting.md), but maybe it is a bit more like [Trading card game](trading-card-game.md)/[Magic: The Gathering](magic-the-gathering-split.md) collecting, in which the cards that you are collecting actually have specific uses and interactions, especially considering that most [metabolic pathways](metabolic-pathway.md) are analogous across many [species](species.md).
