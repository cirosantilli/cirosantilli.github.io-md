# Derivation of the Dirac equation

↑ **Parent:** [Dirac equation](dirac-equation.md)

The [Dirac equation](dirac-equation.md) can be derived basically "directly" from the [Representation theory of the Lorentz group](representation-theory-of-the-lorentz-group.md) for the [spin half](spin-half.md) representation, this is shown for example at [Physics from Symmetry by Jakob Schwichtenberg (2015)](physics-from-symmetry-by-jakob-schwichtenberg-2015.md) 6.3 "Dirac Equation".

The Diract equation is the [spacetime symmetry](spacetime-symmetry.md) part of the [quantum electrodynamics Lagrangian](quantum-electrodynamics-lagrangian.md), i.e. is describes how [spin half](spin-half.md) particles behave without interactions. The full [quantum electrodynamics Lagrangian](quantum-electrodynamics-lagrangian.md) can then be reached by adding the [$U(1)$](unitary-group-of-degree-1.md) [internal symmetry](internal-symmetry.md).

As mentioned at [spin comes naturally when adding relativity to quantum mechanics](spin-number-of-a-field.md), this same method allows us to analogously derive the equations for other [spin numbers](spin-number-of-a-field.md).
