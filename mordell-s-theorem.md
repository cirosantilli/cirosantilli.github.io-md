↑ **Parent:** [Number of elements of an elliptic curve over the rational numbers](number-of-elements-of-an-elliptic-curve-over-the-rational-numbers.md)

The [elliptic curve group](elliptic-curve-group.md) of all [elliptic curve over the rational numbers](elliptic-curve-over-the-rational-numbers.md) is always a [finitely generated group](finitely-generated-group.md).

The number of points may be either finite or infinite. But when infinite, it is still a [finitely generated group](finitely-generated-group.md).

For this reason, the [rank of an elliptic curve over the rational numbers](rank-of-an-elliptic-curve-over-the-rational-numbers.md) is always defined.
