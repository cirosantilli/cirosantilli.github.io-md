# Matrix congruence can be seen as the change of basis of a bilinear form

↑ **Parent:** [Congruent matrix](congruent-matrix.md)

From [effect of a change of basis on the matrix of a bilinear form](effect-of-a-change-of-basis-on-the-matrix-of-a-bilinear-form.md), remember that a change of basis $C$ modifies the [matrix representation of a bilinear form](matrix-representation-of-a-bilinear-form.md) as:

$$
C^T M C
$$

So, by taking $S = C^T$, we understand that two matrices being congruent means that they can both correspond to the same [bilinear form](bilinear-form.md) in different bases.
