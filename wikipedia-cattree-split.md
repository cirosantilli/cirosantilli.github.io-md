# Wikipedia CatTree

↑ **Parent:** [Ciro Santilli's data projects](ciro-santilli-s-data-projects.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Ciro Santilli's minor projects](ciro-santilli-s-minor-projects.md)

This mini-project walks the category hierarchy [Wikipedia dumps](wikipedia-dumps.md) and dumps them in various simple formats, [HTML](html.md) being the most interesting!
- [HTML](html.md) dumps: [https://cirosantilli.com/wikipedia-cattree/](https://cirosantilli.com/wikipedia-cattree/)
- methodology: [https://stackoverflow.com/questions/17432254/wikipedia-category-hierarchy-from-dumps/77313490#77313490](https://stackoverflow.com/questions/17432254/wikipedia-category-hierarchy-from-dumps/77313490#77313490)

Scripts used:
- [wikipedia/import-sqlite.sh](wikipedia/import-sqlite.sh)
- [wikipedia/sqlite_preorder.py](wikipedia/sqlite_preorder.py)
- [wikipedia/wikipedia-cattree.sh](wikipedia/wikipedia-cattree.sh)
