# Finite general linear group

↑ **Parent:** [General linear group](general-linear-group.md)

[general linear group](general-linear-group.md) over a [finite field](finite-field.md) of order $m$. Remember that due to the [classification of finite fields](classification-of-finite-fields.md), there is one single field for each [prime power](prime-power.md) $m$.

Exactly as over the [real numbers](real-number.md), you just put the finite field elements into a $n \times n$ matrix, and then take the invertible ones.
