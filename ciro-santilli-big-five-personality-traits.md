This is how [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) evaluates himself on the [Big Five personality traits](big-five-personality-traits.md):

- Conscientiousness: low, Ciro is driven very strongly by [internal passion](effortless-effort.md) rather than external expectations
- Extraversion: high online, e.g. [Ciro Santilli's campaign for freedom of speech in China](ciro-santilli-s-campaign-for-freedom-of-speech-in-china.md), but much lower in the real world, no patience for something he's not [Googled](google-split.md) for in the last 5 seconds
- Agreeableness: high, see e.g. [Ciro Santilli's self perceived compassionate personality](ciro-santilli-s-self-perceived-compassionate-personality.md). But Ciro has built some tolerance disagreement online for it online during [Ciro Santilli's campaign for freedom of speech in China](ciro-santilli-s-campaign-for-freedom-of-speech-in-china.md), you've got to fight for what is right.
- Neuroticism: medium high, Ciro does have some anxiety. It does help get things done sometimes, but it also sometimes gets in the way.
