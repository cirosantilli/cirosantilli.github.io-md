# Scholar-official

↑ **Parent:** [Chinese scholar](chinese-scholar.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scholar-official)

The "Chinese scholar" is one of the traditional male ideals of [Chinese culture](chinese-culture.md).

Particular attributes the ideal Chinese scholar would have include:
- mastery of the [Four arts](four-arts.md), which also implies the [Four Treasures of the Study](four-treasures-of-the-study.md) for caligraphy and painting
- live in a place that includes
  - a [Chinese garden](chinese-garden.md)
It is in a sense sad (or awesome) that the [computer](computer-split.md) has essentially replaced, or one may argue, enhanced beyong recognition, all of the traditional four arts, to the point that Ciro can't touch anything that is not a computer. Sometimes the old arts live on particularly closely though, e.g. in [computer music](computer-music.md), [computer graphics](computer-graphics.md) and [computer Go](computer-go.md).
