# SARS-CoV-2 S protein

↑ **Parent:** [SARS-CoV-2 structural protein](sars-cov-2-structural-protein.md)

Spike.

Nucleocapsid phosphoprotein, sticks to the [RNA](rna.md) inside.

[https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-020-20768-y](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-020-20768-y) mentions functions:
- helps pack the viral RNA into the capsule
- also has a side function in immune suppression
