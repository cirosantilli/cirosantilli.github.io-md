# Protein tag

↑ **Parent:** [Fluorescent tag](fluorescent-tag.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protein_tag)

You modify the [DNA](dna-split.md) of a cell and stick a [fluorescent protein](fluorescent-protein.md) right before or after another protein. Then when it gets [translated](translation-biology.md), the GFP is stuck to the protein of interest, which hopefully hasn't lost its function as a result, then you can just see the protein of interest.
