One of the most beautiful things is how they paywall even [public domain](public-domain.md) works. E.g. here: [https://www.nature.com/articles/119558a0](https://www.nature.com/articles/119558a0) was published in 1927, and is therefore in the public domain as of 2023. But it is of course just paywalled as usual throughout 2023. There is zero incentive for them to open anything up.
