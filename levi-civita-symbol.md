# Levi-Civita symbol

↑ **Parent:** [Index picking function](index-picking-function.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Levi-Civita_symbol)

Denoted by the [Greek letter epsilon](epsilon-letter.md) with `\varepsilon` encoding in [LaTeX](latex.md).

Definition:
- [odd permutation](odd-permutation.md): -1
- [even permutation](even-permutation.md): 1
- not a [permutation](permutation.md): 0. This happens iff two more more indices are repeated
