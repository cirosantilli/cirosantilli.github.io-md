# Rank of an elliptic curve over the rational numbers

↑ **Parent:** [Mordell's theorem](mordell-s-theorem.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Rank of a group](rank-of-a-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rank_of_an_elliptic_curve)

[Mordell's theorem](mordell-s-theorem.md) guarantees that [the rank](rank-of-a-group.md) (number of elements in the [generating set of the group](generating-set-of-a-group.md)) is always well defined for an [elliptic curve over the rational numbers](elliptic-curve-over-the-rational-numbers.md). But as of 2023 there is no known algorithm which calculates the rank of any curve!

It is not even known if there are elliptic curves of every rank or not: [Largest known ranks of an elliptic curve over the rational numbers](largest-known-ranks-of-an-elliptic-curve-over-the-rational-numbers.md), and it has proven extremely hard to find new ones over time.
