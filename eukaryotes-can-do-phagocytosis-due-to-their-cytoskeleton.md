# Eukaryotes can do phagocytosis due to their cytoskeleton

↑ **Parent:** [Advantage of eukaryote over bacteria](advantage-of-eukaryote-over-bacteria.md)

[Power, Sex, Suicide by Nick Lane (2006)](power-sex-suicide-by-nick-lane-2006.md) page 53 suggests that one tremendous advantage of [eukaryotes](eukaryote.md) over [bacteria](bacteria.md) is their ability to change shape due to the presence of the [cytoskeleton](cytoskeleton.md), and the lack of a rigid [bacterial cell wall](bacterial-cell-wall.md).

Imagine in a world where there are only [bacteria](bacteria.md), and you can eat entire bacteria in one go, what a huge advantage that is!
