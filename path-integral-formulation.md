# Path integral formulation

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical formulation of quantum field theory](mathematical-formulation-of-quantum-field-theory.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Equivalent alternatives to the Schrodinger equation](equivalent-alternatives-to-the-schrodinger-equation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Path_integral_formulation)

This one might actually be understandable! It is what [Richard Feynman](richard-feynman-split.md) starts to explain at: [Richard Feynman Quantum Electrodynamics Lecture at University of Auckland (1979)](richard-feynman-quantum-electrodynamics-lecture-at-university-of-auckland-1979.md).

The difficulty is then proving that the total probability remains at 1, and maybe causality is hard too.

The path integral formulation can be seen as a generalization of the [double-slit experiment](double-slit-experiment.md) to infinitely many slits.

Feynman first stared working it out for [non-relativistic quantum mechanics](non-relativistic-quantum-mechanics.md), with the relativistic goal in mind, and only later on he attained the relativistic goal.
