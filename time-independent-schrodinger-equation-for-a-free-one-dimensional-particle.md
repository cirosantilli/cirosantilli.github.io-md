<h1 id="time-independent-schrodinger-equation-for-a-free-one-dimensional-particle">Time-independent Schrödinger equation for a free one dimensional particle</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Schrödinger equation for a free one dimensional particle](schrodinger-equation-for-a-free-one-dimensional-particle.md)



$$
\pdv{^2}{x^2} \psi(x) = -\frac{2mE}{\hbar^2} \psi(x)
$$

so the solution is:

$$
\psi(x) = e^{\frac{\sqrt{2mE} i x}{\hbar}}
$$

We notice that the solution has [continuous spectrum](continuous-spectrum-functional-analysis.md), since any value of $E$ can provide a solution.
