# Physics engine

↑ **Parent:** [Simulation software](simulation-software.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physics_engine)

There is no clear distinction between "serious simulations" and "physics engines", it's just that "physics engine" have a "for [video game](video-game-split.md)" connotation.

And especially, in the context of gaming, it usually means "[rigid body dynamics](rigid-body-dynamics.md) simulation" in particular.
