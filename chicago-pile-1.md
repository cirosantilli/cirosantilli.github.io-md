Graphite was expensive because it had to be [boron](boron.md)-free, since [boron](boron.md) absorbs [neutrons](neutron.md). But a [boron](boron.md) process was the main way to make [graphite](graphite.md). This type of pure graphite is known as [nuclear graphite](nuclear-graphite.md).
