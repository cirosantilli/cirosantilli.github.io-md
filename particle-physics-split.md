# Particle physics

↑ **Parent:** [Physics](physics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Particle_physics)

Currently an informal name for the [Standard Model](standard-model-split.md)

Chronological outline of the key theories:
- [Maxwell's equations](maxwell-s-equations.md)
- [Schrödinger equation](schrodinger-equation.md)
  - Date: 1926
  - Numerical predictions:
    - [hydrogen spectral line](hydrogen-emission-spectrum.md), excluding finer structure such as 2p up and down split: [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fine-structure_constant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fine-structure_constant)
- [Dirac equation](dirac-equation.md)
  - Date: 1928
  - Numerical predictions:
    - [hydrogen spectral line](hydrogen-emission-spectrum.md) including 2p split, but excluding even finer structure such as [Lamb shift](lamb-shift.md)
  - Qualitative predictions:
    - Antimatter
    - Spin as part of the equation
- [quantum electrodynamics](quantum-electrodynamics.md)
  - Date: 1947 onwards
  - Numerical predictions:
    - [Lamb shift](lamb-shift.md)
  - Qualitative predictions:
    - Antimatter
    - [spin](spin-physics.md) as part of the equation
