# TransferWise

↑ **Parent:** [Financial company](financial-company.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Good](good.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wise_(company))

This is a good company, first they truly helped reduce international transfer fees. They they continued to morph into a decent [challenger bank](challenger-bank.md).

Their Wise Interest account was amazing as of late 2023: [https://wise.com/gb/interest/](https://wise.com/gb/interest/)

Instant access with representative national interests and 0.29% fees.

Brick and mortar banks were way way behind in that regard!

E.g. October 2023, Wise was doing 4.87% interest after fees, while Barclay's best option was 1.16% above 5k pounds on the Rainy Day Saver (5% below). Ridiculous!

Update: On November 2023 unfortunately they more than doubled their fees from 0.19% to 0.46%:
- [https://github.com/cirosantilli/cirosantilli.github.io/issues/118](https://github.com/cirosantilli/cirosantilli.github.io/issues/118)
- [https://www.reddit.com/r/transferwiser/comments/180pilt/why_did_wise_interest_gbp_fees_just_go_up/](https://www.reddit.com/r/transferwiser/comments/180pilt/why_did_wise_interest_gbp_fees_just_go_up/)
- [https://www.quora.com/unanswered/Why-did-Wise-Interest-GBP-fees-went-up-massively-from-0-19-to-0-46-in-November-21-2023](https://www.quora.com/unanswered/Why-did-Wise-Interest-GBP-fees-went-up-massively-from-0-19-to-0-46-in-November-21-2023)
but it still was a good option to keep cash in.
