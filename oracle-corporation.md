# Oracle Corporation

↑ **Parent:** [Software company](software-company.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oracle_Corporation)

[Evil](evil.md) company that desecrated the beauty created by [Sun Microsystems](sun-microsystems.md), and was [trying to bury Java once and or all in the 2010's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_v._Oracle_America).

Their database is already matched by [open source](open-source-software.md) e.g. [PostgreSQL](postgresql.md), and [ERP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enterprise_resource_planning) and [CRM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Customer_relationship_management) specific systems are boring.

Oracle basically grew out of selling one of the first [SQL](sql-split.md) implementations in the late 70's, and notably to the [United States Government](united-states-government.md) and particularly the [CIA](central-intelligence-agency.md). They did deliver a lot of value in those early pre-internet days, but now [open source](open-source-software.md) is and will supplant them entirely.
