# Complete basis

↑ **Parent:** [Hilbert space](hilbert-space.md)

Finding a complete basis such that each vector solves a given [differential equation](differential-equation.md) is the basic method of solving [partial differential equation](partial-differential-equation.md) through [separation of variables](separation-of-variables.md).

The first example of this you must see is [solving partial differential equations with the Fourier series](solving-partial-differential-equations-with-the-fourier-series.md).

Notable examples:
- [Fourier series](fourier-series.md) for the [heat equation](heat-equation.md) as shown at [Fourier basis is complete for $\LTwo$](fourier-basis-is-complete-for-l2.md) and [solving partial differential equations with the Fourier series](solving-partial-differential-equations-with-the-fourier-series.md)
- [Hermite functions](hermite-functions.md) for the [quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator.md)
- [Legendre polynomials](legendre-polynomials.md) for [Laplace's equation](laplace-s-equation.md) in [spherical coordinates](spherical-coordinate-system.md)
- [Bessel function](bessel-function.md) for the [2D wave equation on a circular domain](2d-wave-equation-on-a-circular-domain.md) in [polar coordinates](polar-coordinate-system.md)
