# Norm (mathematics)

↑ **Parent:** [Metric space](metric-space.md)

Vs [metric](metric-mathematics.md):
- a norm is the size of one element. A [metric](metric-mathematics.md) is the distance between two elements.
- a norm is only defined on a [vector space](vector-space.md). A [metric](metric-mathematics.md) could be defined on something that is not a vector space. Most basic examples however are also [vector spaces](vector-space.md).
