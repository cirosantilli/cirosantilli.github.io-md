# Gaseous diffusion

↑ **Parent:** [Isotope separation](isotope-separation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaseous_diffusion)

This [isotope separation](isotope-separation.md) method was the first big successful method, having been used in the [Manhattan Project](manhattan-project.md), notably in the [K-25](k-25.md) reactor.

This method was superseded by the more efficient [gas centrifuges](gas-centrifuge.md).
