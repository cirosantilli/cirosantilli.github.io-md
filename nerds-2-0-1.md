Part 1 - Networking The Nerds talks about the [TCP/IP](tcp-ip.md) and early machines implementing it:
- 21:00: shows inside [The Pentagon](the-pentagon.md). The way the dude who works there opens a his locked office door with an electric switch is just amazing. Cringely also mentions that there's an actual official speed limit in the corridors as he rides a carrier bike slowly through them.
- 21:45: the universities weren't enthusiastic, because people from other locations would be able to use your precious computer time. But finally [ARPA](arpa.md) forced the universities' hands, and they joined.
- 24:24 mentions that some of the guys who created ARPANET were actually previously counting cards at Casinos in Las Vegas, just like in the [21 (2008) film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/21_(2008_film))
- one of the centerpieces of development was at [UCLA](university-of-california-los-angeles.md). The other was the [BBN company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raytheon_BBN). 33:55 shows the first [router](router-computing.md), then called them [Interface Message Processor](interface-message-processor.md)
- the first message was from [UCLA](university-of-california-los-angeles.md) to [Stanford University](stanford-university.md). He was trying to write "Login", and it crashed at the 'g'. Epic. They later debugged it.
- towards the end talks about [ALOHAnet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ALOHAnet), the first wireless computer communication done

Part 2 - Serving the Suits
- Robert Metcalfe. He's nice. [Xerox PARC](xerox-parc.md). [Ethernet](ethernet.md).
- Explains what is a "Workstation", notably showing one by [Sun Microsystems](sun-microsystems.md). This is now an obscure "passé" thing in 2020 that young people like [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) have only heard of in legend (or in outdated university computer labs!). Funny to think that so many people have had this idea before, including e.g. the [Chromebook](chromebook.md)
- 10:46 mentions that all of [Cisco](cisco.md), [Silicon Graphics](silicon-graphics.md) and [Sun Microsystems](sun-microsystems.md) and where founded at Margaret Jacks Hall, Building 460, at [Stanford University](stanford-university.md).
- he then talks a lot about Sun. Sun became dominant in [Wall Street](wall-street.md).
- 19:05: Novell, from [Utah](utah.md). How they almost went bust, but were saved at the last moment by [Ray Noorda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ray_Noorda), who refocused them to their NetWare product which was under recent development. It allowed file and printer sharing in [IBM PCs](ibm-personal-computer.md). 22:55 shows how they had a live radio host for people waiting on customer support calls!
- 33:56 mentions how The Grateful Dead had in impact on the Internet, as people wanted computers to be able to access [The WELL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_WELL) online forum. They still own the domain as of 2022: [https://www.well.com/](https://www.well.com/). It is interesting how [Larry Page](larry-page.md) also liked The Grateful Dead as mentioned at [The Google Story](the-google-story.md), his dad would take him to shows. Larry is a bit younger of course than the people in this documentary.
- 37 show McAfee
- 43:56: fantastic portrait of [Cisco](cisco.md)

Part 3 - Wiring the World:
- Berners-Lee at [CERN](cern.md) and the invention of the [URL](url.md).
- 1992: [US Government](united-states-government.md) allow commerce on the [Internet](internet.md)
- [Web browser](web-browser.md) history, [Mosaic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mosaic_(web_browser)) and Marc Andresseeen.
- 20:45: America Online
- 23:29: [search engines](search-engine.md) and Excite. [Google](google-split.md) was a bit too small to be on his radar!
- 25:50: [porn](pornography.md)
- 27: [The Motley Fool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Motley_Fool) and advertising
- 30: Planet U grocery shopping
- 31:50: [Amazon](amazon-split.md)
- 33:00: immigrant workers, Indians playing cricket, outsourcing, Wipro Systems
- 41:25: [Java](java-programming-language.md)
- 46:30: [Microsoft](microsoft-split.md) joins the [Internet](internet.md). The Internet Tidal Wave Internet memo. Pearl Harbour day talk.
- 56:40: Excite Tour. If they had survived, they would have been [Google](google-split.md) with their quirky offices.
