# 100 Greatest Discoveries by the Discovery Channel (2004-2005)

↑ **Parent:** [Scientific documentary](scientific-documentary.md)

[https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0442715/](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0442715/) on [IMDb](imdb.md)

Hosted by [Bill Nye](bill-nye.md).

[Physics](physics-split.md) topics:
- [Galileo](galileo-galilei.md): objects of different masses fall at the same speed, hammer and feather experiment
- [Newton](isaac-newton.md): [gravity](gravity.md), linking locally observed falls and the movement of celestial bodies
- TODO a few more
- [superconductivity](superconductivity.md), talk only at [Fermilab](fermilab.md) accelerator, no re-enactment even...
- [quark](quark.md), interview with [Murray Gell-Mann](murray-gell-mann.md), mentions it was "an off-beat field, one wasn't encouraged to work on that". High level blablabla obviously.
- [fundamental interactions](fundamental-interaction.md), notably [weak interaction](weak-interaction.md) and [strong interaction](strong-interaction.md), interview with [Michio Kaku](michio-kaku.md). When asked "How do we know that the weak force is there?" the answer is: "We observe [radioactive decay](radioactive-decay.md) with a [Geiger counter](geiger-counter.md)". Oh, come on!

[biology](biology-split.md) topics:
- [Leeuwenhoek microscope](leeuwenhoek-microscope.md) and the discovery of [microorganisms](microorganism.md), and how pond water is not dead, but teeming with life. No sample of course.
- 1831 Robert Brown [cell nucleus](cell-nucleus.md) in plants, and later [Theodor Schwann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theodor_Schwann) in tadpoles. This prepared the path for the idea that "all cells come from other cells", and the there seemed to be an unifying theme to all life: the precursor to [DNA](dna-split.md) discoveries. Re-enactment, yay.
- 1971 [Carl Woese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_Woese) and the discovery of [archaea](archaea.md)

[Genetics](genetics.md):
- Mendel. Reenactment.
- 1909 [Thomas Hunt Morgan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Hunt_Morgan) with [Drosophila melanogaster](drosophila-melanogaster.md). Reenactment. Genes are in [Chromosomes](chromosome.md). He observed that a trait was linked to sex, and it was already known that sex was related to chromosomes.
- 1935 [George Beadle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Beadle) and the [one gene one enzyme hypothesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_gene–one_enzyme_hypothesis) by shooting X-rays at bread mold
- 1942 [Barbara McClintock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbara_McClintock), at [Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory](cold-spring-harbor-laboratory.md)
- 1952 [Hershey–Chase experiment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hershey–Chase_experiment). Determined that [DNA](dna-split.md) is what transmits genetic information, not [protein](protein-split.md), by [radioactive](radioactive-decay.md) labelling both protein and DNA in two sets of [bacteriophages](bacteriophage.md). They observed that only the DNA radioactive material was passed forward.
- Crick Watson
- [messenger RNA](messenger-rna.md), no specific scientist, too many people worked on it, done partially with [bacteriophage](bacteriophage.md) experiments
- 1968 Nirenberg [genetic code](genetic-code.md)
- 1972 [Hamilton O. Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamilton_O._Smith) and the discovery of [restriction enzymes](restriction-enzyme.md) by observing that they were part of anti [bacteriophage](bacteriophage.md) immune-system present in [bacteria](bacteria.md)
- [alternative splicing](alternative-splicing.md)
- RNA interference
- [Human Genome Project](human-genome-project.md), interview with [Craig Venter](craig-venter.md).

Medicine:
- blood circulation
- anesthesia
- [X-ray](x-ray.md)
- [germ theory of disease](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germ_theory_of_disease), with examples from Ignaz Semmelweis and Pasteur
- 1796 Edward Jenner discovery of vaccination by noticing that [cowpox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cowpox) cowpox infected subjects were immune
- [vitamin](vitamin-split.md) by observing scurvy and beriberi in sailors, confirmed by Frederick Gowland Hopkins on mice experiments
- Fleming, Florey and Chain and the discovery of penicillin
- [Prontosil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prontosil)
- diabetes and insulin
