# Elis Regina

↑ **Parent:** [Popular Brazilian music musician](popular-brazilian-music-musician.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elis_Regina)

Mostly interpreter of songs written by others. But she's just too amazing, many of her interpretations are better than the original.

[God](god.md), even [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli-split.md) is slightly shocked by her death, which happened before he was born, can you imagine it at the time? She was MPB's golden girl...
